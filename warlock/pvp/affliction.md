## How to play

PvP has no fixed rotation: it depends on who you fight. No simulator plays PvP, so this build rests on the talents' descriptions and the community's PvP builds.

**Why these talents:** Soul Link moved to Demonology tier 5 (behind Demonic Sacrifice at tier 3), so a Soul Link warlock can now also reach Shadow Mastery, Siphon Life, Malevolence and Curse of Exhaustion: the Classic 20/31/0 becomes 30/21/0 with far more damage.
