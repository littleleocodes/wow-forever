## How to play

**Setup, before the pull:** **Summon Imp**, then **Demonic Sacrifice** it: you fight with its buff and no demon, which the rotation below assumes. Keep **Demon Armor** on you.

**Before the pull:** **Life Tap**.

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Conflagrate**
2. **Life Tap** when your mana is below 5%
3. **Bane of Agony**: keep it on the target
4. **Corruption**: keep it on the target, until the last 10 seconds of the fight
5. **Curse of the Elements**: keep it on the target
6. **Immolate**: keep it on the target, until the last 10 seconds of the fight
7. **Shadow Bolt** (rank 9)

**Shadow Bolt** is your filler: cast it whenever nothing above is ready.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Searing Pain**: in the last 5.25 seconds of the fight, when there is no time left for another **Shadow Bolt**

## Why this rotation

The Shadow Bolt hybrid is the classic DS/Ruin warlock (**Demonic Sacrifice** plus **Ruin**) with Destruction's **Conflagrate** and **Shadow and Flame**: before the pull put on **Demon Armor**, summon the Imp, use **Demonic Sacrifice** (+15% Shadow damage for 2 hours, worth about 10% of your damage in the simulator) and **Life Tap** once. Then keep **Curse of the Elements** and your dots on the boss, use **Conflagrate** when it is ready and cast **Shadow Bolt** the rest of the time.

**Why it works**

- Before the pull: summon the Imp, use **Demonic Sacrifice** (summoning any demon ends it), then **Life Tap** once so you start full. Put a Spellstone (**Create Spellstone**) on your weapon on top of your wizard oil: Forever lets them stack, and the simulator measured about 3% more damage.
- **Curse of the Elements** goes up first: one cast lasts 5 minutes and makes the boss take 10% more damage from every magic school (Curse of Shadow is gone in Forever). As a Curse it sits beside your **Bane of Agony**.
- **Life Tap** waits until you are below 5% mana: every tap is a global cooldown with no damage. **Improved Life Tap** adds 20% to each tap, and the list uses a mana potion or a Demonic Rune when your mana runs low, never in the last 15 seconds.
- **Bane of Agony** grows over its 24 seconds, so recast it only once it has run out. **Corruption** (a 1.2-second cast with this build's 2 points in **Improved Corruption**) goes back up when it drops, except in the last 10 seconds.
- Start a new **Immolate** when less time is left on the old one than its cast (1.5 seconds with the **Bane** talent), so it lands as the old one ends: **Conflagrate** needs it on the boss. With 5 points in **Shadow and Flame** a **Conflagrate** no longer uses it up and gives you 20% more Shadow damage for 20 seconds.
- **Shadow Bolt** is the filler: its crits trigger **Improved Shadow Bolt** (20% more Shadow damage from you for 12 seconds, your dots included) and **Ruin** doubles their bonus.

**Caveats**

- **Searing Pain** has no cooldown: the rotation casts it only in the last few seconds, when a **Shadow Bolt** would not finish before the boss dies. Never use it as a filler.
- With the Imp sacrificed you play without a demon: any summon ends the buff, so if you die, summon the Imp and sacrifice it again before the next pull.
- On the move, use the instants: **Bane of Agony**, **Curse of the Elements**, **Conflagrate** and **Life Tap**; **Corruption** has a 1.2-second cast in this build.

**When to change it**

- Near the end of a fight start no dot the boss will not outlive: the list tells you the last seconds in which **Immolate** and **Corruption** are still started. Cast **Shadow Bolt** then.
- A boss needs only one **Curse of the Elements** (in Classic two never stacked): if another warlock keeps it up, take the curse your raid gives you, such as **Curse of Recklessness** for the physical fighters.
- When healing is tight, each **Life Tap** costs health someone must heal: use your mana potion and Demonic Rune before you tap.
