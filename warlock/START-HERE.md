# Warlock: start here

[All classes](../START-HERE.md)

Classic's Shadow Bolt turret (DS/Ruin or SM/Ruin) with a pet for utility. Forever rebuilds all three trees — 22 new talents, 20 removed — and turns them into three real specs: a DoT-centric Affliction (Drain Hope, Pandemic), a pet Demonology (Demonic Pact, Demonic Knowledge) and a fire Destruction (Conflagrate, Incinerate). Trolls can now be warlocks.
