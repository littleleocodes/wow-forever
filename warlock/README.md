# Warlock

> Classic's Shadow Bolt turret (DS/Ruin or SM/Ruin) with a pet for utility. Forever rebuilds all three trees (21 new talents,
> 19 Classic talents gone) into a damage-over-time Affliction (Pandemic, Wrack), a demon Demonology (Demonic Pact, Demonic
> Knowledge, demons that scale from your gear) and a fire Destruction (Conflagrate, Shadow and Flame, Incinerate). Playable by
> Gnomes, Humans, Orcs, Trolls and Undead.

## Forever headline changes (beta client build 1.60.1.70425, 2026-10-16)

Forever's warlock has 52 talents (17 Affliction, 19 Demonology, 16 Destruction): 20 are new, 2 are Classic talents under a new name, and 18 Classic talents are gone.

- **Removed talents:** Dark Pact, Improved Curse of Weakness, Improved Curse of Exhaustion, Improved Drain Life, Improved Drain Soul, Improved Drain Mana (Affliction); Fel Intellect, Fel Stamina, Improved Healthstone, Improved Firestone, Improved Spellstone, Improved Enslave Demon (Demonology); Devastation, Improved Immolate, Emberstorm, Improved Searing Pain, Improved Firebolt, Improved Lash of Pain (Destruction).
- **New talents:** Soul Harvest, Improved Drains, Pandemic, Malevolence, Soul Siphon, Wrack (a 6-second Shadow channel that raises your other Shadow dots by 10%) in Affliction; Demonic Aegis, Fel Vitality, Demonic Energies, Decimation, Demonic Brand, Improved Felhunter, Demonic Knowledge, Demonic Pact in Demonology; Molten Skin, Agonizing Flames, Bane of Havoc, Fire and Brimstone, Shadow and Flame, Incinerate in Destruction. Malediction (all your periodic damage +5%, new in 70009) was replaced by Classic's Grim Reach (Affliction range +10/20%) in 70425.
- **Renamed:** Improved Curse of Agony is Improved Bane of Agony (2 ranks); Improved Succubus is Improved Sayaad.
- **Moved or reworked:** Improved Life Tap, Unholy Power, Improved Health Funnel and Bane sit at tier 1, Demonic Sacrifice at tier 3, Soul Link at tier 5 (30% of your damage taken goes to the demon, +3% damage for both); Ruin has 5 ranks; Suppression is +5% hit and 20% less threat; Improved Corruption makes Corruption instant and adds 10%; Improved Shadow Bolt gives 20% more Shadow damage from you for 12 seconds after a Shadow Bolt crit; Shadow Mastery is +2/4/6/8/10% Shadow damage (client 70425; +1-5% before); Amplify Curse strengthens Curse of Exhaustion by 50% (was 20%); Destructive Reach covers Destruction spells only; Nightfall procs from Corruption, the drains and Wrack. Demonic Sacrifice gives +15% Shadow damage for the Imp and +15% Fire damage for the Succubus (the reverse of Classic); Master Demonologist gives the demon's bonus to you both.
- **Spells:** Curse of Agony and Curse of Doom are Banes (Bane of Agony, Bane of Doom), so Curse of the Elements and Bane of Agony stay up together; Curse of the Elements covers every magic school and Curse of Shadow is gone. At level 60 the damage spells deal about half their Classic base damage (Shadow Bolt 253-283 against 482-538). Spellstone and Firestone are weapon enchants that stack with weapon oils.
- **Beta notes (to 2026-10-08):** demons scale from your stats (17% of your spell damage as attack power, 10% as spell damage, 35% of your armor, 7.5 health per Stamina, your spell crit; the 70291 client's numbers, this project's simulator patch uses them); Soul Harvesting renamed Soul Harvest; Drain Soul stops when you start another cast; Hellfire can crit; Demonic Brand reworked in 70291 (6 empowered pet attacks of 65-68 damage); Life Tap scales with Spirit.
- **Beta notes (16 Oct, build 70425):** Malediction replaced with Grim Reach; Shadow Mastery 2/4/6/8/10% (was 1-5%); Destructive Reach only
  Destruction spells, 10/20%; Soul Link can be cast on your pet again; only one Curse of Recklessness on a target; wands need 0.5 seconds
  standing still before shooting.
