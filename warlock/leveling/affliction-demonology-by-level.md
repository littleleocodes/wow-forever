# Affliction-Demonology Warlock — leveling, level by level

[Warlock](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Affliction-Demonology](affliction-demonology.md) > Level by level

> **Being checked:** Its filler pass is still running (points that do nothing at the levels they are held); its play check follows. The build and numbers on this page may change when it is done.

What the Affliction/Demonology hybrid (27 Affliction and 24 Demonology points) presses from level 1 to 60. It plays the Demonology list without the brand: the Voidwalker (from 10) holds the mob, **Corruption**, **Bane of Agony**, **Immolate** and from 30 **Siphon Life** tick, and the wand does the rest. The Affliction points come first (levels 10-36) and make your dots stronger; the Demonology points (37-60) make the Voidwalker stronger and end in **Soul Link** (57) and **Demonic Knowledge** (58-60). Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 27/24/0 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/warlock/60/2550002013500112-02552032010000013--12)), the one on the [Affliction-Demonology leveling page](affliction-demonology.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Immolate**, a **Shadow Bolt** while your mana is nearly full, then the wand, while the Imp throws **Firebolt**. |
| 4-7 | **Corruption** is your pull. From here the dots and the wand do the work, and **Shadow Bolt** waits for a nearly full mana bar. |
| 8-9 | **Bane of Agony** joins **Corruption**. **Fear** what reaches you: the Imp cannot hold a mob. |
| 10-19 | **Summon Voidwalker**: it holds the mob while you dot and wand. Talent points start: Affliction until 36, for your dots. |
| 20-29 | **Amplify Curse** (talent) makes the next **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger, every 3 minutes. One macro casts both. |
| 30-41 | **Siphon Life** (talent): a third dot that heals you. |
| 42-60 | **Death Coil** for a mob that reaches you while you are hurt. The rest stays. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; armor, summons, Soul Link, conjured stones, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q your bolt, E the control, R the payoff after it, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions and the pull, 1-4 the rest, the middle mouse button the wand. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and macros: the [warlock keybinds page](keybinds.md), written for Demonology; this guide changes the key its notes name.

## Levels 1-3: Immolate, Shadow Bolt and the Imp

**The trainer teaches:** Demon Skin (1), Firebolt (1), **Immolate** (1), **Shadow Bolt** (1), Summon Imp (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Shadow Bolt; 2 = Immolate; Middle mouse = Wand.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Imp in, then **Immolate** the mob.
2. **Shadow Bolt** while you have more than 80% of your mana: about one per mob.
3. Your wand for the rest.

- **Summon Imp** (1) before the first pull: its **Firebolt** casts by itself. Keep **Demon Skin** up at all times, from here to 20.
- The fight lab gives you a wand from level 1. Buy one as soon as you can: until then **Shadow Bolt** is the filler too.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 14 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Wand 1.9, Immolate 1.0, Shadow Bolt 1.0.*

## Levels 4-7: Corruption, the pull

**The trainer teaches:** Blood Pact (4), **Corruption** (4), Curse of Weakness (4), Life Tap (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Shadow Bolt at 6.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Corruption, pull macro; 4 = Life Tap (at 6).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, Mouse 4 Corruption, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Corruption** to pull (Mouse 4: the macro sends your demon too), on a mob above about a third of its health.
2. **Immolate** while you have more than half your mana.
3. Your wand until the mob dies. **Shadow Bolt** only above 80% of your mana.

- **Life Tap** (6): between fights, turn health into mana until you have about 70% of your mana, while your health stays above 60%. The next pull then starts with mana, and you rest the health back.
- **Curse of Weakness** (4) is not part of the list.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Wand 4.3, Corruption 1.0, Immolate 1.0, Shadow Bolt 0.1.*

## Levels 10-19: The Voidwalker tanks

**The trainer teaches:** Create Healthstone (10), Drain Soul (10), Summon Voidwalker (10), **Torment** (10), Health Funnel (12), Phase Shift (12), Curse of Recklessness (14), Drain Life (14), Fire Shield (14), Sacrifice (16), Unending Breath (16), Consume Shadows (18), Create Soulstone (18), Searing Pain (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Bane of Agony at 18; Corruption at 14; Immolate at 10; Life Tap at 16; Shadow Bolt at 12.

**Talents:** 10-11 Improved Life Tap, 12-15 Suppression, 16-19 Improved Corruption.

**Keys to set:** C = Healthstone macro; Shift+Mouse 4 = Drain Soul; Shift+R = Health Funnel (at 12); R = Drain Life (at 14).

**Your keys in this stage:** E Fear, Mouse 4 Corruption, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Voidwalker in with **Corruption** (Mouse 4), on a mob above about a third of its health; its **Torment** holds the mob.
2. **Bane of Agony** on a mob above about a third of its health.
3. **Immolate** while you have more than half your mana.
4. Your wand until the mob dies.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: **Fear** it and wand it while it runs, until the Voidwalker takes it back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

- **Summon Voidwalker** costs a Soul Shard: **Drain Soul** (10) a mob as it dies to get one (Shift+Mouse 4). The fight lab does not count shards; keep two or three in your bags for the demon and your Healthstone.
- Heal the Voidwalker between fights with **Consume Shadows** (18, its own spell, not usable in a fight) or **Health Funnel** (12). Check that its **Torment** is on autocast on the pet bar.
- **Shadow Bolt** waits for 80% of your mana from here on. With the dots and the wand you rarely have that much past the first pull after a rest, so the fight lab casts almost none.
- **Drain Life** (14) goes on R: from 20 it is the answer after **Fear**.
- **Improved Life Tap** (10-11) adds 20% to every **Life Tap**. **Suppression** (12-15, 33) gives up to 5% hit and cuts your threat by up to 20%, so the Voidwalker keeps the mob. **Improved Corruption** (16-19, 23) shortens **Corruption**'s cast; it is instant from 23.
- **Searing Pain** (18) stays off your bars: the Demonology list casts it for Demonic Brand, a talent this build does not take, and the fight lab casts almost none.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 21 at level 10, 26 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Wand 4.8, Torment 2.7, Bane of Agony 1.1, Immolate 1.0, Corruption 1.0.*

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Voidwalker in with **Corruption** (Mouse 4), on a mob above about a third of its health; its **Torment** holds the mob.

## Between fights

- **Life Tap** back to about 70% mana while your health stays above 60%. Your health comes back while you rest, and the Voidwalker takes the next mob.
- Eat only below 55% of your health. Drink only below 15% of your mana, when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**: the fight lab needs almost no drinking.
- Whenever you sit down to drink, eat as well if your health is not full, and drink while you eat if your mana is not full: the other bar fills in the same seconds at no extra time. Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. This build seldom sits down, so it saves almost nothing, but it never costs time.
- Heal a Voidwalker below 60% of its health with **Consume Shadows** (not usable in a fight), or with **Health Funnel** when it is below half.
- Keep two or three Soul Shards: **Drain Soul** a dying mob for one. **Summon Voidwalker** and **Create Healthstone** use one each, and a new Voidwalker costs one when the old one dies.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of your dots.
