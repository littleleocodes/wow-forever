# Affliction-Demonology Warlock — leveling, level by level

[Warlock](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Affliction-Demonology](affliction-demonology.md) > Level by level

What the Affliction/Demonology hybrid (27 Affliction and 24 Demonology points) presses from level 1 to 60. It plays the Demonology list: the Voidwalker (from 10) holds the mob, **Demonic Brand** (27) turns **Searing Pain** into extra Voidwalker hits, **Corruption** and **Bane of Agony** tick, and the wand does the rest. The Demonology points come first (levels 10-33) and make the Voidwalker a large share of your damage; the Affliction points (34-60) make your dots stronger. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 27/24/0 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/warlock/60/2551002013500102-00250332011003013--12)), the one on the [Affliction-Demonology leveling page](affliction-demonology.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Immolate**, a **Shadow Bolt** while your mana is nearly full, then the wand, while the Imp throws **Firebolt**. |
| 4-7 | **Corruption** is your pull. From here the dots and the wand do the work, and **Shadow Bolt** waits for a nearly full mana bar. |
| 8-9 | **Bane of Agony** joins **Corruption**. **Fear** what reaches you: the Imp cannot hold a mob. |
| 10-26 | **Summon Voidwalker**: it holds the mob while you dot and wand. Talent points start, all for the Voidwalker until 33. |
| 27-41 | **Demonic Brand** (talent): **Searing Pain** brands the mob, and the Voidwalker's next attacks deal extra damage. |
| 42-43 | **Death Coil** for a mob that reaches you while you are hurt. The rest stays. |
| 44-53 | **Amplify Curse** (talent) makes the next **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger, every 3 minutes. One macro casts both. |
| 54-60 | **Siphon Life** (talent): a third dot that heals you. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; armor, summons, Soul Link, conjured stones, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q your bolt, E the control, R the payoff after it, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions and the pull, 1-4 the rest, the middle mouse button the wand. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and macros: the [warlock keybinds page](keybinds.md), written for Demonology; this guide keeps its keys.

## Levels 1-3: Immolate, Shadow Bolt and the Imp

**The trainer teaches:** Demon Skin (1), Firebolt (1), **Immolate** (1), **Shadow Bolt** (1), Summon Imp (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Shadow Bolt; 2 = Immolate; Middle mouse = Wand.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Imp in, then **Immolate** the mob.
2. **Shadow Bolt** while you have more than 80% of your mana: about one per mob.
3. Your wand for the rest.

- **Summon Imp** (1) before the first pull: its **Firebolt** casts by itself. Keep **Demon Skin** up at all times, from here to 20.
- The fight lab gives you a wand from level 1. Buy one as soon as you can: until then **Shadow Bolt** is the filler too.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 14 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Wand 1.8, Shadow Bolt 1.1, Immolate 1.0.*

## Levels 4-7: Corruption, the pull

**The trainer teaches:** Blood Pact (4), **Corruption** (4), Curse of Weakness (4), Life Tap (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Shadow Bolt at 6.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Corruption, pull macro; 4 = Life Tap (at 6).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, Mouse 4 Corruption, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Corruption** to pull (Mouse 4: the macro sends your demon too), on a mob above about a third of its health.
2. **Immolate** while you have more than half your mana.
3. Your wand until the mob dies. **Shadow Bolt** only above 80% of your mana.

- **Life Tap** (6): between fights, turn health into mana until you have about 70% of your mana, while your health stays above 60%. The next pull then starts with mana, and you rest the health back.
- **Curse of Weakness** (4) is not part of the list.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Wand 4.2, Corruption 1.0, Immolate 1.0, Shadow Bolt 0.1.*

**The trainer teaches:** Create Healthstone (10), Drain Soul (10), Summon Voidwalker (10), **Torment** (10), Health Funnel (12), Phase Shift (12), Curse of Recklessness (14), Drain Life (14), Fire Shield (14), Sacrifice (16), Unending Breath (16), Consume Shadows (18), Create Soulstone (18), Searing Pain (18), Curse of the Elements (20), Demon Armor (20), Lash of Pain (20), Rain of Fire (20), Ritual of Summoning (20), Summon Incubus (20), Summon Succubus (20), Eye of Kilrogg (22), Soothing Kiss (22), Drain Mana (24), Sense Demons (24), Suffering (24), Curse of Tongues (26), Detect Invisibility (26), Seduction (26).

**New ranks to buy:** Bane of Agony at 18; Corruption at 14, 24; Drain Life at 22; Drain Soul at 24; Health Funnel at 20; Immolate at 10, 20; Life Tap at 16, 26; Shadow Bolt at 12, 20; Torment at 20.

**Talents:** 10-14 Unholy Power, 15 Demonic Energies, 16-18 Improved Voidwalker, 19 Demonic Energies, 20 Fel Vitality, 21-22 Demonic Embrace, 23-24 Fel Vitality, 25 Demonic Sacrifice, 26 Master Summoner.

**Keys to set:** C = Healthstone macro; Shift+Mouse 4 = Drain Soul; Shift+R = Health Funnel (at 12); R = Drain Life (at 14); Shift+1 = Rain of Fire (at 20).

**Your keys in this stage:** E Fear, Mouse 4 Corruption, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Voidwalker in with **Corruption** (Mouse 4), on a mob above about a third of its health; its **Torment** holds the mob.
2. **Bane of Agony** on a mob above about a third of its health.
3. **Immolate** while you have more than half your mana.
4. Your wand until the mob dies.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: **Fear** it and wand it while it runs, until the Voidwalker takes it back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

- **Summon Voidwalker** costs a Soul Shard: **Drain Soul** (10) a mob as it dies to get one (Shift+Mouse 4). The fight lab does not count shards; keep two or three in your bags for the demon and your Healthstone.
- Heal the Voidwalker between fights with **Consume Shadows** (18, its own spell, not usable in a fight) or **Health Funnel** (12). Check that its **Torment** is on autocast on the pet bar.
- **Shadow Bolt** waits for 80% of your mana from here on. With the dots and the wand you rarely have that much past the first pull after a rest, so the fight lab casts almost none.
- **Drain Life** (14) goes on R: from 27 it is the answer after **Fear**.
- **Unholy Power** (10-14) adds 10% to the Voidwalker's damage, **Demonic Energies** (15, 19) heals it for 15% of your spell damage, **Improved Voidwalker** (16-18) makes **Torment** and **Consume Shadows** 30% stronger, **Fel Vitality** (20, 23-24) adds 15% to the Voidwalker's health and mana and to your mana, and **Demonic Embrace** (21-22) adds 6% to your Stamina.
- **Demonic Sacrifice** (25) is a step: never sacrifice your tank. **Master Summoner** (26) shortens a new summon.
- **Demon Armor** (20) replaces **Demon Skin**. **Rain of Fire** (20) is for packs: one mob at a time never needs it. **Searing Pain** (18) waits for **Demonic Brand** at 27.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 10, 24 at level 20, 26 at level 26. Presses per kill at level 10: Wand 4.1, Torment 2.3, Bane of Agony 1.1, Immolate 1.0, Corruption 1.0.*

## Between fights

- **Life Tap** back to about 70% mana while your health stays above 60%. Your health comes back while you rest, and the Voidwalker takes the next mob.
- Eat only below 55% of your health. Drink only below 15% of your mana, when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**: the fight lab needs almost no drinking.
- Heal a Voidwalker below 60% of its health with **Consume Shadows** (not usable in a fight), or with **Health Funnel** when it is below half.
- Keep two or three Soul Shards: **Drain Soul** a dying mob for one. **Summon Voidwalker** and **Create Healthstone** use one each, and a new Voidwalker costs one when the old one dies.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of your dots.
