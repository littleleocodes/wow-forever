## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger. With **Soul Link**: turn it on once the Voidwalker is out.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. With **Demonic Brand**: **Searing Pain** whenever the brand is gone, the Voidwalker is hitting the mob, the mob has more than a third of its health and you have more than 30% of your mana.
2. **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** on a mob above about a third of its health; **Corruption** only on a mob above 35% of its health; **Immolate** only above half your mana, and not on a mob below about a third of its health.
3. Your wand is the filler: **Shadow Bolt** only above 80% mana, which the dots and brands rarely leave you.
4. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%; **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health.
5. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it, then **Drain Life** while it runs. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

**Between fights:** **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, eat only below 55% health, and drink only below 15% mana (when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**). Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

## Why this rotation

Demonology levels on the Voidwalker: its talents make the demon both the tank and a share of the damage (about a quarter at level 30 and an eighth at 60 in the fight lab, the brand's hits included), **Demonic Brand** (levels 28-29, 34) turns **Searing Pain** into extra Voidwalker hits, and your dots from the Affliction points and the wand do the rest. The page judges Demonology by what the demon adds, not by your own spells.

**Why it works**

- Send the Voidwalker in first: **Unholy Power** (levels 10-14), **Demonic Energies** (15 and 19), **Improved Voidwalker** (16-17, 24), **Demonic Knowledge** (31-33) and **Master Demonologist** (40-43) make it and you stronger, and **Soul Link** (30) moves 30% of the damage you take to it and adds 3% damage for both. **Demonic Sacrifice** (27) is only the step to **Soul Link**: never sacrifice your tank.
- **Searing Pain** whenever the brand is gone, the Voidwalker is hitting the mob, the mob has more than a third of its health and you have more than 30% mana (the 2026-11-06 re-tune, about 1% faster per kill for each of the two limits): **Demonic Brand** makes the Voidwalker's next 2 attacks (at 28), 4 (29) or 6 (from 34) each deal 65 to 68 extra damage at level 60, and the brand halves **Searing Pain**'s own threat. The open search of 2026-11-20 measured the three **Demonic Brand** points at about half a second a kill at 60.
- **Bane of Agony**, **Corruption** and, from level 60, **Siphon Life** on every mob above about a third of its health (**Corruption** only above 35%, the re-tune's rule, about 1% faster per kill). **Immolate** only while you have more than half your mana: common at level 20, rare once the brand costs mana from level 28.
- The wand is your filler. **Shadow Bolt** and filler **Searing Pain** wait for 80% mana, and with the brands and dots you almost never get there: the fight lab casts almost no **Shadow Bolt** from level 30 and needs no drinking.
- **Drain Life** below 60% health and in place of the wand below 85% (with **Soul Link** and the Voidwalker holding the mob, the fight lab rarely needs it); **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health. Between fights, **Life Tap** back to 70% mana, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows**, eat below 55% health. Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).
- The Affliction points (levels 35-39 and 44-60) make your dots stronger: **Improved Corruption** (35-39) makes **Corruption** instant and adds 10% to its damage, **Amplify Curse** (50) strengthens a **Bane of Agony** every 3 minutes, **Pandemic** and **Malevolence** add to the dots' critical strikes.

**Caveats**

- This build has no Decimation and no Improved Health Funnel, so the **Soul Fire** and in-fight **Health Funnel** lines do not apply.
- **Fel Vitality** (levels 18, 20-21: 15% more health and mana for the Voidwalker, 15% more mana for you), **Demonic Embrace** (22-23: 6% more Stamina) and **Demonic Aegis** (25-26: **Demon Armor** 30% stronger) fill the tiers on the way to **Soul Link** (30) and **Master Demonologist**. The leveling filler passes of 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21 moved three points into **Fel Vitality** (0.1-0.2% faster per kill each), the fifth **Master Demonologist** point into **Suppression** (0.2%), and Improved Imp's three points, which only strengthen the Imp, into **Demonic Embrace** and **Improved Voidwalker** (no slower).
- When a mob reaches you, cast **Death Coil** (level 42) if you are below about 60% health (it heals you for its damage), otherwise **Fear** it and let the Voidwalker take it back.
- The Affliction/Demonology hybrid plays this same list with other talents; its page and guide show the difference.

**When to change it**

- If the Voidwalker dies, finish the mob with dots, **Death Coil** and the wand, and summon a new one before the next pull: without a demon out you also lose **Demonic Knowledge**, **Soul Link** and **Master Demonologist**.
- Finish a nearly dead mob with the wand; start no new dot on it.
