# Affliction Warlock — leveling, level by level

[Warlock](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Affliction](affliction.md) > Level by level

What an Affliction warlock presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage. Affliction levels on its damage-over-time spells while the Voidwalker (from 10) holds the mob: **Corruption** and **Bane of Agony** on every mob, **Shadow Bolt** while the mob is healthy, then the wand while the dots finish it. From 40, **Wrack** takes over from most of your bolts. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 39/11/1 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/warlock/60/25510223135001351-00151301-01-12)), the one on the [Affliction leveling page](affliction.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Immolate**, then **Shadow Bolt**, while the Imp throws **Firebolt**. |
| 4-7 | **Corruption** is your pull, on every mob. |
| 8-9 | **Bane of Agony** joins **Corruption**. **Fear** what reaches you: the Imp cannot hold a mob. |
| 10-19 | **Summon Voidwalker** holds the mob; you dot and bolt from range. Talent points start. |
| 20-33 | **Amplify Curse** (talent) on the first **Bane of Agony** every 3 minutes. **Drain Life** heals you when you are hurt. |
| 34-39 | **Siphon Life** (talent): a third dot that heals you. |
| 40-41 | **Wrack** (talent): a 6-second channel once **Corruption** is up. It replaces most of your **Shadow Bolt** casts. |
| 42-60 | **Death Coil** for a mob that reaches you while you are hurt. The rest stays. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; armor, summons, Soul Link, conjured stones, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q your bolt, E the control, R the payoff after it, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions and the pull, 1-4 the rest, the middle mouse button the wand. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and macros: the [warlock keybinds page](keybinds.md), written for Demonology; this guide changes the keys its notes name.

## Levels 1-3: Immolate and Shadow Bolt

**The trainer teaches:** Demon Skin (1), Firebolt (1), **Immolate** (1), **Shadow Bolt** (1), Summon Imp (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Shadow Bolt; 2 = Immolate; Middle mouse = Wand.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Imp in, then **Immolate** the mob.
2. **Shadow Bolt** while the mob has more than about a third of its health.
3. Your wand finishes it.

- **Summon Imp** (1) before the first pull: its **Firebolt** casts by itself. Keep **Demon Skin** up at all times, from here to 20.
- The fight lab gives you a wand from level 1. Buy one as soon as you can: until then **Shadow Bolt** is the filler too.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 14 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Wand 1.8, Shadow Bolt 1.1, Immolate 1.0.*

## Levels 4-7: Corruption, the pull

**The trainer teaches:** Blood Pact (4), **Corruption** (4), Curse of Weakness (4), Life Tap (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Shadow Bolt at 6.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Corruption, pull macro; 4 = Life Tap (at 6).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, Mouse 4 Corruption, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Corruption** to pull (Mouse 4: the macro sends your demon too), on a mob above about a third of its health and while you have more than 15% of your mana.
2. **Immolate** on a mob above about half its health, while you have more than half your mana.
3. **Shadow Bolt** while the mob has more than about a third of its health and you have mana to spare.
4. Your wand while the dots finish the mob.

- **Life Tap** (6): between fights, turn health into mana until you have about 70% of your mana, while your health stays above 60%. The next pull then starts with mana, and you rest the health back.
- **Curse of Weakness** (4) is not part of the list.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Wand 4.2, Corruption 1.0, Immolate 1.0, Shadow Bolt 0.2.*

## Levels 8-9: Bane of Agony, and Fear while the Imp cannot tank

**The trainer teaches:** **Bane of Agony** (8), **Fear** (8).

**Keys to set:** E = Fear; 1 = Bane of Agony.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, E Fear, Mouse 4 Corruption, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

4. **Shadow Bolt** while the mob has more than about a third of its health and you keep the mana for **Fear**.
5. Your wand while the dots finish the mob.

- The Voidwalker arrives at 10. Until then a mob that reaches you stays on you: **Fear** it and keep casting.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 31 at level 8. Presses per kill at level 8: Shadow Bolt 2.5, Wand 1.8, Fear 1.1, Bane of Agony 1.1, Corruption 1.0, Immolate 0.6.*

## Levels 10-19: The Voidwalker tanks, Shadow Bolt fills

**The trainer teaches:** Create Healthstone (10), Drain Soul (10), Summon Voidwalker (10), **Torment** (10), Health Funnel (12), Phase Shift (12), Curse of Recklessness (14), Drain Life (14), Fire Shield (14), Sacrifice (16), Unending Breath (16), Consume Shadows (18), Create Soulstone (18), Searing Pain (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Bane of Agony at 18; Corruption at 14; Immolate at 10; Life Tap at 16; Shadow Bolt at 12.

**Talents:** 10-11 Improved Life Tap, 12-15 Suppression, 16-19 Improved Corruption.

**Keys to set:** C = Healthstone macro; Shift+Mouse 4 = Drain Soul; Shift+R = Health Funnel (at 12); R = Drain Life (at 14).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, E Fear, Mouse 4 Corruption, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Voidwalker in with **Corruption** (Mouse 4), on a mob above about a third of its health and while you have more than 15% of your mana; its **Torment** holds the mob.
2. **Bane of Agony** on a mob above about a third of its health.
3. **Immolate** on a mob above about half its health, while you have more than half your mana.
4. **Shadow Bolt** while the mob has more than about a third of its health and you keep the mana for Fear.
5. Your wand while the dots finish the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: **Fear** it and wand it while it runs, until the Voidwalker takes it back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

- **Summon Voidwalker** costs a Soul Shard: **Drain Soul** (10) a mob as it dies to get one (Shift+Mouse 4). The fight lab does not count shards; keep two or three in your bags for the demon and your Healthstone.
- Heal the Voidwalker between fights with **Consume Shadows** (18, its own spell, not usable in a fight) or **Health Funnel** (12). Check that its **Torment** is on autocast on the pet bar.

- **Drain Life** (14) goes on R: from 20 it is your heal in a fight.
- **Life Tap** in a fight only when the next **Shadow Bolt** would leave too little mana for **Fear**, while you are above 60% health.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 23 at level 10, 29 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Shadow Bolt 2.3, Torment 2.3, Wand 1.3, Bane of Agony 1.1, Corruption 1.0, Immolate 0.6.*

## Between fights

- **Life Tap** back to about 70% mana while your health stays above 60%. Your health comes back while you rest, and the Voidwalker takes the next mob.
- Eat only below 55% of your health. Drink only below 15% of your mana, when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**: Affliction spends more mana than the other warlock lists, on **Shadow Bolt** and **Wrack**.
- Heal a Voidwalker below 60% of its health with **Consume Shadows** (not usable in a fight), or with **Health Funnel** when it is below half.
- Keep two or three Soul Shards: **Drain Soul** a dying mob for one. **Summon Voidwalker** and **Create Healthstone** use one each.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of your dots and **Shadow Bolt**.
