## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Conflagrate**, **Incinerate**, **Shadow and Flame**, **Shadowburn** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Corruption** and **Bane of Agony** first while the Voidwalker builds threat, neither on a mob below about a third of its health.
2. **Immolate** only while you have more than half your mana, the mob has more than about a third of its health and it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker.
3. **Conflagrate** when **Immolate** has about 3 seconds left (at once with 5 points in **Shadow and Flame**, which keeps **Immolate** burning), or to finish a burning mob it would kill; then **Siphon Life**.
4. Your wand is the filler: **Shadow Bolt** only above 80% mana (**Searing Pain** when the mob has too little health left for a bolt), which the dots rarely leave you.
5. With **Incinerate**: cast it instead of **Shadow Bolt** on a burning mob. With **Shadowburn**: finish any mob it would kill.
6. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%; **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health.
7. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it, then **Drain Life** while it runs, until **Torment** takes it back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

**Between fights:** **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, eat only below 55% health, and drink only below 15% mana (when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**).

## Why this rotation

The Shadow Bolt hybrid levels with the Destruction list but has no **Conflagrate**: a Voidwalker holds the mob while you put **Corruption**, **Bane of Agony**, **Immolate** and **Siphon Life** on it and finish with the wand. Despite the name, the tuned list casts almost no **Shadow Bolt**: it waits for 80% mana.

**Why it works**

- **Corruption** and **Bane of Agony** go first: cheap, instant once you have 5 points in **Improved Corruption** (level 23), and they give the Voidwalker time to build threat before your fire spells land. **Amplify Curse** (level 20) makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger; skip **Corruption** and **Bane of Agony** on a mob below about a third of its health.
- **Siphon Life** (level 31) goes on every mob from then on: damage and healing for one global cooldown.
- **Immolate** only while you have more than half your mana (tuned 2026-10-20 on this build, about 3% faster per kill) and it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker; **Aftermath** (levels 54-57) adds 40% to its first hit. The fight lab casts it on every mob to level 40 and on about half of them at 60.
- The wand is your filler: **Shadow Bolt** and filler **Searing Pain** wait for 80% mana, which the dots rarely leave you. **Bane** (levels 49-53) and **Cataclysm** (58-60) still make every **Immolate** faster and cheaper.
- The Demonology points are for the tank: **Unholy Power** (39-43) adds 10% to its damage, **Improved Voidwalker** (45-47) makes **Torment** and **Consume Shadows** 30% stronger, and **Demonic Energies** (44 and 48) gives it the mana your **Life Tap** gives you and heals it for 15% of your spell damage.

**Caveats**

- No **Conflagrate**, **Incinerate** or **Shadowburn**: skip the lines that name them. **Curse of Exhaustion** (level 34) is a step to the next tier; the fight lab never casts it.
- When a mob reaches you, **Death Coil** if you are below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it and **Drain Life** while it runs, until the Voidwalker's **Torment** takes it back.
- **Drain Life** below 60% health and in place of the wand below 85%; **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health; between fights tap back to 70% mana and eat only below 55% health.

**When to change it**

- Finish a nearly dead mob with the wand; start no new **Immolate** on it.
- If the Voidwalker dies, summon a new one before the next pull, and heal a hurt one between fights with **Consume Shadows** or **Health Funnel**.
