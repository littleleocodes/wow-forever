# Warlock leveling keybinds (hand-written)

[Warlock](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > Keybinds

A personal layout, written 2026-11-07 on the player's own two action bars, for a player of League of Legends and Deadlock with a mouse
that has two side buttons (WoW shows them as Button 4 and Button 5). Same scheme as the [mage](../../mage/leveling/keybinds.md) and
[paladin](../../paladin/leveling/keybinds.md) layouts: Q your bolt, E the control, R the payoff after it, F the save, the two thumb
buttons the reaction and the pull, 1-4 the dots and the mana, the middle mouse button the wand, the Shift bar the rarer tools. Keys are
for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there: armor, summons, Soul Link, conjured stones, food, drink, the
mount and the Hearthstone have no key and are clicked between fights. It is built on the [Demonology leveling page](demonology.md); the
Affliction-Demonology hybrid plays the same list. The other warlock specs keep these keys for every spell they share and change only a few
(see "The other warlock specs" below).

**Which spell is on each key at your level:** the "Keybinds by level" tables of the
[Demonology level-by-level guide](demonology-by-level.md), and for the other specs their own guides:
[Affliction](affliction-by-level.md), [Destruction](destruction-by-level.md),
[Affliction-Demonology](affliction-demonology-by-level.md). This page is the layout at 60, the setup and the macros.

This file is hand-written: the regeneration that rewrites the rest of this folder leaves it alone. Edit it by hand.

## Set up first (Esc, Key Bindings and Interface)

- The two action bars: bar 1 is bound to Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 to the same keys
  with Shift, with Shift+X in the last slot (not Shift+middle mouse). The tables below list the slots in that order.
- Movement: Strafe Left on A and Strafe Right on D, turning with the right mouse button held, as in Deadlock. That frees Q and E, which
  WoW uses for strafing by default.
- The pet bar keeps its own keys. Right-click Torment there so the Voidwalker casts it by itself (a gold border shows autocast is on):
  Torment is what keeps the mob on the Voidwalker. Consume Shadows and Sacrifice stay on the pet bar.
- A side bar without keybinds (Interface, Action Bars: the right bars) for the things you click: Demon Skin or Demon Armor, Summon Imp,
  Summon Voidwalker, Soul Link, Create Healthstone, food, water, the mount, the Hearthstone.

## Bar 1: the fight

| Key | Spell (learned at) | Press it when | Habit it matches |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q | Shadow Bolt (1) | At levels 1-3 after Immolate; from 4 only above 80% of your mana, mostly on the first pull after a rest. The wand does the filling | League Q, the basic hit |
| E | Fear (8) | A mob reaches you: it runs for up to 10 seconds (15 from rank 2 at level 32, 20 from rank 3 at level 56), the Voidwalker takes it back | League E, the CC key |
| R | Drain Life (14) | Right after E, while the feared mob runs; and whenever you are below 60% of your health | League R, the payoff after the CC |
| F | Death Coil (42) | A mob is on you and you are below 60% of your health: it runs in horror for 3 seconds and you heal for the damage. 2-minute cooldown | Flash, the save |
| Mouse 5 (front side button) | Searing Pain (18; a key from 27, with Demonic Brand) | The brand has dropped, the Voidwalker is hitting the mob, the mob has more than a third of its health and you more than 30% of your mana | Thumb reaction |
| Mouse 4 (back side button) | Corruption pull macro (4) | The pull, every fight: it sends your demon and casts Corruption, on a mob above about a third of its health | Thumb: the pull, as on the priest and the hunter |
| 1 | Bane of Agony (8); the Amplify Curse macro from 50 | Right after the pull, on a mob above about a third of its health | Deadlock ability 1 |
| 2 | Immolate (1) | On every mob at levels 1-27 while you have more than half your mana; rare after that, since the brand costs mana | Deadlock ability 2 |
| 3 | Siphon Life (60, talent) | With the other dots, on a mob above about a third of its health | Deadlock ability 3 |
| 4 | Life Tap (6) | Between fights, back to about 70% of your mana while your health stays above 60%; in a fight only when you are short of mana | Deadlock 4 |
| C | Healthstone macro (10) | Below 35% of your health | A reaction key next to the movement hand |
| Middle mouse | Shoot (wand) | The filler: whenever no key above applies | The weapon attack |

From 27 the whole fight is Mouse 4 to pull, Mouse 5 each time the brand drops, 1 for the bane, and the middle mouse button in between.
E, R, F and C are for a mob that reaches you.

## Bar 2: the same keys with Shift

| Key | Spell (learned at) | Press it when |
|---|---|---|
| Shift+E | Banish (28) | A second mob joins and it is a demon or an elemental: it stands out of the fight for up to 30 seconds |
| Shift+R | Health Funnel (12) | The Voidwalker is about to die; between fights when it is below half its health |
| Shift+F | Howl of Terror (40) | Several mobs on you: every one within 10 yards runs |
| Shift+Mouse 5 | Shadow Ward (32) | A shadow caster is on you: absorbs its next spells |
| Shift+Mouse 4 | Drain Soul (10) | A mob is about to die and you need a Soul Shard (summons and Healthstones use them) |
| Shift+1 | Rain of Fire (20) | Packs only. The single-target lists never cast it |
| Shift+C | Free: the healing potion macro of the [macro page](../macros.md) fits here | Below 35% of your health with the Healthstone on cooldown |
| Shift+X | Free: the mana potion macro of the macro page fits here | Out of mana and too hurt to Life Tap |
| Shift+Q, Shift+2, Shift+3, Shift+4 | Free | |

Free slots stay free until something earns them.

## The other warlock specs

Each spec's level-by-level guide has its own keybinds table. They keep the keys above for every spell they share with Demonology:

- [Affliction](affliction-by-level.md): Mouse 5 holds Wrack (40) where Demonology keeps Searing Pain. R, E and F matter more here: it casts Fear and Drain Life when a mob reaches you. The Amplify Curse macro takes 1 at
  20 and Siphon Life takes 3 at 34. Q's Shadow Bolt is the main nuke until 40.
- [Destruction](destruction-by-level.md): Mouse 4's pull macro casts Shadow Bolt in place of Corruption, from 10 (the fight lab opens with
  a bolt while the Voidwalker takes the mob, then Immolate). R holds Conflagrate (32) and Mouse 5 Shadowburn (31): the tuned rules of
  2026-11-07 never cast Drain Life, Searing Pain or Fear. 1 and 3 stay free (no Affliction dots). E keeps Fear for a pull that brings a
  second mob, though those rules never cast it.
- [Affliction-Demonology](affliction-demonology-by-level.md) (27/24/0): this layout unchanged. Searing Pain arrives at 27, the Amplify
  Curse macro at 44 and Siphon Life at 54, and Health Funnel on Shift+R is a fight spell from 20 (Improved Health Funnel).

## No key: click these between fights

Demon Skin (Demon Armor from 20), Summon Imp, Summon Voidwalker, Soul Link (once after every summon, from the talent), Create Healthstone,
Create Soulstone, food, water, your mount (40 in Classic) and the Hearthstone. Tab (target nearest enemy) and Space (jump) stay on WoW's
defaults. Curse of Weakness, Curse of Recklessness, Curse of the Elements, Soul Fire and Hellfire are never cast by the leveling lists.

## Macros (Esc, Macros, New; pick the question-mark icon so the button shows the spell or the item count)

The pull macro, on Mouse 4 from level 4. It sends your demon at your target and casts Corruption. The Voidwalker's Torment then keeps
the mob on it. Before 4, send the Imp from the pet bar and open with Immolate on 2.

```
#showtooltip Corruption
/petattack
/cast Corruption
```

Destruction's pull macro, on Mouse 4 from level 10, is the same with Shadow Bolt:

```
#showtooltip Shadow Bolt
/petattack
/cast Shadow Bolt
```

The Amplify Curse macro, on 1 from level 50 (Affliction from 20, Affliction-Demonology from 44). Amplify Curse is off the global
cooldown in the simulator's data, so one press casts both. While it is on its 3-minute cooldown the first line fails and the bane goes
out alone, so the key stays your plain Bane of Agony.

```
#showtooltip Bane of Agony
/cast Amplify Curse
/cast Bane of Agony
```

The Healthstone, on C from level 10. Only the stone you carry goes off. The names are the ones the Create Healthstone ranks make: Minor
at 10, Lesser at 22, the plain Healthstone at 34, Greater at 46 and Major at 58. If a line does nothing, hover the stone in your bag and
copy its exact name into the macro.

```
#showtooltip
/use Major Healthstone
/use Greater Healthstone
/use Healthstone
/use Lesser Healthstone
/use Minor Healthstone
```

The wand stays plain Shoot on the middle mouse button; the [macro page](../macros.md) has a version that never turns itself off.

`/petattack`, `/cast` and `/use` are commands of the Classic client and of Forever's own UI code (the macro page lists them). None of these
macros is tested in Forever's client here. If one fails, put the plain spell back on its key and send the demon from the pet bar.
