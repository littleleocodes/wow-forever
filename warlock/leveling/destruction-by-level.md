# Destruction Warlock — leveling, level by level

[Warlock](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Destruction](destruction.md) > Level by level

What a Destruction warlock presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage. Destruction levels as a nuker behind the Voidwalker (from 10): **Shadow Bolt** and **Immolate**, **Shadowburn** (31) to finish and **Conflagrate** (32) on its cooldown. No Affliction dots, no **Fear** and no **Drain Life**: the tuned rules of 2026-11-07 level faster without them. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 6/11/34 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/warlock/60/15-00050321-2050305133111351-12)), the one on the [Destruction leveling page](destruction.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-9 | **Immolate**, then **Shadow Bolt**, while the Imp throws **Firebolt**. No Affliction dots in this list. |
| 10-30 | **Summon Voidwalker** holds the mob while you nuke. Talent points start: **Bane** makes your casts faster. |
| 31 | **Shadowburn** (talent) finishes a mob instantly. Cast it only when it will kill: the Soul Shard comes back. |
| 32-39 | **Conflagrate** (talent): an instant hit on a burning mob every 10 seconds. Until 39 it uses up your **Immolate**. |
| 40-41 | **Conflagrate** whenever it is ready. On a burning mob the wand fills between casts; **Shadow Bolt** opens the pull. |
| 42-60 | **Death Coil** for a mob that reaches you while you are hurt. The rest stays. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; armor, summons, Soul Link, conjured stones, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q your bolt, E the control, R the payoff after it, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions and the pull, 1-4 the rest, the middle mouse button the wand. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and macros: the [warlock keybinds page](keybinds.md), written for Demonology; this guide changes the keys its notes name.

## Levels 1-9: Immolate and Shadow Bolt

**The trainer teaches:** Demon Skin (1), Firebolt (1), **Immolate** (1), **Shadow Bolt** (1), Summon Imp (1), Blood Pact (4), Corruption (4), Curse of Weakness (4), Life Tap (6), Bane of Agony (8), Fear (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Shadow Bolt at 6.

**Keys to set:** Q = Shadow Bolt; 2 = Immolate; Middle mouse = Wand; 4 = Life Tap (at 6); E = Fear (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Imp in, then **Immolate** the mob above 30% of its health.
2. **Shadow Bolt** while the mob has more than about a third of its health.
3. Your wand finishes it.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: wand it until it dies; nothing hits hard at these levels.

- **Summon Imp** (1) before the first pull: its **Firebolt** casts by itself. Keep **Demon Skin** up at all times, from here to 20.
- The fight lab gives you a wand from level 1. Buy one as soon as you can: until then **Shadow Bolt** is the filler too.
- **Life Tap** (6): between fights, turn health into mana until you have about 70% of your mana, while your health stays above 60%. The next pull then starts with mana, and you rest the health back.
- **Corruption** (4), **Bane of Agony** (8) and **Fear** (8) are not part of this list. **Fear** still gets a key (E) for a pull that brings a second mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 14 at level 1, 31 at level 9. Presses per kill at level 1: Wand 1.8, Shadow Bolt 1.1, Immolate 1.0.*

## Levels 10-30: The Voidwalker tanks, Shadow Bolt fills

**The trainer teaches:** Create Healthstone (10), Drain Soul (10), Summon Voidwalker (10), **Torment** (10), Health Funnel (12), Phase Shift (12), Curse of Recklessness (14), Drain Life (14), Fire Shield (14), Sacrifice (16), Unending Breath (16), Consume Shadows (18), Create Soulstone (18), Searing Pain (18), Curse of the Elements (20), Demon Armor (20), Lash of Pain (20), Rain of Fire (20), Ritual of Summoning (20), Summon Incubus (20), Summon Succubus (20), Eye of Kilrogg (22), Soothing Kiss (22), Drain Mana (24), Sense Demons (24), Suffering (24), Curse of Tongues (26), Detect Invisibility (26), Seduction (26), Banish (28), Create Firestone (28), Devour Magic (30), Hellfire (30), Subjugate Demon (30), Summon Felhunter (30).

**New ranks to buy:** Drain Soul at 24; Health Funnel at 20, 28; Immolate at 10, 20, 30; Life Tap at 16, 26; Shadow Bolt at 12, 20, 28; Torment at 20, 30.

**Talents:** 10-14 Bane, 15-16 Destructive Reach, 17-19 Cataclysm, 20-24 Ruin, 25-27 Agonizing Flames, 28-30 Intensity.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Shadow Bolt pull macro; C = Healthstone macro; Shift+Mouse 4 = Drain Soul; Shift+R = Health Funnel (at 12); Shift+1 = Rain of Fire (at 20); Shift+E = Banish (at 28).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 2 Immolate, 4 Life Tap.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Voidwalker in and open with **Shadow Bolt** (Mouse 4's macro) while its **Torment** takes the mob, then **Immolate** on a mob above 30% of its health, once it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker.
2. **Shadow Bolt** while the mob has more than about a third of its health and you keep a little mana in reserve; below that, your wand.
3. **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: wand it until the Voidwalker's taunt takes it back; a cast in melee gets pushed back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

- **Summon Voidwalker** costs a Soul Shard: **Drain Soul** (10) a mob as it dies to get one (Shift+Mouse 4). The fight lab does not count shards; keep two or three in your bags for the demon and your Healthstone.
- Heal the Voidwalker between fights with **Consume Shadows** (18, its own spell, not usable in a fight) or **Health Funnel** (12). Check that its **Torment** is on autocast on the pet bar.
- **Bane** (10-14) takes 0.5 seconds off **Shadow Bolt** and **Immolate**, **Destructive Reach** (15-16) adds 20% to the range of your Destruction spells (start the pull a little farther out), **Cataclysm** (17-19) makes your Destruction spells 10% cheaper, **Ruin** (20-24) doubles their critical strike bonus and **Agonizing Flames** (25-27) adds 10% to their damage. **Intensity** (28-30): your Destruction spells keep their cast time 70% of the time when a mob hits you.
- **Drain Life** (14) and **Searing Pain** (18) are not part of this list: the tuned rules measured faster kills without them.
- **Demon Armor** (20) replaces **Demon Skin**. **Rain of Fire** (20) is for packs: one mob at a time never needs it.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 23 at level 10, 31 at level 20, 40 at level 30. Presses per kill at level 10: Shadow Bolt 3.2, Torment 2.4, Wand 2.3, Immolate 1.0.*

## Between fights

- **Life Tap** back to about 70% mana while your health stays above 60%. Your health comes back while you rest, and the Voidwalker takes the next mob.
- Eat only below 40% of your health (the tuned rule of 2026-11-07). Drink only below 15% of your mana, when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**.
- Heal a Voidwalker below 60% of its health with **Consume Shadows** (not usable in a fight), or with **Health Funnel** when it is below half.
- Keep two or three Soul Shards: **Drain Soul** a dying mob for one. **Summon Voidwalker** and **Create Healthstone** use one each; a killing **Shadowburn** gives its shard back.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of **Shadow Bolt** and **Immolate**.
