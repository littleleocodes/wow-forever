# Destruction Warlock — leveling, level by level

[Warlock](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Destruction](destruction.md) > Level by level

What a Destruction warlock presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage. Destruction levels as a nuker behind the Voidwalker (from 10), and your wand is the filler: between casts you wand the mob. **Immolate**, **Bane of Agony** on every pull from 8, **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than 35% of its health, **Shadowburn** (29) to finish and **Conflagrate** on its cooldown from 39 (the talent comes at 31, but until **Shadow and Flame** is 5/5 it uses up your **Immolate**, so it waits). No **Corruption**, no **Fear** and no **Drain Life**: the tuned rules level faster without them. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 6/9/36 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/warlock/60/24-00050112-2150355103101351-12)), the one on the [Destruction leveling page](destruction.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-7 | **Immolate**, then **Shadow Bolt** as long as you have more than 60% of your mana; the Imp throws **Firebolt**. No Affliction dots in this list. |
| 8-9 | **Bane of Agony** (8) goes on every mob while it has more than 35% of its health: the tuned rules force it from 8 (by hand, 2026-11-23: 11.6-12.5% faster a kill at 8-9 in the fight lab). |
| 10-19 | **Summon Voidwalker** holds the mob while you nuke. Talent points start: **Bane** makes your casts faster. |
| 20-28 | Nothing new to press: **Ruin** (20-24) doubles your Destruction spells' critical strike bonus, and **Demon Armor** (20) replaces **Demon Skin**. |
| 29-30 | **Shadowburn** (talent) finishes a mob instantly. Cast it only when it will kill: the Soul Shard comes back. |
| 31-38 | **Conflagrate** (talent): an instant hit on a burning mob every 10 seconds. Until **Shadow and Flame** is 5/5 (39) it uses up your **Immolate**, so hold it until then. The rest stays. |
| 39 | **Shadow and Flame** 5/5 (39): **Conflagrate** no longer uses up your **Immolate**, so start pressing it now, whenever it is ready; it also adds 20% to your Shadow damage for 20 seconds. |
| 40-41 | **Conflagrate** whenever it is ready. On a burning mob the wand fills between casts; **Shadow Bolt** opens the pull. |
| 42-60 | **Death Coil** (F) is the save for a mob that reaches you while you are hurt: the fight lab presses it about once in 33 kills. The rest stays. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; armor, summons, Soul Link, conjured stones, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q your bolt, E the control, R the payoff after it, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions and the pull, 1-4 the rest, the middle mouse button the wand. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and macros: the [warlock keybinds page](keybinds.md), written for Demonology; this guide changes the keys its notes name.

## Levels 1-7: Immolate and Shadow Bolt

**The trainer teaches:** Demon Skin (1), Firebolt (1), **Immolate** (1), **Shadow Bolt** (1), Summon Imp (1), Blood Pact (4), Corruption (4), Curse of Weakness (4), Life Tap (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Shadow Bolt at 6.

**Keys to set:** Q = Shadow Bolt; 2 = Immolate; Middle mouse = Wand; 4 = Life Tap (at 6).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Imp in, then **Immolate** the mob above 30% of its health.
2. **Shadow Bolt** while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than 35% of its health.
3. Your wand otherwise, and to finish it.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: wand it until it dies; nothing hits hard at these levels.

- **Summon Imp** (1) before the first pull: its **Firebolt** casts by itself. Keep **Demon Skin** up at all times, from here to 20.
- The fight lab gives you a wand from level 1. Buy one as soon as you can: until then **Shadow Bolt** is the filler too.
- **Life Tap** (6): between fights, turn health into mana until you have about 70% of your mana, while your health stays above 60%. The next pull then starts with mana, and you rest the health back.
- **Corruption** (4) and **Fear** (8) are not part of this list; **Fear** still gets a key (E) for a pull that brings a second mob. **Bane of Agony** (8) joins the list then.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 14 at level 1, 24 at level 7. Presses per kill at level 1: Wand 1.8, Shadow Bolt 1.1, Immolate 1.0.*

## Levels 8-9: Bane of Agony

**The trainer teaches:** **Bane of Agony** (8), Fear (8).

**Keys to set:** E = Fear; 1 = Bane of Agony.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Imp in, then **Bane of Agony** (1) on a mob above 35% of its health.
2. **Immolate** the mob above 30% of its health.
3. **Shadow Bolt** while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than 35% of its health.
4. Your wand otherwise, and to finish it.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: wand it until it dies; nothing hits hard at these levels.

- **Bane of Agony** is one instant cast a kill. The tuned rules put it at the top of the list (from 20 since 2026-11-22; from 8 by hand since 2026-11-23: 11.6-12.5% faster a kill at 8-9, 3-8% at 10-19 and 5-9% at 20-30 than without it) and cast it only on a mob above 35% of its health (1.2-1.4% faster than casting it lower).

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 24 at level 8. Presses per kill at level 8: Wand 6.6, Bane of Agony 1.0, Immolate 1.0, Shadow Bolt 0.3.*

## Levels 10-19: The Voidwalker tanks, the wand fills below 60% mana

**The trainer teaches:** Create Healthstone (10), Drain Soul (10), Summon Voidwalker (10), **Torment** (10), Health Funnel (12), Phase Shift (12), Curse of Recklessness (14), Drain Life (14), Fire Shield (14), Sacrifice (16), Unending Breath (16), Consume Shadows (18), Create Soulstone (18), Searing Pain (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Bane of Agony at 18; Immolate at 10; Life Tap at 16; Shadow Bolt at 12.

**Talents:** 10-14 Bane, 15-16 Destructive Reach, 17-19 Cataclysm.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Shadow Bolt pull macro; C = Healthstone macro; Shift+Mouse 4 = Drain Soul; Shift+R = Health Funnel (at 12).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Voidwalker in and open with **Shadow Bolt** (Mouse 4's macro) while its **Torment** takes the mob, then **Bane of Agony** on a mob above 35% of its health and **Immolate** on a mob above 30%, once it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker.
2. Between casts your wand is the filler: **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than 35% of its health.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: wand it until the Voidwalker's taunt takes it back; a cast in melee gets pushed back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

- **Summon Voidwalker** costs a Soul Shard: **Drain Soul** (10) a mob as it dies to get one (Shift+Mouse 4). The fight lab does not count shards; keep two or three in your bags for the demon and your Healthstone.
- Heal the Voidwalker between fights with **Consume Shadows** (18, its own spell, not usable in a fight) or **Health Funnel** (12). Check that its **Torment** is on autocast on the pet bar.
- **Bane** (10-14) takes 0.5 seconds off **Shadow Bolt** and **Immolate**, **Destructive Reach** (15-16) adds 20% to the range of your Destruction spells (start the pull a little farther out) and **Cataclysm** (17-19) makes your Destruction spells 10% cheaper.
- From 10 to 19 the wand does 35-36% of your damage, **Shadow Bolt** 16-22% and **Bane of Agony** 18-19% (the fight lab, 2026-11-23). The list casts **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana: the wand costs nothing, and every bolt's mana has to be drunk back after the fight, so below that line the wand kills faster overall (the re-tune of 2026-11-21: 3% faster per kill over levels 20-60). No **Life Tap** in a fight: with the bolt kept for spare mana the fight lab never runs short.
- **Drain Life** (14) and **Searing Pain** (18) are not part of this list: the tuned rules measured faster kills without them.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 21 at level 10, 28 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Wand 4.5, Torment 2.6, Shadow Bolt 1.2, Bane of Agony 1.0, Immolate 1.0.*

**Talents:** 20-24 Ruin, 25-27 Agonizing Flames, 28 Improved Shadow Bolt.

**Keys to set:** Shift+1 = Rain of Fire; Shift+E = Banish (at 28).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the Voidwalker in and open with **Shadow Bolt** (Mouse 4's macro) while its **Torment** takes the mob.
2. **Bane of Agony** (1) as soon as the mob is in range, on a mob above 35% of its health.
3. **Immolate** on a mob above 30% of its health, once it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 27 at level 20, 33 at level 28. Presses per kill at level 20: Wand 7.5, Torment 3.4, Shadow Bolt 1.2, Bane of Agony 1.0, Immolate 1.0.*

## Levels 29-30: Shadowburn

**The trainer teaches:** Devour Magic (30), Hellfire (30), Subjugate Demon (30), Summon Felhunter (30).

**New ranks to buy:** Immolate at 30; Torment at 30.

**Talents:** 29 Shadowburn, 30 Bane of Havoc.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Shadowburn.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, Mouse 5 Shadowburn, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadowburn** on a mob it would kill.
2. Send the Voidwalker in and open with **Shadow Bolt** (Mouse 4's macro) while its **Torment** takes the mob, then **Bane of Agony** on a mob above 35% of its health and **Immolate** on a mob above 30%, once it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker.
3. Between casts your wand is the filler: **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than 35% of its health.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: **Shadowburn** if it would kill, otherwise wand it until the Voidwalker's taunt takes it back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

- **Shadowburn** costs a Soul Shard and gives it back when the mob dies within 8 seconds, so a killing **Shadowburn** is free.
- **Bane of Havoc** (30) is the step to **Incinerate** (40), which the tuned rules never cast: this list fights one mob at a time, so **Bane of Havoc** does nothing here.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 32 at level 29, 32 at level 30. Presses per kill at level 29: Wand 11.9, Torment 4.0, Immolate 1.1, Bane of Agony 1.0, Shadowburn 1.0, Shadow Bolt 0.6.*

## Levels 31-38: Conflagrate, held until 39

**The trainer teaches:** Lesser Invisibility (32), Shadow Ward (32), Tainted Blood (32), Create Spellstone (36), Spell Lock (36).

**New ranks to buy:** Bane of Agony at 38; Conflagrate at 32; Drain Soul at 38; Fear at 32; Health Funnel at 36; Life Tap at 36; Rain of Fire at 34; Shadow Bolt at 36; Shadowburn at 32.

**Talents:** 31 Conflagrate, 32-34 Fire and Brimstone, 35-38 Shadow and Flame.

**Keys to set:** R = Conflagrate; Shift+Mouse 5 = Shadow Ward (at 32).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Shadow Bolt, Mouse 5 Shadowburn, 1 Bane of Agony, 2 Immolate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadowburn** on a mob it would kill.
2. Send the Voidwalker in and open with **Shadow Bolt** (Mouse 4's macro) while its **Torment** takes the mob, then **Bane of Agony** on a mob above 35% of its health and **Immolate** on a mob above 30%, once it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker.
3. Between casts your wand is the filler: **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than 35% of its health.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: **Shadowburn** if it would kill, otherwise wand it until the Voidwalker's taunt takes it back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

- Why it waits: before **Shadow and Flame** 5/5 a **Conflagrate** ends the **Immolate** it hits, and the burn is worth more than the hit. Pressing it even in **Immolate**'s last 3 seconds costs 5.2% a kill at 31, 6.3% at 33, 4.3% at 35 and 0.5% at 38 (the fight lab, 2026-11-23, 3,000 fights on each of two seeds).
- **Fire and Brimstone** (32-34) adds 25% critical strike chance to **Conflagrate**, from 39 when you start pressing it.
- From 31 to 38 the wand does 44% of your damage, with **Shadow Bolt** at 4% at most (the fight lab, 2026-11-23). The list keeps **Shadow Bolt** for when you have more than 60% of your mana and spends the rest on **Immolate**, **Bane of Agony** and **Shadowburn**: the wand costs nothing, and a bolt's mana has to be drunk back, so the re-tune of 2026-11-21 measured level 35 18% faster per kill that way (44.7 to 36.5 seconds) than bolting through it.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 33 at level 31, 38 at level 38. Presses per kill at level 31: Wand 12.4, Torment 4.4, Immolate 1.2, Bane of Agony 1.0, Shadowburn 1.0, Shadow Bolt 0.4.*

**The trainer teaches:** Howl of Terror (40).

**New ranks to buy:** Conflagrate at 40; Immolate at 40; Shadowburn at 40; Torment at 40.

**Talents:** 40 Incinerate, 41 Aftermath.

**Keys to set:** Shift+F = Howl of Terror.

## Between fights

- **Life Tap** back to about 70% mana while your health stays above 60%. Your health comes back while you rest, and the Voidwalker takes the next mob.
- Eat only below 40% of your health (the tuned rule of 2026-11-07). Drink only below 15% of your mana, when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**.
- Whenever you sit down to drink, eat as well if your health is not full, and drink while you eat if your mana is not full: the other bar fills in the same seconds at no extra time. Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. It was 2.5% faster per kill for this build in the fight lab (2026-11-16), and the tuned rules play it.
