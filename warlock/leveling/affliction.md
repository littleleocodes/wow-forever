## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Corruption**, **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** once each, none of them on a mob below about a third of its health; **Corruption** only while you have more than 15% mana.
2. **Immolate** only on a mob above about half its health and while you have more than half your mana (from **Wrack** on, that mana goes to **Wrack**).
3. With **Wrack**: channel it once **Corruption** is up, on a mob above a quarter of its health, while you have more than 30% mana.
4. Your wand while the dots finish the mob, never **Shadow Bolt**: the fight lab levels faster without it, since its mana costs more time than it saves.
5. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%.
6. **Life Tap** in a fight only when your mana is down to about 15%, you stay above 60% health after it and the mob is not nearly dead.
7. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health; otherwise keep casting (never **Fear** it) until the Voidwalker's **Torment** takes it back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

**Between fights:** **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, eat only below 55% health, and drink only below 15% mana (when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**).

## Why this rotation

Affliction levels on its damage-over-time spells and the wand while the Voidwalker holds the mob: **Corruption**, **Bane of Agony** and **Immolate** on a healthy mob (**Siphon Life** from level 34), the wand while they finish it, **Drain Life** when you are hurt, and from level 40 **Wrack**, channelled while you have the mana. No **Shadow Bolt** and no **Fear**.

**Why it works**

- Send the Voidwalker in first: its **Torment** holds the mob while you stand at range. **Suppression** (levels 12-15 and 31) gives 5% hit and cuts your threat by 20%, so the Voidwalker keeps the mob. **Amplify Curse** (level 20) goes on whenever it is ready (3-minute cooldown): it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger.
- No **Shadow Bolt** once you own a wand. The re-tune of 2026-11-16 measured kills about a quarter faster without it at levels 20 and 30 (23.5 against 30.6 seconds at 20), and a check at level 10 found 15%. While the Voidwalker holds the mob, a bolt hardly shortens the fight: the dots and the wand kill it in about the same time. But it triples the mana a kill costs, and drinking that back took 3 seconds a kill at level 10 and 7 to 10 seconds at 20 and 30 in the fight lab.
- **Corruption**, **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** (level 34) go on once each, none of them on a mob below about a third of its health: it dies before the spell pays for its mana. **Corruption** goes on only while you have more than 15% mana. **Immolate** only on a mob above about half its health, while you have more than half your mana: without bolts that is nearly every mob until 40, and from 40 **Wrack** spends that mana and the fight lab no longer casts it.
- **Wrack** (level 40, changed by the hotfix of 2026-11-10) is a 6-second channel of Shadow damage, 60 a second at level 60, that makes your **Corruption**, **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** tick twice as fast while you channel it. Channel it once **Corruption** is on a mob with more than a quarter of its health, and only while you have more than 30% of your mana: the re-tune measured that mana rule about 3% faster per kill. **Soul Siphon** (levels 32-33 and 41) adds 12% to it for each of your other Affliction effects on the mob, up to 36%. **Wrack** and **Siphon Life** are the build's two most valuable talents at 60: about 3.5 seconds a kill each in the open search, twice **Suppression**'s five points.
- **Drain Life** below 60% health, and in place of the wand below 85%. **Life Tap** in a fight only when your mana is down to about 15% and you stay above 60% health; between fights tap back to about 70% mana, and eat only below 55% health.

**Caveats**

- No **Curse of the Elements** while leveling: the re-tune of 2026-11-04 measured about 3% faster per kill without it (a global cooldown and mana on a mob that dies quickly), and the re-tune of 2026-11-16 kept it off.
- No **Fear**: with the Voidwalker holding the mob the fight lab hardly needs it, and the re-tune measured kills about 0.7% faster without it over levels 20-60. At level 8, before the Voidwalker, dotting and wanding a mob that reaches you was 3% faster than fearing it. Keep it for a pull that brings a second mob.
- This build has no **Nightfall** and no **Soul Link**. Its 10 Demonology points (levels 45-55 and 60) are for the Voidwalker: **Unholy Power** adds 10% to its damage, **Improved Voidwalker** makes **Torment** and **Consume Shadows** 30% stronger, **Demonic Energies** heals it for 15% of your spell damage and gives it the mana of each **Life Tap** too. **Destructive Reach** (56), **Grim Reach** (57 and 59) and the third point of **Improved Drains** (58) close the build: the fight lab measures them at about nothing. The leveling filler pass of 2026-11-16 put into them the points that measured nothing at all: the build no longer takes **Improved Shadow Bolt** (this list never casts **Shadow Bolt**), nor **Demonic Aegis**, nor **Demonic Embrace**, and keeps two of the three **Fel Concentration** points.
- Without **Shadow Bolt** Affliction spends little mana: the fight lab drinks well under a second a kill. When you are too hurt to **Life Tap**, drink below 15% mana.
- When a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** (level 42) if you are below about 60% health (it heals you for its damage); otherwise keep dotting and wanding, with **Drain Life** when you are hurt, until the Voidwalker's **Torment** takes it back.

**When to change it**

- Finish a nearly dead mob with the wand; start no new spell on it.
- No wand yet (the first levels): **Shadow Bolt** fills until you buy one.
- A second mob joins: **Fear** it (or **Banish** a demon or an elemental) and keep dotting the first; the single-mob list does not cover it.
- Below about a third of your health with a mob on you, use a Healthstone (**Create Healthstone** between fights).
- If the Voidwalker dies, summon a new one before the next pull rather than fight without your tank, and heal a hurt one between fights with **Consume Shadows** (not usable in combat) or **Health Funnel**.
