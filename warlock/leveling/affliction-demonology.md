## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Decimation**, **Improved Health Funnel** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger. With **Soul Link**: turn it on once the Voidwalker is out.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. With **Demonic Brand**: **Searing Pain** whenever the brand is gone and the Voidwalker is hitting the mob.
2. **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** on a mob above about a third of its health; **Corruption** only while you have more than half your mana; **Immolate** only above half your mana too, and not on a mob below about a third of its health.
3. Your wand is the filler: **Shadow Bolt** only above 80% mana (**Searing Pain** when the mob has too little health left for a bolt), which the dots and brands rarely leave you.
4. With **Decimation**: **Soul Fire** while its buff lasts (a **Shadow Bolt** or **Searing Pain** on a mob below 35% health starts it).
5. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%; **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health.
6. With **Improved Health Funnel**: **Health Funnel** a Voidwalker below 30% health while the mob still has more than a third of its health and you have more than 65% of yours.
7. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it, then **Drain Life** while it runs. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

**Between fights:** **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, eat only below 55% health, and drink only below 15% mana (when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**).

## Why this rotation

The 25/26/0 split plays the Demonology list: a Voidwalker made stronger by its first 26 points holds the mob, **Searing Pain** keeps **Demonic Brand** on it, **Bane of Agony**, **Siphon Life** and (with mana to spare) **Corruption** tick, and the wand fills.

**Why it works**

- The first 26 points (levels 10-35) are Demonology and make the Voidwalker the main damage source: **Unholy Power** (10-14) adds 10% to its damage, **Improved Voidwalker** (16-18) makes **Torment** and **Consume Shadows** 30% stronger, **Demonic Energies** (15 and 19) heals it for 15% of your spell damage, **Soul Link** (30) moves 30% of your damage taken to it and adds 3% damage for both, **Demonic Knowledge** (31-33) adds spell damage while it is out.
- **Searing Pain** whenever the brand is gone and the Voidwalker is hitting the mob: **Demonic Brand** (levels 26-28) makes its next 6 attacks deal about 65 extra damage each at level 60 and halves **Searing Pain**'s threat. The fight lab casts it about twice per kill from level 30.
- **Bane of Agony** on every mob above about a third of its health, **Siphon Life** from level 56. **Corruption** only while you have more than half your mana (the 2026-10-20 re-tune, about 3% faster per kill): about one mob in two at 40-60. **Immolate** needs half your mana too: about one mob in four after level 20, almost none at 60.
- The wand is your filler: **Shadow Bolt** waits for 80% mana, which the brands and dots never leave you, and the fight lab needs no drinking. **Improved Corruption** (42-45 and 49) only makes **Corruption** instant late.
- **Drain Life** below 60% health, and in place of the wand below 85%. **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health; between fights tap back to about 70% mana and eat only below 55% health.

**Caveats**

- This build has no **Decimation** and no **Improved Health Funnel**: skip the **Soul Fire** and in-fight **Health Funnel** lines. **Improved Sayaad**, **Fel Domination** and **Grim Reach** are steps to deeper talents; the fight lab gets nothing from them.
- When a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** if you are below about 60% health (it heals you for its damage), otherwise **Fear** it and **Drain Life** while it runs, until the Voidwalker's **Torment** takes it back.
- This split used to play the Affliction list (**Shadow Bolt** to a third of the mob's health); the open search found it in the Demonology list, where it levels about 20% faster per kill at levels 20-60 than the Affliction builds.

**When to change it**

- If the Voidwalker dies mid-fight, finish the mob with dots, **Death Coil** and the wand; **Master Summoner** shortens the next summon.
- Below about a third of your health with a mob on you, use a Healthstone (**Create Healthstone** between fights).
- Finish a nearly dead mob with the wand; start no new spell on it.
