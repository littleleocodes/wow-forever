## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger. With **Soul Link**: turn it on once the Voidwalker is out.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. With **Demonic Brand**: **Searing Pain** whenever the brand is gone, the Voidwalker is hitting the mob, the mob has more than a third of its health and you have more than 30% of your mana.
2. **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** on a mob above about a third of its health; **Corruption** only on a mob above 35% of its health; **Immolate** only above half your mana, and not on a mob below about a third of its health.
3. Your wand is the filler: **Shadow Bolt** only above 80% mana, which the dots and brands rarely leave you.
4. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%; **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health.
5. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it, then **Drain Life** while it runs. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

**Between fights:** **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, eat only below 55% health, and drink only below 15% mana (when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**). Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

## Why this rotation

The 25/26/0 split plays the Demonology list: a Voidwalker made stronger by the Demonology points holds the mob, **Searing Pain** keeps **Demonic Brand** on it, **Bane of Agony**, **Corruption** and **Siphon Life** tick, and the wand fills.

**Why it works**

- The Demonology points (levels 10-34 and 38) make the Voidwalker the main damage source: **Unholy Power** (10-14) adds 10% to its damage, **Improved Voidwalker** (16-18) makes **Torment** and **Consume Shadows** 30% stronger, **Demonic Energies** (15 and 19) heals it for 15% of your spell damage, **Soul Link** (30) moves 30% of your damage taken to it and adds 3% damage for both, **Demonic Knowledge** (31-33) adds spell damage while it is out, and **Master Demonologist** (38) takes 2% off the physical damage you both take.
- **Searing Pain** whenever the brand is gone, the Voidwalker is hitting the mob, the mob has more than a third of its health and you have more than 30% mana (the 2026-11-06 re-tune, about 1% faster per kill for each of the two limits): **Demonic Brand** (levels 27-29) makes the Voidwalker's next 2, 4 and from 29 6 attacks each deal 65 to 68 extra damage and halves **Searing Pain**'s threat.
- **Bane of Agony** and **Corruption** on every mob above about a third of its health (**Corruption** only above 35%, about 1% faster per kill), **Siphon Life** from level 60. **Immolate** only while you have more than half your mana: on every mob at level 20, almost none after 30.
- The wand is your filler: **Shadow Bolt** waits for 80% mana, which the brands and dots never leave you, and the fight lab needs no drinking. The Affliction points (levels 35-60) make your dots stronger: **Improved Corruption** makes **Corruption** instant at 55, **Pandemic** and **Malevolence** add to their critical strikes.
- **Drain Life** below 60% health, and in place of the wand below 85%. **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health; between fights tap back to about 70% mana and eat only below 55% health. Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).

**Caveats**

- This build has no **Improved Health Funnel**, so the in-fight **Health Funnel** line does not apply: heal the Voidwalker between fights. **Demonic Aegis** (22-23) makes **Demon Armor** 30% stronger; **Improved Imp** (20-21), **Improved Sayaad** (24) and **Improved Felhunter** (34) only fill tiers, the Voidwalker being your demon. **Master Summoner** shortens a new summon; **Demonic Sacrifice** (25) is the step to **Soul Link**, and **Grim Reach** (46) only widens the range of your Affliction spells.
- When a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** (level 42) if you are below about 60% health (it heals you for its damage), otherwise **Fear** it and **Drain Life** while it runs, until the Voidwalker's **Torment** takes it back.

**When to change it**

- If the Voidwalker dies mid-fight, finish the mob with dots, **Death Coil** and the wand, and summon a new one.
- Below about a third of your health with a mob on you, use a Healthstone (**Create Healthstone** between fights).
- Finish a nearly dead mob with the wand; start no new spell on it.
