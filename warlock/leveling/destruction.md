## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Finish first: **Shadowburn** on a mob it would kill, your wand for the last sliver of health.
2. From level 8, **Bane of Agony** on every mob (from 10 right after your opening **Shadow Bolt**), on a mob above 35% of its health: one cast a kill (the tuned rules: from 20 since 2026-11-22, from 8 by hand since 2026-11-23).
3. **Immolate** on a fresh mob above 30% of its health, unless it would pull the mob off the Voidwalker or a mob is already hitting you.
4. **Conflagrate** from 39, when **Shadow and Flame** is 5/5 and keeps **Immolate** burning: whenever it is ready, while the mob has more than a fifth of its health. Hold it before that: until then it uses up your **Immolate**, and pressing it even in **Immolate**'s last 3 seconds costs 4-6% a kill at 31-35 (the fight lab, 2026-11-23).
5. Between casts your wand is the filler. **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than 35% of its health. From level 40, while **Immolate** burns, the wand instead of the bolt: with Incinerate switched off, the fight lab levels faster wanding a burning mob. No Corruption, Searing Pain, Drain Life or Incinerate: the fight lab levels faster without them.
6. No **Life Tap** in a fight: with **Shadow Bolt** kept for spare mana the fight lab almost never needs one.
7. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, and let the Voidwalker's **Torment** take it back. No Fear: the tuned rules of 2026-11-07 never cast it. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

**Between fights:** **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, eat only below 40% health (the tuned rule), and drink only below 15% mana (when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**). Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

## Why this rotation

Destruction levels as a nuker behind the Voidwalker, and the wand is the filler: between casts you wand the mob. **Immolate** on a fresh mob, **Bane of Agony** on every pull from level 8, **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than 35% of its health, **Conflagrate** from level 39, whenever it is ready (the talent comes at 31, but until **Shadow and Flame** is 5/5 it uses up your **Immolate**, so it waits) and **Shadowburn** (29) to finish. The wand does 35-45% of your damage from 10 to 30 and 22-28% from 39 (the fight lab, 2026-11-23): the tuned rules keep **Shadow Bolt** for spare mana and switch **Incinerate** off. The list casts no **Corruption**.

**Why it works**

- The Destruction points come first: **Bane** (levels 10-14) makes **Shadow Bolt** and **Immolate** 0.5 seconds faster, **Destructive Reach** (15-16) adds 20% to the range of your Destruction spells, **Cataclysm** (17-19) makes your Destruction spells 10% cheaper, **Ruin** (20-24) doubles their critical strike bonus, **Agonizing Flames** (25-27) adds 10% to their damage and **Improved Shadow Bolt** (28) makes a **Shadow Bolt** critical strike raise the Shadow damage the mob takes from you by 4% for 12 seconds.
- Send the Voidwalker in first and put **Immolate** on a mob above 30% of its health, once it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker. From level 8 **Bane of Agony** goes on every mob (from 10 right after your opening **Shadow Bolt**), on a mob above 35% of its health: the tuned rules force it, from 20 since 2026-11-22 (6% faster per kill over levels 20-60 in the fight lab) and from 8 by hand since 2026-11-23 (11.6-12.5% faster at 8-9, 3-8% at 10-19).
- With 5 points in **Shadow and Flame** (levels 35-39), **Conflagrate** no longer eats your **Immolate**, so from 39 cast it whenever it is ready on a burning mob. Before that, hold it: a **Conflagrate** ends the **Immolate** it hits, and pressing it even in **Immolate**'s last 3 seconds costs 4-6% a kill at 31-35 and 0.5% at 38 (the fight lab, 2026-11-23; the rules hold it since the same day). **Shadow and Flame** also gives 20% more Shadow damage for 20 seconds after a **Conflagrate** and 20% more Fire damage after a **Shadowburn**; **Fire and Brimstone** (32-34) adds 25% critical strike chance to **Conflagrate**.
- Between casts the wand is the filler. **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana (the re-tune of 2026-11-21: 3% faster per kill over levels 20-60, 18% at 35) and never on a mob below 35% of its health (the 2026-11-07 re-tune, about 3% faster per kill). Finish with **Shadowburn** (or **Conflagrate** from 39) when either would kill it; **Conflagrate** itself is cast only on a mob above a fifth of its health.
- The same re-tune switched off **Fear** (about 5% faster per kill), **Drain Life** (about 3%), **Searing Pain** (about 1%) and **Incinerate** (about 3%). No **Life Tap** in a fight: with **Shadow Bolt** kept for spare mana the fight lab almost never needs one. **Corruption** stays off as well: forced in from level 20 it was 4% slower per kill (2026-11-22). Between fights tap back to 70% mana, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** or **Health Funnel**, eat below 40% health (the tuned rule). Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).

**Caveats**

- **Suppression** (levels 42-45): 4% hit and 16% less threat keep the mob on the Voidwalker while you nuke, about 1.7 seconds a kill at 60 in the open search. **Aftermath** (41, 46-49) adds 50% to **Immolate**'s first hit, and **Unholy Power** (50-54), **Improved Voidwalker** (55), **Demonic Energies** (56-57) and **Fel Vitality** (58) make the Voidwalker stronger, and **Improved Life Tap** (59-60) adds 20% to the mana of **Life Tap**.
- **Bane of Havoc** (30) is the step to **Incinerate** and does nothing here: this list fights one mob. The rules never cast **Incinerate** (40), but with it the list wands a burning mob instead of bolting it, which the fight lab measured faster: the open search of 2026-11-20 measured the build 1.8 seconds a kill slower at 60 without it.
- When a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** (level 42) below 60% health and let the Voidwalker's **Torment** take it back. The tuned rules never **Fear** it. A Healthstone below 35% health.

**When to change it**

- Start no **Immolate** on a mob below about a third of its health: finish it with **Shadowburn**, **Conflagrate** or the wand.
- If the Voidwalker dies, summon a new one before the next pull: this list builds threat fast and needs a tank.
