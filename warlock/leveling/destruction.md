## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Finish first: **Shadowburn** on a mob it would kill, your wand for the last sliver of health.
2. **Immolate** on a fresh mob above 30% of its health, unless it would pull the mob off the Voidwalker or a mob is already hitting you.
3. **Conflagrate** when **Immolate** has about 3 seconds left (whenever it is ready with 5 points in **Shadow and Flame**, which keeps **Immolate** burning), while the mob has more than a fifth of its health.
4. The filler is **Shadow Bolt** while you have more than 60% of your mana and the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health; otherwise your wand. From level 40, while **Immolate** burns, the wand instead of the bolt: with Incinerate switched off, the fight lab levels faster wanding a burning mob. No Affliction dots, Searing Pain, Drain Life or Incinerate: the fight lab levels faster without them.
5. No **Life Tap** in a fight: with **Shadow Bolt** kept for spare mana the fight lab never needs one.
6. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, and let the Voidwalker's **Torment** take it back. No Fear: the tuned rules of 2026-11-07 never cast it. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

**Between fights:** **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, eat only below 40% health (the tuned rule), and drink only below 15% mana (when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**). Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

## Why this rotation

Destruction levels as a nuker behind the Voidwalker: **Immolate** on a fresh mob, **Shadow Bolt** while you have more than 60% of your mana and the wand below that, **Conflagrate** (level 30) whenever it is ready and **Shadowburn** (29) to finish. The wand does a third to almost half of your damage from 10 to 39 and a quarter to a third from 40 (the fight lab, 2026-11-22): the tuned rules keep **Shadow Bolt** for spare mana and switch **Incinerate** off. This build has no Affliction dots and the list casts none.

**Why it works**

- The Destruction points come first: **Bane** (levels 10-14) makes **Shadow Bolt** and **Immolate** 0.5 seconds faster, **Destructive Reach** (15-16) adds 20% to the range of your Destruction spells, **Cataclysm** (17-19) makes your Destruction spells 10% cheaper, **Ruin** (20-24) doubles their critical strike bonus and **Agonizing Flames** (25-27) adds 10% to their damage.
- Send the Voidwalker in first and put **Immolate** on a mob above about a third of its health, once it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker.
- With 5 points in **Shadow and Flame** (levels 35-39), **Conflagrate** no longer eats your **Immolate**, so cast it whenever it is ready on a burning mob; before that, cast it when **Immolate** has about 3 seconds left. **Shadow and Flame** also gives 20% more Shadow damage for 20 seconds after a **Conflagrate** and 20% more Fire damage after a **Shadowburn**; **Fire and Brimstone** (32-34) adds 25% critical strike chance to **Conflagrate**.
- Fill with **Shadow Bolt** only while you have more than 60% of your mana (the re-tune of 2026-11-21: 3% faster per kill over levels 20-60, 18% at 35) and never on a mob below 35% of its health (the 2026-11-07 re-tune, about 3% faster per kill); otherwise the wand, and finish with **Shadowburn** or **Conflagrate** when either would kill it. **Conflagrate** itself is cast only on a mob above a fifth of its health.
- The same re-tune switched off **Fear** (about 5% faster per kill), **Drain Life** (about 3%), **Searing Pain** (about 1%) and **Incinerate** (about 3%). No **Life Tap** in a fight: with **Shadow Bolt** kept for spare mana the fight lab never needs one. Between fights tap back to 70% mana, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** or **Health Funnel**, eat below 40% health (the tuned rule). Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).

**Caveats**

- **Suppression** (levels 42-45): 4% hit and 16% less threat keep the mob on the Voidwalker while you nuke, about 1.7 seconds a kill at 60 in the open search. **Aftermath** (41, 46-49) adds 50% to **Immolate**'s first hit, and **Unholy Power** (50-54), **Improved Voidwalker** (55, 59-60), **Demonic Energies** (56-57) and **Fel Vitality** (58) make the Voidwalker stronger.
- **Bane of Havoc** (31) is a tier point and does nothing here: this list fights one mob. The rules never cast **Incinerate** (40), but with it the list wands a burning mob instead of bolting it, which the fight lab measured faster: the open search of 2026-11-20 measured the build 1.8 seconds a kill slower at 60 without it. **Intensity** (28) keeps your casts from being pushed back 23% of the time a mob hits you.
- When a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** (level 42) below 60% health and let the Voidwalker's **Torment** take it back. The tuned rules never **Fear** it. A Healthstone below 35% health.

**When to change it**

- Start no **Immolate** on a mob below about a third of its health: finish it with **Shadowburn**, **Conflagrate** or the wand.
- If the Voidwalker dies, summon a new one before the next pull: this list builds threat fast and needs a tank.
