## demonology-leveling

Played by the page build of [Demonology](../../leveling/demonology.md) ([`demonology-leveling`](builds/demonology-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger. With **Soul Link**: turn it on once the Voidwalker is out.

1. With **Demonic Brand**: **Searing Pain** whenever the brand is gone and the Voidwalker is hitting the mob.
2. **Corruption** (only while you have more than 30% mana), **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** on the mob, and **Immolate** while you have more than half your mana; none of them on a mob below about a third of its health.
3. With **Decimation**: **Soul Fire** while its buff lasts (a **Shadow Bolt** or **Searing Pain** on a mob below 35% health starts it).
4. Above 80% mana, **Shadow Bolt** (**Searing Pain** when the mob has too little health left for it); below that, your wand.
5. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%; **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health.
6. With **Improved Health Funnel**: **Health Funnel** a Voidwalker below 30% health while the mob still has more than a third of its health and you have more than 65% of yours.
7. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it, then **Drain Life** while it runs. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

Between fights: **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, and eat only below 55% health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-15, on top of the priority below): Corruption only above 30% mana; Corruption only while the mob has more than 35% health. Together +2.4% time per kill saved for `demonology-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`demonology-leveling`](builds/demonology-leveling.md): not recorded (its times per kill come from the race records of the search that found it, which keep times only; the table above has them).

## destruction-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Shadow Bolt hybrid](../../leveling/shadow-bolt-hybrid.md) ([`shadow-bolt-hybrid-open-leveling`](builds/shadow-bolt-hybrid-open-leveling.md)), [Destruction](../../leveling/destruction.md) ([`destruction-leveling`](builds/destruction-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger.

1. **Corruption** and **Bane of Agony** first while the Voidwalker builds threat: no **Corruption** on a mob below a quarter of its health, no **Bane of Agony** below a third.
2. **Immolate** while you have more than 30% of your mana, the mob has more than a third of its health and it will not pull the mob off the Voidwalker.
3. **Conflagrate** when **Immolate** has about 3 seconds left (at once with 5 points in **Shadow and Flame**, which keeps **Immolate** burning), or to finish a burning mob it would kill; then **Siphon Life**.
4. Above 80% mana, **Shadow Bolt** (**Searing Pain** when the mob has too little health left for it); below that, your wand.
5. With **Incinerate**: cast it instead of **Shadow Bolt** on a burning mob. With **Shadowburn**: finish any mob it would kill.
6. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%; **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana and above 60% health.
7. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it, then **Drain Life** while it runs, until **Torment** takes it back. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

Between fights: **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, and eat only below 55% health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-15, on top of the priority below): Immolate only above 30% mana. Together +2.7% time per kill saved for `shadow-bolt-hybrid-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`shadow-bolt-hybrid-open-leveling`](builds/shadow-bolt-hybrid-open-leveling.md), [`destruction-leveling`](builds/destruction-leveling.md): not recorded (their times per kill come from the race records of the searches that found them, which keep times only; the table above has them).

## affliction-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Affliction](../../leveling/affliction.md) ([`affliction-leveling`](builds/affliction-leveling.md)), [Affliction-Demonology](../../leveling/affliction-demonology.md) ([`affliction-demonology-open-leveling`](builds/affliction-demonology-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a Voidwalker: the pet runs in and meets the mob halfway, Torments it every 5 s, and holds it until your threat passes 130% of its own (it takes the mob back once its threat passes yours by 10%). You cast Corruption, Bane of Agony, Siphon Life and Immolate, channel Wrack once the DoTs are up, fill with Shadow Bolt while mana is plentiful and the wand otherwise, Drain Life when hurt, Life Tap when low on mana, Fear or Death Coil a mob that reaches you. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Life Tap back to 70% mana, Consume Shadows or Health Funnel for the pet, food only below 55% health, a 10-12 s re-summon after a pet death; a death costs 90 s.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger. With **Soul Link**: turn it on once the Voidwalker is out.

1. **Curse of the Elements** first on a mob above half its health, while you have more than 30% mana.
2. **Corruption**, **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** on the mob, none of them on a mob below about a third of its health.
3. **Immolate** only on a mob above half its health and while you have more than half your mana.
4. With **Wrack**: channel it once **Corruption** is up.
5. **Shadow Bolt** while the mob has more than about a third of its health and you have mana to spare; your wand otherwise.
6. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%.
7. **Life Tap** in a fight only when you are short of mana, above 60% health and the mob is below about a third of its health.
8. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it, then **Drain Life** while it runs. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

Between fights: **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, and eat only below 55% health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-09, on top of the priority below): Shadow Bolt only while the mob has more than 35% health; Corruption only while the mob has more than 35% health; Life Tap only when the mob is below 35% health; Curse of the Elements only above 30% mana. Together +10.8% time per kill saved for `leveling-affliction` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
