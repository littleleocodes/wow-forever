## affliction-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Affliction](../../leveling/affliction.md) ([`affliction-leveling`](builds/affliction-leveling.md)), [Demonology](../../leveling/demonology.md) ([`demonology-leveling`](builds/demonology-leveling.md)), [Destruction](../../leveling/destruction.md) ([`destruction-leveling`](builds/destruction-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a Voidwalker: the pet runs in and meets the mob halfway, Torments it every 5 s, and holds it until your threat passes 130% of its own (it takes the mob back once its threat passes yours by 10%). You cast Corruption, Bane of Agony, Siphon Life and Immolate, channel Wrack once the DoTs are up, fill with Shadow Bolt while mana is plentiful and the wand otherwise, Drain Life when hurt, Life Tap when low on mana, Fear or Death Coil a mob that reaches you. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Life Tap back to 70% mana, Consume Shadows or Health Funnel for the pet, food only below 55% health, a 10-12 s re-summon after a pet death; a death costs 90 s.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Demon Skin** (**Demon Armor** from level 20) up, **Summon Voidwalker**. Send the Voidwalker in first and let it hold the mob with **Torment**. Open with **Amplify Curse** whenever it is ready: it makes that fight's **Bane of Agony** 50% stronger. With **Soul Link**: turn it on once the Voidwalker is out.

1. **Corruption**, **Bane of Agony** and **Siphon Life** once each, none of them on a mob below about a third of its health; **Corruption** only while you have more than 15% mana.
2. **Immolate** only on a mob above about half its health and while you have more than half your mana.
3. With **Wrack**: channel it once **Corruption** is up.
4. **Shadow Bolt** while the mob has more than about a third of its health and you keep the mana for **Fear** and **Drain Life**; your wand otherwise while the dots finish the mob.
5. **Drain Life** whenever you drop below 60% health, and in place of the wand while you are below 85%.
6. **Life Tap** in a fight only when the next **Shadow Bolt** would eat into that mana reserve, you are above 60% health and the mob still has more health than one bolt takes.
7. If a mob reaches you: **Death Coil** below 60% health, otherwise **Fear** it, then **Drain Life** while it runs. Your Healthstone below 35% health.

Between fights: **Life Tap** back to 70% mana while your health stays above 60%, heal the Voidwalker with **Consume Shadows** (or **Health Funnel**) when it is below 60%, eat only below 55% health, and drink only below 15% mana (when you are too hurt to **Life Tap**).

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-01, on top of the priority below): Shadow Bolt only while the mob has more than 35% health; never cast Curse of the Elements; Corruption only while the mob has more than 35% health; Corruption only above 15% mana. Together +11.6% time per kill saved for `leveling-affliction` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
