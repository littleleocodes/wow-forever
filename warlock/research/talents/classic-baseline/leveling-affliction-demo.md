## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Improved Corruption** — Instant Corruption from 10 — DoT everything, let the Voidwalker tank.
2. **Improved Drain Life** — Drain tanking keeps you full while the Voidwalker holds threat.
3. **Siphon Life** — Another self-healing DoT at 30.
4. **Shadow Mastery** — +10% Shadow damage at 39.
5. **Nightfall** — Instant Shadow Bolts.

## Notes

- Multi-DoT 2-3 mobs while the Voidwalker tanks; Drain Life to refill; Life Tap between pulls.
