## How to play

**Before the pull, then keep up all fight:** **Seal of Command**.

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Wrath** when the target is below 20% health
2. **Consecration**
3. **Holy Strike**

**Holy Strike** is your filler: cast it whenever nothing above is ready.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Seal of Righteousness**: part of the rotation (about 41 times a fight)
- Use your trinkets, your racial and a potion together with your big cooldowns.
