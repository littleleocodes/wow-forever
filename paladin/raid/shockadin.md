## How to play

**Press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Holy Shock**
2. **Hammer of Wrath** when the target is below 20% health
3. **Judgement** when Seal of Righteousness is active
4. **Consecration** when you have at least 25% mana
5. **Holy Strike**

Between these, your white swings do the rest.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Seal of Righteousness**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 6 times a fight)
- **Divine Favor**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 2 times a fight)
- Use your trinkets and a potion together with your big cooldowns.
