## How to play

**Press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Judgement**; no global cooldown, so press it alongside the others
2. **Consecration**
3. **Holy Strike**
4. **Divine Favor** right before your next big heal; no global cooldown, so press it alongside the others
5. **Holy Shock**
6. **Hammer of Wrath** when the target is below 20% health

Between these, your white swings do the rest.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Seal of Righteousness**: part of the rotation (about 10 times a fight)
- **Seal of the Crusader**: put it up at the start and keep it up
- **Retribution Aura**: put it up at the start and keep it up
