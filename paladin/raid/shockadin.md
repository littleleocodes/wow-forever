## How to play

**Talents with no line below:** **Seal of Command**: taken because Vengeance requires it.

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Judgement**
2. **Consecration**
3. **Holy Strike**
4. **Divine Favor** right before your next big heal
5. **Holy Shock**
6. **Hammer of Wrath** when the target is below 20% health

Between these, your white swings do the rest.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Seal of Righteousness**: part of the rotation (about 10 times a fight)
- **Seal of the Crusader**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 1 time a fight)
- **Retribution Aura**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 1 time a fight)
- Use your trinkets and a potion together with your big cooldowns.
