# Paladin

> Classic: the Alliance raid healer (Holy, with Protection and Retribution dips for raid blessings); Retribution and Protection were
> dungeon and PvP specs. Forever makes Kings and Consecration baseline, gives Retribution stored seals (Unsealed Wrath, called Twist of Light until build 70753), gives
> Protection a full tank kit (Holy Shield, Templar's Bulwark, Iron Creed, Swift Judgement) and adds Undead paladins to the Horde.

## Forever headline changes (beta client build 1.60.1.70753)

Forever's paladin has 51 talents (17 Holy, 17 Protection, 17 Retribution): 21 are new, 30 carry over from Classic (22 with changed text, 5 moved, 3 unchanged), and 13 Classic talents are gone.

- **Baseline now:** Blessing of Kings and Consecration; blessings last 1 hour (Classic: 5 minutes); Holy Strike (8 ranks from level 6, weapon damage plus Holy damage, 10-second cooldown); Seal of Fury (7 ranks from level 10: Holy damage on every swing and, with a shield, an absorb of half of it; its judgement taunts for 4 seconds). Judgement no longer uses up your seal.
- **Removed talents (Classic):** Improved Seal of Righteousness, Consecration, Improved Lay on Hands, Improved Blessing of Wisdom, Lasting Judgement (Holy); Improved Devotion Aura, Blessing of Kings, Improved Concentration Aura, Blessing of Sanctuary (Protection); Improved Blessing of Might, Improved Seal of the Crusader, Improved Retribution Aura, Sanctity Aura (Retribution). Removed during the beta: Improved Holy Strike, Crusade, Voice of Truth (now a spell), Improved Seal of Fury, One-Handed Weapon Specialization, Instrument of Law.
- **New talents:** Reverence, Improved Seals, Purifying Power, Holy Conduit, Divine Precision, Infusion of Light, Oath of Light, Consecrated Ground, Boundless Light, Light's Vigil (Holy); Sacred Duty, Templar's Bulwark, Swift Judgement, Stoicism, Iron Creed, Shieldsworn (Protection); Sanctified Judgement, Sacred Arbiter, Turn Evil, Champion of the Light, Unsealed Wrath (Retribution).
- **Moved or reworked (70753 tooltips):** Holy Shock, Holy Shield and Repentance sit 20 points deep; Light's Vigil and Unsealed Wrath are the 31-point talents. Unsealed Wrath (called Twist of Light until build 70753) stores up to 3 copies of your active Seal for 15 seconds; landing an attack under a different Seal spends them. Swift Judgement is a second Judgement on its own 1-minute cooldown. Sacred Duty shortens the cooldown of Divine Shield, Divine Protection and Templar's Bulwark (30 seconds a rank) and Forbearance (5 seconds a rank). Righteous Fury: 33% more threat and 90% more Holy threat. Holy Power: 10% a rank to the critical healing bonus of Holy spells. Vengeance: 1% a rank per stack, up to 5 stacks, 30 seconds. Champion of the Light: 5 ranks of 12% of Intellect as spell damage, and 0.2 seconds a rank off Hammer of Wrath's cast. Reckoning: 8% a rank after a block, 20% after a critical hit taken, up to 4 stored extra attacks, blocks at most once every 1.5 seconds. Shield Specialization (2 ranks) returns 4% of your mana on a block, once every 3 seconds. Illumination: 20% a rank to return half a critical heal's base cost. Reverence: 15% a rank of mana regeneration while casting (2 ranks). Improved Seals: 3% a rank to Seals and Judgements (5 ranks).
- **Spells:** top ranks against Classic: Holy Light 1,495-1,665 (Classic 1,590-1,770), Flash of Light 291-325 (348-388), Holy Shock 307-333 healing (365-395); Blessing of Might 133 attack power (185), Blessing of Wisdom 40 mana every 5 seconds (33); Consecration 96 Holy damage over 8 seconds plus 216 more to the first 4 enemies in it (384 to every enemy), 4.2% of spell power a tick; Holy Wrath stuns for 2 seconds; Divine Shield halves the damage you deal; Holy Shield 221 damage per block (130).
- **Beta history:** 2026-10-02: Holy Shield's block chance 20% to 30%; spell ranks far below your level get less from your spell damage and healing (no formula published; this project assumes TBC's). Build 70291 (2026-10-08): Retribution Aura uses the paladin's spell power with Classic's base damage; Seal of Fury returns mana only with Improved Seal of Fury (since removed). Build 70425 (2026-10-16): Seal of Righteousness had gained almost twice its intended benefit from spell damage, so Judgement of Righteousness and Judgement of Fury got instant-spell coefficients (42.9%); Consecration 0.042 per tick (was 0.095); Righteous Fury 90% Holy threat (was 60%); Shield Specialization 4% mana (was 6%); Sanctified Judgement's mana return no longer generates threat; Seal of Fury is no longer overwritten by Power Word: Shield.
