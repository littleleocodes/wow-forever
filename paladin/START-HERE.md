# Paladin: start here

[All classes](../START-HERE.md)

Classic's Alliance raid healer (Holy, with Protection/Retribution dips for raid blessings); Retribution and Protection were dungeon/PvP specs. Forever makes Kings and Consecration baseline, gives Retribution seal twisting (Twist of Light), gives Protection a full tank kit (Templar's Bulwark, Iron Creed, Sacred Duty) and adds Undead paladins to the Horde.
