# Shockadin Paladin — leveling, level by level

[Paladin](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Shockadin](shockadin.md) > Level by level

What a Shockadin presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage. It fights like a Retribution paladin with a two-handed weapon and plays the Holy list: **Seal of Righteousness** (**Seal of Command** from 41), **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Blessing of Wisdom** for mana, and from 30 **Holy Shock**, Holy's signature spell, whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 35% of its health. **Hammer of Wrath** (44) finishes the mob. Holy takes the first 21 points (10-30: **Divine Favor** at 27, **Holy Shock** at 30), Retribution the other 30 (31-60: **Seal of Command** at 41, **Vengeance** at 51-53, **Champion of the Light** at 56-60). Below 40% health you heal yourself in the fight. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 21/0/30 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/paladin/60/05025111220101--1523330100130035-12)), the one on the [Shockadin leveling page](shockadin.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Seal of Righteousness** on yourself, then your weapon does the work. |
| 4-5 | **Judgement** unleashes your seal on the mob every 10 seconds. In Forever the seal stays up afterwards. |
| 6-7 | **Holy Strike**, an instant weapon strike every 10 seconds. With **Judgement** it is your whole rotation. |
| 8-9 | **Hammer of Justice**, a stun: stop a caster, or buy time to heal. |
| 10-19 | Talent points start, in Holy. **Lay on Hands** is a full heal for emergencies. Nothing new to press in a normal fight. |
| 20-26 | Nothing new in the rotation: **Seal of Righteousness** stays your seal until **Seal of Command** at 41. **Flash of Light** joins the heals. |
| 27-29 | **Divine Favor** (talent): a sure critical for your next heal, and from 30 for **Holy Shock**. |
| 30-40 | **Holy Shock** (talent): an instant Holy blast from 40 yards, or an instant heal on yourself. The Holy list casts it at the mob whenever it is ready. |
| 41-43 | **Seal of Command** (talent) replaces **Seal of Righteousness** as your seal: a chance on each swing to add Holy damage equal to 70% of your weapon's, and a judgement that hits a stunned mob twice as hard. |
| 44-60 | **Hammer of Wrath** finishes a mob below 20% health from range. The Holy list presses it. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; the blessing, the aura, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The Shockadin keeps the Retribution layout, so muscle memory carries over: Q the strike you press most, E the stun, R the seal and **Judgement**, F the bubble, the thumb buttons the heals. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the macros: the [paladin keybinds page](keybinds.md).

## Levels 1-3: Seal of Righteousness

**The trainer teaches:** Devotion Aura (1), Holy Light (1), **Seal of Righteousness** (1).

**Keys to set:** R = Seal of Righteousness; Mouse 5 = Holy Light; C = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** R Seal of Righteousness.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Righteousness** before the pull. It lasts 30 seconds: put it back when it runs out.
2. Walk in and swing.

- Keep **Devotion Aura** on. Heal with **Holy Light** between fights.
- Use a two-handed weapon from the first one you find: the Holy list fights with one, and every seal and strike hits harder with a slow, heavy weapon.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 12 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Melee 1.7, Seal of Righteousness 1.0.*

## Levels 4-5: Judgement

**The trainer teaches:** Blessing of Might (4), **Judgement** (4).

**Keys to set:** R = Judgement, macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** R Judgement.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
2. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 16 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Melee 3.0, Judgement 1.2, Seal of Righteousness 1.0.*

## Levels 6-7: Holy Strike

**The trainer teaches:** Divine Protection (6), **Holy Strike** (6), Seal of the Crusader (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Holy Light at 6.

**Keys to set:** Q = Holy Strike; F = Divine Protection.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
2. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
3. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 40% of your health, with the mob above a fifth of its own: **Holy Light**. Below 30%, **Divine Protection** first.

- **Seal of the Crusader** (6) is not part of the leveling rotation. Stay on **Seal of Righteousness**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 16 at level 6. Presses per kill at level 6: Melee 2.9, Judgement 1.2, Holy Strike 1.2, Seal of Righteousness 1.0.*

## Levels 10-19: Talents begin, Lay on Hands

**The trainer teaches:** Blessing of Protection (10), Lay on Hands (10), Righteous Fury (10), Seal of Fury (10), Redemption (12), Blessing of Wisdom (14), Retribution Aura (16), Blessing of Freedom (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Divine Protection at 18; Holy Light at 14; Holy Strike at 12; Seal of Righteousness at 10, 18.

**Talents:** 10-14 Divine Strength, 15-17 Improved Seals, 18-19 Reverence.

**Keys to set:** 1 = Blessing of Protection; Shift+Mouse 5 = Lay on Hands; Shift+F = Blessing of Freedom (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, E Hammer of Justice, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light, Shift+Mouse 5 Lay on Hands.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells. It stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
3. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
4. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 40% of your health, with the mob above a fifth of its own: **Divine Protection** first if you are below 30%, then **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**. **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

- The first points: **Divine Strength** (10-14) adds 10% Strength, **Improved Seals** (15-17, then 23-24) makes your seals and judgements hit 15% harder at five points, **Reverence** (18-19) keeps 30% of your mana regeneration going while you cast.
- **Seal of Fury** (10) and **Righteous Fury** (10) are for a shield and for tanking. Leave them alone: this build fights with a two-hander.
- **Blessing of Protection** (10) and **Blessing of Freedom** (18) are tools for a bad pull, not rotation.
- Buy the next rank of the defensive spells on your bars too: **Divine Protection** at 18 (8 seconds instead of 6), **Blessing of Protection** at 24 and 38 (8, then 10 seconds), **Lay on Hands** at 30 and 50 and **Divine Shield** at 50.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 18 at level 10, 21 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Melee 3.2, Judgement 1.4, Holy Strike 1.3, Seal of Righteousness 1.0, Hammer of Justice 0.1.*

**New ranks to buy:** Blessing of Protection at 24; Flash of Light at 26; Hammer of Justice at 24; Holy Light at 22; Holy Strike at 20; Seal of Righteousness at 26.

**Talents:** 20-21 Holy Conduit, 22 Purifying Power, 23-24 Improved Seals, 25-26 Divine Precision.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Flash of Light; 2 = Exorcism; Shift+Q = Consecration; Shift+Mouse 4 = Turn Undead (at 24).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, E Hammer of Justice, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light, Mouse 4 Flash of Light, Shift+Mouse 5 Lay on Hands.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells. It stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
3. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
4. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 40% of your health, with the mob above a fifth of its own: **Divine Protection** first if you are below 30%, then **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**. **Flash of Light** below a quarter when you cannot afford **Holy Light**. **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

- **Consecration** (20): the Holy list never casts it against a single mob. It is for packs.
- **Exorcism** (20) only works on Undead and Demons. Use it on cooldown against those; the fight lab does not model it.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 21 at level 20, 24 at level 26. Presses per kill at level 20: Melee 4.1, Judgement 1.8, Holy Strike 1.7, Seal of Righteousness 1.0, Hammer of Justice 0.1.*

## Levels 30-40: Holy Shock

**The trainer teaches:** Divine Intervention (30), Seal of Light (30), Frost Resistance Aura (32), Divine Shield (34), Fire Resistance Aura (36), Seal of Wisdom (38).

**New ranks to buy:** Blessing of Protection at 38; Consecration at 30, 40; Exorcism at 36; Flash of Light at 34; Hammer of Justice at 40; Holy Light at 30, 38; Holy Shock at 40; Holy Strike at 36; Lay on Hands at 30; Seal of Righteousness at 34; Turn Undead at 38.

**Talents:** 30 Holy Shock, 31-35 Benediction, 36-38 Sanctified Judgement, 39-40 Conviction.

**Keys to set:** Middle mouse = Holy Shock; F = Divine Shield (at 34).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, E Hammer of Justice, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light, Mouse 4 Flash of Light, 3 Divine Favor, Middle mouse Holy Shock, Shift+Mouse 5 Lay on Hands.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells. It stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. **Holy Shock** at the mob whenever it is ready, while the mob has more than 35% of its health: from 40 yards as it walks in, then in melee. **Divine Favor** first while your own health is above 70%, for a sure critical.
3. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
4. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
5. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 40% of your health, with the mob above a fifth of its own: **Holy Shock** on yourself if it is ready, then **Divine Protection** if you are below 30%, then **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Divine Favor** before the first of those heals if it is ready. **Flash of Light** below a quarter when you cannot afford **Holy Light**. **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

- **Holy Shock** has a 10-second cooldown and costs mana: one or two casts a kill in the fight lab, so you drink more often than before 30.
- With a mob targeted, **Holy Shock** hits the mob. Hold Alt, the self-cast key on the [keybinds page](keybinds.md), to heal yourself with it instead.
- Wear melee gear (Strength), not spell power and Intellect: **Holy Shock** is a cast or two a kill, and your swings, seal and **Judgement** do the rest. A check of 2026-11-16 found this build 6-10% faster per kill in melee gear at 30-45 and 2-5% at 60, and the tuned rules took melee gear at every level, so the seconds on this page are melee gear's (the fight lab had dressed every Holy build with **Holy Shock** in caster gear).
- From 31 the points go into Retribution: **Benediction** (31-35) makes instant spells such as **Holy Shock**, your seal and **Judgement** 10% cheaper, **Sanctified Judgement** (36-38) returns 60% of the judged seal's mana cost, **Conviction** (39-40) adds critical chance to spells and attacks. Nothing new to press until 41.
- From 34, **Divine Shield** replaces **Divine Protection** as your bubble: longer, and it takes the same key.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 28 at level 30, 32 at level 40. Presses per kill at level 30: Melee 4.2, Judgement 1.9, Holy Strike 1.8, Holy Shock 1.3, Seal of Righteousness 1.0, Divine Favor 0.2, Hammer of Justice 0.1.*

## Between fights

- Heal yourself back to about 80% with **Holy Light**, or with **Flash of Light** from level 20.
- Drink below 15% of your mana, and eat if you are still below 80% of your health after the heals: the tuned rules (eating at 80% since 2026-11-16, against the Holy list's own two thirds; drinking at 15% since 2026-11-17, against its quarter: about 1% faster per kill once you eat while you drink).
- Whenever you sit down to drink, eat as well if your health is not full, and drink while you eat if your mana is not full: the other bar fills in the same seconds at no extra time. Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. It was 1.4% faster per kill for this build in the fight lab (2026-11-16), and the tuned rules play it.
- Re-cast **Blessing of Wisdom** when it runs out, and your seal just before each pull.
