## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Divine Favor**, **Holy Shock** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Wisdom** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. **Seal of Righteousness** before each pull; **Seal of Command** instead once your build has it.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Once you have **Holy Shock**: cast it at the mob as it walks in while your mana is above 70%, after **Divine Favor** for a sure critical hit while you are healthy.
3. Keep your seal up.
4. With **Seal of Command**: **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left (the judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard).
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. No Hammer of Wrath and no Consecration: the tuned rotation casts neither.
6. Low on health (below 40%, with the mob above a fifth): **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink below a quarter of your mana.

## Why this rotation

The Shockadin leveling build is a two-handed Retribution paladin with Holy's **Improved Seals** and **Divine Strength**; despite the name it takes no **Holy Shock**. It plays the Holy leveling list, so **Blessing of Wisdom**, no **Consecration**, and from level 20 **Seal of Command** as your seal. Over levels 20-60 it is the fastest paladin leveling build in the fight lab, about level with Retribution.

**Why it works**

- The fight lab's Holy list uses **Seal of Command** once you have it (level 20; **Seal of Righteousness** before): it adds a Holy hit of 70% of your weapon damage on some swings, so a slow two-hander suits it, and its judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard. When **Hammer of Justice** and **Judgement** are both ready and the mob has more than 30% health, stun first, then judge.
- **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready: **Sanctified Judgement** (levels 21-23) returns 60% of your seal's mana cost each time, and **Improved Judgement** (24 and 34) brings **Judgement** back every 8 seconds. In the open search **Sanctified Judgement** is the build's most valuable talent at 60 (about 3.5 seconds per kill), ahead of **Conviction** (15-19) and **Improved Seals** (31-33).
- No **Consecration** and no **Hammer of Wrath**: the Holy list never casts **Consecration**, and its tuned rules (re-tuned 2026-10-19 on this build) skip **Hammer of Wrath**, which has a cast time without **Instrument of Law** (about 3% faster kills).
- Heal in the fight once you drop below 40% health and the mob has more than a fifth left: **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. Against same-level mobs the fight lab almost never needed them.

**Caveats**

- This build takes neither **Holy Shock** nor **Divine Favor**: skip those lines.
- Drink below a quarter of your mana (the Holy list's rule); between fights heal back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**. **Sanctified Judgement** keeps the drinking close to none.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** hits them hard; the fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the Holy list was not measured on several mobs with this build.
