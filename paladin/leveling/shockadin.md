## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Wisdom** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. **Seal of Righteousness** before each pull; **Seal of Command** instead once your build has it.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Once you have **Holy Shock**: cast it at the mob whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 10% of its health left, from range as it walks in and then in melee, after **Divine Favor** for a sure critical hit while you are healthy.
3. Keep your seal up.
4. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
5. With **Seal of Command**: **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left (the judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard).
6. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration: the Holy list never casts it.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink only below 15% of your mana, and eat if you are still below 80% of your health.

## Why this rotation

The Shockadin leveling page is the open search's winner for the Holy rotation (2026-11-16): 22/0/29, Holy's talents first (10-30), then Retribution's (31-60, with one more Holy point at 59). It plays the Holy list with a two-hander: **Seal of Righteousness** (**Seal of Command** from 41), **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Hammer of Wrath** to finish from 44, and from level 30 **Holy Shock** at the mob whenever it is ready, about twice a kill.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-30 are Holy: **Divine Strength** (10-14, 2% Strength a rank), **Improved Seals** (15-17 and 23-24, 3% seal and judgement damage a rank), **Reverence** (18-19, 30% of mana regeneration while casting), **Holy Conduit** (20-21: **Exorcism**, **Consecration**, **Holy Wrath** and **Hammer of Wrath** 40% cheaper), **Purifying Power** (22), **Divine Precision** (25-26, 8% hit for Holy spells), **Divine Favor** (27), **Infusion of Light** (28), **Oath of Light** (29) and **Holy Shock** (30).
- **Holy Shock** (level 30, 10-second cooldown): at the mob whenever it is ready while it has more than 10% health left, from range as it walks in and then in melee, about twice a kill; **Divine Favor** first while your health is above 70%, for a sure critical. From 30 the fight lab dresses the build in caster gear (spell power and Intellect), as it does every Holy build that casts **Holy Shock**, and the page's seconds are that gear's. A check of 2026-11-16 ran the same build and rules in melee gear (Strength): 6-10% faster per kill at 30-55 and 2-3% at 60, so for solo leveling wear melee gear if you have it.
- Then Retribution: **Benediction** (31-35, instants 10% cheaper), **Sanctified Judgement** (36-38: **Judgement** returns 60% of the judged seal's mana cost), **Conviction** (39-40 and 42, 6% critical strike chance), **Seal of Command** (41), **Improved Judgement** (43-44, 2 seconds off **Judgement**'s cooldown), **Vindication** (45, 50 and 54: your swings can lower the mob's attack power by 204), **Sacred Arbiter** (46: **Holy Strike** 20% harder, and it refreshes your judgement on the mob), **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** (47-49, 6% Holy and physical damage with a two-hander), **Vengeance** (51-53: 3% damage a stack for 30 seconds after a critical, up to five), **Deflection** (55) and **Champion of the Light** (56-58 and 60: spell damage equal to 12% of your Intellect a rank, and 0.2 seconds a rank off **Hammer of Wrath**'s cast). **Consecrated Ground** (59) is the last Holy point.
- With **Seal of Command** (41): **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: the seal's judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard. **Hammer of Wrath** below 20% health from 44. No **Consecration** against a single mob.
- Heal in the fight once you drop below 40% health and the mob has more than a fifth left: **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** (**Divine Shield** from 34) below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. Drink below 15% of your mana and eat if you are still below 80% of your health (the tuned rules of 2026-11-15); between fights heal back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**.

**Caveats**

- **Purifying Power** (22), **Infusion of Light** (28) and **Oath of Light** (29) fill Holy's rows on the way to **Holy Shock**, and **Consecrated Ground** (59) only works inside **Consecration**: the fight lab measures none of them against a single mob.
- The Holy list switches to **Seal of Command** as soon as you have it (41). In the lab's caster gear, staying on **Seal of Righteousness** instead is 6-7% slower per kill at 41 and about 1% slower at 45-50, but 1-2% faster at 55 and 3% faster at 60 (0.7 seconds a kill), because that seal's damage grows with spell power. In melee gear **Seal of Command** is faster at 41-55 and ties at 60 (both checked on 2026-11-16).
- Undead and Demons: use **Exorcism** on cooldown (**Holy Conduit** makes it 40% cheaper, **Purifying Power** its cooldown 17% shorter). Unmeasured: the fight lab has no Undead or Demon mobs.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all (**Holy Conduit** makes it 40% cheaper). The Holy list was not measured on several mobs with this build.
