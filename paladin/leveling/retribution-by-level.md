# Retribution Paladin — leveling, level by level

[Paladin](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Retribution](retribution.md) > Level by level

What a Retribution paladin presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. A paladin's fight is simple: a seal on yourself, your weapon's swings, and two strikes on 10-second cooldowns. Most new spells are safety tools, not rotation. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 12/9/30 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/paladin/60/050232-50301-0523330120130133-12)), the one on the [Retribution leveling page](retribution.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Seal of Righteousness** on yourself, then your weapon does the work. |
| 4-5 | **Judgement** unleashes your seal on the mob every 10 seconds. In Forever the seal stays up afterwards. |
| 6-7 | **Holy Strike**, an instant weapon strike every 10 seconds. With **Judgement** it is your whole rotation until 20. |
| 8-9 | **Hammer of Justice**, a stun: stop a caster, or buy time to heal. |
| 10-19 | Talent points start. **Lay on Hands** is a full heal for emergencies. Nothing new to press in a normal fight. |
| 20-43 | **Seal of Command** (talent) replaces Seal of Righteousness. Its **Judgement** hits a stunned mob twice as hard, so stun first. |
| 44-60 | **Hammer of Wrath**, a ranged finisher for mobs below 20% health. Optional: the tuned rotation leaves it out. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; the blessing, the aura, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme matches your mage: Q the strike you press most, E the control, R the payoff after it, F the escape, the thumb buttons the reactions. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the macros: the [paladin keybinds page](keybinds.md).

- The combo from 20 is E then R: **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement** on the stunned mob. From 49, **Repentance** on Shift+E sets up the same doubled hit on a humanoid.
- Two changes on bar 1 in the whole climb: the R macro's seal becomes **Seal of Command** at level 20, and **Divine Shield** takes F at 34.
- Levels 1-3 fight with R alone, for the seal. From 4, R is the seal and **Judgement** macro on the [keybinds page](keybinds.md): the first press on a new mob casts the seal, the next ones **Judgement**. Right-click the mob to start your swings. From 6, Q is the Holy Strike macro: it starts your swings too, so the middle mouse button stays free.
- The seal has no key of its own: the R macro casts it once per mob and again after three **Judgement** presses, about when its 30 seconds run out. From 20, press R once before you stun, because the first press on a mob is the seal.
- No key: **Blessing of Might**, **Devotion Aura**, food, drink, bandages, the mount and the Hearthstone. Put them on a side bar and click them between fights.
- The tuned rotation leaves **Hammer of Wrath** and **Consecration** out against single mobs. Their keys are for a mob that runs and for packs.

## Levels 1-3: Seal of Righteousness

**The trainer teaches:** Devotion Aura (1), Holy Light (1), **Seal of Righteousness** (1).

**Keys to set:** R = Seal of Righteousness; Mouse 5 = Holy Light; C = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** R Seal of Righteousness.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Righteousness** before the pull. It lasts 30 seconds: put it back when it runs out.
2. Walk in and swing.

- Keep **Devotion Aura** on. Heal with **Holy Light** between fights.
- Use a two-handed weapon from the first one you find. Every seal and strike hits harder with a slow, heavy weapon.

## Levels 4-5: Judgement

**The trainer teaches:** Blessing of Might (4), **Judgement** (4).

**Keys to set:** R = Judgement, macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** R Judgement.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
2. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.

- **Blessing of Might** (4) on yourself, always.

## Levels 6-7: Holy Strike

**The trainer teaches:** Divine Protection (6), **Holy Strike** (6), Seal of the Crusader (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Holy Light at 6.

**Keys to set:** Q = Holy Strike; F = Divine Protection.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
2. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
3. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below a third of your health: **Divine Protection**, then **Holy Light** inside it.

- **Seal of the Crusader** (6) is not part of the leveling rotation. Stay on **Seal of Righteousness**.

## Levels 8-9: Hammer of Justice

**The trainer teaches:** **Hammer of Justice** (8), Purify (8).

**Keys to set:** E = Hammer of Justice; 4 = Purify.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, E Hammer of Justice, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells. It stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
3. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
4. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below a third of your health, with the mob still above a quarter of its own: **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and **Holy Light** during the stun.

- **Purify** (8) removes a poison or a disease. It is not part of the fight.

## Levels 10-19: Talents begin, Lay on Hands

**The trainer teaches:** Blessing of Protection (10), Lay on Hands (10), Righteous Fury (10), Seal of Fury (10), Redemption (12), Blessing of Wisdom (14), Retribution Aura (16), Blessing of Freedom (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Divine Protection at 18; Holy Light at 14; Holy Strike at 12; Seal of Righteousness at 10, 18.

**Talents:** 10-14 Benediction, 15-17 Sanctified Judgement, 18-19 Conviction.

**Keys to set:** 1 = Blessing of Protection; Shift+Mouse 5 = Lay on Hands; Shift+F = Blessing of Freedom (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, E Hammer of Justice, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light, Shift+Mouse 5 Lay on Hands.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. **Seal of Righteousness** up before the pull: the first press of R.
3. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
4. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below a third of your health, with the mob still above a quarter of its own: **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and **Holy Light** during the stun. **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

- **Seal of Fury** (10) and **Righteous Fury** (10) are for tanking. Leave them alone while you level as Retribution.
- **Retribution Aura** (16) hurts whatever hits you. The fight lab assumes **Devotion Aura**; against single mobs either is fine.
- **Blessing of Protection** (10) and **Blessing of Freedom** (18) are tools for a bad pull, not rotation.
- Buy the next rank of the defensive spells on your bars too: **Divine Protection** at 18 (8 seconds instead of 6), **Blessing of Protection** at 24 and 38 (8, then 10 seconds), **Lay on Hands** at 30 and 50 and **Divine Shield** at 50.

## Levels 20-43: Seal of Command and the stun combo

**The trainer teaches:** Blessing of Kings (20), Consecration (20), Exorcism (20), Flash of Light (20), Voice of Truth (20), Concentration Aura (22), Seal of Justice (22), Turn Undead (24), Shadow Resistance Aura (28), Divine Intervention (30), Seal of Light (30), Frost Resistance Aura (32), Divine Shield (34), Fire Resistance Aura (36), Seal of Wisdom (38), Cleanse (42).

**New ranks to buy:** Blessing of Protection at 24, 38; Consecration at 30, 40; Exorcism at 28, 36; Flash of Light at 26, 34, 42; Hammer of Justice at 24, 40; Holy Light at 22, 30, 38; Holy Strike at 20, 28, 36; Lay on Hands at 30; Seal of Command at 30, 40; Turn Undead at 38.

**Talents:** 20 Seal of Command, 21-22 Pursuit of Justice, 23 Conviction, 24 Improved Judgement, 25 Sacred Arbiter, 26 Improved Judgement, 27-29 Two-Handed Weapon Specialization, 30-32 Vengeance, 33-37 Divine Strength, 38-40 Improved Seals, 41-43 Divine Intellect.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Flash of Light; 2 = Exorcism; Shift+Q = Consecration; Shift+Mouse 4 = Turn Undead (at 24); F = Divine Shield (at 34); 4 = Cleanse (at 42).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, E Hammer of Justice, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light, Mouse 4 Flash of Light, Shift+Mouse 5 Lay on Hands.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. **Seal of Command** up before the pull: the first press of R.
3. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% of its health left. The stun has a 1-minute cooldown, so this line applies to about one pull in three; on the others skip it.
4. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
5. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below a third of your health, with the mob still above a quarter of its own: **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and **Holy Light** during the stun. **Flash of Light** below a quarter. **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

- Change the seal in your R macro to **Seal of Command** when you take the talent at 20.
- **Consecration** (20): leave it off your bar against single mobs. The tuned rotation never casts it. It is for packs.
- **Exorcism** (20) only works on Undead and Demons. Use it on cooldown against those; the fight lab does not model it.
- **Blessing of Kings** (20) is an alternative to **Blessing of Might**; the fight lab plays **Blessing of Might**. From 34, **Divine Shield** replaces **Divine Protection** as your bubble: longer, and it takes the same key.
- If you spent **Hammer of Justice** on the combo, a mob that casts spells in the next fight runs free until the stun is back: when you see a caster, keep the stun for it.

**New ranks to buy:** Consecration at 50, 60; Divine Shield at 50; Exorcism at 44, 52, 60; Flash of Light at 50, 58; Hammer of Justice at 54; Hammer of Wrath at 52, 60; Holy Light at 46, 54, 60; Holy Strike at 44, 52, 60; Holy Wrath at 60; Lay on Hands at 50; Seal of Command at 50, 60; Turn Undead at 52.

**Talents:** 44-45 Divine Intellect, 46-48 Precision, 49 Repentance, 50 Vindication, 51-53 Champion of the Light, 54-55 Vindication, 56 Sacred Duty, 57-58 Reverence, 59-60 Purifying Power.

**Keys to set:** Shift+R = Hammer of Wrath; Shift+E = Repentance (at 49), talent; Shift+2 = Holy Wrath (at 50).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, E Hammer of Justice, R Judgement, F Divine Shield, Mouse 5 Holy Light, Mouse 4 Flash of Light, Shift+Mouse 5 Lay on Hands.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. **Seal of Command** up before the pull: the first press of R.
3. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% of its health left. The stun has a 1-minute cooldown, so this line applies to about one pull in three; on the others skip it.
4. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
5. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below a third of your health, with the mob still above a quarter of its own: **Divine Shield** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and **Holy Light** during the stun. **Flash of Light** below a quarter. **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

- **Hammer of Wrath** (44) hits a mob below 20% health from range. The tuned rules of 2026-11-02 switch it off against single mobs: kills measured no faster with it. Use it on a mob that runs.
- **Holy Wrath** (50) only works on Undead and Demons, like **Exorcism**.

## Between fights

- Heal yourself back to about 80% with **Holy Light**, or with **Flash of Light** from level 20.
- Drink when you are below about a third of your mana: the fight lab's default, which the tuned rules of 2026-11-07 keep (the 2026-11-02 tune drank below 15%, about 0.4% faster per kill; the re-tune did not keep it). Eat only if you are still below 65% of your health after the heals.
- Re-cast **Blessing of Might** when it runs out, and your seal just before each pull.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of your seal and **Holy Strike**.

## Other ways to level this class

- A paladin who wants to arrive at 60 as a healer or a tank can level into that build instead. The Holy and Protection leveling pages in this folder show how, and what it costs in speed.
- Packs of mobs: **Consecration** and the Protection tree's tools. Single pulls are faster for Retribution.
