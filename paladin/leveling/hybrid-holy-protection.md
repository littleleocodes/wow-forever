## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Wisdom** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. **Seal of Righteousness** before each pull; **Seal of Command** instead once your build has it.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Once you have **Holy Shock**: cast it at the mob whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 10% of its health left, from range as it walks in and then in melee, after **Divine Favor** for a sure critical hit while you are healthy.
3. Keep your seal up.
4. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
5. With **Seal of Command**: **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left (the judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard).
6. With **Swift Judgement**: whenever it is ready, with your seal up and the mob above a quarter of its health (free and off the global cooldown).
7. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration: the Holy list never casts it.
8. Low on health (below 40%, with the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark** first if your build has it and Forbearance is not on you, then **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink only below 15% of your mana, and eat if you are still below 80% of your health.

## Why this rotation

The Holy-Prot leveling page is the open search's winner for the Holy rotation (2026-11-15): 21/16/14, Holy's talents first (10-30), then Retribution's (31-44), then Protection's (45-60). It plays the Holy list with a two-hander: **Seal of Righteousness** (**Seal of Command** from 41), **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Hammer of Wrath** to finish from 44, and from level 30 **Holy Shock** at the mob whenever it is ready, about twice a kill.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-30 are Holy: **Divine Strength** (10-14, 2% Strength a rank), **Improved Seals** (15-17 and 27-28, 3% seal and judgement damage a rank), **Reverence** (18-19, 30% of mana regeneration while casting), **Holy Conduit** (20-21: **Exorcism**, **Consecration**, **Holy Wrath** and **Hammer of Wrath** 40% cheaper), **Illumination** (22-24 and 26: a critical heal refunds mana), **Divine Precision** (25, 4% hit for Holy spells), **Divine Favor** (29) and **Holy Shock** (30).
- **Holy Shock** (level 30, 10-second cooldown): at the mob whenever it is ready while it has more than 10% health left, from range as it walks in and then in melee, about twice a kill; **Divine Favor** first while your health is above 70%, for a sure critical. From 30 the fight lab dresses the build in caster gear (spell power and Intellect), as it does every Holy build that casts **Holy Shock**.
- Then Retribution: **Benediction** (31-35, instants 10% cheaper), **Sanctified Judgement** (36-38: **Judgement** returns 60% of the judged seal's mana cost), **Conviction** (39-40 and 42, 6% critical strike chance), **Seal of Command** (41) and **Improved Judgement** (43-44, 2 seconds off **Judgement**'s cooldown). Then Protection: **Divine Intellect** (45-49, 10% Intellect), **Precision** (50-52, 3% hit), **Sacred Duty** (53, 30 seconds off your bubbles and **Templar's Bulwark**) and **Guardian's Favor** (54) to open the next row, **Templar's Bulwark** (55), **Toughness** (56-59) and **Swift Judgement** (60).
- With **Seal of Command** (41): **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: the seal's judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard. **Hammer of Wrath** below 20% health from 44. **Swift Judgement** (60) whenever it is ready, with the seal up and the mob above a quarter of its health. No **Consecration** against a single mob.
- Heal in the fight once you drop below 40% health and the mob has more than a fifth left: **Templar's Bulwark** (55) unless Forbearance is on you, **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** (**Divine Shield** from 34) below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. Drink below 15% of your mana and eat if you are still below 80% of your health (the tuned rules of 2026-11-15); between fights heal back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**.

**Caveats**

- The row before **Templar's Bulwark** takes **Sacred Duty** and **Guardian's Favor**, whose point only opens Protection's third row for a solo leveler. The build takes no **Redoubt**, the row's other choice: it adds block, which needs a shield (the search had taken it; it was swapped for **Sacred Duty** by hand, which the fight lab measures alike).
- **Templar's Bulwark** applies Forbearance for a minute, so **Divine Shield** is not available right after it.
- Undead and Demons: use **Exorcism** on cooldown (**Holy Conduit** makes it 40% cheaper). Unmeasured: the fight lab has no Undead or Demon mobs.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all (**Holy Conduit** makes it 40% cheaper). The Holy list was not measured on several mobs with this build.
