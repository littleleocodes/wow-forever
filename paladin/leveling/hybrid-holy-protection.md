## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Iron Creed** is a talent this build does not take: where a line below names it, that part does not apply. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a one-handed weapon and a shield; **Righteous Fury** too: **Iron Creed** and **Improved Righteous Fury** cut your damage taken only while it is on (the fight lab keeps it up). **Seal of Fury** before each pull: with a shield it also gives you an absorb.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Keep **Seal of Fury** up.
2. **Holy Shield** whenever it is down.
3. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready; **Swift Judgement** (its own 1-minute cooldown) whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left: a second, free **Judgement**.
4. Low on health (below about a third, with the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark**, then **Holy Light** behind its absorb; otherwise **Divine Protection** (below 30%) and **Holy Light** while the mob cannot hit you; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%. The tuned rules cast no Hammer of Justice, Hammer of Wrath or Consecration against a single mob.

**Between fights:** Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.

## Why this rotation

The Holy/Protection hybrid plays the Protection list: a one-hander and a shield, **Seal of Fury** before each pull, **Holy Shield** from level 30, **Judgement**, **Holy Strike** and **Swift Judgement** (25) for damage. Its talents are Protection's first (10-30), then Retribution's mana and crit talents (31-42) and Holy's Strength and seal damage. Against a single mob the tuned rules cast no **Consecration**, **Hammer of Wrath** or **Hammer of Justice**.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-30 are Protection: **Divine Intellect** (10-12), **Redoubt** (13-14, 18-19 and 21), **Precision** (15-17), **Templar's Bulwark** (20), **Shield Specialization** (22-23), **Toughness** (24), **Swift Judgement** (25), **Anticipation** (26-28), **Improved Righteous Fury** (29) and **Holy Shield** (30). The Protection page's reasons for them apply: block, the mana a block returns, and a free second **Judgement** every minute.
- Then **Benediction** (31-35), **Sanctified Judgement** (36-38), **Conviction** (39-41), **Improved Judgement** (42 and 45), **Divine Strength** (43-44 and 46-48), **Reverence** (49-50), **Improved Seals** (51-53 and 56), **Vindication** (54) and **Reckoning** (55 and 57-60). Keep **Righteous Fury** up even alone: **Improved Righteous Fury** only works while it is on.
- **Holy Shield** whenever it is down and the mob has more than a fifth of its health; **Swift Judgement** whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left. Below about a third of your health: **Templar's Bulwark**, then **Holy Light** behind it; **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. Drink below 15% of your mana.

**Caveats**

- This build takes no **Iron Creed**, so skip the lines that use it. Before level 30 skip the **Holy Shield** line, before 20 the **Templar's Bulwark** one and before 25 **Swift Judgement**.
- **Templar's Bulwark** applies Forbearance for a minute, so **Divine Protection** is not available right after it.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** hits them hard; the fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured. Since 2026-11-09 the build takes no **Holy Conduit**: it only makes **Exorcism**, **Consecration**, **Holy Wrath** and **Hammer of Wrath** cheaper, none of which the list casts against a single mob, and its two points went to **Reckoning** (measured faster) and **Vindication**.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast, although the tuned rules never cast it against a single mob.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the tuned rules were set on single mobs.
