**Signature spells:** 100% of the damage at level 60 in the fight lab comes from melee, Seal of Command, Seal of Righteousness, Judgement, Holy Strike, Vengeance (the spec's rule: at least 60%; **Judgement** cast in every fight).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. Before each pull: **Seal of Command** (**Seal of Righteousness** before you have it).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells: it stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. Keep your seal up: put it back if it runs out.
3. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: **Seal of Command**'s judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration: the tuned rotation never casts it.
6. Low on health (below a third, with the mob above a quarter): **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and heal during the stun; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink only below 15% of your mana.

## Why this rotation

Retribution levels with one seal: **Seal of Command** before each pull, **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Hammer of Wrath** to finish, and your mana kept for judgements, the hammer and heals instead of **Consecration**.

**Why it works**

- **Seal of Command** goes up before each pull (from level 20; **Seal of Righteousness** before that) and lasts 30 seconds, longer than most fights. It adds a Holy hit of 70% of your weapon damage about 7 times a minute, so a slow two-hander suits it; this build has no **Unsealed Wrath**, so there is no seal swapping while leveling.
- **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready: **Judgement** is off the global cooldown and comes back every 8 seconds with both points of **Improved Judgement** (levels 24 and 26), **Sanctified Judgement** (15-17) returns 60% of your seal's mana cost each time, and **Sacred Arbiter** (25) adds 20% to **Holy Strike**. At 60 the fight lab finds **Sanctified Judgement** the build's most valuable talent (about 2.7 seconds per kill without it), for the drinking it saves; then **Conviction** (18-19 and 23, about 2.4 s), **Vengeance** (30-32, about 1.5 s), **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** (27-29, about 1.1 s) and **Improved Seals** (38-40, about 0.9 s).
- **Consecration** is for two or more mobs. Against a single mob the fight lab's tuned rules (re-tuned from scratch on 2026-10-23) never cast it and kill about 5% faster that way: its mana costs more drinking than its damage saves.
- **Hammer of Justice** doubles **Seal of Command**'s judgement: against a stunned mob it hits for 339 to 373 instead of 169 to 187 at rank 5. When both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left, stun first, then judge; otherwise keep the stun for a caster or for when you are hurt.
- **Hammer of Wrath** comes at 44, with a 1-second cast. The tuned rules leave it out, because never casting it measured faster: keep it for a mob that runs.
- The points from level 41 on are small: **Divine Intellect** (41-45), **Precision** (46-48, about 0.7 seconds per kill at 60), **Repentance** (49), **Vindication** (50 and 54-55), **Champion of the Light** (51-53, about 0.3 s), **Sacred Duty** (56), **Reverence** (57-58, about 0.7 s) and **Purifying Power** (59-60). **Repentance**, **Vindication**, **Sacred Duty** and **Purifying Power** measure nothing against single mobs of your level.
- Below about a third of your health, while the mob still has a quarter of its own: **Divine Protection** (8 seconds immune, but you cannot attack) with **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and heal during the stun; **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. Against same-level mobs the fight lab almost never needed these. Between fights heal back to 80%; the tuned rules drink only below 15% of your mana.

**Caveats**

- Several mobs: **Consecration** hits all of them and its bonus damage goes to the first four in it, so it earns its mana there. The fight lab's three-mob runs still played without it, so the gain is unmeasured.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** (instant, 30 yards, 15-second cooldown, a third shorter with **Purifying Power** from 59-60) hits them hard, and **Holy Wrath** handles packs. The fight lab has no Undead or Demon mobs, so neither was measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that stands back and casts: **Hammer of Justice** (10 yards) stops the cast; the fight lab uses it that way.
- A mob that runs at low health: **Hammer of Wrath** (30 yards) finishes it, or **Hammer of Justice** holds it.

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-25; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
