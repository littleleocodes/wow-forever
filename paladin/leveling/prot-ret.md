## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. Before each pull: **Seal of Command** (**Seal of Righteousness** before you have it).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells: it stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. Keep your seal up: put it back if it runs out.
3. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: **Seal of Command**'s judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard. The stun has a 1-minute cooldown, so most pulls skip this line: it is ready for about one pull in three.
4. If your build has **Swift Judgement**: press it whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left. It is a second, free **Judgement** on its own 1-minute cooldown.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration and no Hammer of Wrath: the tuned rotation never casts either.
6. Low on health (below a third, with the mob above a quarter): **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and heal during the stun; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink below about a third of your mana, and eat only if you are still below 65% of your health.

## Why this rotation

The Prot-Ret leveling page is the open search's winner for the Retribution rotation: a two-handed Retribution paladin (**Seal of Command**, **Judgement**, **Holy Strike**) with 25 points in Retribution, 16 in Protection and 10 in Holy. It plays the Retribution list plus **Swift Judgement** from level 57. For its speed against the other paladin pages see the leveling index.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-49 are Retribution's mana and damage talents plus Holy's Strength and seal damage: **Benediction** (10), **Deflection** (11-14 and 33, parry), **Sanctified Judgement** (15-17), **Conviction** (18-19 and 21), **Seal of Command** (20), **Improved Judgement** (22 and 60), **Vindication** (23-24 and 29), **Sacred Arbiter** (25), **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** (26-28), **Vengeance** (30-32), **Divine Strength** (34-38), **Improved Seals** (39-41), **Divine Intellect** (42-46) and **Precision** (47-49). Play it like the Retribution page: **Seal of Command** before each pull, **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Hammer of Justice** before a **Judgement** on a mob above 30% health when the stun is ready.
- No **Consecration** and no **Hammer of Wrath** against a single mob: the Retribution list's tuned rules never cast either. Drink below about a third of your mana.
- **Swift Judgement** (57) is a second, free **Judgement** on its own 1-minute cooldown, pressed whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left. It needs **Templar's Bulwark** (52), which the Retribution list never casts, but the 8-second absorb is there as an emergency button (it applies Forbearance, like **Divine Protection**).
- In the search's value table for its winner (seconds per kill lost without the talent, level 60) **Sanctified Judgement** leads at about 3.2, then **Conviction** (2.7), **Swift Judgement** and **Vengeance** (1.6 each), **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** (1.5), **Improved Seals** (1.2), **Precision** (1.0), **Seal of Command** (0.9) and **Vindication** (0.9).
- **Improved Hammer of Justice** (53 and 55-56) takes 15 seconds off the stun's 1-minute cooldown, so the stun-then-judge combo comes up more often. **Vindication** lowers the attack power of the mob you hit, so it hits you softer; the fight lab models it since 2026-11-09.

**Caveats**

- **Sacred Duty** (50-51, a minute off **Divine Shield**, **Divine Protection** and **Templar's Bulwark**) and **Toughness** (54, armor) measure about nothing against single mobs of your level. They are there because **Swift Judgement** needs 15 Protection points above it. The build takes no **Redoubt**, the other choice in those rows: it adds block, which needs a shield, and with a two-hander it does nothing. Since 2026-11-09 those points are **Sacred Duty**, and **Improved Hammer of Justice** took two of the three **Toughness** points, measured faster.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** hits them hard; the fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the single-target rule does not apply there.
- If you carry a shield and use **Seal of Fury** instead, play the Protection page's list; that is not what this build is made for and was not measured.
