## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. Before each pull: **Seal of Command** (**Seal of Righteousness** before you have it).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells: it stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. Keep your seal up: put it back if it runs out.
3. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: **Seal of Command**'s judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration: the tuned rotation never casts it.
6. Low on health (below a third, with the mob above a quarter): **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and heal during the stun; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink only below 15% of your mana.

## Why this rotation

The Prot-Ret leveling build is the stats model's pick for solo play at 60: a two-handed Retribution paladin (**Seal of Command**, **Judgement**, **Holy Strike**) whose last 16 points (levels 45-60) go into Protection. It plays the Retribution list; the fight lab has not raced it.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-44 are a Retribution leveling build: **Benediction** (10-14), **Conviction** (15-19), **Seal of Command** (20), **Sanctified Judgement** (21-22 and 26), **Improved Judgement** (27-28), **Vengeance** (30-32), **Instrument of Law** (35-36), then **Divine Strength** and **Improved Seals** from Holy. Play it like the Retribution page: **Seal of Command** before each pull, **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Hammer of Justice** before a **Judgement** on a mob above 30% health.
- No **Consecration** against a single mob: the Retribution list's tuned rules never cast it (about 5% faster kills).
- **Hammer of Wrath** below 20% health from level 44, instant with **Instrument of Law**. Drink below 15% of your mana.

**Caveats**

- The Protection points (**Redoubt**, **Shield Specialization**, **Improved Seal of Fury**, **Swift Judgement**) need a shield, **Seal of Fury** or the Protection list: with a two-hander in the Retribution list they do nothing, and the fight lab plays them that way. The Retribution page's build is the measured one.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** hits them hard; the fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the single-target rule does not apply there.
- If you carry a shield and **Seal of Fury** instead, play the Protection page's list: that is what these points are for.
