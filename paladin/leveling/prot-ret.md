## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. Before each pull: **Seal of Command** (**Seal of Righteousness** before you have it).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells: it stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. Keep your seal up: put it back if it runs out.
3. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: **Seal of Command**'s judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration: the tuned rotation never casts it.
6. Low on health (below a third, with the mob above a quarter): **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and heal during the stun; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink only below 15% of your mana.

## Why this rotation

The Prot-Ret leveling page is the stats model's pick for solo play at 60: a two-handed Retribution paladin (**Seal of Command**, **Judgement**, **Holy Strike**) whose last 16 points (levels 45-60) go into Protection. It plays the Retribution list; the fight lab's signature check times it at about 29.6 seconds per kill over levels 20-60, about 3% slower than the Retribution page in the same check (28.6).

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-44 are a Retribution leveling build: **Benediction** (10-14), **Conviction** (15-19), **Seal of Command** (20), **Sanctified Judgement** (21-22 and 26), **Sacred Arbiter** (25), **Improved Judgement** (27-28), **Vengeance** (30-32), **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** (33-34), **Instrument of Law** (35-36), then **Divine Strength** and **Improved Seals** from Holy (37-44). Play it like the Retribution page: **Seal of Command** before each pull, **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Hammer of Justice** before a **Judgement** on a mob above 30% health.
- No **Consecration** against a single mob: the Retribution list's tuned rules never cast it (about 5% faster kills). **Hammer of Wrath** below 20% health from level 44, instant with **Instrument of Law**. Drink below 15% of your mana.
- Of the Protection points, **Precision** (50-52, 3% hit), **Anticipation** (53-54, Defense) and **Sacred Duty** (55-56, more Stamina and a shorter **Divine Protection** cooldown) work with a two-hander in the fight lab. **Pursuit of Justice** (23-24) is travel speed, which the fight lab does not model.

**Caveats**

- **Redoubt**, **Shield Specialization** and **Improved Seal of Fury** need a shield and **Seal of Fury**, and the Retribution list never presses **Swift Judgement**: with a two-hander in the Retribution list these 9 points do nothing in the fight lab. The Retribution page's build is the faster measured one.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** hits them hard; the fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the single-target rule does not apply there.
- **Swift Judgement** (60) right after a **Judgement** gives you a free second one; the Retribution list does not press it, so its worth for this build is unmeasured.
- If you carry a shield and **Seal of Fury** instead, play the Protection page's list: that is what these points are for (not measured for this build).

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-25; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
