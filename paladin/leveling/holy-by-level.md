# Holy Paladin — leveling, level by level

[Paladin](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Holy](holy.md) > Level by level

What a Holy paladin presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, while leveling into the Holy raid build (32/5/14). It fights like a Retribution paladin with a two-handed weapon: **Seal of Righteousness**, **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Blessing of Wisdom** for mana. **Holy Shock** (30), the spec's signature spell, opens the fight from range while your mana is high, and **Hammer of Wrath** (44) finishes it. Below 40% health you heal yourself in the fight. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 32/5/14 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/paladin/60/54320215001101151-5-250300202-12)), the one on the [Holy leveling page](holy.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Seal of Righteousness** on yourself, then your weapon does the work. |
| 4-5 | **Judgement** unleashes your seal on the mob every 10 seconds. In Forever the seal stays up afterwards. |
| 6-7 | **Holy Strike**, an instant weapon strike every 10 seconds. With **Judgement** it is your whole rotation. |
| 8-9 | **Hammer of Justice**, a stun: stop a caster, or buy time to heal. |
| 10-19 | Talent points start. **Lay on Hands** is a full heal for emergencies. Nothing new to press in a normal fight. |
| 20-29 | Nothing new in the rotation: this build takes no **Seal of Command**, so **Seal of Righteousness** stays your seal. **Flash of Light** joins the heals. |
| 30-43 | **Holy Shock** (talent): an instant Holy blast from 40 yards as the mob walks in, or an instant heal on yourself. |
| 44-50 | **Hammer of Wrath** finishes a mob below 20% health from range. The Holy list presses it. |
| 51-60 | **Divine Favor** (talent): a sure critical for your next **Holy Shock** or heal. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; the blessing, the aura, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. Holy keeps the Retribution layout, so muscle memory carries over: Q the strike you press most, E the stun, R the seal and **Judgement**, F the bubble, the thumb buttons the heals. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the macros: the [paladin keybinds page](keybinds.md).

## Levels 1-3: Seal of Righteousness

**The trainer teaches:** Devotion Aura (1), Holy Light (1), **Seal of Righteousness** (1).

**Keys to set:** R = Seal of Righteousness; Mouse 5 = Holy Light; C = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** R Seal of Righteousness.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Righteousness** before the pull. It lasts 30 seconds: put it back when it runs out.
2. Walk in and swing.

- Keep **Devotion Aura** on. Heal with **Holy Light** between fights.
- Use a two-handed weapon from the first one you find: the Holy list fights with one, and every seal and strike hits harder with a slow, heavy weapon.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 12 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Melee 1.7, Seal of Righteousness 1.0.*

## Between fights

- Heal yourself back to about 80% with **Holy Light**, or with **Flash of Light** from level 20.
- Drink below 15% of your mana, and eat only if you are still below 40% of your health after the heals: the tuned rules of 2026-11-06 (the Holy list's own defaults are a quarter of your mana and about two thirds of your health).
- Re-cast **Blessing of Wisdom** when it runs out, and your seal just before each pull.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of your seal, **Holy Strike** and **Holy Light**.

## Other ways to level this class

- The [Holy leveling page](holy.md) compares this build's time per kill with Retribution's.
- Packs of mobs: **Consecration** on Shift+Q hits them all. The single-target list never casts it.
- Dungeons on the way: you can heal them with **Holy Light**, **Flash of Light** and **Holy Shock**. The raid build's healing talents come early: **Illumination** at 20-24, **Healing Light** at 28-29 and 50, **Holy Power** at 54-58.
