## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Holy Shock**, **Seal of Command** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Wisdom** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. **Seal of Righteousness** before each pull; **Seal of Command** instead once your build has it.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Once you have **Holy Shock**: cast it at the mob as it walks in while your mana is above 70%, after **Divine Favor** for a sure critical hit while you are healthy.
3. Keep your seal up.
4. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
5. With **Seal of Command**: **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left (the judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard).
6. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration: the Holy list never casts it.
7. Low on health (below 40%, with the mob above a fifth): **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink only below 15% of your mana.

## Why this rotation

Holy levels as a Retribution paladin first: the first 21 points (levels 10-30) go into Retribution (**Seal of Command**, **Sanctified Judgement**, **Improved Judgement**), Holy starts at 31 and **Holy Shock** comes at 51. You play the Holy list with a two-hander: **Seal of Command**, **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Hammer of Wrath** to finish, **Holy Shock** from 51. Over levels 20-60 it is about 7% slower per kill than the Retribution page in the fight lab (30.2 against 28.2 seconds).

**Why it works**

- Up to level 50 it plays like Retribution with **Blessing of Wisdom** instead of **Blessing of Might**: **Seal of Command** from level 20 (**Seal of Righteousness** before), **Hammer of Justice** before a **Judgement** on a mob above 30% health (the judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard), then **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. The fight lab times it within about 2% of the Retribution page there (19.8, 23.6, 28.0 and 30.1 seconds per kill at 20, 30, 40 and 50, against 19.7, 23.3, 28.6 and 29.5).
- **Sanctified Judgement** (levels 21-23) returns 60% of your seal's mana cost each time you judge: the open search found it the build's most valuable talent at 60 (about 2.6 seconds per kill without it), ahead of **Improved Seals** (36-38, about 1.7 s), **Holy Power** (56-60, about 1.6 s), **Conviction** (15-19) and **Improved Judgement** (24 and 26).
- **Holy Shock** (level 51) at the mob as it walks in, only while your mana is above 70% and the mob has more than 10% health left (the list's hand-set rule); at rank 4 (level 56) it hits for 334 to 362 on a 10-second cooldown. With **Divine Favor** (54) ready and your health above 70%, press it first for a sure critical hit. The mana rule keeps it to about 0.6 casts per kill at 60; **Holy Power** adds 15% crit to it and to **Holy Strike**, and **Divine Precision** (52-53) 12% hit to both.
- **Hammer of Wrath** below 20% health from level 44 (the tuned rules no longer skip it): it has a 1-second cast, and **Holy Conduit** (28-29) takes 40% off its mana (about 1.2 seconds per kill at 60). No **Consecration** against a single mob: the Holy list never casts it (its mana buys more as **Holy Shock** and less drinking), so **Consecrated Ground** (55) does nothing there.
- Heal in the fight once you drop below 40% health and the mob has more than a fifth left: **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. **Illumination** (46-50) returns half the base cost of a critical heal, **Spiritual Focus** (40 and 45) keeps 70% of your heals from being pushed back, and **Reverence** (41-43) keeps 30% of your mana regeneration going while you cast. Against same-level mobs the fight lab rarely needed these heals.
- Drink below 15% of your mana (the tuned rule of 2026-10-23; the list's own default was a quarter); between fights heal back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**.

**Caveats**

- From level 51, once the build has **Holy Shock**, the fight lab dresses it in caster gear (spell power and Intellect) instead of melee gear, and its time per kill jumps from 30.1 seconds at 50 to 38.5 at 60 (Retribution: 32.1). Whether melee gear would be faster at 60 for this build was not measured.
- **Purifying Power** (44) does nothing in the fight lab: **Exorcism** and **Holy Wrath** are for Undead and Demons, which the lab has none of. Against them use **Exorcism** on cooldown (instant, 40% cheaper with **Holy Conduit**, a 17% shorter cooldown with **Purifying Power**); unmeasured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all and **Consecrated Ground** then adds 5% to your Holy damage on them; the Holy list was not measured on several mobs with this build.
- If you only want to reach 60, level as Retribution and respec at 60: its page is about 7% faster per kill over 20-60.

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-25; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
