> 🔬 These are your **raid talents** ([Holy — Raid healing](../raid/holy.md)), taken in the order that levels fastest: the fight lab raced three orders to them (own tree first: 38 s per kill; stats model's order: 38 s per kill; own tree last: 39 s per kill). A model of the beta, to check at launch.

**Leveling as Holy or as Retribution?** This build takes 38 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Retribution](../leveling/retribution.md) 28 s: Holy is 38% slower. Your first Holy point comes at level 10. Level this way if you want to heal dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Retribution and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Holy Shock**, **Seal of Command** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Wisdom** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. **Seal of Righteousness** before each pull (**Seal of Command** instead if your build takes it).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Once you have **Holy Shock**: cast it at the mob as it walks in while your mana is above 70%, after **Divine Favor** for a sure critical hit while you are healthy.
3. Keep your seal up.
4. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready.
5. Low on health (below 40%, with the mob above a fifth): **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**, drink below a quarter of your mana.

## Why this rotation

Holy levels with the raid healer build as a two-handed **Seal of Righteousness** paladin who heals itself: **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** for damage, heals in the fight when you are low. It is the slowest paladin leveling page; Retribution and Shockadin kill about a quarter faster.

**Why it works**

- **Blessing of Wisdom** instead of **Blessing of Might**: the fight lab found the blessings within about 2% of each other for Holy, and Wisdom's mana keeps coming while you fight and cast. **Seal of Righteousness** is your only damage seal: this build has no **Seal of Command**.
- **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready; **Holy Power** (levels 35-39) adds 15% crit to **Holy Strike**. This build takes neither **Improved Judgement** nor **Sanctified Judgement**, so each judgement costs its full price.
- Heal in the fight once you drop below 40% health and the mob has more than a fifth left: **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Divine Favor** (level 42) makes that heal a sure crit. **Illumination** (25-29) returns half the base cost of each critical heal, and **Spiritual Focus** (40-41) keeps 70% of your heals from being pushed back.
- Not on the list: **Consecration** and **Hammer of Wrath**. The Holy rotation never casts **Consecration** (its mana buys more as heals and less drinking), and its tuned rules (2026-10-15) skip **Hammer of Wrath** for about 3% faster kills.
- Drink below a quarter of your mana; between fights heal back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**.

**Caveats**

- This build takes no **Holy Shock**: skip the list's **Holy Shock** lines.
- **Consecrated Ground** (level 43) only helps Holy spells against mobs in your **Consecration**, which this rotation never casts: it is there for the raid build.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** and **Holy Wrath** come back a third faster with **Purifying Power** (levels 23-24), so use **Exorcism** on cooldown against them. The fight lab has no such mobs, so this is unmeasured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- If you only want to reach 60, level as Retribution or Shockadin and respec: their pages kill about a quarter faster per mob.
