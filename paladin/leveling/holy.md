## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Seal of Command** is a talent this build does not take: where a line below names it, that part does not apply. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Wisdom** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. **Seal of Righteousness** before each pull; **Seal of Command** instead once your build has it.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Once you have **Holy Shock**: cast it at the mob as it walks in while your mana is above 70% and the mob has more than 10% of its health left, after **Divine Favor** for a sure critical hit while you are healthy.
3. Keep your seal up.
4. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration: the Holy list never casts it.
6. Low on health (below 40%, with the mob above a fifth): **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink only below 15% of your mana, and eat only if you are still below 40% of your health.

## Why this rotation

Holy levels with its raid healing talents in the order that levels fastest, so Retribution's damage talents come late (from 31). You play the Holy list with a two-hander and **Seal of Righteousness**, because this build has no **Seal of Command**: **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Hammer of Wrath** to finish, and from level 30 **Holy Shock** at the mob as it walks in. The page's signature check fails (**Holy Shock** is cast too rarely at levels 30-50), so treat this build as provisional.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-29 are the raid build's Holy talents: **Divine Strength** (10-13, 2% Strength a rank), **Spiritual Focus** (14, 19 and 25-27, 14% a rank), **Reverence** (15-16, 30% of mana regeneration while casting), **Purifying Power** (17-18: **Cleanse** and **Purify** 20% cheaper, **Exorcism** and **Holy Wrath** a third quicker to come back), **Illumination** (20-24) and **Healing Light** (28-29). The list is plain until 30: seal, **Judgement**, **Holy Strike**, **Hammer of Justice** on a caster.
- **Holy Shock** (level 30; ranks at 40, 48 and 56, 129 to 139 damage at rank 1, 334 to 362 at rank 4, 10-second cooldown): cast at the mob as it walks in, only while your mana is above 70% and the mob has more than 10% health left. With **Divine Favor** (51) ready and your health above 70%, press it first for a sure critical hit. The mana rule means it is cast in only some fights (the page's signature banner has the counts).
- From level 30 the fight lab dresses the build in caster gear (spell power and Intellect) instead of melee gear, because it casts **Holy Shock**; the melee row would be 4-5% faster at 60 for a list that never casts it, so the gear is a cost of this build's talents.
- **Benediction** (31-35) makes instants 10% cheaper, **Conviction** (36-38) adds 6% critical strike chance, **Deflection** (39-40) 2% parry, **Pursuit of Justice** (41-42) 15% movement speed, **Divine Intellect** (43-47) 10% Intellect, **Unyielding Faith** (48-49) 30% shorter fears and disorients, **Holy Power** (54-58) 10% a rank to the critical healing bonus of your Holy spells. The lab applies **Holy Power** to heals only: it does nothing for **Holy Shock** or **Holy Strike** damage.
- **Hammer of Wrath** below 20% health from level 44 (the tuned rules do not skip it for this list). No **Consecration** against a single mob: the Holy list never casts it.
- Heal in the fight once you drop below 40% health and the mob has more than a fifth left: **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. **Illumination** (five ranks: every critical heal refunds half its base cost), **Spiritual Focus** (70% of heals not pushed back), **Healing Light** (28-29 and 50, 12%) and **Infusion of Light** (52: a critical **Flash of Light** or **Holy Shock** makes your next **Holy Light** half a second faster) all feed these heals. Against same-level mobs the fight lab rarely needed them.
- Drink below 15% of your mana and eat only if you are still below 40% of your health (the tuned rules of 2026-11-06; the list's own defaults are a quarter and 65%); between fights heal back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**.

**Caveats**

- **Oath of Light** (53), **Boundless Light** (59) and **Light's Vigil** (60) are raid healing talents the lab does not use for the Holy leveling list (**Light's Vigil** costs more mana than it returns for a solo leveler).
- Undead and Demons: use **Exorcism** on cooldown (instant; **Purifying Power** at 17-18 takes a third off its 15-second cooldown). Unmeasured: the fight lab has no Undead or Demon mobs.
- The build has no **Seal of Command**, so the stun combo (**Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**) gains nothing here.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all. The Holy list was not measured on several mobs with this build.
- If you only want to reach 60, level as Retribution and respec at 60: the leveling index shows how much faster its page is.
