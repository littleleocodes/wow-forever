**Leveling as Protection or as Retribution?** This build takes 32 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Retribution](../leveling/retribution.md) 28 s: Protection is 13% slower. Your first Protection point comes at level 10. Level this way if you want to tank dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Retribution and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a one-handed weapon and a shield; **Righteous Fury** too if you have **Improved Righteous Fury** (less damage taken while it is on). **Seal of Fury** before each pull: with a shield it also gives you an absorb.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Keep **Seal of Fury** up.
3. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Holy Shield** whenever it is down.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready; **Swift Judgement** right after a **Judgement** for a free second one.
6. **Consecration** while your mana is above 60% and the mob has more than 30% health left.
7. Low on health (below about a third, with the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark**, then **Holy Light** behind its absorb; otherwise **Divine Protection** (below 30%) or **Hammer of Justice**, and **Holy Light** while the mob cannot hit you; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.

## Why this rotation

Protection levels with the raid tank build behind a shield: **Seal of Fury** turns half of its Holy damage into an absorb, blocks and judgements pay your mana back, and **Judgement**, **Holy Strike** and **Consecration** do the damage, so you rarely stop to eat. The order takes Protection first (levels 10-41) and the Retribution points last (42-60).

**Why it works**

- **Seal of Fury** before each pull: at rank 7 it adds 35 Holy damage to every swing, and with a shield each swing also gives you an absorb of half that damage; **Improved Seal of Fury** (level 24) returns 60 mana each time such a shield is used up, and **Shield Specialization** (20-22) 6% of your mana on a block, once every 3 seconds.
- Keep **Righteous Fury** up even alone: its threat does nothing solo, but **Improved Righteous Fury** (6% less damage taken at 3 points, levels 28-31) and **Iron Creed** (36-40: **Holy Strike** cuts your damage taken by 10% for 6 seconds) only work while it is on; the fight lab assumes it always up.
- **Holy Shield** (level 41) whenever it is down: 30% more block for 10 seconds or 4 blocks, and each block deals Holy damage. In the open leveling search it was the most valuable talent of a Protection leveling build at 60.
- **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready, with **Swift Judgement** (level 25) right after a **Judgement**: it finishes the cooldown and makes the next one free. **Sanctified Judgement** comes late in this order (52-54) and then returns 60% of your seal's mana cost each time you judge.
- **Consecration** only while your mana is above 60% and the mob has more than 30% health, a hand-set rule the fight lab plays (no search has tuned this rotation); **Holy Conduit** (47-48) makes it and **Hammer of Wrath** 40% cheaper. **Hammer of Wrath** finishes mobs below 20% health from level 44.
- Below about a third of your health: **Templar's Bulwark** first (level 30; an absorb as large as your health for 8 seconds), then **Holy Light** behind it. Otherwise the fight lab casts **Holy Light** only while the mob cannot hurt you: during **Divine Protection** (below 30%) or while **Hammer of Justice** holds it; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Caveats**

- Before level 41 skip the **Holy Shield** line, and before 30 the **Templar's Bulwark** one: the order takes them there.
- This order spends levels 10-19 on defensive talents (**Redoubt**, **Precision**, **Anticipation**), so early kills are slow: about 33 seconds at level 20 against 28 at 30.
- **Templar's Bulwark** applies Forbearance for a minute, so **Divine Protection** is not available right after it.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** (instant, 15-second cooldown, 40% cheaper with **Holy Conduit**) hits them hard. The fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the 60% mana rule was set for single mobs, and this rotation was not measured on several.
- Tanking a dungeon on the way: **Seal of Fury**'s judgement taunts for 4 seconds, and **Righteous Fury** makes your Holy damage 60% more threat.
