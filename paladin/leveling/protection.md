**Leveling as Protection or as Retribution?** This build takes 31 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Retribution](../leveling/retribution.md) 28 s: Protection is 11% slower. The fastest order puts its first point into Protection at level 26: the damage talents come first. Level this way if you want to tank dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Retribution and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a one-handed weapon and a shield; **Righteous Fury** too if you have **Improved Righteous Fury** (less damage taken while it is on). **Seal of Fury** before each pull: with a shield it also gives you an absorb.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Keep **Seal of Fury** up.
3. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Holy Shield** whenever it is down.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready; **Swift Judgement** right after a **Judgement** for a free second one.
6. **Consecration** while your mana is above 60% and the mob has more than 30% health left.
7. Low on health (below about a third, with the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark**, then **Holy Light** behind its absorb; otherwise **Divine Protection** (below 30%) or **Hammer of Justice**, and **Holy Light** while the mob cannot hit you; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.

## Why this rotation

Protection levels behind a shield: **Seal of Fury** turns half of its Holy damage into an absorb, blocks and judgements pay your mana back, and **Judgement**, **Holy Strike** and **Consecration** do the damage, so you rarely stop to eat. The order takes Retribution's mana talents first (levels 10-25) and Protection from 26, with **Holy Shield** last at 60.

**Why it works**

- **Seal of Fury** before each pull: at rank 7 it adds 35 Holy damage to every swing, and with a shield each swing also gives you an absorb of half that damage. **Shield Specialization** (levels 37-39) returns 4% of your mana on a block, once every 3 seconds, and **Improved Seal of Fury** (40) 60 mana each time such a shield is used up; the open search values them at about 2 seconds per kill each at 60.
- **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready, with **Swift Judgement** (level 41) right after a **Judgement**: it finishes the cooldown and makes the next one free. **Sanctified Judgement** (20-22) returns 60% of your seal's mana cost each time you judge: the open search found it the build's most valuable talent at 60 (about 3 seconds per kill), ahead of **Holy Shield** and **Holy Conduit**.
- **Holy Shield** (level 60) whenever it is down: 30% more block for 10 seconds or 4 blocks, and each block deals Holy damage; about 2.8 seconds per kill at 60.
- **Consecration** only while your mana is above 60% and the mob has more than 30% health, a hand-set rule the fight lab plays (no search has tuned this rotation); **Holy Conduit** (levels 19 and 23) makes it and **Hammer of Wrath** 40% cheaper. **Hammer of Wrath** finishes mobs below 20% health from level 44.
- Keep **Righteous Fury** up even alone: **Improved Righteous Fury** (level 45) and **Iron Creed** (55-59: **Holy Strike** cuts your damage taken for 6 seconds) only work while it is on. Against same-level mobs the fight lab finds both worth almost nothing per kill; they are there for the tank build.
- Below about a third of your health: **Templar's Bulwark** first (level 47; an absorb as large as your health for 8 seconds), then **Holy Light** behind it. Otherwise the fight lab casts **Holy Light** only while the mob cannot hurt you: during **Divine Protection** (below 30%) or while **Hammer of Justice** holds it; **Lay on Hands** below 15%. Drink below 15% of your mana.

**Caveats**

- Before level 60 skip the **Holy Shield** line, and before 47 the **Templar's Bulwark** one: the order takes them there.
- Until about level 50 the build is a one-handed paladin with a shield and Retribution's mana talents; **Reckoning** (50-54), **Iron Creed** and **Holy Shield** come last. It kills about 10% slower than the Retribution page over levels 20-60, most of it at 20 and 30.
- **Templar's Bulwark** applies Forbearance for a minute, so **Divine Protection** is not available right after it.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** (instant, 15-second cooldown, 40% cheaper with **Holy Conduit**) hits them hard. The fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the 60% mana rule was set for single mobs, and this rotation was not measured on several.
- Tanking a dungeon on the way: **Seal of Fury**'s judgement taunts for 4 seconds, and **Righteous Fury** makes your Holy damage 90% more threat.
