> 🔬 Derived build: found by this project's open leveling search (every legal build, no tree rule, in the fight lab, this project's leveling simulator; the winner picked by a race at levels 20-60). A model of the beta, to check at launch.

**Signature spells:** 71% of the damage at level 60 in the fight lab comes from Holy Shield, Seal of Fury, Judgement, melee, Consecration, Seal of Righteousness (the spec's rule: at least 60%; **Holy Shield** cast in every fight).

**Leveling as Protection or as Retribution?** This build takes 31 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Retribution](../leveling/retribution.md) 28 s: Protection is 9% slower. The fastest order puts its first point into Protection at level 26: the damage talents come first. Level this way if you want to tank dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Retribution and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Improved Righteous Fury** is a talent this build does not take: skip the lines that use it. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a one-handed weapon and a shield; **Righteous Fury** too: **Iron Creed** and **Improved Righteous Fury** cut your damage taken only while it is on (the fight lab keeps it up). **Seal of Fury** before each pull: with a shield it also gives you an absorb.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Keep **Seal of Fury** up.
3. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Holy Shield** whenever it is down.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready; **Swift Judgement** right after a **Judgement** for a free second one.
6. **Consecration** while your mana is above 60% and the mob has more than 30% health left.
7. Low on health (below about a third, with the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark**, then **Holy Light** behind its absorb; otherwise **Divine Protection** (below 30%) or **Hammer of Justice**, and **Holy Light** while the mob cannot hit you; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

**Between fights:** Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.

## Why this rotation

Protection levels behind a shield: **Seal of Fury** turns half of its Holy damage into an absorb, blocks and judgements pay your mana back, and **Judgement**, **Holy Strike**, **Consecration** and, at 60, **Holy Shield** do the damage. The order takes Retribution's mana talents first (levels 10-25) and Protection from 26, with **Holy Shield** last at 60. Over levels 20-60 it is about 9% slower per kill than the Retribution page in the fight lab (30.8 against 28.2 seconds).

**Why it works**

- **Seal of Fury** before each pull: at rank 7 (level 58) it adds 35 Holy damage to every swing, and with a shield each swing also gives you an absorb of half that damage. **Shield Specialization** (levels 36-38) adds block value and returns 4% of your mana on a block, at most once every 3 seconds, and **Improved Seal of Fury** (40) 60 mana each time such an absorb is used up; the open search values them at about 2.2 and 1.8 seconds per kill at 60.
- **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready, with **Swift Judgement** (level 41) right after a **Judgement**: it finishes the cooldown and makes the next one free. **Sanctified Judgement** (20-22) returns 60% of your seal's mana cost each time you judge: the open search found it the build's most valuable talent at 60 (about 2.7 seconds per kill without it).
- **Holy Shield** (level 60) whenever it is down: 30% more block for 10 seconds or 4 blocks, and 221 Holy damage for each block at rank 3. The fight lab casts it about twice a kill at 60 and values it at about 2.4 seconds per kill.
- **Consecration** only while your mana is above 60% and the mob has more than 30% health, a hand-set rule the fight lab plays (no search has tuned the Protection list). **Holy Conduit** (levels 24 and 45) makes it and **Hammer of Wrath** 40% cheaper (about 2.3 seconds per kill at 60). **Hammer of Wrath** finishes mobs below 20% health from level 44; this build has no **Instrument of Law**, so it keeps its 1-second cast.
- **Reckoning** (53-57): a block or a critical hit taken can store an extra attack (up to 4), all released with your next swing; about 1.4 seconds per kill at 60. **Redoubt** (27-30 and 33) and **One-Handed Weapon Specialization** (46-47 and 50) are worth about 0.9 seconds each.
- Keep **Righteous Fury** up even alone: **Iron Creed** (58-59: **Holy Strike** cuts your damage taken for 6 seconds) only works while it is on, and the fight lab keeps it up all the time. This build takes no **Improved Righteous Fury**.
- Below about a third of your health (the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark** first (level 49; an absorb as large as your health for 8 seconds), then **Holy Light** behind it. Otherwise the fight lab casts **Holy Light** only while the mob cannot hurt you: during **Divine Protection** (below 30%) or while **Hammer of Justice** holds it; **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.

**Caveats**

- Before level 60 skip the **Holy Shield** line, before 49 the **Templar's Bulwark** one and before 41 **Swift Judgement**: the order takes them there.
- The gap to Retribution is mostly at levels 20-40 (22.9, 28.8 and 32.9 seconds per kill against 19.7, 23.3 and 28.6); at 50 and 60 Protection is within about 4% (30.8 and 33.1 against 29.5 and 32.1).
- **Templar's Bulwark** applies Forbearance for a minute, so **Divine Protection** is not available right after it.
- Undead and Demons: **Exorcism** (instant, 15-second cooldown, 40% cheaper with **Holy Conduit**) hits them hard. The fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the 60% mana rule was set for single mobs, and this list was not measured on several.
- Tanking a dungeon on the way: **Seal of Fury**'s judgement taunts for 4 seconds, and **Righteous Fury** makes your Holy attacks cause 90% more threat.
