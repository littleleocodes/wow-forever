**Signature spells:** 73% of the damage at level 60 in the fight lab comes from the spec's core spells (Holy Shield, Seal of Fury, Judgement, melee; this build never casts Consecration, Seal of Righteousness) (the spec's rule: at least 60%; **Holy Shield** cast at level 60).

Your first Protection point comes at level 10. Level this way if you want to tank dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Retribution and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a one-handed weapon and a shield; **Righteous Fury** too: **Iron Creed** and **Improved Righteous Fury** cut your damage taken only while it is on (the fight lab keeps it up). **Seal of Fury** before each pull: with a shield it also gives you an absorb.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Keep **Seal of Fury** up.
2. **Holy Shield** whenever it is down.
3. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready; **Swift Judgement** (its own 1-minute cooldown) whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left: a second, free **Judgement**.
4. Low on health (below about a third, with the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark**, then **Holy Light** behind its absorb; otherwise **Divine Protection** (below 30%) and **Holy Light** while the mob cannot hit you; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%. The tuned rules cast no Hammer of Justice, Hammer of Wrath or Consecration against a single mob.

**Between fights:** Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.

## Why this rotation

Protection levels behind a shield, with its raid tank talents in the order that levels fastest. **Seal of Fury** goes up before each pull (with a shield it turns half of its Holy damage into an absorb), **Judgement**, **Holy Strike** and, from level 25, **Swift Judgement** do the damage, and **Holy Shield** joins at 30. Against a single mob the tuned rules cast no **Consecration**, **Hammer of Wrath** or **Hammer of Justice**. For its speed against the other paladin pages see the leveling index.

**Why it works**

- **Seal of Fury** before each pull: at rank 7 (level 58) it adds 35 Holy damage to every swing, and with a shield each swing also gives you an absorb of half that damage. **Redoubt** (10-14): a damaging melee hit against you has a 10% chance to add block (6% a rank, 30% at five) for 10 seconds or 5 blocks. It opens **Shield Specialization** (21-22): 30% more absorbed, and each block restores 4% of your mana, at most once every 3 seconds.
- **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. **Swift Judgement** (25) is a second, free **Judgement** on its own 1-minute cooldown: press it whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left. It needs **Templar's Bulwark** (20), so the order takes that first. **Sanctified Judgement** (36-38) returns 60% of your seal's mana cost each time you judge.
- **Holy Shield** (level 30) whenever it is down and the mob has more than a fifth of its health: 30% more block for 10 seconds or 4 blocks, and 75 Holy damage for each block at rank 1, 221 at rank 4 (level 60). The ranks arrive at 30, 40, 50 and 60.
- **Shieldsworn** (56) takes 4 seconds off **Holy Strike**'s cooldown; the lab models only that, not the shield strike's extra triggers. **Reckoning** (52-55 and 57): a block (8% a rank) or a critical hit taken (20% a rank) can store an extra attack, up to 4, released with your next swing.
- Keep **Righteous Fury** up even alone: **Improved Righteous Fury** (49-51, 3% less damage taken a rank) and **Iron Creed** (58-60: **Holy Strike** cuts your damage taken by 2% a rank for 6 seconds) only work while it is on, and the fight lab keeps it up all the time.
- The smaller points: **Precision** (15-17, 3% hit), **Anticipation** (26-28, 4 Defense a rank), **Toughness** (23-24, 29 and 47-48, 2% armor a rank), **Deflection** (33-35 and 44-45, 1% parry a rank), **Sacred Duty** (18, 30 seconds a rank off the cooldown of **Divine Protection**, **Divine Shield** and **Templar's Bulwark**), **Benediction** (31-32), **Conviction** (39-41), **Improved Judgement** (42-43) and **Sacred Arbiter** (46). **Guardian's Favor** (19) is not used by the lab.
- Below about a third of your health (the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark** first (an absorb as large as your health for 8 seconds), then **Holy Light** behind it. Otherwise the fight lab casts **Holy Light** only while the mob cannot hurt you: during **Divine Protection** (below 30%) or while a stun holds it; **Flash of Light** below a quarter, **Lay on Hands** below 15%. Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.

**Caveats**

- Before level 30 skip the **Holy Shield** line, before 20 the **Templar's Bulwark** one and before 25 **Swift Judgement**: the order takes them there.
- **Templar's Bulwark** applies Forbearance for a minute, so **Divine Protection** is not available right after it.
- This build takes no **Holy Conduit**, so **Exorcism** (instant, 15-second cooldown) costs full price against Undead and Demons; the fight lab has no such mobs, so it was not measured.

**When to change it**

- A mob that casts spells: **Hammer of Justice** stops the cast. The tuned rules never cast it against a single mob (a measured loss), but nothing else stops a caster.
- Two or more mobs: **Consecration** hits them all; the tuned rules were set on single mobs, and this list was not measured on several.
- Tanking a dungeon on the way: **Seal of Fury**'s judgement taunts for 4 seconds, and **Righteous Fury** makes your Holy attacks cause 90% more threat.
