# Holy/Protection hybrid Paladin — leveling, level by level

[Paladin](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Holy/Protection hybrid](hybrid-holy-protection.md) > Level by level

What a Holy/Protection paladin presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage. It levels behind a shield and plays the Protection list: a one-handed weapon and a shield, **Seal of Fury** from 10 (Holy damage on every swing and, with the shield, an absorb), **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** on cooldown, **Swift Judgement** from 25 and **Holy Shield** from 30. Protection takes the first 21 points (10-30) and the last 4; Retribution's mana talents come at 31-45 and Holy's at 43-56, with nothing new to press. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 13/25/13 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/paladin/60/050240002-353001021113014-05233-12)), the one on the [Holy/Protection hybrid leveling page](hybrid-holy-protection.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | A one-handed weapon and a shield, **Seal of Righteousness** on yourself, then your swings. |
| 4-5 | **Judgement** unleashes your seal on the mob every 10 seconds. In Forever the seal stays up afterwards. |
| 6-9 | **Holy Strike**, an instant weapon strike every 10 seconds. With **Judgement** it is the whole fight until 10. |
| 10-19 | **Seal of Fury** replaces Seal of Righteousness: Holy damage on every swing and, with the shield, an absorb. Talent points start. |
| 20-24 | **Templar's Bulwark** (talent), an absorb as large as your health: your first save. **Flash of Light** for a quick heal. |
| 25-29 | **Swift Judgement** (talent): a free extra Judgement once a minute, off the global cooldown. |
| 30-43 | **Holy Shield** (talent): more block for 10 seconds and Holy damage on every block. The fight is now four keys. |
| 44-60 | **Hammer of Wrath**, a finisher below 20% health. The tuned rules leave it out: nothing new to press. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; the blessing, the aura, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The shield specs keep the Retribution layout, so muscle memory carries over: Q the strike you press most, E the stun, R the seal and **Judgement**, F the bubble, the thumb buttons the heals. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the macros: the [paladin keybinds page](keybinds.md).

- The tuned Protection list never presses **Hammer of Justice**, **Hammer of Wrath** or **Consecration** against single mobs. E is for a caster you want stopped or a bad pull, Shift+R for a mob that runs, Shift+Q for packs.

## Levels 1-3: Shield and Seal of Righteousness

**The trainer teaches:** Devotion Aura (1), Holy Light (1), **Seal of Righteousness** (1).

**Keys to set:** R = Seal of Righteousness; Mouse 5 = Holy Light; C = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** R Seal of Righteousness.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Righteousness** before the pull. It lasts 30 seconds: put it back when it runs out.
2. Walk in and swing.

- Keep **Devotion Aura** on. Heal with **Holy Light** between fights.
- Use a one-handed weapon and a shield from the first ones you find: the fight lab plays this build with them from level 1, and the shield carries **Seal of Fury**'s absorb and the build's block talents.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 13 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Melee 2.5, Seal of Righteousness 1.0, Shield block 0.1.*

## Levels 10-19: Seal of Fury, talents begin

**New ranks to buy:** Divine Protection at 18; Holy Light at 14; Holy Strike at 12; Seal of Fury at 18.

**Talents:** 10-12 Divine Intellect, 13-14 Redoubt, 15-17 Precision, 18-19 Redoubt.

**Keys to set:** 1 = Blessing of Protection; Shift+Mouse 5 = Lay on Hands; Shift+F = Blessing of Freedom (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Holy Strike, R Judgement, F Divine Protection, Mouse 5 Holy Light, Shift+Mouse 5 Lay on Hands.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Seal of Fury** up before the pull: the first press of R.
2. **Judgement** whenever it is ready.
3. **Holy Strike** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 30% of your health, with the mob above a fifth of its own: **Divine Protection**, then **Holy Light** inside it. **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

- Change the seal in your R macro to **Seal of Fury** at 10. With a shield, every swing it adds also gives you an absorb of half its Holy damage. Its judgement taunts the mob, which a solo fight does not need.
- **Lay on Hands** (10) is a full heal for emergencies. **Blessing of Protection** (10) and **Blessing of Freedom** (18) are tools for a bad pull, not rotation.
- Buy the next rank of the defensive spells on your bars too: **Divine Protection** at 18 (8 seconds instead of 6), **Blessing of Protection** at 24 and 38 (8, then 10 seconds), **Lay on Hands** at 30 and 50 and **Divine Shield** at 50.

## Between fights

- Heal yourself back to about 80% with **Holy Light**, or with **Flash of Light** from level 20.
- Drink below 15% of your mana: the Protection list's default, which its tuned rules of 2026-11-06 keep. Eat only if you are still below about two thirds of your health after the heals.
- Re-cast **Blessing of Might** when it runs out, your seal just before each pull, and from 29 **Righteous Fury** every 30 minutes.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of your seal and **Holy Strike**.

## Other ways to level this class

- The [Protection guide](protection-by-level.md) plays the same Protection list with the Protection page's deeper Protection build.
- Packs of mobs: **Consecration** on Shift+Q hits them all. The single-target rules never cast it.
- Dungeons on the way: you can tank them. **Seal of Fury**'s judgement taunts, and **Righteous Fury** adds threat.
