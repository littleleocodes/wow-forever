## Summary

Holy Shield, Blessing of Sanctuary, Redoubt/Shield Specialization block chance and Improved Righteous Fury threat, with Consecration from Holy. Excellent AoE dungeon tank; raid tanking is limited by no taunt and mana.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Holy Shield** — +30% block chance and Holy damage on each block — threat and mitigation.
2. **Improved Righteous Fury** — Righteous Fury +90% Holy threat instead of +60%.
3. **Blessing of Sanctuary** — Damage reduction and Holy damage on block for the tank.
4. **Redoubt** — +30% block chance after being crit; prerequisite for Shield Specialization.
5. **Shield Specialization** — +30% block value.
6. **Anticipation** — +10 Defense.
7. **Toughness** — +10% armor.
8. **Consecration** — AoE threat.
9. **Precision** — +3% hit for threat.
10. **One-Handed Weapon Specialization** — +10% one-hander damage.

## Notes

- Consecration + Holy Shield + Retribution Aura/Blessing of Sanctuary reflect damage: pull big, hold everything.
- Raid tanking as a paladin in Classic Era is a niche (no taunt); Warriors and Bears are the raid tanks.
