## How the fights go

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a two-hander: Blessing of Might and Devotion Aura up, a Seal recast before each pull (Seal of Command once talented, Seal of Righteousness before), the mob walks in (paladins have no charge), Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown, Consecration while mana allows, Hammer of Justice into a doubled Seal of Command judgement or when hurt, Hammer of Wrath under 20%; Divine Protection and a Holy Light when low, Lay on Hands in an emergency. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Holy Light or Flash of Light back to 80% health, water below 35% mana; a death costs 90 s.

The rotation in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. Before each pull: **Seal of Command** (**Seal of Righteousness** before you have it).

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells: it stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. Keep your seal up: put it back if it runs out.
3. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: **Seal of Command**'s judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard. The stun has a 1-minute cooldown, so most pulls skip this line: it is ready for about one pull in three.
4. If your build has **Swift Judgement**: press it whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left. It is a second, free **Judgement** on its own 1-minute cooldown.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration and no Hammer of Wrath: the tuned rotation never casts either.
6. Low on health (below a third, with the mob above a quarter): **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and heal during the stun; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

Between fights: Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink below about a third of your mana, and eat only if you are still below 65% of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-09, on top of the priority below): never cast Consecration; never cast Hammer of Wrath. Together +12.7% time per kill saved for `leveling-retribution` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
