## ret-dps

Played by the page builds of [Retribution](../../leveling/retribution.md) ([`leveling-retribution`](builds/leveling-retribution.md)), [Prot-Ret](../../leveling/prot-ret.md) ([`prot-ret-open-leveling`](builds/prot-ret-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a two-hander: Blessing of Might and Devotion Aura up, a Seal recast before each pull (Seal of Command once talented, Seal of Righteousness before), the mob walks in (paladins have no charge), Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown, Consecration while mana allows, Hammer of Justice into a doubled Seal of Command judgement or when hurt, Hammer of Wrath under 20%; Divine Protection and a Holy Light when low, Lay on Hands in an emergency. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Holy Light or Flash of Light back to 80% health, water below 35% mana; a death costs 90 s.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. Before each pull: **Seal of Command** (**Seal of Righteousness** before you have it).

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells: it stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. Keep your seal up: put it back if it runs out.
3. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: **Seal of Command**'s judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard. The stun has a 1-minute cooldown, so most pulls skip this line: it is ready for about one pull in three.
4. If your build has **Swift Judgement**: press it whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left. It is a second, free **Judgement** on its own 1-minute cooldown.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration and no Hammer of Wrath: the tuned rotation never casts either.
6. Low on health (below a third, with the mob above a quarter): **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and heal during the stun; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

Between fights: Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink below about a third of your mana, and eat only if you are still below 65% of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-09, on top of the priority below): never cast Consecration; never cast Hammer of Wrath. Together +12.7% time per kill saved for `leveling-retribution` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## prot-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Holy/Protection hybrid](../../leveling/hybrid-holy-protection.md) ([`hybrid-holy-protection-open-leveling`](builds/hybrid-holy-protection-open-leveling.md)), [Protection](../../leveling/protection.md) ([`protection-leveling`](builds/protection-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a one-handed weapon and a shield; **Righteous Fury** too: **Iron Creed** and **Improved Righteous Fury** cut your damage taken only while it is on (the fight lab keeps it up). **Seal of Fury** before each pull: with a shield it also gives you an absorb.

1. Keep **Seal of Fury** up.
2. **Holy Shield** whenever it is down.
3. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready; **Swift Judgement** (its own 1-minute cooldown) whenever it is ready while your seal is up and the mob has more than a quarter of its health left: a second, free **Judgement**.
4. Low on health (below about a third, with the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark**, then **Holy Light** behind its absorb; otherwise **Divine Protection** (below 30%) and **Holy Light** while the mob cannot hit you; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%. The tuned rules cast no Hammer of Justice, Hammer of Wrath or Consecration against a single mob.

Between fights: Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-06, on top of the priority below): never cast Consecration; never cast Hammer of Wrath; never cast Hammer of Justice. Together +6.0% time per kill saved for `protection-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`hybrid-holy-protection-open-leveling`](builds/hybrid-holy-protection-open-leveling.md), [`protection-leveling`](builds/protection-leveling.md): not recorded (their times per kill come from the race records of the searches that found them, which keep times only; the table above has them).

## holy-leveling

Played by the page build of [Holy](../../leveling/holy.md) ([`holy-leveling`](builds/holy-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Blessing of Wisdom** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. **Seal of Righteousness** before each pull; **Seal of Command** instead once your build has it.

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Once you have **Holy Shock**: cast it at the mob as it walks in while your mana is above 70% and the mob has more than 10% of its health left, after **Divine Favor** for a sure critical hit while you are healthy.
3. Keep your seal up.
4. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
5. With **Seal of Command**: **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left (the judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard).
6. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready. Against a single mob, no Consecration: the Holy list never casts it.
7. Low on health (below 40%, with the mob above a fifth): **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

Between fights: Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**; drink only below 15% of your mana, and eat only if you are still below 40% of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-06, on top of the priority below): drink below 15% mana; eat below 40% health. Together +1.0% time per kill saved for `holy-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`holy-leveling`](builds/holy-leveling.md): not recorded (its times per kill come from the race records of the search that found it, which keep times only; the table above has them).
