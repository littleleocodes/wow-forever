## holy-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Shockadin](../../leveling/shockadin.md) ([`shockadin-open-leveling`](builds/shockadin-open-leveling.md)), [Holy](../../leveling/holy.md) ([`holy-leveling`](builds/holy-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Blessing of Wisdom** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. **Seal of Righteousness** before each pull (**Seal of Command** instead if your build takes it).

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Once you have **Holy Shock**: cast it at the mob as it walks in while your mana is above 70%, after **Divine Favor** for a sure critical hit while you are healthy.
3. Keep your seal up.
4. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready.
5. Low on health (below 40%, with the mob above a fifth): **Holy Shock** on yourself (after **Divine Favor** if it is ready), **Divine Protection** below 30%, **Hammer of Justice**, then **Holy Light**; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

Between fights: Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**, drink below a quarter of your mana.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-15, on top of the priority below): never cast Hammer of Wrath. Together +3.0% time per kill saved for `shockadin-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`shockadin-open-leveling`](builds/shockadin-open-leveling.md), [`holy-leveling`](builds/holy-leveling.md): not recorded (their times per kill come from the race records of the searches that found them, which keep times only; the table above has them).

## ret-dps

Played by the page build of [Retribution](../../leveling/retribution.md) ([`retribution-open-leveling`](builds/retribution-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a two-hander: Blessing of Might and Devotion Aura up, a Seal recast before each pull (Seal of Command once talented, Seal of Righteousness before), the mob walks in (paladins have no charge), Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown, Consecration while mana allows, Hammer of Justice into a doubled Seal of Command judgement or when hurt, Hammer of Wrath under 20%; Divine Protection and a Holy Light when low, Lay on Hands in an emergency. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Holy Light or Flash of Light back to 80% health, water below 35% mana; a death costs 90 s.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a two-handed weapon. Before each pull: **Seal of Command** (**Seal of Righteousness** before you have it).

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells: it stands back and casts until you stop it.
2. Keep your seal up: put it back if it runs out.
3. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Hammer of Justice**, then **Judgement**, when both are ready and the mob has more than 30% health left: **Seal of Command**'s judgement hits a stunned mob twice as hard.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready.
6. Low on health (below a third, with the mob above a quarter): **Divine Protection** and **Holy Light** inside it, or **Hammer of Justice** and heal during the stun; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

Between fights: Heal yourself back to 80% with **Holy Light** or **Flash of Light**, drink below 15% of your mana.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-15, on top of the priority below): never cast Consecration; drink below 15% mana. Together +12.4% time per kill saved for `retribution-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## prot-leveling

Played by the page build of [Protection](../../leveling/protection.md) ([`protection-open-leveling`](builds/protection-open-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Blessing of Might** and **Devotion Aura** up, a one-handed weapon and a shield; **Righteous Fury** too if you have **Improved Righteous Fury** (less damage taken while it is on). **Seal of Fury** before each pull: with a shield it also gives you an absorb.

1. **Hammer of Justice** a mob that casts spells.
2. Keep **Seal of Fury** up.
3. **Hammer of Wrath** when the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Holy Shield** whenever it is down.
5. **Judgement** and **Holy Strike** whenever ready; **Swift Judgement** right after a **Judgement** for a free second one.
6. **Consecration** while your mana is above 60% and the mob has more than 30% health left.
7. Low on health (below about a third, with the mob above a fifth): **Templar's Bulwark**, then **Holy Light** behind its absorb; otherwise **Divine Protection** (below 30%) or **Hammer of Justice**, and **Holy Light** while the mob cannot hit you; **Flash of Light** below a quarter; **Lay on Hands** below 15%.

Between fights: Drink below 15% of your mana; you rarely need to eat.
