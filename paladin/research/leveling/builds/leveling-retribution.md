## Summary

Allocation and order adopted on 2026-09-26 from `cand-ret-r5`, the fastest build in the fight-lab race over levels 20/30/40/50/60 (+5.1% vs the previous draft, level-weighted time per kill; the previous draft is kept under leveling/fightlab/candidates/). Core talents are ranked by the sim's removal cost at 60. Previous summary: Classic Ret leveling: Benediction, Improved Judgement, Seal of Command at 20, Conviction, Vengeance, Repentance at 40. Forever keeps Seal of Command at tier 3 but moves the biggest kill-speed talents into Holy tier 1-2 (Divine Strength, Improved Seals: +15% seal/judgement damage), so this order dips Holy at 21-28 before finishing Retribution: Sacred Arbiter (Holy Strike refreshes Judgements) at 34, Vengeance at 41-43, Champion of the Light and Twist of Light at 48, then Precision from Protection. Ends in the Forever Ret raid build.

## Leveling rotation (fight lab)

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a two-hander: Blessing of Might and Devotion Aura up, a Seal recast before each pull (Seal of Command once talented, Seal of Righteousness before), the mob walks in (paladins have no charge), Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown, Consecration while mana allows, Hammer of Justice into a doubled Seal of Command judgement or when hurt, Hammer of Wrath under 20%; Divine Protection and a Holy Light when low, Lay on Hands in an emergency. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Holy Light or Flash of Light back to 80% health, water below 35% mana; a death costs 90 s.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-23, on top of the priority below): never cast Consecration; drink below 15% mana. Together +6.1% time per kill saved for `leveling-retribution` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## Notes

- Adopted from the fight-lab race on 2026-09-26: cand-ret-r5 beat the previous draft by +5.1% (level-weighted time per kill); the rationale below the fight-lab note predates the adoption where it names talents no longer taken.
- Loop: Seal of Command -> Judgement on cooldown -> Holy Strike (refreshes the Judgement from 34) -> Consecration (baseline) on 2+ mobs; heal between pulls.
- Consecration and Blessing of Kings are baseline in Forever, so no points are spent on them.
- Build 1.60.1.70009 removed Crusade: its 2 points go to Vindication 2 (+2% attack power self-buff on melee hits), which also keeps the 20 points below tier 5.
- 2026-09-27 scenario audit: Divine Intellect 3, Voice of Truth 1, Divine Favor 1, Holy Shock 1 -> Vindication 2, Vengeance 2, Pursuit of Justice 2. The melee leveling loop never casts Holy Shock or Divine Favor, and Voice of Truth and the extra Divine Intellect ranks only opened their tier (fight lab 0.0% each). Vindication and Vengeance finish their ranks, and Pursuit of Justice adds 15% run speed between pulls, which the fight lab's fixed travel time does not count. Fight lab race (1500 fights per level): equal at 20-50, 0.4% less time per kill at 60.
- Fight lab (leveling/fightlab/ret-dps.md, 2026-10-25, generated by render_fightlab_sections.py): time per kill at L20/30/40/50/60 = 20/24/30/30/33 s with Classic 1.12 creature stats and quest greens; the optimizer's opt-ret-dps-leveling-st is +1% at 60 and -0% at 30 (negative = faster); race winner over the leveling: leveling-retribution; top talents at 60: Sanctified Judgement +7%, Conviction +5%, Improved Seals +4%, Holy Conduit +3%; about zero: Benediction, Champion of the Light, Divine Intellect, Pursuit of Justice, Repentance.
