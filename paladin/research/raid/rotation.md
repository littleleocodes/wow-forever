# Paladin — raid rotations (generated)

> Generated by `_reference/data/render_rotations.py` on 2026-11-11 from the raid simulator's rotation files and its runs of every build (the official wowsims/forever simulator at ff9babb (client 1.60.1.70888)): each build's rotation is the simulator's priority list with the lines that build never casts removed, and the casts per minute measured on the training dummy (tanks: in the raid setting). Our builds and the simulator's own builds; builds that cast the same lines share a table. A line's condition reads left to right (spell names from the simulator's database). The leveling priority the fight lab plays is on the [leveling rotation page](../leveling/rotation.md). Models of the beta, not measurements. Do not hand-edit.
