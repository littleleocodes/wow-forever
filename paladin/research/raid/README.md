## Raid sim (community simulator, level 60)

[`raidsim/`](raidsim/) runs every raid build through the community's raid simulator (these reports: the official wowsims/forever simulator at ff9babb (client 1.60.1.70888)) with the entry's gear set and rotation, 3000 iterations per build, next to the simulator's preset talent builds. **DPS specs are ranked on a boss-level training dummy with only your own buffs** (the class's self-buffs and the spec's standard consumables; no raid or party buffs, no debuffs from others): the races, calibrations and the adopt step run there, while the pages rank raid builds by DPS in a full raid (2026-10-02).
