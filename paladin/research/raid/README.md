DOWNRANK: cast the lowest rank that covers the deficit; HOT_REFRESH_DEFICIT: keep the HoT on the tank while it misses more than this share of its health; EMERGENCY: below this share a target gets the fastest big heal; RAID_HEAL_AT: raid members below this share get healed; GROUP_HEAL_MIN: hurt members for a group heal; POTION_BELOW / POTION_FIRST: mana potion timing.
