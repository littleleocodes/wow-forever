## Mechanics simulated

- **Illumination**: a critical Holy Light, Flash of Light, Holy Shock or Light's Vigil heal returns 50% of that spell's base mana cost (20%/rank chance; once per spell per cast)
- **Divine Favor**: a sure critical top-rank Holy Light on the tank every 2 min (its mana paid)
- **Holy Shock**: instant 307-333 heal on a 10 s cooldown: the emergency heal, and the trigger of Light's Vigil
- **Holy Power**: +3%/rank critical chance with Holy Shock, +1%/rank with every other heal
- **Infusion of Light**: a critical Holy Shock or Flash of Light makes the next Holy Light within 15 s 0.5 s/rank faster
- **Benediction**: -2%/rank mana cost of the instants (Holy Shock, Divine Favor)
- **Light's Vigil**: the group heal: Light's Vigil (1.5 s, 1,340 mana) on the lowest raid member when enough of the group is hurt (tables.GROUP_HEAL_MIN below RAID_HEAL_AT), then a Holy Shock on them that triggers no cooldown: their party (the whole group here) heals for 684-724 + 14.3% of healing each
- **Lay on Hands**: once, when the tank is under 20% (a 20 min cooldown): heals for the paladin's maximum health (tables.RAID_HP, a raid member's; the lab gives the healer no health of its own) and drains all mana

Everything else the build takes is either a plain tooltip number the lab applies (healing, mana cost, crit, cast time, regen, stats) or not simulated.
