## How to play

PvP has no fixed rotation: it depends on who you fight. No simulator plays PvP, so this build rests on the talents' descriptions and the community's PvP builds.

**Why these talents:** Holy Shock (tier 5) with Holy Power's +15% Holy Shock crit, Divine Favor, Light's Vigil, Voice of Truth (silence immunity) and Unyielding Faith; Retribution for Benediction, Improved Judgement, Conviction, Seal of Command, Sanctified Judgement and Crusade. Consecration is baseline, so nothing is lost there.
