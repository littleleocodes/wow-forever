The fightWhat to press

Tank for the group from level 22

  1. Before the pull: Righteous Fury on (it lasts 30 minutes) and Seal of Fury up (middle mouse). Put the seal back when its 30 seconds run out.
  2. A mob turns to the healer or another player: tab to it and Judgement it (R, 10 yards). With Seal of Fury up the judgement taunts it for 3 seconds; then press Q so your swings and Holy Strike go to it.
  3. Hammer of Justice (E, 10 yards) a caster that stands back and casts, or a mob still on the healer after the taunt.
  4. Judgement (R) on your target whenever it is ready. It is off the global cooldown: press it with the others.
  5. Consecration (1) when two or more mobs stand on you, and again every 8 seconds while they do. On a single mob, only while your mana is above half.
  6. Holy Strike (Q) whenever it is ready.
  7. Exorcism (2) on an Undead or Demon whenever it is ready.
  • Your points up to 22 are passive except Templar's Bulwark: Redoubt (10-14) and Shield Specialization (20-21) need the shield, and the second gives you 4% of your mana back on a block, once every 3 seconds; Precision (15-17) is 3% hit, fewer misses on your threat; Guardian's Favor (18) brings Blessing of Protection back a minute sooner; Sacred Duty (19) brings Templar's Bulwark and the bubble back 30 seconds sooner. Toughness (23-24): more armor from items.
  • Righteous Fury adds 33% threat, and 90% more on Holy damage: your seal's swings, Judgement, Consecration and the Holy part of Holy Strike. Keep it on in every group fight, quests included.
  • Consecration is your only hit on several mobs at once (the first 4 that enter it take the most). It costs 135 mana at rank 1, a lot at this level, hence the mana rule on single mobs. Advice: this project's simulators play one mob or one boss, never a pack.
