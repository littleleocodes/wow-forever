Puts the Nova out in the middle of a bolt. Press E only when Frost Nova is lit: /stopcasting runs even when Nova is on cooldown, so the bolt you were casting is thrown away and nothing comes out. If you lose bolts while questing, put plain Frost Nova back on E.

V Polymorph where you point

#showtooltip Polymorph
/cast [@mouseover,harm,nodead][@focus,harm,nodead][] Polymorph

Sheeps the enemy under your mouse pointer (its model or its portrait), else your focus, else your target. An enemy under the pointer wins over your target, so move the pointer off enemies to sheep your target. While you hold the right mouse button the pointer is hidden and there is no mouseover: V then sheeps your focus, else your target, so let go of the right button for that moment to sheep what you point at.

The fightWhat to press

The finished rotation from level 40

  1. Ice Barrier before the pull whenever it is ready (a 30-second cooldown) and your last shield is used up, and again mid-fight when its minute of extra Frost damage is about to run out.
  2. Counterspell a mob that starts casting.
  3. Frostbolt from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a tenth of its health.
  4. Frost Nova when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
  5. Ice Lance whenever the mob is frozen or Fingers of Frost lights up, as soon as its 8-second cooldown allows.
  6. If the mob reaches you with Frost Nova on cooldown, keep casting: the tuned rules save Fire Blast for the finish.
  7. Below a fifth of its health: Fire Blast when it will kill or the mob is on you, or an Ice Lance that will kill. Below a tenth: no Fire Blast, only the Ice Lance or your wand.
