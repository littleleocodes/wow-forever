## How to play

**Setup, before the pull:** **Dismiss Pet**: **Lone Wolf** only works while you have no pet out.

**Before the pull:** **Hunter's Mark**, **Aspect of the Beast**.

**Keep up all fight:** **Aspect of the Beast**.

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Raptor Strike**; no global cooldown, so press it alongside the others
2. **Hunter's Mark**: keep it on the target
3. **Mongoose Bite** when it lights up (Expose Prey on your marked target; a dodge of yours too)
4. **Strider Kick**
5. **Immolation Trap**
6. **Summon Hawk**
7. **Wing Clip**

**Out of melee:** step out to re-apply **Serpent Sting** when it falls off while **Strider Kick**'s speed is up, and shoot **Arcane Shot** and **Multi-Shot** on the way back in; never step out only to shoot.

**Wing Clip** is your filler: cast it whenever nothing above is ready.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Rapid Fire**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 1 time a fight)
- Use your trinkets and a potion together with your big cooldowns.

## Why this rotation

Beast-Survival plays the Survival melee list with no pet out: **Lone Wolf** adds 20% to everything you do while no pet is out, and the simulator counts hawks as guardians, not pets, so **Summon Hawk** keeps two hawks on the boss most of the fight without turning it off. This is the official simulator's own melee rotation (since 2026-11-20, when it beat ours by 2.2% on this build). In a 3-minute raid fight: **Wing Clip** 37 casts, **Raptor Strike** 24, **Summon Hawk** 22, **Strider Kick** 20, **Mongoose Bite** 20, **Serpent Sting** 9, **Immolation Trap** 7; your white swings 29% of the damage, the hawks 15% (swings 11%, dives 4%), **Serpent Sting** 9%, Windfury Totem's extra swings 9%, **Raptor Strike** 8%, **Mongoose Bite** 8%, **Immolation Trap** 7%, **Strider Kick** 7%.

**Why it works**

- Before the pull: **Dismiss Pet** (a 5-second cast), then **Hunter's Mark** and **Aspect of the Beast** (+110 melee attack power; with **Deadly Aspects** each melee auto attack has a 10% chance to speed your swings by 30% for 12 seconds). The simulator walks you in from 25 yards, casting **Serpent Sting**, **Arcane Shot** and **Multi-Shot** on the way. From 5 seconds in the other cooldowns go as they come ready: **Rapid Fire** (40% faster melee attacks too, for 15 seconds) once, on-use trinkets and a Major Mana Potion.
- **Raptor Strike** first: it replaces your next main-hand swing and costs no global cooldown, so queue it whenever its 6-second cooldown ends.
- **Hunter's Mark** whenever it is missing: with **Expose Prey**, each of your hits on a marked target has a 10% chance to light up **Mongoose Bite** for 10 seconds, and in a raid the boss hits your tank, so your own dodges, the bite's other trigger, rarely come. Bite at once: weapon damage plus 57 on a 5-second cooldown.
- **Strider Kick** on cooldown (full weapon damage, 8 seconds), and its 3 seconds of 30% speed are your window for **Serpent Sting**: with the sting off the boss and more than 30% mana, run out past your shots' 8-yard minimum range (the simulator goes to about 13 yards), sting, shoot **Arcane Shot** and **Multi-Shot** if they are ready, and walk back in, pausing for an **Auto Shot** when one is due.
- **Immolation Trap** whenever it is ready, set in melee at the boss: 690 Fire damage over 15 seconds, 30% more with **Clever Traps**, every 24 seconds with 1 point in **Survivalist's Discipline**.
- **Summon Hawk** next: a dive for 108 plus 5% of your ranged attack power, then a hawk that attacks for 18 seconds, two at a time, 15% harder with **Unleashed Fury**. **Endurance Training** does nothing without a pet; its 5 points only open **Unleashed Fury**'s row.
- **Wing Clip** fills every global cooldown left: its own hit is 50 damage, but each hit on your marked target is another 10% chance at **Mongoose Bite**.

**Caveats**

- Full raid: every raid buff and debuff, phase-1 gear, 3-minute fights. Windfury Totem's extra swings are 9% of your damage: you need a shaman's totem in your group. There it does 864 DPS, against 915 for the Survival page's build with a Raptor and 836 for Melee Survival with **Lone Wolf**, all on this rotation.
- Mana: no **Resourcefulness** or **Efficiency**, and each hawk costs 190 mana. On the simulator's training dummy (your own buffs only), the raid's mana buffs alone (Mana Spring Totem, Mana Tide Totem, Greater Blessing of Wisdom, Judgement of Wisdom) add 43% to its damage, and there it does 303 DPS against 364 for the Survival page's build. Without Blessing of Wisdom or Mana Spring Totem, play Survival.
- The build rests on the simulator's reading that a hawk is a guardian, not a pet: if the game counts hawks as pets, **Lone Wolf** turns off while they are out. Check it at launch.
- The class page says **Expose Prey** needs your own **Hunter's Mark**; the simulator accepts any hunter's. With several hunters, make sure the mark on the boss is yours.
- Traps in combat are the simulator's assumption, still to check in game: a trap lies where you stand and springs on the boss beside you, so the list sets it only in melee.
- Track the boss's creature type: **Improved Tracking** adds 5% only against the tracked type. Ranks: **Serpent Sting** rank 8 (rank 9 is a class book, not in Forever on the evidence there is) and **Aspect of the Beast** rank 4.

**When to change it**

- Adds around you: **Multi-Shot** on the way in hits 3 targets, and for a pack set **Explosive Trap** instead of **Immolation Trap** (everything within 10 yards). The official list never casts it, and the simulator gives all traps one cooldown, so it is one or the other.
- Pulled off the boss by a mechanic: **Summon Hawk** reaches 35 yards, so keep the hawks coming, and make **Strider Kick** your last hit before you run (30% movement speed for 3 seconds).
- Short on mana: the list already skips the **Serpent Sting** trips below 30%; let **Wing Clip** go next, about 80 damage for 80 mana in this build's full-raid numbers, the least for its mana of anything you press.
- If you pull threat, **Feign Death** (30-second cooldown).
