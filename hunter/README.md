# Hunter

> Classic's ranged physical DPS: Beast Mastery/Marksmanship in raids for Trueshot Aura and personal damage, Marksmanship/Survival
> in PvP. Forever removes Aimed Shot, Lethal Shots and Improved Hunter's Mark as talents, moves Trueshot Aura to the 16-point slot,
> adds Lone Wolf (petless), Sniper Shot, Summon Hawk, and turns Survival into a melee tree. Human hunters are new; Skyborne too.

## Forever headline changes (beta client build 1.60.1.70291, data refreshed 2026-10-08)

- **Removed:** Improved Aspect of the Hawk (now Deadly Aspects), Improved Eyes of the Beast, Thick Hide (merged into Endurance Training) (BM); Improved Hunter's Mark,
  Lethal Shots (now Lethal Attacks), Aimed Shot (now baseline), Improved Serpent Sting and Improved Scorpid Sting (now Improved Stings) (MM); Monster Slaying and Humanoid
  Slaying (now Improved Tracking), Trap Mastery (now Survival Tactics), Improved Feign Death, Killer Instinct, Wyvern Sting (Survival).
- **New:** Deadly Aspects, Focused Fire, Summon Hawk (BM); Lethal Attacks, Improved Stings, Careful Aim, Rapid Killing, Lone Wolf, Rapid Recuperation, Sniper Shot (MM);
  Improved Tracking, Survival Tactics, Predator's Edge, Resourcefulness, Expose Prey, Survivalist's Discipline, Strider Kick, Lacerating Strikes (Survival).
- **Moved and reworked:** Trueshot Aura to row 4, a party buff you cast (30 min); Sniper Shot requires it. Mortal Shots requires Careful Aim; Intimidation requires
  Bestial Swiftness and its pet hit also gains 100% crit chance; Hawk Eye moved to row 1. Deadly Aspects also speeds melee swings under Aspect of the Beast. Savage Strikes
  covers every melee ability; Surefooted's hit applies to all attacks; Deflection is 1% parry a rank (was 2%); Lightning Reflexes 2% Agility a rank. Expose Prey opens
  Mongoose Bite for 10 s (was 5). Counterattack is 50% weapon damage plus a flat amount; Resourcefulness's crit proc is 60% at 2 points (was 100% before 70291).
- **Baseline abilities:** Aimed Shot from level 20 (6 ranks, 2 s cast, +166 at 60, a 6 s cooldown shared with Multi-Shot). Multi-Shot is one rank (0.5 s cast, 3
  targets, weapon damage). Arcane Shot no longer shares a cooldown with Aimed Shot; Summon Hawk shares Arcane Shot's. Sniper Shot ranks at 40/48/58 (+160/225/295, 45 yd,
  15 s cooldown). Rapid Fire speeds up melee as well as ranged attacks (40%, 15 s). Aspect of the Beast adds melee attack power (+50/70/90/110 at 30/40/50/60); Aspect
  of the Monkey gives 8% dodge. Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite deal weapon damage plus a flat amount. Traps work in combat (30 s cooldown; fire and frost traps have
  separate cooldowns; still placed at your own feet). Volley has no cooldown. Lacerate is a new trained melee bleed (levels 30-60). Disengage's threat reduction is doubled.
- **Pets:** pets get stats from the hunter (the 70291 client: 10% of the higher attack power, 100% of the crit, 30% of the armor, 2 health per Stamina) and no
  longer take player stat buffs; every family learns Bite or Claw and Dash or Dive, plus an ability of its own; Foxes are new (Trickster's Dance); Furious Howl is 40%
  weaker. A faster pet does more damage: Forever does not scale a pet's hit by its attack speed (forum logs at levels 20 and 30; this project's model at 60).
- **Data quirks to check at launch:** the 70291 tooltips read Trueshot Aura rank 5 at +50 (rank 4: +75), Hunter's Mark rank 4 at +71 (rank 3: +98) and Aspect of the
  Hawk rank 6 at +55 (rank 5: +90). Talenting out of Lone Wolf no longer leaves the -4% it once did.
