# Hunter

> Classic's ranged physical DPS: Beast Mastery/Marksmanship in raids for Trueshot Aura and personal damage, Marksmanship/Survival
> in PvP. Forever removes Aimed Shot, Lethal Shots and Improved Hunter's Mark as talents, moves Trueshot Aura to the 16-point slot,
> adds Lone Wolf (petless), Sniper Shot, Summon Hawk, and turns Survival into a melee tree. Human hunters are new; Skyborne too.

## Roles and specs

| Spec | Raid role | Classic standing | Forever outlook |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beast Mastery | Solo / dip | 20-point dip for Improved Aspect of the Hawk + pet damage | Deadly Aspects, Focused Fire, Summon Hawk; still the dip and the farming spec |
| Marksmanship | Raid DPS | The raid spec (Trueshot Aura) | Same, plus Lone Wolf, Careful Aim, Sniper Shot |
| Survival | PvP / hit dip | Surefooted for hit; otherwise weak | Melee Survival (Strider Kick, Lacerating Strikes) — experimental |

## Forever headline changes (beta client build 1.60.1.70009, 2026-09-25)

- **Removed:** Improved Aspect of the Hawk, Improved Eyes of the Beast, Thick Hide (BM); Improved Hunter's Mark, Lethal Shots, Aimed Shot,
  Improved Serpent Sting, Improved Scorpid Sting (MM); Monster Slaying, Humanoid Slaying, Trap Mastery, Improved Feign Death, Killer Instinct, Wyvern Sting (Survival).
- **New:** Deadly Aspects, Focused Fire, Summon Hawk (BM); Lethal Attacks, Improved Stings, Careful Aim, Rapid Killing, Lone Wolf, Rapid Recuperation, Sniper Shot (MM);
  Improved Tracking, Survival Tactics, Predator's Edge, Resourcefulness, Expose Prey, Survivalist's Discipline, Strider Kick, Lacerating Strikes (Survival).
- **Moved/reworked:** Trueshot Aura → tier 4, Mortal Shots requires Careful Aim, Intimidation requires Bestial Swiftness, Hawk Eye → tier 1.
- **Beta client (build 70009):** Aimed Shot is baseline (8 ranks from 20, 2s cast, +166 at 60), Multi-Shot is one rank sharing Aimed Shot's 6s cooldown, Sniper Shot has ranks at 40/48/58 (+160/225/295), Trueshot Aura ranks at 25-60 (+30..75 RAP), Strider Kick adds 30% speed, Focused Fire also buffs the pet, Furious Howl -40%, Lone Wolf no longer leaves a -4% debuff after talenting out.
