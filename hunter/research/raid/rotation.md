Our builds and the simulator's own builds; builds that cast the same lines share a table. A line's condition reads left to right (spell names from the simulator's database). The leveling priority the fight lab plays is on the [leveling rotation page](../leveling/rotation.md). Models of the beta, not measurements. Do not hand-edit.
