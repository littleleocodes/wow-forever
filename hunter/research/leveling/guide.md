## How the fights go

The rotation in the player page's words:

Before the fight: Pet out, **Aspect of the Monkey** (**Aspect of the Beast** from level 30). **Serpent Sting** as the mob comes, then walk in behind the pet.

1. **Raptor Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health: it replaces your next swing. The tuned rules keep it for the finish; its mana buys more in the sting and the trap.
2. **Strider Kick** (a talent; Survival takes it at 54) once the mob is below 20% of its health, on its 8-second cooldown, next to **Raptor Strike**.
3. Below 40% health while the mob has more than 30% of its health: **Deterrence**.
4. **Counterattack** after you parry, **Mongoose Bite** when it lights up (after you dodge, or from **Expose Prey**).
5. **Lacerate** (from level 30) while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
6. **Immolation Trap** at your feet while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.

Between fights: Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 35% of your mana, eat below 65% of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-03, on top of the priority below): never cast Hunter's Mark; Raptor Strike only when the mob is below 20% health; Strider Kick only when the mob is below 20% health. Together +2.9% time per kill saved for `leveling-melee` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
