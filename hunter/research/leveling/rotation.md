## melee-dps

Played by the page builds of [Beast-Survival](../../leveling/beast-survival.md) ([`beast-survival-open-leveling`](builds/beast-survival-open-leveling.md)), [Survival](../../leveling/survival.md) ([`survival-open-leveling`](builds/survival-open-leveling.md)).

The melee hunter: Serpent Sting from range, then stand and fight beside the pet: Raptor Strike queued on the next swing whenever it is ready, Deterrence when low, Counterattack after a parry, Mongoose Bite when a dodge or Expose Prey activates it, Strider Kick on cooldown, Immolation Trap at your feet, then (with Expose Prey) Hunter's Mark in a free global cooldown; Mend Pet for the pet.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: Pet out, **Aspect of the Monkey** (**Aspect of the Beast** from level 30). **Serpent Sting** as the mob comes, then walk in behind the pet.

1. **Raptor Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health: it replaces your next swing. The tuned rules keep it for the finish; its mana buys more in the sting and the trap.
2. **Strider Kick** (a talent; Survival takes it at 54) once the mob is below 20% of its health, on its 8-second cooldown, next to **Raptor Strike**.
3. Below 40% health while the mob has more than 30% of its health: **Deterrence**.
4. **Counterattack** after you parry, **Mongoose Bite** when it lights up (after you dodge, or from **Expose Prey**).
5. **Lacerate** (from level 30) while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
6. **Immolation Trap** at your feet while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.

Between fights: Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 35% of your mana, eat below 65% of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-03, on top of the priority below): never cast Hunter's Mark; Raptor Strike only when the mob is below 20% health; Strider Kick only when the mob is below 20% health. Together +2.9% time per kill saved for `leveling-melee` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## mm-raid

Played by the page build of [Marksmanship](../../leveling/marksmanship.md) ([`marksmanship-open-leveling`](builds/marksmanship-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a cat: the pet runs in and meets the mob halfway, Growls every 5 s and holds it until your threat passes 130% of its own. You shoot from 35 yd: Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting, Summon Hawk or Arcane Shot, Multi-Shot / Aimed Shot on their shared cooldown, Auto Shot in between (the bow cannot fire inside 8 yd); Bestial Wrath, Intimidation and Rapid Fire on the pull when ready; Mend Pet when the pet drops below 35%; Feign Death, Wing Clip and Raptor Strike when a mob reaches you. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Mend Pet below 70% pet health, water below 35% mana, food below 65% health, a 10 s Revive Pet after a pet death; a death costs 90 s.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: Pet out (a cat), **Aspect of the Hawk** and **Trueshot Aura** up. Send the pet in first and let it hold the mob.

1. **Hunter's Mark** while the mob has more than 30% of its health, **Serpent Sting** while it has more than 40%.
2. **Rapid Fire** at the start when it is ready.
3. **Arcane Shot** only once the mob is below about a third of its health (the tuned rule); no Multi-Shot on a single mob: the fight lab levels faster keeping the mana.
4. **Aimed Shot** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than a quarter of its health.
5. **Auto Shot** does the rest. Keep your distance: the bow cannot fire within 8 yards. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.
6. If a mob reaches you while it has more than about a third of its health: **Feign Death** to send it back to the pet; when that is on cooldown, **Mongoose Bite** after a dodge, **Raptor Strike** and **Wing Clip**.

Between fights: Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 15% mana, eat below 65% of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-03, on top of the priority below): never cast Multi-Shot; Arcane Shot only when the mob is below 35% health; Feign Death only while the mob has more than 35% health; drink below 15% mana. Together +6.1% time per kill saved for `opt-mm-raid-leveling-st` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
