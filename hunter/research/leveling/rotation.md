## melee-dps

Played by the page builds of [Beast-Survival](../../leveling/beast-survival.md) ([`beast-survival-open-leveling`](builds/beast-survival-open-leveling.md)), [Survival](../../leveling/survival.md) ([`leveling-melee`](builds/leveling-melee.md)).

The melee hunter: Serpent Sting from range, then stand and fight beside the pet: Raptor Strike queued on the next swing whenever it is ready, Deterrence when low, Counterattack after a parry, Mongoose Bite when a dodge or Expose Prey activates it, Strider Kick on cooldown, Immolation Trap at your feet, then (with Expose Prey) Hunter's Mark in a free global cooldown; Mend Pet for the pet.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: Pet out, **Aspect of the Monkey** (**Aspect of the Beast** from level 30). Send the pet in and walk in behind it. No sting from range: the fight lab levels faster without it.

1. **Raptor Strike** every time it is ready: it replaces your next swing and costs no global cooldown.
2. Below 40% health while the mob has more than 30% of its health: **Deterrence**.
3. **Counterattack** after you parry, **Mongoose Bite** when it lights up (after you dodge, or from **Expose Prey**).
4. **Strider Kick** on cooldown.
5. **Lacerate** (from level 30) while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
6. **Immolation Trap** at your feet while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. With **Expose Prey**: **Hunter's Mark** in a free global cooldown while the mob has more than 30% of its health, so your hits can light up **Mongoose Bite**.
8. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.

Between fights: Mend the pet below half its health, drink below a third of your mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-18, on top of the priority below): never cast Serpent Sting. Together +2.4% time per kill saved for `leveling-melee` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## bm-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Beast-Marks](../../leveling/beast-marks.md) ([`beast-marks-open-leveling`](builds/beast-marks-open-leveling.md)), [Beast Mastery](../../leveling/beast-mastery.md) ([`beast-mastery-leveling`](builds/beast-mastery-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a cat: the pet runs in and meets the mob halfway, Growls every 5 s and holds it until your threat passes 130% of its own. You shoot from 35 yd: Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting, Summon Hawk or Arcane Shot, Multi-Shot / Aimed Shot on their shared cooldown, Auto Shot in between (the bow cannot fire inside 8 yd); Bestial Wrath, Intimidation and Rapid Fire on the pull when ready; Mend Pet when the pet drops below 35%; Feign Death, Wing Clip and Raptor Strike when a mob reaches you. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Mend Pet below 70% pet health, water below 35% mana, food below 65% health, a 10 s Revive Pet after a pet death; a death costs 90 s.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: Pet out (a cat), **Aspect of the Hawk** up. Send the pet in first and let it hold the mob.

1. **Hunter's Mark** while the mob has more than 30% of its health, **Serpent Sting** while it has more than 40%.
2. **Bestial Wrath**, **Intimidation** and **Rapid Fire** at the start when they are ready.
3. **Aimed Shot** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than a quarter of its health.
4. No other shots on a single mob: the fight lab levels faster skipping Arcane Shot, Multi-Shot and the hawk and keeping the mana.
5. **Auto Shot** does the rest. Keep your distance: the bow cannot fire within 8 yards. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.
6. If a mob reaches you: **Feign Death** to send it back to the pet; when that is on cooldown, **Mongoose Bite** after a dodge, **Raptor Strike** and **Wing Clip** until the pet takes it back.

Between fights: Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 15% mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-18, on top of the priority below): never cast Multi-Shot; never cast Arcane Shot; never cast Summon Hawk; drink below 15% mana. Together +8.2% time per kill saved for `leveling-beast-mastery` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
