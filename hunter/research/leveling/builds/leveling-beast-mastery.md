## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Summon Hawk** — New 16-pt at 25: a second attacker for 18s that Unleashed Fury/Ferocity also buff. Demo damage is low (53 at level 38), so it is valued as 'an extra pet on pulls', not a burst cooldown. Fight lab (2026-09-26): removing it costs +8% time per kill at 60, +2% at 30.
2. **Efficiency** — Fight lab (2026-09-26): removing it costs +5% time per kill at 60.
3. **Unleashed Fury** — +3%/rank pet damage (est. 15%) as soon as tier 3 opens at 20. Fight lab (2026-09-26): removing it costs +4% time per kill at 60, +5% at 30.
4. **Rapid Recuperation** — Fight lab (2026-09-26): removing it costs +4% time per kill at 60.
5. **Improved Stings** — Fight lab (2026-09-26): removing it costs +2% time per kill at 60.
6. **Lethal Attacks** — Fight lab (2026-09-26): removing it costs +2% time per kill at 60, +1% at 30.
7. **Focused Fire** — New: +1%/rank all your damage while the pet is out (est. 2%). Tier 2, but taken at 33-34 — earlier rows are always in play when the current row has nothing better. Fight lab (2026-09-26): removing it costs +2% time per kill at 60, +1% at 30.
8. **Endurance Training** — +3%/rank pet health and armor (est. 15%/15%) — the pet is the tank; Classic took it 5/5 at 15-19 and the Forever version is stronger. Fight lab (2026-09-26): removing it costs +1% time per kill at 60, +0% at 30.
9. **Ferocity** — +10% crit for the pet and the hawk at 5/5 (3 ranks here): the pet does a large share of the leveling damage.

## Leveling rotation (fight lab)

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a cat: the pet runs in and meets the mob halfway, Growls every 5 s and holds it until your threat passes 130% of its own. You shoot from 35 yd: Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting, Summon Hawk or Arcane Shot, Multi-Shot / Aimed Shot on their shared cooldown, Auto Shot in between (the bow cannot fire inside 8 yd); Bestial Wrath, Intimidation and Rapid Fire on the pull when ready; Mend Pet when the pet drops below 35%; Feign Death, Wing Clip and Raptor Strike when a mob reaches you. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Mend Pet below 70% pet health, water below 35% mana, food below 65% health, a 10 s Revive Pet after a pet death; a death costs 90 s.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-09-28, on top of the priority below): never cast Multi-Shot; never cast Arcane Shot; drink below 15% mana; never cast Feign Death. Together +6.0% time per kill saved for `leveling-beast-mastery` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## Notes

- Adopted from the fight-lab race on 2026-09-26: cand-bm-r2 beat the previous draft by +3.8% (level-weighted time per kill); the rationale below the fight-lab note predates the adoption where it names talents no longer taken.
- Loop: pet in, Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting, Auto Shot (+ hawk from 25); Feign Death if the pet loses the mob; Mend Pet between pulls.
- Lone Wolf (+20% damage with no pet) is not a leveling choice — the pet is the leveling engine.
- 2026-09-27 scenario audit: Improved Revive Pet 2, Bestial Swiftness 1 -> Ferocity 3. Fight lab race (1500 fights per level): equal at 20, 0.3-0.5% less time per kill at 30-50, 1.5% less at 60; Pathfinding moves to 18-19 to keep Unleashed Fury at 20.
