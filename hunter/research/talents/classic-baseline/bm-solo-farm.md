## Summary

Bestial Wrath, Frenzy, Ferocity and Unleashed Fury make the pet a monster: Maraudon/DM farming, leveling and pre-raid dungeons. Icy Veins notes it out-damages the raid spec before raid gear, but lacks Trueshot Aura.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Bestial Wrath** — +50% pet damage and CC immunity for 18s.
2. **Frenzy** — Pet haste after crits (with Ferocity, near-permanent).
3. **Ferocity** — +10% pet crit.
4. **Unleashed Fury** — +20% pet damage.
5. **Intimidation** — Pet stun — solo utility and threat.
6. **Improved Aspect of the Hawk** — Ranged haste.
7. **Endurance Training** — +15% pet health.
8. **Mortal Shots** — Your own crit damage.
