## Summary

Trueshot Aura for the melee, Lethal/Mortal Shots and Ranged Weapon Specialization for your own damage, Aimed Shot and Improved Hunter's Mark, plus Improved Aspect of the Hawk (ranged haste procs), Unleashed Fury and Ferocity so the pet is real damage. The Icy Veins/Wowhead standard raid build.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Trueshot Aura** — +100 attack power to the whole group — why raids bring hunters.
2. **Improved Aspect of the Hawk** — 10% chance per auto-shot for +30% ranged haste for 12s: the best personal DPS talent.
3. **Lethal Shots** — +5% ranged crit.
4. **Mortal Shots** — +30% ranged crit damage.
5. **Ranged Weapon Specialization** — +5% ranged damage.
6. **Aimed Shot** — Big shot for the rotation (weave between Auto Shots).
7. **Efficiency** — -10% mana on shots and stings.
8. **Unleashed Fury** — +20% pet damage.
9. **Ferocity** — +10% pet crit.
10. **Improved Hunter's Mark** — +attack power on the target for all physical DPS; only one hunter per raid needs it.
11. **Hawk Eye** — +6 yards range — safer positioning.
12. **Barrage** — +15% Multi-Shot/Volley; prerequisite for Trueshot Aura.

## Notes

- Rotation: Auto Shot with Aimed Shot / Multi-Shot woven between shots (shot-timer addon), Serpent Sting only if the debuff slot is free.
- Ranged hit cap vs. level 63 bosses is 9%; gear it before taking the Surefooted variant.
