## How to play

**Before the fight:** Pet out (a cat), **Aspect of the Hawk** and **Trueshot Aura** up. Send the pet in first and let it hold the mob.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hunter's Mark** while the mob has more than 30% of its health, **Serpent Sting** while it has more than 40%.
2. **Rapid Fire** at the start when it is ready.
3. **Arcane Shot** only once the mob is below about a third of its health (the tuned rule); no Multi-Shot on a single mob: the fight lab levels faster keeping the mana.
4. **Aimed Shot** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than a quarter of its health.
5. **Auto Shot** does the rest. Keep your distance: the bow cannot fire within 8 yards. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.
6. If a mob reaches you while it has more than about a third of its health: **Feign Death** to send it back to the pet; when that is on cooldown, **Mongoose Bite** after a dodge, **Raptor Strike** and **Wing Clip**.

**Between fights:** Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 15% mana, eat below 65% of your health.

## Why this rotation

Marksmanship levels with the pet tanking, but more of the damage is your own: **Aimed Shot** on its cooldown, **Serpent Sting** on every mob, **Arcane Shot** to finish once the mob is below 35% of its health, **Trueshot Aura** always on and **Auto Shot** in between. The build spends its first 30 points on Marksmanship (**Lethal Attacks**, **Careful Aim**, **Efficiency**) and the other 21 on the pet.

**Why it works**

- Send the pet first, then **Hunter's Mark** and **Serpent Sting** while the mob is healthy (the fight lab: above 30% and 40% of its health). The cat holds the mob until your threat passes 130% of its own.
- **Aimed Shot** gets the cooldown it shares with **Multi-Shot**, while the mob has more than a quarter of its health. The tuned rules (2026-11-03) never cast **Multi-Shot** on a single mob, and cast **Arcane Shot** only once the mob is below 35% of its health: earlier, its mana did less than the next **Aimed Shot** and **Serpent Sting**. In the band between a quarter and 35% of the mob's health both are allowed, and there the fight lab fires **Arcane Shot** before **Aimed Shot**, as the list above puts it.
- **Lethal Attacks** and **Careful Aim** (levels 10-19) first, **Improved Stings** (20-22), then **Rapid Killing** (23-24), which cuts **Rapid Fire** to 3 minutes. **Trueshot Aura** (25) is a buff you cast in Forever: keep it on. **Efficiency** (26-29 and 31) is the build's most valuable talent at 60: about a second a kill in the fight lab's removal runs, ahead of **Unleashed Fury** (50-54, 0.9) and **Lethal Attacks** (0.6).
- From level 40 the points go to the pet: **Deadly Aspects**, **Focused Fire**, **Endurance Training**, then **Unleashed Fury** and **Ferocity** (50-59) and **Bestial Discipline** (60).
- Your shots and stings are 72% of the damage at 60 in the fight lab, the cat most of the rest. **Auto Shot** cannot fire within 8 yards: step back out rather than melee while the pet holds the mob.
- Drink below 15% mana rather than at 35%: it measured slightly faster (under 1% per kill). **Feign Death** only while the mob has more than 35% of its health; below that, finish it.

**Caveats**

- **Rapid Recuperation** (30, one point; it needs both points of **Rapid Killing**): **Serpent Sting** lets a quarter of your mana regeneration continue while casting for 15 seconds, half after you spend a **Rapid Killing**. It measured about 0.4 seconds a kill at 60. **Barrage** (32-34) adds 15% to **Aimed Shot**, **Multi-Shot** and **Volley**: little on one mob (0.16 seconds), more on several.
- **Rapid Killing** also makes your next Shot within 20 seconds of a kill hit 20% harder; the fight lab leaves that half out, so let the next pull's first **Aimed Shot** spend it. Without the bonus the talent measured almost nothing: its points open **Rapid Recuperation**.
- **Endurance Training** and **Improved Aspect of the Monkey** (49) measured nothing at 60 in the fight lab: they fill tiers. **Ranged Weapon Specialization** (35-39) is 5% more damage with your bow.
- A mob that turns on you while it still has more than 35% of its health: **Feign Death** sends it back to the pet; while it is on cooldown, **Mongoose Bite** after a dodge, **Raptor Strike** and **Wing Clip**.
- Buy the new ranks: in the beta client (build 70753) the top ranks are the strongest. **Aspect of the Hawk** rank 7 gives +120 ranged attack power (rank 6 +110), **Hunter's Mark** rank 4 +143 (rank 3 +98) and **Trueshot Aura** rank 5 +100 (rank 4 +75). Cast the highest rank you know; the fight lab does.
- **Auto Shot** keeps firing while you cast **Aimed Shot**, but after you step back it waits until you have stood still for half a second (Blizzard's 16 Oct notes), so don't shuffle between shots.

**When to change it**

- Several mobs: **Multi-Shot** and **Volley** (no cooldown in Forever) instead of single-target shots.
- A mob on you while the pet is down: slow it with **Wing Clip** or **Concussive Shot**, step back past 8 yards and shoot.
