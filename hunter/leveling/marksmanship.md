## How to play

**Before the fight:** Pet out (a cat), **Aspect of the Hawk** and **Trueshot Aura** up. Send the pet in first and let it hold the mob.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hunter's Mark** and **Serpent Sting** while the mob has most of its health.
2. **Rapid Fire** at the start when it is ready.
3. **Aimed Shot** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than a quarter of its health; on a single mob never **Multi-Shot** (they share a cooldown).
4. **Arcane Shot** between them while the mob has more than a third of its health.
5. **Auto Shot** does the rest. Keep your distance: the bow cannot fire within 8 yards. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.
6. If a mob reaches you: **Wing Clip** and **Raptor Strike**, or **Feign Death** while it still has more than a third of its health.

**Between fights:** Mend the pet, drink below a third of your mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

Marksmanship levels with the pet tanking, but more of the damage is your own: **Aimed Shot** and **Arcane Shot** on their cooldowns, **Serpent Sting** on every mob and **Trueshot Aura** always on.

**Why it works**

- Send the pet first, then **Hunter's Mark** and **Serpent Sting** while the mob is healthy (the fight lab: above 30% and 40% of its health). The cat holds the mob until your threat passes 130% of its own.
- **Aimed Shot** gets the cooldown it shares with **Multi-Shot**: on one mob the fight lab never casts **Multi-Shot**, which measured about 2% faster kills.
- **Arcane Shot** goes between **Aimed Shot** casts while the mob has more than 35% of its health; below that the fight lab saves the mana for the next pull, about 0.7% faster per kill.
- **Trueshot Aura** (level 27) is a buff you cast in Forever, not a passive aura: keep it on. **Rapid Fire** at the start of a fight when ready; **Rapid Killing** (levels 25-26) cuts its cooldown to 3 minutes.
- **Auto Shot** is about a third of your damage at 60 and cannot fire within 8 yards: step back out rather than melee while the pet holds the mob.

**Caveats**

- **Sniper Shot** (level 42) and **Scatter Shot** (32) are not in the list: the fight lab does not cast them, so their worth while leveling is unmeasured. A 4-second **Sniper Shot** is long next to fights of 20 to 30 seconds.
- **Rapid Killing** also makes your next Shot within 20 seconds of a kill hit 20% harder, and with 2 points in **Rapid Recuperation** spending it lets all your mana regeneration continue while casting for 15 seconds. The fight lab leaves this out: let the next pull's first **Aimed Shot** spend it.
- **Feign Death** only while the mob still has more than 35% of its health (measured about 0.7% faster); a nearly dead mob that reaches you is quicker to finish with **Raptor Strike** and **Wing Clip**.
- From level 45, **Improved Tracking** adds damage only against the creature type you are tracking: switch tracking to what you are killing.

**When to change it**

- Several mobs: **Multi-Shot** and **Volley** (no cooldown in Forever) instead of single-target shots.
- A mob on you while the pet is down: **Scatter Shot** disorients it for 4 seconds (any damage breaks it), so step back past 8 yards and shoot.

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-09; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
