## How to play

**Before the fight:** Pet out (a cat), **Aspect of the Hawk** up. Send the pet in first and let it hold the mob.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Hunter's Mark** while the mob has more than 30% of its health, **Serpent Sting** while it has more than 40%.
2. **Aimed Shot** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 35% of its health.
3. No other shots on a single mob: the fight lab levels faster skipping **Arcane Shot** and **Multi-Shot** and keeping the mana.
4. **Auto Shot** does the rest. Keep your distance: the bow cannot fire within 8 yards. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.
5. If a mob reaches you: **Feign Death** to send it back to the pet; when that is on cooldown, **Mongoose Bite** after a dodge, **Raptor Strike** and **Wing Clip** until the pet takes it back.

**Between fights:** Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 15% of your mana, eat below 65% of your health. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

## Why this rotation

Beast-Marks levels like Beast Mastery without its two cooldowns: the cat tanks and you shoot from range. The split takes Marksmanship first (levels 10-30 and 32: **Lethal Attacks**, **Efficiency**, **Improved Stings**, **Careful Aim**, **Trueshot Aura**, **Rapid Killing** and **Rapid Recuperation**), **Improved Tracking** at 31, 33-35 and 60, and the pet talents (36-59: **Deadly Aspects**, **Focused Fire**, **Unleashed Fury**, **Ferocity**, **Bestial Discipline**).

**Why it works**

- Send the pet first, then **Hunter's Mark** and **Serpent Sting** while the mob is still healthy (the fight lab: above 30% and 40% of its health). The cat holds the mob until your threat passes 130% of its own. In the fight lab's removal runs at 60 (seconds a kill costs without the talent), **Unleashed Fury** (46-50) is worth about 1.0 second, **Efficiency** (15-19) 0.75 and **Ferocity** (51-55) 0.7: the build's most valuable talents, ahead of **Improved Stings** (20-22) and **Lethal Attacks** (10-14), about 0.5 each.
- **Aimed Shot** (from level 20) is your only cooldown shot on a single mob, cast while the mob has more than 35% of its health: below that, **Auto Shot** and the pet finish the mob, which the tuned rules (2026-11-15) measured slightly faster. The rules never cast **Multi-Shot** (it shares the cooldown) or **Arcane Shot**: their mana is worth more as fewer drinks between pulls. This build has no **Summon Hawk** point anyway.
- **Rapid Fire** at the start of a fight when it is ready: **Rapid Killing** (27 and 29) cuts its cooldown from 5 minutes to 3 and opens **Rapid Recuperation** (30, one point). After **Serpent Sting**, that lets a quarter of your mana regeneration continue while casting for 15 seconds (half after you spend a **Rapid Killing**). Until **Bestial Discipline** (57, one point), which gives the quarter all the time, it is the build's mana in a fight; at 60 the two overlap, and the removal runs measure **Rapid Recuperation** at nothing and **Bestial Discipline** at about 0.2 seconds.
- **Auto Shot** with **Aimed Shot** and the sting is most of your own damage, the cat the rest, and **Auto Shot** cannot fire within 8 yards: step back out if the mob ends up beside you. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below about a third.
- **Trueshot Aura** (level 25) is a buff you cast in Forever: keep it on.

**Caveats**

- This build does not take **Bestial Wrath**, nor **Intimidation**, so the list leaves out the step that names them, and **Rapid Fire** with it: still press **Rapid Fire** at the start of a fight when it is ready.
- **Endurance Training** (43-44), **Improved Revive Pet** (45) and **Spirit Bond** (59) measured nothing at 60 in the fight lab, where the pet rarely dies. The leveling filler passes moved the other points that measured nothing into **Mortal Shots** (58), **Improved Tracking** (31 and 60) and **Bestial Discipline**'s second point (56, Bestial Swiftness until 2026-11-22). **Rapid Killing** measured almost nothing: its shot bonus after a kill is not in the fight lab.
- **Improved Tracking** (levels 33-35, 3 points) adds 1% a point, and only against the creature type you are tracking: switch tracking to what you are killing.
- The lab drinks below 15% of your mana (the tuned rules of 2026-11-15: fewer stops measured slightly faster) and eats below 65% of your health. Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17). A mob that turns on you: **Feign Death** sends it back to the pet; while it is on cooldown, **Mongoose Bite**, **Raptor Strike** and **Wing Clip** hold it until the pet's Growl takes it back.
- Buy the new ranks: in the beta client (build 70753) the top ranks are the strongest. **Aspect of the Hawk** rank 7 gives +120 ranged attack power (rank 6 +110), **Hunter's Mark** rank 4 +143 (rank 3 +98) and **Trueshot Aura** rank 5 +100 (rank 4 +75). Cast the highest rank you know; the fight lab does.
- **Auto Shot** keeps firing while you cast **Aimed Shot**, but after you step back it waits until you have stood still for half a second (Blizzard's 16 Oct notes), so don't shuffle between shots.

**When to change it**

- Several mobs: **Multi-Shot** and **Volley** (no cooldown in Forever) instead of single-target shots.
- If the pet dies, finish the mob in melee or **Feign Death**, and revive it before the next pull. When a mob is nearly dead, stop spending mana and let **Auto Shot** and the pet finish it.
