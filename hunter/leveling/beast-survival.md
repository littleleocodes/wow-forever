## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Counterattack**, **Deterrence**, **Strider Kick** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** Pet out, **Aspect of the Monkey** (**Aspect of the Beast** from level 30). Send the pet in and walk in behind it. No sting from range: the fight lab levels faster without it.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Raptor Strike** every time it is ready: it replaces your next swing and costs no global cooldown.
2. Below 40% health while the mob has more than 30% of its health: **Deterrence**.
3. **Counterattack** after you parry, **Mongoose Bite** when it lights up (after you dodge, or from **Expose Prey**).
4. **Strider Kick** on cooldown.
5. **Lacerate** (from level 30) while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
6. **Immolation Trap** at your feet while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. With **Expose Prey**: **Hunter's Mark** in a free global cooldown while the mob has more than 30% of its health, so your hits can light up **Mongoose Bite**.
8. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.

**Between fights:** Mend the pet below half its health, drink below a third of your mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

Beast-Survival levels on **Immolation Trap** and a strong pet: the cat tanks and hits hard, you walk in behind it, drop a trap at your feet and fight beside it with **Raptor Strike** and your white swings.

**Why it works**

- **Immolation Trap** is the heart of the build: **Clever Traps** (levels 23-24) adds 30%, **Survival Tactics** (29) helps it hit, and **Survivalist's Discipline** (32-33) brings it back every 18 seconds. In the fight lab's removal runs at 60, **Resourcefulness** (60% cheaper traps and strikes, about 3.5 seconds a kill) and **Survivalist's Discipline** (about 2 seconds) were the build's most valuable talents.
- Set the trap at your feet beside the mob (traps work in combat in Forever but land where you stand), and only while the mob has more than 40% of its health: the burn lasts 15 seconds.
- **Raptor Strike** on every swing it is ready (it replaces your next main-hand swing and costs no global cooldown); **Mongoose Bite** after you dodge. **Lacerate** (from level 30) only while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
- From level 34 the points go to **Lethal Attacks** and then Beast Mastery: **Deadly Aspects** for your swings, then the pet: **Focused Fire**, **Endurance Training** for a sturdier tank, **Unleashed Fury** and **Ferocity** for damage. Send it first and let it hold the mob.
- No **Serpent Sting** and no **Hunter's Mark**: the tuned rules never cast the sting, and without **Expose Prey** the mark adds nothing to your melee. **Aspect of the Monkey** until 30, then **Aspect of the Beast** for melee attack power.

**Caveats**

- This build takes none of **Strider Kick**, **Counterattack**, **Deterrence** or **Expose Prey**: skip those steps. Between traps your global cooldown is mostly free, and white swings and the pet do most of the damage.
- **Improved Aspect of the Monkey** (levels 51-53) only fills the row to **Ferocity**: after level 30 you use **Aspect of the Beast**, so it adds nothing in the fight lab.
- **Improved Tracking** (levels 10-14) adds damage only against the creature type you are tracking: keep tracking set to what you are killing.
- Watch your ranks at the top levels: in the beta client (build 70425) **Trueshot Aura** rank 5 gives +50 attack power against rank 4's +75, **Aspect of the Hawk** rank 6 +55 against rank 5's +90 and **Hunter's Mark** rank 4 +71 against rank 3's +98. Keep casting the stronger lower rank from your spellbook (the fight lab does) until Blizzard fixes the numbers.

**When to change it**

- Two or more mobs: **Explosive Trap** instead of **Immolation Trap**; it hits everything within 10 yards and shares the fire-trap cooldown.
- Low on health with no **Deterrence**: **Mend Pet** keeps your tank up, and **Feign Death** drops the mob back on the pet if it turns on you.

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-22; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
