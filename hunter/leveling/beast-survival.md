## How to play

**Before the fight:** Pet out, **Aspect of the Monkey** (**Aspect of the Beast** from level 30). **Serpent Sting** as the mob comes, then walk in behind the pet.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Raptor Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health: it replaces your next swing. The tuned rules keep it for the finish; its mana buys more in the sting and the trap.
2. **Lacerate** (from level 30) while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
3. **Immolation Trap** at your feet while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
4. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.

**Between fights:** Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 35% of your mana, eat below 65% of your health. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

## Why this rotation

Beast-Survival levels on **Immolation Trap** and a strong pet: the cat tanks and hits hard, you walk in behind it, drop a trap at your feet and fight beside it with **Raptor Strike** and your white swings. Survival comes first (levels 10-34), then **Lethal Attacks** (35-37) and the pet (38-60).

**Why it works**

- **Immolation Trap** is the heart of the build: **Clever Traps** (levels 23-24) adds to it, **Survival Tactics** (29) helps it hit, and **Survivalist's Discipline** (33-34) brings it back every 18 seconds. In the fight lab's removal runs at 60 (seconds a kill costs without the talent), **Resourcefulness** (levels 30 and 32: cheaper traps and strikes) is worth about 2.0 seconds and **Survivalist's Discipline** 1.1, the build's most valuable talents; **Bestial Discipline** and **Unleashed Fury**, about 0.6 each, and **Clever Traps**, 0.55, come next.
- Set the trap at your feet beside the mob (traps work in combat in Forever but land where you stand), only while the mob has more than 40% of its health (the burn lasts 15 seconds).
- **Serpent Sting** as the mob runs in, from range (more than 8 yards away, mob above 40% of its health), then walk in behind the pet. **Raptor Strike** only once the mob is below 20% of its health: the tuned rules (2026-11-03) hold it for the finish (it replaces your next main-hand swing and costs no global cooldown). **Mongoose Bite** lights up only after you dodge, which is rare while the pet holds the mob: the fight lab casts it almost never (none at 60). **Lacerate** (from level 30) only while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
- From level 35 the points go to **Lethal Attacks** (35-37, after a first point at 31) and then Beast Mastery: **Deadly Aspects** (38-42) for your swings, then the pet: **Focused Fire** (43-44), **Endurance Training** (45-47), **Unleashed Fury** (48-52), **Ferocity** (53-57) and **Bestial Discipline** (58-59); the fifth **Predator's Edge** point comes at 60. The cat's swings and Claw are a large part of the damage at 60: send it first and let it hold the mob.
- The tuned rules never cast **Hunter's Mark**, so the build takes no **Expose Prey**, which lights up **Mongoose Bite** only from hits on a marked mob: the leveling filler pass of 2026-11-15 moved that point to **Lethal Attacks** (31), 0.4% faster per kill. **Aspect of the Monkey** until level 30, then **Aspect of the Beast**. Between fights the lab drinks below 35% of your mana and eats below 65% of your health. Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).

**Caveats**

- This build takes no **Strider Kick**, nor **Counterattack**, nor **Deterrence**: the list above leaves their lines out. Between traps your global cooldown is mostly free, and white swings and the pet do most of the damage.
- The list is shared with Survival: the tuned rules hold **Raptor Strike** (this build lacks **Strider Kick**) for the last 20% of the mob's health. The project counts a melee hunter's damage as swings, strikes, bleeds and the pet (at least half at 60; this build reaches 60%).
- **Savage Strikes** (15-16), **Entrapment** (17-18) and **Deflection** (19) measured almost nothing at 60 in the fight lab: they fill the tree's first rows (**Entrapment** took Survivalist's two points on 2026-11-22).
- **Improved Tracking** (levels 10-14) adds 1% a point, and only against the creature type you are tracking: switch tracking to what you are killing.
- Buy the new ranks: in the beta client (build 70753) the top ranks are the strongest. **Aspect of the Beast** rank 4 gives +110 melee attack power at 60 (rank 3 +90); the other ranks of the spells you press only add damage. Cast the highest rank you know; the fight lab does.

**When to change it**

- Two or more mobs: **Explosive Trap** instead of **Immolation Trap**; it hits everything within 10 yards and shares the fire-trap cooldown.
- Low on health: **Mend Pet** keeps your tank up, and **Feign Death** drops the mob back on the pet if it turns on you.
