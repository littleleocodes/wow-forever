# Beast Mastery Hunter — leveling, level by level

[Hunter](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Beast Mastery](beast-mastery.md) > Level by level

What a Beast Mastery hunter presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. The cat is your tank and a large part of your damage: it holds the mob while you shoot from range with **Auto Shot**, **Serpent Sting** and, from 20, **Aimed Shot**. **Bestial Wrath** at 40 is the build's signature. Your job is to keep the mob on the pet and to spend mana only where it shortens the fight. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 31/16/4 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/hunter/60/5320001505011251-0053520001-4-12)), the one on the [Beast Mastery leveling page](beast-mastery.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | Send the pet, then shoot from range: **Auto Shot** fires on its own. |
| 4-5 | **Serpent Sting** on every mob: a poison that ticks for 15 seconds. |
| 6-9 | **Hunter's Mark** first: your shots hit harder on the marked mob. |
| 10-11 | **Tame Beast** and **Aspect of the Hawk**. Talent points start with **Deadly Aspects**. |
| 12-19 | **Mend Pet** keeps your tank alive. **Wing Clip** for a mob that gets to you. |
| 20-25 | **Aimed Shot** whenever it is ready: your one cooldown shot. |
| 26-29 | **Rapid Fire** at the pull: 40% faster shots for 15 seconds. |
| 30-33 | **Feign Death** sends a mob that reaches you back to the pet. |
| 34-39 | **Intimidation** at the pull: the pet's next hit stuns the mob and crits. |
| 40-60 | **Bestial Wrath** at the pull: the pet does 50% more damage for 18 seconds. The rotation is complete. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; aspects, Trueshot Aura, tracking, pet feeding and reviving, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. Auto Shot has no key either: the pull macro on Mouse 4 starts it, and it fires on its own. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the rarer tools. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [hunter keybinds page](keybinds.md); its tables are the melee hunter's, so take the spells from the tables below.

- The keys of the [Beast-Survival guide](beast-survival-by-level.md) where the spells are the same: **Wing Clip** E, **Feign Death** F, **Mongoose Bite** Mouse 5, the **Serpent Sting** macro Mouse 4, **Freezing Trap** 2, **Mend Pet** 3 and the Shift bar's shots and traps. Changed for a ranged hunter: **Aimed Shot**, the shot you press most, takes Q; **Raptor Strike**, only for a mob that reaches you, moves from Q to 4 (Auto Shot needs no key here); **Hunter's Mark** takes 1 (no **Lacerate** at range); **Bestial Wrath** takes R (no **Immolation Trap**: the trap belongs to the melee hunter); **Intimidation** takes the middle mouse button; **Rapid Fire** on C; **Multi-Shot** and **Volley** on Shift+2 and Shift+3.
- Nothing on bar 1 ever swaps: every key gets its spell once and keeps it. The Mouse 4 macro gains its **Serpent Sting** line at 4. Q waits for **Aimed Shot** at 20: before that, the pull keys and **Auto Shot** do the work.
- Mouse 4 and 1 are macros that also send the pet, so the first press of a pull sends it, whichever key that is. Mouse 4 is the Sting and send macro of the [macro page](../macros.md): `#showtooltip Serpent Sting`, `/petattack`, `/cast !Auto Shot`, `/cast Serpent Sting` (at levels 1-3 leave out the sting line). 1: `#showtooltip Hunter's Mark`, `/petattack`, `/cast Hunter's Mark`. The `!` keeps a second press from turning **Auto Shot** off. Pet commands and **Auto Shot** are off the global cooldown, so one press does all of it.
- The Everything at the pull macro of the [macro page](../macros.md) casts **Bestial Wrath**, **Rapid Fire** and **Intimidation** in one press (the first two are off the global cooldown) and frees C and the middle mouse button.
- The pet bar keeps its own keys. Keep the pet's Growl on: it is your tank.
- No key: **Aspect of the Hawk**, **Trueshot Aura** (56), the tracking, **Call Pet**, **Feed Pet**, **Revive Pet**, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone. Click them between fights.
- **Arcane Shot** on Shift+Mouse 5 is not in the single-mob rotation: it is there for a mob that runs. The Shift bar is for packs and trouble: **Multi-Shot** shares **Aimed Shot**'s cooldown, so the single-mob rotation never casts it; **Volley** (40) is a channel for a pack.

## Levels 1-3: Auto Shot and the pet

**The trainer teaches:** **Auto Shot** (1), Bite (1), Charge (1), Claw (1), Growl (1), Lightning Breath (1), Raptor Strike (1), Track Beasts (1).

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Pet and Auto Shot (macro); 4 = Raptor Strike.

**Your keys in this stage:** 4 Raptor Strike.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the pet at the mob and let it take the mob.
2. Start **Auto Shot** from range. It fires on its own until the mob is dead.
3. Keep your distance: the bow cannot fire within 8 yards. If the mob ends up next to you while the pet holds it, step back.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: **Raptor Strike** until the pet takes it back.

- The fight lab gives the hunter a pet from level 1. In Classic, **Tame Beast** comes at 10, so levels 1-9 read faster here than they will play. Without a pet, shoot as the mob comes and finish it with **Raptor Strike** and your weapon.
- **Auto Shot** keeps firing while you cast, but after you move it waits until you have stood still for half a second (Blizzard's notes of 2026-10-16): step back once, not in small shuffles.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 10 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Auto Shot 1.3, Claw 1.1, Growl 1.0, Bite 0.8.*

## Levels 4-5: Serpent Sting

**The trainer teaches:** Aspect of the Monkey (4), **Serpent Sting** (4), Cower (5).

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Serpent Sting, macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** Mouse 4 Serpent Sting, 4 Raptor Strike.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the pet in.
2. **Serpent Sting** while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
3. **Auto Shot** does the rest from range.

**If the fight goes wrong:** A mob on you: **Raptor Strike** until the pet takes it back.

- **Aspect of the Monkey** (4) is the melee hunter's aspect. The fight lab's ranged hunter fights without an aspect until **Aspect of the Hawk** at 10.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 12 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Claw 2.7, Auto Shot 2.2, Serpent Sting 1.0, Growl 1.0, Bite 0.9.*

2. **Hunter's Mark** while the mob has more than 30% of its health.

## Between fights

- Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 35% of your mana, eat below 65% of your health. Feed the pet when it sulks, and revive it before the next pull if it died.
- Keep **Aspect of the Hawk** on, and **Trueshot Aura** from 56. From 57, keep the right tracking on: **Improved Tracking** only pays for the mobs you track.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Serpent Sting**, **Aimed Shot** and **Mend Pet** have many ranks.
