**Signature spells:** 50% of the damage at level 60 in the fight lab comes from melee, Raptor Strike, Mongoose Bite, Lacerate, Wing Clip (the spec's rule: at least 50%).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** Pet out, **Aspect of the Monkey** (**Aspect of the Beast** from level 30). Send the pet in and walk in behind it. No sting from range: the fight lab levels faster without it.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Raptor Strike** every time it is ready: it replaces your next swing and costs no global cooldown.
2. Below 40% health while the mob has more than 30% of its health: **Deterrence**.
3. **Counterattack** after you parry, **Mongoose Bite** when it lights up (after you dodge, or from **Expose Prey**).
4. **Strider Kick** on cooldown.
5. **Lacerate** (from level 30) while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
6. **Immolation Trap** at your feet while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. With **Expose Prey**: **Hunter's Mark** in a free global cooldown while the mob has more than 30% of its health, so your hits can light up **Mongoose Bite**.
8. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.

**Between fights:** Mend the pet below half its health, drink below a third of your mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

Melee Survival levels with the pet as the tank: you walk in behind it and fight beside it with two one-handers. **Raptor Strike** and **Strider Kick** are your steady hits, **Immolation Trap** burns the longer fights, and white swings do the rest.

**Why it works**

- No **Serpent Sting** from range: the fight lab's tuned rules never cast it, about 2.5% faster per kill. Walk in while the pet takes the mob.
- **Raptor Strike** whenever it is ready: it replaces your next main-hand swing and costs no global cooldown, so queue it first; the fight lab lost about 1% per kill when it waited behind other buttons. **Strider Kick** (level 30) on its 8-second cooldown.
- **Immolation Trap** goes at your feet beside the mob: traps work in combat in Forever but land where you stand. Only while the mob has more than 40% of its health, because the burn lasts 15 seconds.
- **Lacerate** (trained from level 30) only while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more: cast freely its mana cost more time than its bleed saved. It is about 2% of your damage at 60.
- **Hunter's Mark** matters to a melee hunter only through **Expose Prey** (levels 31-32): from then on mark the mob in a free global cooldown, and each of your hits on it has a chance to light up **Mongoose Bite**. **Counterattack** (level 57) lights up after you parry.
- **Aspect of the Monkey** until level 30 (its dodges also light up **Mongoose Bite**), then **Aspect of the Beast**: melee attack power, and with **Deadly Aspects** (levels 49-53) a chance per swing for 30% faster swings for 12 seconds.

**Caveats**

- The pet is still your tank and about a fifth of your damage: **Mend Pet** below about a third in the fight and below half between fights, and let it build threat before you hit hard.
- **Deterrence** (level 23) when you fall below 40% health while the mob has more than 30% of its health: +25% dodge and parry for 10 seconds. With the pet tanking, the fight lab almost never needs it on one mob.
- **Improved Tracking** (levels 10-14) adds damage only against the creature type you are tracking: switch tracking to what you are killing.
- Watch your ranks at the top levels: in the beta client (build 70425) **Trueshot Aura** rank 5 gives +50 attack power against rank 4's +75, **Aspect of the Hawk** rank 6 +55 against rank 5's +90 and **Hunter's Mark** rank 4 +71 against rank 3's +98. Keep casting the stronger lower rank from your spellbook (the fight lab does) until Blizzard fixes the numbers.

**When to change it**

- Two or more mobs: **Explosive Trap** instead of **Immolation Trap**; it hits everything within 10 yards and shares the fire-trap cooldown.
- If the pet dies mid-fight, **Deterrence** and finish the mob, or **Feign Death** if it is going badly; revive the pet before the next pull.
