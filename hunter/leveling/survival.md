## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Counterattack**, **Deterrence** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** Pet out, **Aspect of the Monkey** (**Aspect of the Beast** from level 30). **Serpent Sting** as the mob comes, then walk in behind the pet.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Raptor Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health: it replaces your next swing. The tuned rules keep it for the finish; its mana buys more as the sting and the trap.
2. Below 40% health while the mob has more than 30% of its health: **Deterrence**.
3. **Counterattack** after you parry, **Mongoose Bite** when it lights up (after you dodge, or from **Expose Prey**).
4. **Lacerate** (from level 30) while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more.
5. **Immolation Trap** at your feet while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
6. **Mend Pet** when the pet drops below a third of its health.

**Between fights:** Mend the pet below half its health, drink below about a third of your mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

Melee Survival levels with the pet as the tank: you walk in behind it and fight beside it with two one-handers. **Raptor Strike** is your steady hit, **Mongoose Bite** and **Counterattack** come when they light up, **Immolation Trap** burns the longer fights, and white swings do the rest.

**Why it works**

- No **Serpent Sting** from range: the fight lab's tuned rules (2026-10-24) never cast it, and it was faster without it (about 2.5% per kill). Walk in while the pet takes the mob.
- **Raptor Strike** whenever it is ready: it replaces your next main-hand swing and costs no global cooldown, so queue it first; the fight lab lost about 1% per kill when it waited behind other buttons.
- **Immolation Trap** goes at your feet beside the mob (traps work in combat in Forever but land where you stand), while the mob has more than 40% of its health (the burn lasts 15 seconds) and you have half your mana or more.
- **Lacerate** (trained from level 30) only while the mob has more than half its health and you have 80% of your mana or more: cast freely its mana cost more time than its bleed saved.
- **Hunter's Mark** matters to a melee hunter only through **Expose Prey** (levels 31-32): from then on mark the mob in a free global cooldown, and each of your hits on it has a chance to light up **Mongoose Bite**. **Counterattack** (level 57) lights up after you parry; **Deflection** raises your parry.
- **Aspect of the Monkey** until level 30 (its dodges also light up **Mongoose Bite**), then **Aspect of the Beast**: melee attack power, and with **Deadly Aspects** (levels 49-53) a chance per swing for 30% faster swings for 12 seconds.
- At 60 your white swings are about 27% of the damage, **Raptor Strike** about 13%, the trap about 10% and the cat about 30%. Drink below 60% mana: the tuned rules measured it slightly faster than waiting (under 1% per kill), since the trap and **Lacerate** need the mana.

**Caveats**

- **Strider Kick** and **Immolation Trap** are not melee damage under this project's rule for a melee hunter (more than half the damage from white swings and strikes). The fight lab's tuning of 2026-10-24 keeps the rotation over that line: no **Strider Kick** at all and the trap only from half your mana up. That play is a little slower than pressing both freely (about 3.5% per kill on the tuning's check seed, 24.4 against 23.6 seconds). **Strider Kick** (level 30) stays in the build, but the tuned rotation never presses it.
- The pet is still your tank: **Mend Pet** below about a third in the fight and below half between fights, and let it build threat before you hit hard.
- **Deterrence** (level 23) when you fall below 40% health while the mob has more than 30% of its health: +25% dodge and parry for 10 seconds. With the pet tanking, the fight lab almost never needs it on one mob.
- **Improved Tracking** (levels 10-14) adds damage only against the creature type you are tracking: switch tracking to what you are killing.
- The search found a split Survival build (10/11/30) about 2% faster per kill, but its melee share sat just under half (49.5%), so the page keeps this one.
- Watch your ranks at the top levels: in the beta client (build 70425) **Trueshot Aura** rank 5 gives +50 attack power against rank 4's +75, **Aspect of the Hawk** rank 6 +55 against rank 5's +90 and **Hunter's Mark** rank 4 +71 against rank 3's +98. Keep casting the stronger lower rank from your spellbook (the fight lab does) until Blizzard fixes the numbers.

**When to change it**

- Two or more mobs: **Explosive Trap** instead of **Immolation Trap**; it hits everything within 10 yards and shares the fire-trap cooldown.
- If the pet dies mid-fight, **Deterrence** and finish the mob, or **Feign Death** if it is going badly; revive the pet before the next pull.

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-26; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
