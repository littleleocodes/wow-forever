# Survival Hunter — leveling, level by level

[Hunter](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Survival](survival.md) > Level by level

What a Survival hunter presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. This is a melee hunter: two one-handed weapons, a pet that holds the mob and does a part of the damage, **Serpent Sting** at the pull, **Immolation Trap** under your feet and **Raptor Strike** to finish. It plays the same rotation as the Beast-Survival build, with Survival's crit, hit and Agility talents in place of most of the pet talents. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 3/16/32 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/hunter/60/3-0053431-51022023025020215-12)), the one on the [Survival leveling page](survival.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | One **Auto Shot** as the mob comes, then your weapons. **Raptor Strike** finishes. |
| 4-9 | **Serpent Sting** at the pull: a poison that ticks for 15 seconds. |
| 10-15 | **Tame Beast**: the pet holds the mob and does a part of the damage. Talent points start with **Improved Tracking**. |
| 16-19 | **Immolation Trap** under your feet burns the mob. **Mongoose Bite** lights up after a dodge. |
| 20-25 | **Freezing Trap** holds a second mob. Nothing new in a one-mob fight. |
| 26-29 | **Rapid Fire** at the pull: your swings are 40% faster for 15 seconds. |
| 30-39 | **Lacerate** is a bleed for when your mana is full. **Feign Death** is the exit. **Aspect of the Beast** replaces the Monkey. |
| 40-53 | The points go to Marksmanship and the aspect until **Strider Kick** at 54. Buy every new rank. |
| 54-60 | **Strider Kick**: a weapon hit on an 8-second cooldown. Like **Raptor Strike**, it waits for the finish. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; aspects, tracking, pet feeding and reviving, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the rarer tools. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [hunter keybinds page](keybinds.md).

## Levels 1-3: Auto Shot, then melee

**The trainer teaches:** **Auto Shot** (1), Bite (1), Charge (1), Claw (1), Growl (1), Lightning Breath (1), **Raptor Strike** (1), Track Beasts (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Raptor Strike; 4 = Auto Shot.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Raptor Strike, 4 Auto Shot.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Auto Shot** once as the mob comes at you.
2. Melee until it is dead.
3. **Raptor Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health. It replaces your next swing.

- The tuned rules keep **Raptor Strike** for the finish: its mana buys more as a sting and a trap later on.
- The fight lab gives the hunter a pet and two weapons from level 1. In Classic, **Tame Beast** comes at 10 and a second weapon at 20, so levels 1-19 read faster here than they will play.

## Levels 4-9: Serpent Sting

**The trainer teaches:** Aspect of the Monkey (4), **Serpent Sting** (4), Cower (5), Arcane Shot (6), Hunter's Mark (6), Concussive Shot (8), Demoralizing Screech (8), Scorpid Poison (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Raptor Strike at 8.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Serpent Sting; Shift+Mouse 5 = Arcane Shot (at 6); Shift+Q = Concussive Shot (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Raptor Strike, Mouse 4 Serpent Sting.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Serpent Sting** as the mob comes at you, while it has more than 40% of its health.
2. Melee until it is dead.
3. **Raptor Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health.

- **Aspect of the Monkey** (4) stays on from here to 30: more dodges, and from 16 each dodge lights up **Mongoose Bite**. No key, click it.
- **Arcane Shot** (6) and **Concussive Shot** (8) are ranged tools, and the fight lab's melee hunter never casts **Hunter's Mark**. Walk in instead.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 12 at level 4, 13 at level 9. Presses per kill at level 4: Claw 2.5, Melee (off-hand) 1.4, Melee 1.1, Auto Shot 1.1, Growl 1.0, Bite 1.0, Serpent Sting 1.0, Raptor Strike 0.3.*

## Levels 16-19: Immolation Trap and Mongoose Bite

**New ranks to buy:** Raptor Strike at 16; Serpent Sting at 18.

**Talents:** 16 Savage Strikes, 17 Deflection, 18-19 Survivalist.

**Keys to set:** R = Immolation Trap; Mouse 5 = Mongoose Bite.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Raptor Strike, R Immolation Trap, Mouse 5 Mongoose Bite, Mouse 4 Serpent Sting, 3 Mend Pet.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Send the pet in, then **Serpent Sting** as you walk in behind it.
2. **Raptor Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health. It replaces your next swing and costs no global cooldown.
3. **Mongoose Bite** when it lights up, after you dodge.

- **Deflection** (17) adds parries, **Survivalist** (18-19) 4% more health. Nothing to press.
- **Multi-Shot** (18) is a ranged spell. Not for this build.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 15 at level 16. Presses per kill at level 16: Claw 4.6, Melee (off-hand) 2.7, Melee 2.1, Growl 1.7, Auto Shot 1.1, Bite 1.0, Serpent Sting 1.0, Raptor Strike 0.6, Immolation Trap 0.5.*

## Between fights

- Mend the pet below half its health, drink below 35% of your mana, eat below 65% of your health. Feed the pet when it sulks.
- Keep the right tracking on: **Improved Tracking** only pays for the mobs you track.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Raptor Strike**, **Serpent Sting** and **Immolation Trap** have many ranks.
