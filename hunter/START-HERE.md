# Hunter: start here

[All classes](../START-HERE.md)

Classic's ranged physical DPS: Beast Mastery/Marksmanship in raids for Trueshot Aura and personal damage, Marksmanship/Survival in PvP. Forever removes Aimed Shot, Lethal Shots and Improved Hunter's Mark as talents, moves Trueshot Aura to the 16-point slot, adds Lone Wolf (petless), Sniper Shot, Summon Hawk, and turns Survival into a melee tree. Human hunters are new; Skyborne too.
