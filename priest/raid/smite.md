## How to play

**Before the pull:** **Holy Fire**.

**Talents with no line below:** **Holy Nova**: a defensive spell: cast it when you need it, it is not part of the damage rotation.

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain**: keep it on the target
2. **Inner Focus**, then straight away **Devouring Plague**
3. **Holy Fire**: keep it on the target
4. **Penance**
5. **Smite**

**Smite** is your filler: cast it whenever nothing above is ready.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Power Infusion**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 1 time a fight)
- Use your trinkets, your racial and a potion together with your big cooldowns.
