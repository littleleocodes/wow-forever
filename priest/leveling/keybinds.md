# Priest leveling keybinds (hand-written)

[Priest](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > Keybinds

A personal layout, written 2026-11-06 on the player's own two action bars, for a player of League of Legends and Deadlock with a mouse
that has two side buttons (WoW shows them as Button 4 and Button 5). Same scheme as the [mage](../../mage/leveling/keybinds.md)
and [paladin](../../paladin/leveling/keybinds.md) layouts: Q the filler you press most, E the control, R the big hit, F the
save, the two thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the middle mouse button the wand, the Shift bar the rarer tools. Keys are for
spells you press in a fight; Power Word: Fortitude, Inner Fire, Shadowform, Renew between fights, food, water, the mount and the
Hearthstone have no key and are clicked. It is built on the [Shadow leveling page](shadow.md).

**Which spell is on each key at your level:** the "Keybinds by level" tables of the
[Shadow level-by-level guide](shadow-by-level.md). This page is the layout at 60 and the setup.

This file is hand-written: the regeneration that rewrites the rest of this folder leaves it alone. Edit it by hand.

## Set up first (Esc, Key Bindings and Interface)

- The two action bars: bar 1 is bound to Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 to the same keys
  with Shift, with Shift+X in the last slot. The tables below list the slots in that order.
- Movement: Strafe Left on A and Strafe Right on D, turning with the right mouse button held, as in Deadlock.
- Interface, Combat: Auto Self Cast on, Self Cast Key Alt, so the shield and the heals land on you with a mob targeted.
- A side bar without keybinds for the things you click: Power Word: Fortitude, Inner Fire, Shadowform, Renew, food, water, the mount,
  the Hearthstone.

## Bar 1: the fight

| Key | Spell (learned at) | Press it when |
|---|---|---|
| Q | Smite (1), Mind Flay from 20 (talent) | Smite until you own a wand; Mind Flay in between Mind Blasts from 20 |
| E | Psychic Scream (14) | A mob is on you and you are below 40% of your health |
| R | Mind Blast (10) | Whenever it is ready |
| F | Power Word: Shield (6) | Before a pull you start below half your health; in an emergency |
| Mouse 5 (front side button) | Shadow Word: Death (32) | When one cast will kill the mob; from 35 with Early Demise, whenever the mob is below 20% |
| Mouse 4 (back side button) | Shadow Word: Pain (4) | The pull, every fight |
| 1 | Vampiric Embrace (39, talent) | At the pull whenever it is ready |
| 2 | Free | |
| 3 | Flash Heal (20) | Below a third of your health, outside Shadowform |
| 4 | Desperate Prayer (10) | The panic heal, on a long cooldown |
| C | Free | |
| Middle mouse | Shoot (wand) | The filler until 20; the finishing hits after |

## Bar 2: the same keys with Shift

| Key | Spell (learned at) | Press it when |
|---|---|---|
| Shift+E | Shackle Undead (20) | A second Undead mob joins |
| Shift+Mouse 5 | Dispel Magic (18) | A mob has a buff worth removing, or a magic debuff is on you |
| Shift+Mouse 4 | Cure Disease (14) | A disease is on you |
| Shift+Q, Shift+R, Shift+F, Shift+1 to Shift+X | Free | |

## No key: click these between fights

Power Word: Fortitude, Inner Fire, Shadowform (once, it stays on), Renew (a between-fights heal before 40), food, water, your mount
(40 in Classic) and the Hearthstone. Devouring Plague and Holy Fire are never cast by the tuned rotation. Tab and Space stay on WoW's
defaults.
