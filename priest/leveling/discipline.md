**Leveling as Discipline or as Shadow?** This build takes 45 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Shadow](../leveling/shadow.md) 25 s: Discipline is about 1.8 times as slow. Your first Discipline point comes at level 10. Level this way if you want to heal dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Shadow and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up. **Power Word: Shield** on yourself before every pull; **Power Infusion** on yourself when it is ready, and **Inner Focus** right before **Mind Blast** or **Smite** (that cast is free).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull.
2. **Penance** and **Mind Blast** whenever they are ready.
3. **Smite** in between while the mob has more than a third of its health and you have more than 30% mana; your wand otherwise.
4. Skip **Holy Fire**: its long cast costs more than it deals while leveling.
5. Hurt: **Power Word: Shield** again below half your health, **Flash Heal** below a third (with **Renewed Hope** it crits more and brings the next shield sooner), **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.

**Between fights:** Drink only below 15% mana; **Renew** yourself after the fight and eat only if you are still below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

You level with your raid talents, all Discipline first: **Penance** at 31 and **Power Infusion** at 40 carry the damage. The fight is a Smite priest's: an instant pull with **Shadow Word: Pain**, **Penance** and **Mind Blast** on cooldown, **Smite** in between, your wand to finish.

**Why it works**

- **Shadow Word: Pain** pulls because it is instant and ticks for the whole fight. **Penance** (from 31) and **Mind Blast** hit harder for their cast time than **Smite**, so they go out whenever ready.
- **Power Infusion** (from 40) at the start of a pull whenever it is ready: 20% more spell damage for 15 seconds, once every 3 minutes. **Inner Focus** goes with it: press it right before **Mind Blast** or **Smite** to make that cast free with 25% more crit chance.
- **Smite** only while the mob has more than about a third of its health and you have more than 30% mana; below that your wand finishes. The fight lab measured these limits about 5% and 3.5% faster, and skipping **Holy Fire** (a slow cast whose damage Forever cut about in half) another 5%.
- **Renewed Hope** (from 32) ties your heals to your shield: a **Flash Heal** on yourself while Weakened Soul is on you crits 10% more often and cuts 5 seconds off Weakened Soul, so your next **Power Word: Shield** comes sooner.
- Mana: **Meditation** keeps half your regeneration going while you cast, so drink only below 15% mana (measured about 3% faster than at 35%). After a fight, **Renew** heals you while you loot and walk; eat only if you are still low.

**Caveats**

- Discipline levels at Holy's pace: 45 seconds per kill in the fight lab, Shadow 26. The fight lab does not count **Divine Aegis**, **Inspiration** or **Binding Heal**: they are raid talents.
- The pre-pull **Power Word: Shield** is part of this rotation: while it holds, damage does not push your **Smite** back, and **Soul Warding** (from 30) removes its 4-second cooldown and cuts its cost by 15%.
- **Power Infusion** costs 20% of your base mana; the fight lab casts it only with enough mana left.

**When to change it**

- Hurt in a fight: shield again below half your health, **Flash Heal** below about a third; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.
- The fight lab spends **Power Infusion** on the next pull; if a harder pull is coming (an elite, two mobs), holding it for that one is your call.
