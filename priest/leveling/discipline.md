**Leveling as Discipline or as Shadow?** This build takes 43 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Shadow](../leveling/shadow.md) 24 s: Discipline is about 1.8 times as slow. Your first Discipline point comes at level 10. Level this way if you want to heal dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Shadow and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Power Infusion**, **Renewed Hope** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up. **Power Word: Shield** on yourself before every pull; **Power Infusion** on yourself when it is ready, and **Inner Focus** right before **Mind Blast** (that cast is free).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, and again when it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready while you have more than 30% mana and the mob more than 10% of its health.
3. **Smite** in between while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana; your wand otherwise.
4. Below 30% mana: wand only. You drink only below 15%, so most of a fight ends on the wand.
5. Skip Holy Fire, Penance and Devouring Plague: they deal less per mana than **Mind Blast**, and mana is what limits you.
6. Hurt: **Power Word: Shield** again in melee below 80% health once Weakened Soul is gone (below half at any time), **Flash Heal** below a third (with **Renewed Hope** it crits more and brings the next shield sooner), **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.

**Between fights:** Drink only below 15% mana; **Renew** yourself after the fight and eat only if you are still below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

You level with your raid talents, all Discipline first. The fight is a Smite priest's: an instant pull with **Shadow Word: Pain**, **Mind Blast** on cooldown, **Smite** in between, both only above 30% of your mana, and your wand for the rest.

**Why it works**

- **Shadow Word: Pain** pulls because it is instant and ticks for the whole fight. **Mind Blast** hits hardest for its mana, so it goes out whenever ready while you have more than 30% mana and the mob more than 10% of its health.
- **Smite** only while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana; below that your wand finishes.
- Mana sets the pace. The tuned rules (2026-10-20) cast **Smite** and **Mind Blast** only above 30% of your mana and drink only below 15%: between the two you only wand while your mana creeps back, so most fights end on the wand (about half the damage at 60 in the fight lab). **Meditation** (levels 22-24) keeps half your regeneration going while you cast.
- **Inner Focus** (level 26) at the start of a pull: press it right before **Mind Blast** to make that cast free with 25% more crit chance.
- After a fight, **Renew** heals you while you loot and walk; eat only if you are still below two thirds of your health.

**Caveats**

- **Penance** (level 30) is in the build for the raid, but the tuned rules never cast it while leveling: it deals less per mana than **Mind Blast**, and casting it above 30% mana measured about 1% slower (at level 40 it took **Mind Blast**'s mana). The same goes for Holy Fire and Devouring Plague.
- This is a slow way to level: about 43 seconds per kill in the fight lab (level-weighted), Shadow 25. The fight lab does not count **Divine Aegis** or **Inspiration**: they are raid talents.
- This build takes no **Power Infusion** or **Renewed Hope**: skip the list's lines for them.
- The pre-pull **Power Word: Shield** is part of this rotation: while it holds, damage does not push your **Smite** back, and **Soul Warding** (level 36) removes its 4-second cooldown and cuts its cost by 15%.

**When to change it**

- Hurt in a fight: shield again below half your health, **Flash Heal** below about a third; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.
- If you only want to reach 60, level as Shadow and respec: well under half the time per kill in the fight lab.
