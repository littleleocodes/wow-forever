## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up. **Power Word: Shield** before a pull only when you start it below 80% health; **Inner Focus** when it is ready, as the pull starts (your first spell is free).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, then **Devouring Plague**; each again when it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Penance** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 35% of its health.
3. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready, at any mana, while the mob has more than 10% of its health (also to finish a mob one cast will kill).
4. **Smite** in between while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana; your wand otherwise, and for the last hit or two.
5. Skip Holy Fire: the tuned rules never cast it.
6. Hurt: **Power Word: Shield** in melee below 80% health once Weakened Soul is gone (below half at any time), **Flash Heal** below a third, **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.

**Between fights:** Drink only below 15% mana; **Renew** yourself after a fight below 85% health, and eat only if you are still below two thirds.

## Why this rotation

Leveling as Discipline in your raid talents: **Shadow Word: Pain** and **Devouring Plague** on the mob, **Penance** and **Mind Blast** whenever they are ready, **Smite** in the gaps while you have more than 30% of your mana, and your wand for the rest. The build is the Discipline raid healer's (29/22/0): Discipline's damage and mana talents first, **Penance** at 30, then the Holy healing talents. In the fight lab a kill takes about 46 seconds level-weighted (46 at level 60), 1.9 times the Shadow page's 24.

**Why it works**

- **Shadow Word: Pain** pulls: it is instant and ticks for the whole fight. **Devouring Plague** (level 20) follows while the mob has more than 30% of its health: the Discipline list keeps it (the Shadow and Holy lists do not), and it heals you for its damage. **Twin Disciplines** (10-13) adds 4% to both, and to the shield and **Renew**.
- **Penance** (30) whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 35% of its health: three Holy hits in 2 seconds. **Power in Light** (14, one point) makes it and **Smite** 2% stronger while your **Shadow Word: Pain** burns, **Holy Specialization** (35-39) adds crit to both, and **Improved Healing** (48-50) makes **Penance** 15% cheaper.
- **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready, at any mana, while the mob has more than 10% of its health; it also finishes a mob one cast will kill.
- **Smite** only while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% of your mana: otherwise your wand fills the gaps and finishes. **Divine Fury** (40) shortens its cast.
- Mana sets the pace: you drink only below 15%. **Mental Strength** (15-19) gives 10% more Intellect, **Meditation** (22-24) keeps half your regeneration going while you cast, and **Mental Agility** (20-21, 25) cuts the cost of **Smite** and your instants by 10%.
- **Inner Focus** (26) goes up as the pull starts: your first spell is free.

**Caveats**

- The tuned rules shield before a pull only when you start it below 80% health. In melee, shield again below 80% once Weakened Soul is gone, so damage does not push your **Smite** back; **Twilight Focus** (31-33) stops most of that pushback too.
- The tuned rules never cast **Holy Fire**. This build takes no **Power Infusion** or **Renewed Hope**.
- From 51 the points are the raid healer's: **Litany of Light**, **Silent Resolve**, **Soul Warding** and **Divine Aegis** do little solo.
- These are your raid healing talents: healers level in their raid build, so there is no respec at 60. The search's Discipline build for leveling alone (under Other builds) kills about 15% faster.
- After a fight, **Renew** heals you while you loot and walk on (below 85% health); eat only if you are still below two thirds of your health.

**When to change it**

- Hurt in a fight: shield below half your health, **Flash Heal** below about a third, **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.
- If you only want to reach 60, level as Shadow and respec: about 1.9 times as fast per kill in the fight lab.
