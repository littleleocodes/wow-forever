## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Power Infusion**, **Renewed Hope** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up. **Power Word: Shield** on yourself before every pull; **Power Infusion** on yourself when it is ready, and **Inner Focus** right before **Mind Blast** (that cast is free).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, and again when it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready while you have more than 30% mana and the mob more than 10% of its health.
3. **Smite** in between while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana; your wand otherwise.
4. Below 30% mana: wand only. You drink only below 15%, so most of a fight ends on the wand.
5. Skip Holy Fire, Penance and Devouring Plague: they deal less per mana than **Mind Blast**, and mana is what limits you.
6. Hurt: **Power Word: Shield** again in melee below 80% health once Weakened Soul is gone (below half at any time), **Flash Heal** below a third (with **Renewed Hope** it crits more and brings the next shield sooner), **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.

**Between fights:** Drink only below 15% mana; **Renew** yourself after the fight and eat only if you are still below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

The Discipline-Shadow build plays the Discipline leveling list (a Smite priest's) with Shadow's damage talents behind it: an instant pull with **Shadow Word: Pain**, **Mind Blast** on cooldown, **Smite** in between, both only above 30% of your mana, your wand for the rest, a **Power Word: Shield** before every pull. Discipline comes first (levels 10-24), then **Spirit Tap** (25-29), then mostly Shadow.

**Why it works**

- **Shadow Word: Pain** and **Mind Blast** are your Shadow spells, and the Shadow points make them hit harder: **Improved Shadow Word: Pain** (levels 39-40) makes the pain last longer, **Shadow Weaving** (41-43) and **Darkness** (57-60) add Shadow damage, and **Improved Mind Blast** (37-38) brings **Mind Blast** back sooner.
- Mana sets the pace. The tuned rules (2026-10-20) cast **Smite** and **Mind Blast** only above 30% of your mana and drink only below 15%: between the two you only wand while your mana creeps back, so most fights end on the wand (about half the damage at 60 in the fight lab). The build pays for it: in the fight lab its most valuable talents at 60 are **Spirit Tap** (double Spirit for 15 seconds after a kill), **Wand Specialization** (levels 10-11), **Improved Shadow Word: Pain** and **Meditation** (half your regeneration while casting).
- **Smite** only while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana; **Mind Blast** above 30% mana while the mob has more than 10% of its health.
- **Inner Focus** (level 30) right before **Mind Blast** makes that cast free with 25% more crit chance.

**Caveats**

- This build takes neither **Penance**, **Power Infusion** nor **Renewed Hope**: skip those lines. The tuned rules never cast Holy Fire or Devouring Plague.
- The Discipline list never casts **Mind Flay** or **Vampiric Embrace**: the build's **Mind Flay** and **Improved Mind Flay** points (54-56) only open the **Darkness** tier. The fight lab measured this build at about 34 seconds per kill (level-weighted), against 25 for the Shadow page.
- Drink below 15% of your mana; after a fight, **Renew** heals you while you loot and walk, and you eat only below two thirds of your health.

**When to change it**

- Hurt in a fight: shield again below half your health, **Flash Heal** below about a third, **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.
- For speed, the Shadow page levels about a quarter faster per kill.
