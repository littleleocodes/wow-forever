# Discipline Priest — leveling, level by level

[Priest](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Discipline](discipline.md) > Level by level

What a Discipline priest presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. This build is the Discipline raid healer's (29/22/0): it levels as a damage priest on Discipline's damage and mana talents, and from 31 takes the Holy healing talents. **Shadow Word: Pain** and **Mind Blast** carry the fight, **Smite** fills the gaps while you have mana, and the wand does the rest. **Devouring Plague** joins at 20 and **Penance**, the spec's own spell, at 30. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 29/22/0 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/priest/60/10431503133001103-3150102323002--12)), the one on the [Discipline leveling page](discipline.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Smite** is your only attack. Buy or beg a wand as early as you can. |
| 4-9 | **Shadow Word: Pain** opens every pull. The shield (6) and **Renew** (8) arrive for when a fight goes wrong. |
| 10-19 | **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready. Talent points start with **Twin Disciplines**. |
| 20-29 | **Devouring Plague** follows **Shadow Word: Pain** on every pull. It heals you for the damage it does. |
| 30-60 | **Penance** (talent) whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 35% of its health: three Holy hits in 2 seconds. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape or the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the middle mouse button the wand, the Shift bar the wider or rarer version of the key under it. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Every spell this guide shares with the Shadow guide sits on the same key, so you can switch specs without relearning. Setup: the [priest keybinds page](keybinds.md), written for Shadow.

## Levels 1-3: Smite, until you own a wand

**The trainer teaches:** Lesser Heal (1), Power Word: Fortitude (1), **Smite** (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Smite; 3 = Lesser Heal; Middle mouse = Wand.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Smite, 3 Lesser Heal.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Smite** while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% of your mana.
2. Your wand for the rest, once you own one. Until then, **Smite** to the end.

**If the fight goes wrong:** **Lesser Heal** below a third of your health.

- Keep **Power Word: Fortitude** on yourself at all times, from here to 60.
- The fight lab gives you a wand from level 1. Without one, every fight is **Smite** and you drink more often.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 13 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Smite 2.4, Wand 1.2.*

## Levels 4-9: Shadow Word: Pain

**The trainer teaches:** **Shadow Word: Pain** (4), Power Word: Shield (6), Fade (8), Renew (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Lesser Heal at 4; Smite at 6.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Shadow Word: Pain; F = Power Word: Shield (at 6); Shift+3 = Renew (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Smite, Mouse 4 Shadow Word: Pain, 3 Lesser Heal.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, and again if it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Smite** in between, while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% of your mana.
3. Your wand for the rest.

**If the fight goes wrong:** **Lesser Heal** below a third of your health.

- **Power Word: Shield** (6): before a pull only when you start it below 80% of your health. In melee, again below 80% once Weakened Soul has worn off, so hits do not push your casts back.
- **Renew** (8): in a fight below 60% of your health, and after a fight below 85%: it heals while you loot and walk on.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 4, 25 at level 9. Presses per kill at level 4: Smite 3.3, Wand 2.5, Shadow Word: Pain 1.0.*

## Levels 10-19: Mind Blast, and talents begin

**The trainer teaches:** Confounding Flash (10, Gnome only), Desperate Prayer (10, Dwarf only), Divine Grace (10, Human only), Hex of Weakness (10, Troll only), **Mind Blast** (10), Resurrection (10), Starshards (10, Night Elf only), Touch of Weakness (10, Undead only), Inner Fire (12), Cure Disease (14), Psychic Scream (14), Heal (16), Dispel Magic (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Desperate Prayer at 18; Lesser Heal at 10; Mind Blast at 16; Power Word: Shield at 12, 18; Renew at 14; Shadow Word: Pain at 10, 18; Smite at 14.

**Talents:** 10-13 Twin Disciplines, 14 Power in Light, 15-19 Mental Strength.

**Keys to set:** R = Mind Blast; 4 = Desperate Prayer; E = Psychic Scream (at 14); Shift+Mouse 4 = Cure Disease (at 14); Shift+Mouse 5 = Dispel Magic (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Smite, R Mind Blast, F Power Word: Shield, Mouse 4 Shadow Word: Pain, 3 Lesser Heal, Shift+3 Renew.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, and again if it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 10% of its health. It also finishes a mob one cast will kill.
3. **Smite** in between, while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% of your mana.
4. Your wand otherwise, and for the last hit or two.

**If the fight goes wrong:** In melee below 80% of your health: **Power Word: Shield** once Weakened Soul has worn off (below half at any time). **Lesser Heal** below a third of your health, **Renew** below 60% if it is not already on you.

- **Twin Disciplines** (10-13) strengthens your instant spells: **Shadow Word: Pain** now, **Devouring Plague** from 20.
- **Power in Light** (14) makes **Smite** a little stronger while your **Shadow Word: Pain** burns on the mob. **Mental Strength** (15-19): 10% more Intellect, so more mana.
- **Psychic Scream** (14): when a mob is on you and you are below 40% of your health, fear it and heal.
- **Starshards** (10) is not part of this rotation.
- Racial spells in the list above: Divine Grace (Human), Desperate Prayer (Dwarf), Starshards (Night Elf), Confounding Flash (Gnome), Touch of Weakness (Undead), Hex of Weakness (Troll). Only that race can learn its own.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 24 at level 10, 38 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Wand 4.6, Mind Blast 1.8, Shadow Word: Pain 1.0, Smite 0.6.*

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Smite, E Psychic Scream, R Mind Blast, F Power Word: Shield, Mouse 4 Shadow Word: Pain, 2 Devouring Plague, 3 Flash Heal, Shift+3 Renew.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, then **Devouring Plague**; each again when it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 10% of its health. It also finishes a mob one cast will kill.

- **Flash Heal** (20) replaces **Lesser Heal** on your emergency key.

- **Holy Fire** (20): leave it off your bar. The tuned rotation never casts it for this build.

## Between fights

- Drink only when you are below about 15% of your mana.
- After a fight below 85% of your health, **Renew** yourself: it heals while you loot and walk on. Eat only if you are still below two thirds.
- Recast **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** when they run out.
- Visit the trainer every even level, and replace your wand whenever a better one turns up: it lands most of your hits.
