## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up. **Power Word: Shield** on yourself before every pull: while it holds, damage does not push your **Smite** back. **Inner Focus** when it is ready, right before **Mind Blast** (that cast is free).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, and again when it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready while you have more than 30% mana and the mob more than 20% of its health.
3. **Smite** in between while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana; your wand otherwise.
4. Below 30% mana: wand only. You drink only below 15%, so most of a fight ends on the wand.
5. Skip Holy Fire, Penance and Devouring Plague: they deal less per mana than **Mind Blast**, and mana is what limits you.
6. Hurt: **Power Word: Shield** again in melee below 80% health once Weakened Soul is gone (below half at any time), **Flash Heal** below a third, **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.

**Between fights:** Drink only below 15% mana; **Renew** yourself after the fight and eat only if you are still below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

Leveling as Smite: pull with the instant **Shadow Word: Pain**, cast **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready and **Smite** while the mob has more than 35% of its health, both only above 30% of your mana, and let your wand do the rest. A **Power Word: Shield** before every pull lets you keep casting in melee.

**Why it works**

- **Shadow Word: Pain** pulls because it is instant and ticks for the whole fight; **Improved Shadow Word: Pain** (levels 59-60) makes it last longer. **Power in Light** (17-19 and 33) adds 8% to **Smite** while it burns.
- **Mind Blast** hits hardest for its mana, so it goes first whenever ready (above 30% mana and while the mob has more than 20% of its health).
- **Smite** only while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana: below that your wand finishes without spending mana or starting a cast the mob may not live through.
- Mana sets the pace. The tuned rules (2026-10-20) cast **Smite** and **Mind Blast** only above 30% of your mana and drink only below 15%: between the two you only wand while your mana creeps back, so most fights end on the wand (about half the damage at 60 in the fight lab). That is why **Wand Specialization** (levels 10-11) and **Spirit Tap** (35-39) are the build's most valuable talents in the fight lab at 60, with **Meditation** (half your regeneration while casting).
- **Inner Focus** at the start of a pull, whenever it is ready: press it right before **Mind Blast** to make that cast free with 25% more crit chance.

**Caveats**

- The pre-pull shield is part of this rotation: **Smite** takes 2 seconds or more to cast, and while **Power Word: Shield** holds, damage does not push your casts back. In melee, shield again once Weakened Soul is gone and you are below 80% health.
- The tuned rules never cast Holy Fire or Devouring Plague: per point of mana they deal less than **Mind Blast**. This build has no **Penance**.
- This build has no area damage: pull one mob at a time. With two on you, **Psychic Scream** sends them running.

**When to change it**

- Hurt in a fight: shield again below half your health, **Flash Heal** below about a third, **Renew** below 60% if it is not already on you.
- For pure speed, level as Shadow: about 25 seconds per kill in the fight lab (level-weighted) against this build's 35.
