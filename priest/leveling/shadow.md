## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up; **Shadowform** once you have it. **Power Word: Shield** before a pull only when you start it below 80% health; **Inner Focus** when it is ready, right before a **Mind Blast** (that cast is free).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Pull with the instants: **Vampiric Embrace** when it is ready, then **Shadow Word: Pain**.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready, **Mind Flay** in between (it slows the mob while you channel).
3. **Shadow Word: Death** when one cast will kill the mob, and below 20% health once you have **Early Demise**.
4. Finish a mob with a hit or two left with your wand; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40% health.
5. Skip Devouring Plague and Smite: the tuned rules never cast them (the plague costs too much mana for what it does before the mob dies).
6. Below a third of your health outside **Shadowform**: **Flash Heal**.

**Between fights:** Drink only below 15% mana, eat below two thirds of your health (**Renew** yourself instead outside **Shadowform**).

## Why this rotation

Shadow levels on a short loop: your opening spells land while the mob runs in, **Mind Flay** slows and kills it, and **Shadow Word: Death** or your wand finishes it. **Shadowform** at 40 is the big step: the fight lab's time per kill drops from 36 to 22 seconds between levels 30 and 40.

**Why it works**

- Open from as far away as you can: the fight lab casts the instants first (**Vampiric Embrace** when it is ready, then **Shadow Word: Pain**) and **Mind Blast**, your biggest hit, right after, so all three land while the mob walks in.
- **Mind Flay** (level 20) is your filler and your biggest damage share (about 45% at 60): it slows the mob while you channel, so a mob walking in reaches you later. **Mind Blast** goes out whenever it is ready.
- From 32, **Shadow Word: Death** finishes. The fight lab casts it when one cast will kill the mob, so its backlash (10% of your health when the target survives) never lands, and below 20% health once you have **Early Demise** (level 35). From 40 it does a sixth to a quarter of your damage and the wand is rarely needed.
- Before 20 your wand finishes: when a mob has a hit or two of wand damage left, a new spell would be wasted mana. **Mind Blast** also finishes a mob when one cast kills it.
- **Vampiric Embrace** (level 28) is your heal once **Shadowform** stops real heals: it heals you for a fifth of your Shadow damage, and the fight lab casts it whenever it is ready.
- Mana: **Spirit Tap** doubles your Spirit for 15 seconds after kills and **Shadowform** halves your Shadow costs, so the tuned rules (2026-10-20) drink only below 15% mana. They also skip the pre-pull **Power Word: Shield** unless you start a pull below 80% health (about 13% faster than shielding every pull: the shield is not a Shadow spell and costs full mana).

**Caveats**

- The tuned rules never cast **Devouring Plague** (about 11% faster without it): at top rank it costs 985 mana for 848 damage over 24 seconds, and most mobs die before it ends. Nor **Smite** (about 7% faster without it).
- **Shadowform** blocks your heals but cuts the physical damage you take by 15%: from 40 your only healing in a fight is **Vampiric Embrace**, and between fights you eat (below about two thirds of your health) instead of casting **Renew**.
- Two or three mobs at once: put **Shadow Word: Pain** on each and use **Psychic Scream** (below 40% of your health with a mob on you) to buy time. The no-shield rule was tuned on single pulls.

**When to change it**

- When a pull goes wrong (an add, a caster pack), shield yourself: **Power Word: Shield** absorbs damage and stops pushback on your casts while it holds.
- Below 40% health with the mob on you: **Psychic Scream**, then **Flash Heal** if you are not in **Shadowform** yet.
