## How to play

PvP has no fixed rotation: it depends on who you fight. No simulator plays PvP, so this build rests on the talents' descriptions and the community's PvP builds.

**Why these talents:** Shadowform (+10% damage, -50% Shadow mana cost), Silence, Vampiric Embrace, Blackout, Improved Psychic Scream (now requires Blackout 5), Improved Mind Flay, Darkness and Early Demise (Shadow Word: Death exists in Forever), with Twin Disciplines and Silent Resolve replacing the removed Unbreakable Will.
