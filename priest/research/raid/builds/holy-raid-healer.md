## Summary

Allocation adopted on 2026-09-26 from `cand-holy-r1`, the build with the highest healing capacity in the healer lab (+4.4% vs the previous draft; kept under raid/heallab/candidates/). Previous summary: Divine Spirit, Unbreakable Will, Improved Fortitude and Healing Focus are gone, so the Discipline dip is rebuilt around Twin Disciplines (instant heals), Improved Power Word: Shield + Soul Warding, Inner Focus, Meditation (now 51% regen while casting) and Mental Agility. Holy keeps Spiritual Guidance, Spiritual Healing, Inspiration, Divine Fury and Improved Healing, adds Twilight Focus (the Healing Focus replacement), Binding Heal and the Prayer of Mending capstone.
