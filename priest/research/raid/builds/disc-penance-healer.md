2. **Divine Aegis** — New: crit heals shield the target for 5%/rank of the heal (est. 15%).
3. **Renewed Hope** — New: +2%/rank crit (est. 10%) on direct heals to Weakened Soul targets and shorter Weakened Soul.
4. **Power Infusion** — +20% spell damage/healing to a target for 15s.

6. **Improved Power Word: Shield** — +21% shield (est.).

8. **Inspiration** — Armor on crit.
9. **Holy Specialization** — Crit for Divine Aegis/Inspiration/Renewed Hope.
