## shadow-dps

Played by the page build of [Shadow](../../leveling/shadow.md) ([`shadow-open-leveling`](builds/shadow-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level: Power Word: Shield and Inner Fire before the pull, Mind Blast at 30 yd, Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Flay channels (50% slow) and Mind Blast on cooldown while the mob walks in and once it is on you (the shield stops pushback while it holds), Vampiric Embrace and Devouring Plague heal, Shadow Word: Death below 20%, wand to finish without overkill; Psychic Scream when health runs low. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Renew (not in Shadowform), conjured water below 35% mana, food below 65% health; a death costs 90 s.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up; **Shadowform** once you have it. **Power Word: Shield** before a pull only when you start it below 80% health; **Inner Focus** when it is ready, right before a **Mind Blast** (that cast is free).

1. Pull with the instants: **Vampiric Embrace** when it is ready, then **Shadow Word: Pain**.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready, **Mind Flay** in between (it slows the mob while you channel).
3. **Shadow Word: Death** when one cast will kill the mob, and below 20% health once you have **Early Demise**.
4. Finish a mob with a hit or two left with your wand; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40% health.
5. Skip Devouring Plague and Smite: the tuned rules never cast them (the plague costs too much mana for what it does before the mob dies).
6. Below a third of your health outside **Shadowform**: **Flash Heal**.

Between fights: Drink only below 15% mana, eat below two thirds of your health (**Renew** yourself instead outside **Shadowform**).

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-20, on top of the priority below): SHIELD_BELOW = 0.8; never cast Devouring Plague; never cast Smite; drink below 15% mana. Together +28.9% time per kill saved for `leveling-shadow` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## discipline-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Discipline-Shadow](../../leveling/hybrid-discipline-shadow.md) ([`hybrid-discipline-shadow-open-leveling`](builds/hybrid-discipline-shadow-open-leveling.md)), [Discipline](../../leveling/discipline.md) ([`discipline-leveling`](builds/discipline-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up. **Power Word: Shield** on yourself before every pull; **Power Infusion** on yourself when it is ready, and **Inner Focus** right before **Mind Blast** (that cast is free).

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, and again when it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready while you have more than 30% mana and the mob more than 10% of its health.
3. **Smite** in between while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana; your wand otherwise.
4. Below 30% mana: wand only. You drink only below 15%, so most of a fight ends on the wand.
5. Skip Holy Fire, Penance and Devouring Plague: they deal less per mana than **Mind Blast**, and mana is what limits you.
6. Hurt: **Power Word: Shield** again in melee below 80% health once Weakened Soul is gone (below half at any time), **Flash Heal** below a third (with **Renewed Hope** it crits more and brings the next shield sooner), **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.

Between fights: Drink only below 15% mana; **Renew** yourself after the fight and eat only if you are still below two thirds of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-20, on top of the priority below): never cast Devouring Plague; Smite only while the mob has more than 35% health; WAND_BELOW = 0.3; drink below 15% mana; never cast Holy Fire; Penance only above 30% mana; Mind Blast only above 30% mana; never cast Penance; Mind Blast only while the mob has more than 10% health. Together +21.4% time per kill saved for `discipline-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`hybrid-discipline-shadow-open-leveling`](builds/hybrid-discipline-shadow-open-leveling.md), [`discipline-leveling`](builds/discipline-leveling.md): not recorded (their times per kill come from the race records of the searches that found them, which keep times only; the table above has them).
