## shadow-dps

Played by the page build of [Shadow](../../leveling/shadow.md) ([`shadow-open-leveling`](builds/shadow-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level: Power Word: Shield and Inner Fire before the pull, Mind Blast at 30 yd, Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Flay channels (50% slow) and Mind Blast on cooldown while the mob walks in and once it is on you (the shield stops pushback while it holds), Vampiric Embrace and Devouring Plague heal, Shadow Word: Death below 20%, wand to finish without overkill; Psychic Scream when health runs low. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Renew (not in Shadowform), conjured water below 35% mana, food below 65% health; a death costs 90 s.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up; **Shadowform** once you have it. **Power Word: Shield** before a pull only when you start it below half your health; **Inner Focus** when it is ready, as the pull starts (your first spell is free).

1. Pull with the instants: **Vampiric Embrace** when it is ready, then **Shadow Word: Pain**.
2. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready, **Mind Flay** in between (it slows the mob while you channel).
3. **Shadow Word: Death** when one cast will kill the mob, and below 20% health once you have **Early Demise**.
4. Finish a mob with a hit or two left with your wand; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40% health.
5. Skip Devouring Plague and Smite: the tuned rules never cast them (the plague costs too much mana for what it does before the mob dies).
6. Below a third of your health outside **Shadowform**: **Flash Heal**.

Between fights: Drink only below 15% mana, eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rule) (**Renew** yourself instead outside **Shadowform**).

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-02, on top of the priority below): SHIELD_BELOW = 0.5; never cast Devouring Plague; never cast Smite; eat below 40% health; drink below 15% mana. Together +28.9% time per kill saved for `leveling-shadow` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## discipline-leveling

Played by the page build of [Discipline](../../leveling/discipline.md) ([`discipline-open-leveling`](builds/discipline-open-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up. **Power Word: Shield** before a pull only when you start it below 80% health; **Inner Focus** when it is ready, as the pull starts (your first spell is free); **Power Infusion** on yourself when it is ready.

1. **Shadow Word: Pain** to pull, then **Devouring Plague**; each again when it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
2. **Penance** whenever it is ready while the mob has more than 35% of its health.
3. **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready, at any mana, while the mob has more than 10% of its health (also to finish a mob one cast will kill).
4. **Smite** in between while the mob has more than 35% of its health and you have more than 30% mana; your wand otherwise, and for the last hit or two.
5. Skip Holy Fire: the tuned rules never cast it.
6. Hurt: **Power Word: Shield** in melee below 80% health once Weakened Soul is gone (below half at any time), **Flash Heal** below a third (with **Renewed Hope** it crits more and brings the next shield sooner), **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.

Between fights: Drink only below 15% mana; **Renew** yourself after a fight below 85% health, and eat only if you are still below two thirds.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-23, on top of the priority below): WAND_BELOW = 0.3; never cast Holy Fire; drink below 15% mana; SHIELD_BELOW = 0.8; Smite only while the mob has more than 35% health; Penance only while the mob has more than 35% health; Mind Blast only while the mob has more than 10% health. Together +17.2% time per kill saved for `discipline-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`discipline-open-leveling`](builds/discipline-open-leveling.md): not recorded (its times per kill come from the race records of the search that found it, which keep times only; the table above has them).

## holy-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Smite](../../leveling/smite.md) ([`smite-open-leveling`](builds/smite-open-leveling.md)), [Holy](../../leveling/holy.md) ([`holy-leveling`](builds/holy-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Power Word: Fortitude** and **Inner Fire** up. **Power Word: Shield** on yourself before every pull: while it holds, damage does not push your casts back. **Inner Focus** when it is ready, as the pull starts: your first spell (from level 20 the opening **Holy Fire**) is free and 25% more likely to crit.

1. Open with **Holy Fire** from range, and cast it again once its burn has ended while the mob has more than 40% of its health (in melee only while your shield holds).
2. **Shadow Word: Pain** next, and again when it runs out while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
3. **Penance** whenever it is ready; **Mind Blast** whenever it is ready while you have more than 30% mana (also to finish a mob one cast will kill).
4. **Smite** in every gap, at any mana, until the mob has less health left than about half a **Smite** hit; then your wand.
5. Skip Devouring Plague: the tuned rules never cast it.
6. Hurt: **Power Word: Shield** again in melee below 80% health once Weakened Soul is gone (below half at any time), **Flash Heal** below a third, **Renew** below 60%; **Psychic Scream** if a mob is on you and you are below 40%.

Between fights: Drink only below 15% mana; **Renew** yourself after a fight below 85% health, and eat below 80%.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-23, on top of the priority below): never cast Devouring Plague; Mind Blast only above 15% mana; Mind Blast only above 30% mana; drink below 15% mana; eat below 80% health. Together +2.3% time per kill saved for `smite-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`smite-open-leveling`](builds/smite-open-leveling.md), [`holy-leveling`](builds/holy-leveling.md): not recorded (their times per kill come from the race records of the searches that found them, which keep times only; the table above has them).
