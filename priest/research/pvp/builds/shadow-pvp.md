## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Shadowform** — +10% Shadow damage, -50% Shadow mana cost (demo).
2. **Silence** — 5s silence + 3s interrupt.
3. **Blackout** — Stun procs; now tier 1 and the Improved Psychic Scream prerequisite.
4. **Vampiric Embrace** — Party healing from Shadow damage (now on a 1-minute cooldown).
5. **Shadow Weaving** — Reworked into a self-buff: 33% chance per Shadow spell for +2% Shadow damage, 5 stacks.
6. **Darkness** — +10% Shadow damage.
7. **Improved Mind Flay** — +20% damage and +10 yards at 2/2, and a 20% slow (70291 tooltip).
8. **Early Demise** — +15%/rank Shadow Word: Death crit below 20% — the execute.
9. **Improved Mind Blast** — Shorter Mind Blast cooldown.
10. **Shadow Focus** — 5% Shadow hit.
11. **Silent Resolve** — -15% duration of stuns, fears and silences on you at 3/3: the only crowd-control reduction left in the Forever priest trees.
12. **Improved Power Word: Shield** — +20% absorb on the shield a PvP priest recasts every 15 s; casts are not pushed back while it holds.
13. **Inner Focus** — A free spell with +25% crit on a 3 min cooldown: a near-certain crit Mind Blast in a burst window.

## Notes

- Shadow raid DPS may finally be viable: Shadow Weaving no longer needs debuff slots, Vampiric Embrace heals the party, and Shadowform halves mana costs. The same 7/0/44 with Shadow Affinity 3 is the raid variant.
- 2026-09-27 scenario audit: Spirit Tap 5, Improved Shadow Word: Pain 2, Improved Fade 2, Wand Specialization 2 -> Silent Resolve 1, Twin Disciplines 2, Improved Power Word: Shield 3, Inner Focus 1, Martyrdom 2, Meditation 2. Fade only lowers NPC threat, so Improved Fade does nothing against players; Spirit Tap procs only on a kill (one kill ends a duel); a longer Shadow Word: Pain is dispelled or outlasts the fight; wands are not a PvP weapon. The points buy the Discipline half of a PvP shadow priest: Silent Resolve 3/3 (-15% stun, fear and silence duration), Improved Power Word: Shield (+20% absorb; casts are not pushed back while it holds), Inner Focus (a free, +25% crit Mind Blast), Martyrdom and Meditation (PvP profile +8.8 points; the 2026-09-27 priest audit's 11/0/40 direction).
