## Summary

Deep Discipline for Power Infusion (+20% spell damage for a mage/warlock every 3 minutes) and Mental Strength, keeping Divine Spirit and Inner Focus, with Holy taken to Inspiration and Improved Healing. Less raw healing than 21/30/0; taken once the raid is over-geared on healing and wants DPS.
