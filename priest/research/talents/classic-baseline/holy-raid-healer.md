## Summary

The standard Classic raid priest: Spiritual Healing (+10% healing), Spiritual Guidance (Spirit to healing), Holy Specialization + Inspiration for tank mitigation, Divine Fury for Greater Heal cast time, plus Divine Spirit, Inner Focus, Meditation and Mental Agility from Discipline.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Spiritual Healing** — +10% to all healing.
2. **Spiritual Guidance** — 25% of Spirit becomes healing power — Spirit is your best stat.
3. **Divine Spirit** — +40 Spirit raid buff (one priest per raid must have it).
4. **Inspiration** — +25% armor on the target after a crit heal — tank mitigation.
5. **Holy Specialization** — +5% Holy crit feeds Inspiration.
6. **Divine Fury** — -0.5s on Greater Heal/Heal/Smite/Holy Fire.
7. **Improved Healing** — -15% mana on Heal/Greater Heal.
8. **Meditation** — +15% mana regen while casting.
9. **Inner Focus** — Free +25% crit spell every 3 minutes.
10. **Healing Focus** — 70% pushback resistance — mandatory on any fight with raid damage.
11. **Mental Agility** — -10% mana on instants (Renew, Shield, Fade).
12. **Improved Power Word: Fortitude** — +30% Fortitude for the raid.

## Notes

- Greater Heal on tanks, Flash Heal/Renew on the raid; use Inner Focus with Greater Heal or Prayer of Healing.
- Improved Prayer of Healing (2) is worth taking on heavy raid-damage fights by dropping Healing Focus.
