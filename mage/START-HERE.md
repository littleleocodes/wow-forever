# Mage: start here

[All classes](../START-HERE.md)

Frost for Molten Core/Blackwing Lair, Fire once fire immunity ends, one Winter's Chill mage per raid — that was Classic. Forever keeps every Frost talent and adds Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost and a 3-rank Shatter; Fire gains Hot Streak; Arcane gains Arcane Blast and Missile Barrage. Playable by 6 races (Orc is new) plus Skyborne (Alliance).
