# Mage

> Classic: Frost for Molten Core and Blackwing Lair, Fire once fire immunity stops mattering, one Winter's Chill mage per raid.
> Forever adds Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost and a 3-rank Shatter to Frost, Heating Up to Fire, Arcane Blast, Missile Barrage and Arcane
> Wrath to Arcane, and the new Frostfire Bolt; Improved Scorch becomes a personal debuff and Winter's Chill (client 70753) a stacking
> boost to your next Cone of Cold. Playable by 6 races (Orc is new) plus Skyborne (Alliance).

## Forever headline changes (beta client build 1.60.1.70753, captured 2026-11-03)

Forever's mage has 53 talents (18 Arcane, 17 Fire, 18 Frost). Against Classic Era, 3 Classic talents are gone, 2 are renamed and about 10 are new. Spell numbers are in the [spell page](research/spells/README.md); talent trees in the [talent trees](research/talents/forever/talent-trees.md).

- **Removed talents:** Magic Attunement, Improved Arcane Explosion, Improved Mana Shield (Arcane). Dropped since the 2026-09-13 demo: Arcane Focus, Wake of Fire and Piercing Ice (70753).
- **New talents:** Arcane Geometry, Arcane Impact, Arcane Blast, Arcane Shielding, Missile Barrage, Arcane Wrath (Arcane; Arcane Wrath since 70425, the 16-point talent); Heating Up (Fire; called Hot Streak until build 70170); Ice Lance, Fingers of Frost (Frost).
- **Renamed:** Improved Arcane Missiles is Improved Channeling (5 ranks: Arcane Missiles 20% a rank, Arcane Blast and Arcane Wrath 14% a rank, so 100% and 70% at 5 points); Incinerate is Incineration (5 ranks, +2% crit a rank for Fire Blast, Ice Lance, Arcane Blast and Scorch); Improved Fireball is Accelerant (5 ranks, 0.5 seconds off Fireball, Frostfire Bolt and Arcane Wrath in all; 70753); Elemental Precision is Precision (3 ranks, +2% hit with all spells each; 70753).
- **Moved or reworked:** Improved Fire Blast 2 ranks (Fire Blast's cooldown -2 sec and +15 yards range; Classic had 3 ranks of -0.5 sec); Missile Barrage 2 ranks in tier 1 (20% a rank for Arcane Blast, 10% a rank for Arcane Wrath, Fireball, Frostbolt and Frostfire Bolt: 40% and 20% at 2 points); Critical Mass 3 ranks (+2% Fire crit each); Fire Power 3 ranks, 1/2/3% more Fire damage per Fire effect of yours on the target, at most 4/8/12%; Arcane Meditation 3 ranks (17/33/50% of your mana regeneration while casting); Shatter 3 ranks (+17/33/50% crit against frozen targets); Magic Absorption 2 ranks; Arcane Resilience 3 ranks (armor equal to 25% of your Intellect a rank, 70753); Impact, Burning Soul 3 ranks; Ice Block to tier 4, Cold Snap to tier 5; Arcane Mind 5 ranks (+2% Intellect and +20% Arcane crit damage each). Improved Scorch raises only your own Fire damage (+3% a stack, 5 stacks, 30 seconds): no longer a raid debuff. Winter's Chill (5 ranks, 20% a rank, 70425 and later) no longer adds crit: Frostbolt and a Fingers of Frost Ice Lance put a stack on the target (5 stacks), and each stack raises the damage of your next Cone of Cold (20% and 7% of your spell damage, per the tooltip).
- **Spells:** Frostfire Bolt (ranks at 40, 50 and 60; a 3-second cast, 270-314 plus 57 over 9 seconds at rank 3, a 40% slow; counts as Fire and Frost, checked against the lower of the two resistances) and Teleport: Dalaran (level 50). Frost Blast, which the 70425 notes listed, is not in the 70753 spell data. Arcane Blast: a 1.5-second cast, 218-254 at rank 5; each cast gives you 12% of your Intellect as spell damage and adds 100% to its own next cost, 4 stacks, 18 seconds from the first. Arcane Wrath (the talent): 406-496 Arcane damage at rank 4, a 3.5-second cast, 6-second cooldown, plus spell damage equal to 5% of your maximum mana. Cone of Cold: 5 ranks to level 58 (328-358 at rank 5), and the first 4 targets are slowed 10% more for 3 seconds longer. Fingers of Frost: 15% per chill, your next 2 spells act as if the target were frozen. Ice Lance: 300% more damage to frozen targets. Heating Up: a non-periodic crit from Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast or Scorch takes 25% off your next Pyroblast's cast, 3 stacks, and every Fire Blast hit adds a stack.
- **Beta notes (to 2026-10-08):** wands no longer gain spell damage; ranks far below your level get less of your spell damage and fewer talent procs; Arcane Missiles checks line of sight once, at the start of the channel; Ignite no longer double-dips on damage bonuses; Improved Scorch and Winter's Chill no longer roll a second resist.
- **Beta notes (16 Oct, build 70425):** wands need 0.5 seconds standing still before shooting; Presence of Mind is consumed by spell casts,
  and Heating Up is not consumed when Presence of Mind made the Pyroblast instant; Fire Blast always adds a Heating Up charge (a crit adds
  two); Clearcasting rolls once per spell (not per target struck), not from Dampen or Amplify Magic and not out of combat; Missile Barrage
  no longer reduces a second Arcane Missiles; Frost Channeling reduces threat; Frostbite and Fingers of Frost trigger from Ice Armor and
  Frost Armor.
- **Simulator assumptions:** the raid numbers on the generated pages come from the community's wowsims/forever simulator, a model of the beta rather than a measurement; our runs assume TBC's formula for the low-rank penalty, since Blizzard published none.
