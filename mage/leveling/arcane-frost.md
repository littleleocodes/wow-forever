## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Arcane Power**, **Presence of Mind** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up. Pull from as far away as you can; **Presence of Mind** at the pull, and **Arcane Power** too while you have more than half your mana.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Arcane Blast** while the mob walks in: at most two in a row, only if the cast ends before the mob reaches you and costs no more than a quarter of your mana.
2. **Arcane Missiles** to spend the stacks, and at once when **Missile Barrage** lights up (after your two stacks, or when the mob is on you); none below about a third of the mob's health.
3. **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you (**Cone of Cold** if it is on cooldown), then step back; **Ice Lance** while it is frozen.
4. **Frostbolt** when **Arcane Missiles** is out (low mana, or the mob under a third of its health); below a fifth, finish with **Fire Blast**, **Ice Lance** or your wand.
5. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below a third of your mana, **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

This hybrid levels on the Arcane list: **Arcane Missiles** from range, **Frost Nova** and a step back when the mob arrives, **Ice Lance** into the frozen mob. **Arcane Blast** comes at level 49 and **Missile Barrage** at 50; until then, skip the **Arcane Blast** line.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-31 go into Frost for **Ice Lance** (20), **Shatter** (25-29) and **Fingers of Frost** (30-31). In the fight lab **Ice Lance**, **Missile Barrage** and **Shatter** are this build's three most valuable talents at level 60: a frozen mob takes 300% more damage from **Ice Lance**, with 50% more crit chance from **Shatter**.
- **Arcane Missiles** is the main spell at range, but only while the mob has more than 35% health: a full 5-second channel would overkill. Below that, **Frostbolt**; below 20%, **Fire Blast**, **Ice Lance** or the wand.
- From level 49, **Arcane Blast** twice while the mob walks in. Each cast makes your next other spell 10% stronger (**Arcane Missiles** 15%, by beta logs) and the next **Arcane Blast** 175% more expensive, so the fight lab stops at two, and starts one only while it costs at most a quarter of your mana and will finish before the mob reaches you. **Arcane Missiles** then spends the stacks.
- From level 50, **Missile Barrage** (40% per **Arcane Blast**, 20% per **Frostbolt**, counting only spells that land) makes your next Missiles free and twice as fast: cast them once your two stacks are up or the mob is on you. The fight lab rates it the build's second most valuable talent.
- **Frost Nova** and a step back when the mob arrives: **Arcane Blast** and **Arcane Missiles** both need you to stand, and this build has no **Improved Channeling**: every hit you take cuts the channel short.

**Caveats**

- This build has no **Arcane Power** or **Presence of Mind**: skip the pull cooldowns in the list.
- Mana: **Arcane Missiles** costs 655 at top rank and every **Arcane Blast** stack makes the next one dearer. Drink below a third of your mana and use **Evocation** between fights when it is ready.
- Pulls of several mobs: **Arcane Explosion** hits everything within 10 yards.

**When to change it**

- On a nearly dead mob, skip **Arcane Missiles**, even a free one from **Missile Barrage**; finish with **Fire Blast**, **Ice Lance** or the wand.
- If the mob would reach you before an **Arcane Blast** finishes, do not start it: **Frost Nova**, step back, and cast while it is frozen.

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-15; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
