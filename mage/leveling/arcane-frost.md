## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Cold Snap** is a talent this build does not take: where a line below names it, that part does not apply. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30, **Mage Armor** from 34) and **Arcane Intellect** up. Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in (it slows it) and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a fifth of its health; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends, wand it until it arrives (the tuned rules keep **Fire Blast** for the finish).
2. **Arcane Missiles** whenever **Missile Barrage** lights up (your Frostbolts give it): free and twice as fast; not while the mob hits you in melee unless it is frozen.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee; if your build has **Cold Snap**, it resets **Frost Nova** when more than 8 seconds of cooldown are left and the mob still has 40% of its health. No Cone of Cold.
4. **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen (**Frost Nova**, **Frostbite** or **Fingers of Frost**).
5. To finish: a **Fire Blast** (not below a tenth of the mob's health) or **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand.
6. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana, **Evocation** in place of a drink when it is ready and you are under 15% mana, eat below 40% of your health. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. **Evocation** is not a sit and brings no food.

## Why this rotation

This hybrid plays the Frost list with one Arcane addition: **Frostbolt** at range, **Frost Nova** and a step back when the mob arrives, **Ice Lance** into the freeze, and a free, fast **Arcane Missiles** whenever **Missile Barrage** lights up. Its Frost points come first (**Ice Lance** at 20, **Shatter** complete by 29, **Fingers of Frost** at 30-31), then Arcane from 32 to 60 (**Wand Specialization**, **Arcane Resilience**, **Improved Channeling**, **Missile Barrage** complete at 38, **Arcane Concentration** by 46, **Arcane Meditation** by 50, **Presence of Mind** at 53, **Arcane Mind** at 54-56, **Arcane Resilience**'s second point at 57, **Arcane Instability** at 58-60), with **Arctic Reach**'s second point at 39 (its first is at 27). In the open leveling search's 20-60 race of 2026-11-16 it was the fastest mage leveling build, about 27.8 seconds per kill level-weighted against 29.1 for the Arcane page's build and 28.9 for the Frost page's; the page's numbers table has each level.

**Why it works**

- **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can and as the filler while the mob has more than a fifth of its health: its slow buys casts before the mob arrives, its chill rolls **Frostbite** (15% to freeze) and **Fingers of Frost** (15%: the next 2 spells count as cast on a frozen target), and with **Missile Barrage** each one has a 20% chance to light up a free **Arcane Missiles**. If the mob would reach you before the cast ends and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives; if it is on you with **Frost Nova** down, keep casting.
- **Arcane Missiles** only on a **Missile Barrage** proc: half the channel (2.5 seconds), no mana, missiles every half second. Cast it at range or into a freeze, not while the mob is hitting you unless it is frozen: hits can cut the channel short; **Improved Channeling** (4 of 5 points by level 51) gives each hit an 80% chance to cost no time. Without **Missile Barrage** a kill takes about 1.8 seconds longer at 60 in the open search.
- **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back; **Ice Lance** while it is frozen: 300% more damage to a frozen target, 50% more crit chance from **Shatter** and bigger crits from **Ice Shards**. **Ice Lance** has an 8-second cooldown, one per freeze. At 60 the build loses about 2.9 seconds per kill without **Shatter**, 2.0 without **Ice Lance**, 1.7 without **Ice Shards** and 1.1 without **Fingers of Frost** (open search, 2026-11-15, with **Mage Armor**).
- **Fire Blast** is the finish and nothing else: the tuned rules (2026-11-06) cast it only while the mob is between a tenth and a fifth of its health, and start no **Frostbolt** below a fifth. Below a tenth an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or the wand, finishes it. It is not the answer to a mob that reaches you.
- The rest of the Arcane half is mana: **Arcane Concentration** gives a 10% chance after a damage spell hits that your next one is free, and **Arcane Meditation** keeps another half of your regeneration going while you cast (all of it with **Mage Armor**); **Wand Specialization** makes the finishing wand hits 25% stronger. **Presence of Mind** (level 53) makes one cast instant every 3 minutes: the fight lab opens a pull with an instant **Frostbolt**. The last seven points: **Arcane Mind** (54-56) 6% more Intellect, a second **Arcane Resilience** point (57) for armor, **Arcane Instability** (58-60) 3% more spell damage and crit. The tuned rules drink only below 15% of your mana and eat below 40% of your health, later than the Frost page's list eats.

**Caveats**

- This build takes no Cold Snap: skip that part of the list. Before the Arcane half it is a plain Frost mage without **Missile Barrage**.
- **Cone of Cold** stays out of single pulls: the tuned rules switch it off (kills measured faster without).
- Rest: drink below 15% of your mana, **Evocation** in place of that drink when it is ready (under 15% mana), eat below 40% of your health. Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).
- Armor: **Mage Armor** from 34 (the tuned rules of 2026-11-15): it keeps half your mana regeneration going while you cast, about 4% faster per kill at 34-40 and 2% at 46 in the fight lab. From 50, with **Arcane Meditation** complete, the two armors are even (within about 1%): wear either.
- This is the open leveling search's 28/0/23 winner of 2026-11-15, 0.6% faster per kill at levels 20-60 than the 23/0/28 build it replaced: its last five Frost points (Improved Frost Nova, Permafrost, Winter's Chill) moved to **Arcane Mind** and **Arcane Instability**. The leveling filler pass of the same day, under **Mage Armor**, moved an **Ice Shards** point to **Arctic Reach** at 27 and an **Improved Channeling** point to **Improved Counterspell** at 52. Arcane Blast is not in this build: skip any line for it.

**When to change it**

- On a nearly dead mob, skip even a free **Arcane Missiles**: an **Ice Lance** or **Fire Blast** that will kill, or the wand.
- Mob on you with **Frost Nova** down: keep casting **Frostbolt**; the list does not use **Fire Blast** or **Cone of Cold** here.
