## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Cold Snap** is a talent this build does not take: where a line below names it, that part does not apply. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30, **Mage Armor** from 34) and **Arcane Intellect** up. Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in (it slows it) and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a tenth of its health; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends, wand it until it arrives (the tuned rules keep **Fire Blast** for the finish).
2. **Arcane Missiles** whenever **Missile Barrage** lights up (your Frostbolts give it): free and twice as fast; not while the mob hits you in melee unless it is frozen.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee; if your build has **Cold Snap**, it resets **Frost Nova** when more than 8 seconds of cooldown are left and the mob still has 40% of its health. No Cone of Cold.
4. **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen (**Frost Nova**, **Frostbite** or **Fingers of Frost**).
5. To finish: a **Fire Blast** (not below a tenth of the mob's health) or **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand.
6. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana, **Evocation** in place of a drink when it is ready and you are under 15% mana, eat below 40% of your health. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. **Evocation** is not a sit and brings no food.

## Why this rotation

This hybrid plays the Frost list with one Arcane addition: **Frostbolt** at range, **Frost Nova** and a step back when the mob arrives, **Ice Lance** into the freeze, and a free, fast **Arcane Missiles** whenever **Missile Barrage** lights up. Its Frost points come first (**Ice Lance** at 20, **Shatter** complete by 28), then Arcane from 29 (**Wand Specialization**, **Arcane Resilience**, **Improved Channeling**, **Missile Barrage** complete at 35), **Arctic Reach**'s second point at 36 (its first is at 26) and **Fingers of Frost** at 37-38, then Arcane to 60 (**Arcane Concentration** by 46, **Arcane Meditation** by 50, **Presence of Mind** at 53, **Arcane Mind** at 54-56, **Arcane Resilience**'s second point at 57, **Arcane Instability** at 58-60) with **Frost Channeling**'s third point at 42. In the 20-60 race of the redo of 2026-11-21 (your own regeneration running while you eat and drink) it takes about 26.9 seconds per kill level-weighted: a tie within the noise with the Frost page's build (26.9) and the Arcane page's (26.3). The page's numbers table has each level.

**Why it works**

- **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can and as the filler while the mob has more than a tenth of its health (a fifth before the re-tune of 2026-11-20): its slow buys casts before the mob arrives, its chill rolls **Frostbite** (one point: 5% to freeze the mob) and, from 37, **Fingers of Frost** (15%: the next 2 spells count as cast on a frozen target; the fight lab rolls the two together, so that 15% also freezes), and with **Missile Barrage** each one has a 20% chance to light up a free **Arcane Missiles**. If the mob would reach you before the cast ends and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives; if it is on you with **Frost Nova** down, keep casting.
- **Arcane Missiles** only on a **Missile Barrage** proc: half the channel (2.5 seconds), no mana, missiles every half second. Cast it at range or into a freeze, not while the mob is hitting you unless it is frozen: hits can cut the channel short; **Improved Channeling** (4 of 5 points by level 51) gives each hit an 80% chance to cost no time. Taking out **Missile Barrage** costs about 2.4 seconds per kill at 60 in the open search of 2026-11-20.
- **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back; **Ice Lance** while it is frozen: 300% more damage to a frozen target, 50% more crit chance from **Shatter** and bigger crits from **Ice Shards**. **Ice Lance** has an 8-second cooldown, one per freeze. In the open search of 2026-11-20, taking out **Shatter** costs about 2.6 seconds per kill at 60, **Improved Frostbolt** 2.1, **Ice Lance** 1.9 and **Fingers of Frost** 1.1.
- **Fire Blast** is the finish and nothing else: the tuned rules cast it only while the mob is between a tenth and a fifth of its health, when it will kill or the mob is on you, and since 2026-11-20 they keep **Frostbolt** going down to a tenth (a fifth before). Below a tenth an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or the wand, finishes it. It is not the answer to a mob that reaches you with health to spare.
- The rest of the Arcane half is mana: **Arcane Concentration** gives a 10% chance after a damage spell hits that your next one is free, and **Arcane Meditation** keeps another half of your regeneration going while you cast (all of it with **Mage Armor**); **Wand Specialization** makes the finishing wand hits 25% stronger. **Presence of Mind** (level 53) makes one cast instant every 3 minutes: the fight lab opens a pull with an instant **Frostbolt**. The last seven points: **Arcane Mind** (54-56) 6% more Intellect, a second **Arcane Resilience** point (57) for armor, **Arcane Instability** (58-60) 3% more spell damage and crit. The tuned rules drink only below 15% of your mana and eat below 40% of your health.

**Caveats**

- This build takes no Cold Snap: skip that part of the list. Until **Missile Barrage** at 34 it plays as a plain Frost mage, and its **Fingers of Frost** comes only at 37.
- **Cone of Cold** stays out of single pulls: the tuned rules switch it off (kills measured faster without).
- Rest: drink below 15% of your mana, **Evocation** in place of that drink when it is ready (under 15% mana), eat below 40% of your health. Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).
- Armor: **Mage Armor** from 34 (the tuned rules of 2026-11-15): it keeps half your mana regeneration going while you cast, about 4% faster per kill at 34-40 and 2% at 46 in the fight lab. From 50, with **Arcane Meditation** complete, the two armors are even (within about 1%): wear either.
- This is the open leveling search's 29/0/22 winner of 2026-11-20, 1.7% faster per kill at levels 20-60 than the build it replaced. The leveling filler pass of the same day moved Arcane Geometry and an **Ice Shards** point to **Frost Channeling** (42 and 23). Arcane Blast is not in this build: skip any line for it.

**When to change it**

- On a nearly dead mob, skip even a free **Arcane Missiles**: an **Ice Lance** or **Fire Blast** that will kill, or the wand.
- Mob on you with **Frost Nova** down: keep casting **Frostbolt**; the list uses **Fire Blast** only in the mob's last fifth, and never **Cone of Cold**.
