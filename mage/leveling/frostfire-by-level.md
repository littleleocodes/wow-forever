# Frostfire Mage — leveling, level by level

[Mage](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Frostfire](frostfire.md) > Level by level

What a Frostfire mage presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. It levels as a Frost mage until 40: **Frostbolt** at range, **Frost Nova** and a step back when the mob arrives, **Ice Lance** into the freeze. At 40 **Frostfire Bolt** replaces **Frostbolt**, and the Fire points that follow make it hit harder. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 2/25/24 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/mage/60/2-55501300000033-0325320301003002-12)), the one on the [Frostfire leveling page](frostfire.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Fireball** is your only attack. |
| 4-9 | **Frostbolt** replaces Fireball. It slows the mob, so you get more casts before it arrives. |
| 10-19 | **Frost Nova** roots the mob at your feet: step back and keep casting. Talent points start. |
| 20-23 | **Ice Lance** (talent): an instant that deals four times its damage to a frozen mob. |
| 24-29 | **Counterspell** for mobs that cast. **Shatter** makes spells crit far more often on a frozen mob. |
| 30-39 | **Fingers of Frost** (talent): a chill can make your next 2 spells hit as if the mob were frozen. **Ice Armor** replaces Frost Armor. |
| 40-60 | **Frostfire Bolt** replaces Frostbolt: it counts as Frost and Fire, so both trees' talents apply. Below a third of the mob's health, no bolt. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, conjuring, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes, so you never relearn a key. Bold marks what arrives in that stage: drag it onto the slot when you train it. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [mage keybinds page](keybinds.md).

## Levels 1-3: Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Arcane Intellect (1), **Fireball** (1), Frost Armor (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Fireball; Middle mouse = Wand; Shift+C = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Fireball.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Fireball** until the mob is dead.
2. If it reaches you, keep casting. Nothing hits hard at these levels.

- Keep **Frost Armor** and **Arcane Intellect** up at all times, from here to 60.
- The fight lab does not simulate these three levels: this rotation starts with **Frostbolt** at 4.

## Levels 4-9: Frostbolt replaces Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Conjure Water (4), **Frostbolt** (4), Conjure Food (6), Fire Blast (6), Arcane Missiles (8), Polymorph (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Frostbolt at 8.

**Keys to set:** Q = Frostbolt; Mouse 5 = Fire Blast (at 6); 2 = Polymorph (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can, again and again, also when the mob is on you.
2. Your wand finishes a mob that is almost dead, once you own one.

- **Conjure Water** (4) and **Conjure Food** (6) save you buying drink and food. Take **Fireball** off your bar.
- **Fire Blast** (6): the tuned rules never cast it in this list, at any level. Put it on Mouse 5 anyway, for a mob that runs away.
- **Polymorph** (8): when a second mob joins, sheep it, kill the first, then fight the sheep. **Arcane Missiles** (8) is not part of this list.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 22 at level 4, 30 at level 9. Presses per kill at level 4: Frostbolt 4.8, Wand 0.8.*

## Levels 10-19: Frost Nova: the kite begins

**The trainer teaches:** **Frost Nova** (10), Dampen Magic (12), Slow Fall (12), Arcane Explosion (14), Flamestrike (16), Amplify Magic (18), Remove Lesser Curse (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Fire Blast at 14; Frostbolt at 14.

**Talents:** 10-11 Precision, 12-14 Improved Frostbolt, 15-17 Frostbite, 18-19 Ice Shards.

**Keys to set:** E = Frost Nova; Shift+E = Arcane Explosion (at 14); Shift+3 = Remove Lesser Curse (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies.
2. If the mob would reach you before a **Frostbolt** finishes and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
4. If it reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting **Frostbolt**.
5. Your wand finishes a mob that is almost dead.

- **Precision** (10-11) for hit, **Improved Frostbolt** (12-14) for a faster **Frostbolt**.
- **Frostbite** (15-17) sometimes freezes a mob your chill hits. Treat it as a free **Frost Nova**: step back and cast. **Ice Shards** (18-19) makes your Frost crits bigger.
- **Arcane Explosion** (14) and **Flamestrike** (16) are for packs. One mob at a time never needs them.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 29 at level 10, 40 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Frostbolt 5.5, Wand 1.0, Frost Nova 0.7.*

## Levels 20-23: Ice Lance into every freeze

**The trainer teaches:** Blink (20), Blizzard (20), Evocation (20), Fire Ward (20), Mana Shield (20), Frost Ward (22), Scorch (22).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Explosion at 22; Fire Blast at 22; Frostbolt at 20; Polymorph at 20.

**Talents:** 20 Ice Lance, 21-23 Ice Shards.

**Keys to set:** R = Ice Lance; F = Blink; 3 = Mana Shield; 4 = Evocation; Shift+R = Blizzard; Shift+Mouse 5 = Fire Ward; Shift+Mouse 4 = Frost Ward (at 22).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, F Blink, 3 Mana Shield.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies.
2. If the mob would reach you before a **Frostbolt** finishes and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
4. **Ice Lance** the moment the mob is frozen, by **Frost Nova** or by **Frostbite**. One per freeze: it has an 8-second cooldown.
5. If it reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting **Frostbolt**.
6. Finish with an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- **Ice Shards** (21-23) gets its last points.
- **Evocation** (20) is for between fights: channel it whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana. **Blizzard** (20) is for packs.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 30 at level 20. Presses per kill at level 20: Frostbolt 4.2, Ice Lance 1.2, Wand 0.7, Frost Nova 0.6.*

## Levels 24-29: Counterspell and Shatter

**The trainer teaches:** **Counterspell** (24), Cone of Cold (26), Conjure Mana Agate (28).

**New ranks to buy:** Blizzard at 28; Frost Nova at 26; Frostbolt at 26; Ice Lance at 28; Mana Shield at 28.

**Talents:** 24 Frost Channeling, 25-27 Shatter, 28-29 Frost Channeling.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Counterspell; C = Cone of Cold (at 26).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, F Blink, Mouse 4 Counterspell, 3 Mana Shield.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting: it stops standing back and walks to you.
2. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies.
3. If the mob would reach you before a **Frostbolt** finishes and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives.
4. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
5. **Ice Lance** the moment the mob is frozen. One per freeze.
6. If it reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting **Frostbolt**.
7. Finish with an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- **Shatter** (25-27) is why the **Ice Lance** into a freeze is your biggest hit from here on. **Frost Channeling** (24, 28-29) makes Frost spells cheaper.
- **Cone of Cold** (26) goes on C for packs. The tuned rules never cast it on a single mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 34 at level 24, 35 at level 29. Presses per kill at level 24: Frostbolt 5.0, Ice Lance 1.7, Wand 1.1, Frost Nova 0.8, Counterspell 0.2.*

## Levels 30-39: Fingers of Frost

**The trainer teaches:** Ice Armor (30), Mage Armor (34), Conjure Mana Jade (38).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Explosion at 30, 38; Blizzard at 36; Cone of Cold at 34; Fire Blast at 30, 38; Fire Ward at 30; Frost Ward at 32; Frostbolt at 32, 38; Ice Lance at 34; Mana Shield at 36.

**Talents:** 30-31 Fingers of Frost, 32 Improved Frost Nova, 33-34 Wand Specialization, 35-39 Accelerant.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, F Blink, Mouse 4 Counterspell, 3 Mana Shield.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting.
2. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies.
3. If the mob would reach you before a **Frostbolt** finishes and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives.
4. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
5. **Ice Lance** whenever the mob is frozen or **Fingers of Frost** lights up, as soon as its 8-second cooldown allows.
6. If it reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting **Frostbolt**.
7. Finish with an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- **Ice Armor** (30) replaces **Frost Armor** as your armor spell.
- **Improved Frost Nova** (32) brings **Frost Nova** back 2 seconds sooner.
- **Wand Specialization** (33-34) makes your wand hits stronger: from 40 the wand finishes a lot of mobs.
- **Accelerant** (35-39) shortens **Frostfire Bolt** once you have it at 40; it does nothing for **Frostbolt**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 35 at level 30, 38 at level 39. Presses per kill at level 30: Frostbolt 4.5, Ice Lance 1.7, Frost Nova 0.8, Wand 0.8, Counterspell 0.2.*

## Levels 40-60: Frostfire Bolt replaces Frostbolt

**The trainer teaches:** **Frostfire Bolt** (40), Conjure Mana Citrine (48), Arcane Brilliance (56), Conjure Mana Ruby (58).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Explosion at 46, 54; Blizzard at 44, 52, 60; Cone of Cold at 42, 50, 58; Fire Blast at 46, 54; Fire Ward at 40, 50, 60; Frost Nova at 40, 54; Frost Ward at 42, 52, 60; Frostfire Bolt at 50, 60; Ice Lance at 42, 48, 56; Mana Shield at 44, 52, 60; Polymorph at 40, 60.

**Talents:** 40-44 Ignite, 45-47 Burning Soul, 48-49 Incineration, 50-52 Master of Elements, 53-54 Incineration, 55 Improved Frost Nova, 56-58 Critical Mass, 59 Incineration, 60 Impact.

**Keys to set:** Q = Frostfire Bolt.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostfire Bolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, F Blink, Mouse 4 Counterspell, 3 Mana Shield.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting.
2. **Frostfire Bolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than about a third of its health.
3. If the mob would reach you before a **Frostfire Bolt** finishes and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives.
4. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
5. **Ice Lance** whenever the mob is frozen or **Fingers of Frost** lights up, as soon as its 8-second cooldown allows.
6. If it reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting **Frostfire Bolt**.
7. Below about a third of its health: no bolt. An **Ice Lance** into a freeze or one that will kill, else your wand.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- **Frostfire Bolt** slows like **Frostbolt**, so its chill still rolls **Frostbite** and **Fingers of Frost**. Take **Frostbolt** off your bar.
- The tuned rules keep the bolt for the mob's first two thirds; below that the wand does the rest.
- **Ignite** (40-44), **Burning Soul** (45-47), **Master of Elements** (50-52) and **Critical Mass** (56-58) work on **Frostfire Bolt**; **Incineration** (48-49, 53-54, 59) adds crit to **Ice Lance**.
- **Improved Frost Nova** (55) gets its second point: **Frost Nova** comes back 4 seconds sooner. **Impact** (60) gives your Fire spells, **Frostfire Bolt** included, a small chance to stun the mob for 2 seconds.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 45 at level 40, 36 at level 50, 32 at level 60. Presses per kill at level 40: Wand 5.0, Frostfire Bolt 4.2, Ice Lance 2.6, Frost Nova 1.0, Counterspell 0.1.*

## Between fights

- Drink your conjured water when you are below about 15% of your mana, and eat below 40% of your health. Those are the tuned rules: resting earlier cost more time in the fight lab than it saved.
- **Evocation** (from 20) whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- Conjure both food and water yourself.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Frostbolt** to 40, then **Frostfire Bolt** at 50 and 60.

## Other ways to level this class

- The [Frost guide](frost-by-level.md) plays the same loop with **Frostbolt** all the way, and [Arcane-Frost](arcane-frost-by-level.md), the recommended mage leveling spec, adds free **Arcane Missiles**.
- Pulling packs is a different game: the AoE Frost build on the Frost page gathers about four mobs, freezes them with **Frost Nova** and channels **Blizzard**.
