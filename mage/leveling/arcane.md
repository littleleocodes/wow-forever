## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30, **Mage Armor** from 34) and **Arcane Intellect** up. Pull from as far away as you can; **Presence of Mind** at the pull (your first cast goes out instantly); **Arcane Power** at the pull when it is ready and you have more than half your mana (once you have the talent, at 42).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. With **Arcane Wrath**: cast it first on the pull if it will land before the mob reaches you, and again whenever it is ready and there is time.
2. **Arcane Blast** while the mob walks in: at most two in a row, only if the cast ends before the mob reaches you and costs no more than a quarter of your mana. Each stack gives all your spells spell damage (12% of your Intellect) for 18 seconds.
3. **Arcane Missiles** as your channel, also once the mob is on you (with **Improved Channeling**, hits do not cut it short), and at once when **Missile Barrage** lights up (after your two stacks, or when the mob is on you).
4. Mob on you: **Frost Nova** while it has more than about a tenth of its health, then step back and keep casting; **Cone of Cold** only when the mob is on you and below a fifth of its health, then step back; **Ice Lance** if the mob is frozen.
5. When you cannot afford **Arcane Missiles**: **Fire Blast** if the mob is on you, else **Frostbolt**; finish with a **Fire Blast** that will kill, or your wand.
6. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana and eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rules), **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana.

## Why this rotation

Arcane levels on its own spells: **Arcane Wrath** to open (from 25), two **Arcane Blast** stacks while the mob walks in, then **Arcane Missiles**, which you keep channeling when the mob reaches you; **Frost Nova** and a step back when it arrives. The page's order takes Arcane first (**Arcane Blast** at 20, **Missile Barrage** at 21-22, **Presence of Mind** at 30, **Arcane Power** at 42) with two Frost points for hit at 38-39, and puts the other 16 Frost points last, from 45. In the open leveling search's 20-60 race of 2026-11-15 it took about 29.4 seconds per kill level-weighted, behind the Arcane-Frost page's 28.3 and level with the Frost page's 29.5; the page's numbers table has each level.

**Why it works**

- **Presence of Mind** (level 30) at the pull makes your first cast instant (the opening **Arcane Wrath** in the fight lab). From level 42 **Arcane Power** goes at the pull too, whenever it is ready and you have more than half your mana: 15 seconds of 30% more spell damage at 30% more mana cost.
- **Arcane Wrath** opens when its cast (3.5 seconds) lands before the mob arrives, then goes out whenever it is ready (6-second cooldown) and the cast fits. It gains spell damage equal to 5% of your maximum mana, and each cast has a 20% chance to light up **Missile Barrage**.
- **Arcane Blast** twice while the mob walks in: each cast gives you 12% of your Intellect as spell damage for 18 seconds and doubles the next cast's cost, so the fight lab stops at two and starts one only while it costs at most a quarter of your mana and will finish before the mob reaches you. Each cast rolls 40% for **Missile Barrage**. Without **Arcane Blast** a kill at 60 takes about 2.4 seconds longer in the open search.
- **Arcane Missiles** is the main spell, at range and in melee: **Improved Channeling** at 5 points gives a 100% chance that hits do not interrupt the channel (70% for **Arcane Blast** and **Arcane Wrath**). **Missile Barrage** makes the next one free, 2.5 seconds long and twice as dense: cast it at once after your two stacks, or when the mob is on you. At level 60 the build loses about 4.7 seconds per kill without **Missile Barrage**, 1.9 without **Improved Channeling**, then **Arcane Concentration** (1.4), **Arcane Mind** (1.4), **Precision** (1.3), **Arcane Meditation** (1.3) and **Arcane Instability** (1.1).
- **Frost Nova** is in the list: when the mob reaches you and has more than about a tenth of its health, freeze it and step back, then keep channeling. The tuned rules (2026-11-06) hold back only **Cone of Cold**: it is cast just when the mob is on you, **Frost Nova** is down and the mob is below a fifth of its health.
- **Fire Blast** finishes a mob it will kill, and when you cannot afford **Arcane Missiles** it is the instant for a mob on you, else **Frostbolt**. The 18 Frost points: **Precision** (38-39) for hit, then **Improved Frostbolt** (45-47), **Ice Shards** (48, 52), **Improved Frost Nova** (49-50), **Frost Warding** (51), **Ice Lance** (53), **Improved Blizzard** (54-55), **Permafrost** (56-57) and **Shatter** (58-60). Only **Frost Nova** freezes the mob in this build (it has no **Frostbite**); in the open search **Ice Lance** into the freeze is worth about 0.5 seconds per kill at 60 and **Shatter** 0.8.

**Caveats**

- This build takes **Ice Lance** only at level 53: before that, skip its line of the list.
- Mana: **Arcane Missiles** costs 655 at top rank, every **Arcane Blast** stack makes the next one dearer. **Frostbolt** (290) is for when you cannot afford the Missiles. The tuned rules of 2026-11-15 drink below 15% of your mana, eat below 40% of your health and wear **Mage Armor** from 34 (with **Arcane Meditation** it keeps all of your regeneration going while you cast: 4% faster per kill at 34 in the fight lab, 0-1% from 40); use **Evocation** between fights when it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- Single pulls only: with several mobs on you, **Frost Nova** and **Arcane Explosion** are back in play.
- This is the open leveling search's 33/0/18 winner of 2026-11-15, 1.6% faster per kill at levels 20-60 than the 31/0/20 build it replaced: two points moved from **Ice Shards** to **Arcane Subtlety**, **Arcane Power** at 42 instead of 59 and **Ice Lance** at 53 instead of 50. The filler pass of the same day, under **Mage Armor**, moved a Frost Warding point to **Ice Shards** at 52.

**When to change it**

- If the mob would reach you before an **Arcane Blast** or **Arcane Wrath** finishes, do not start it: channel **Arcane Missiles** instead (an instant cast with **Presence of Mind** is the exception).
- A mob that hits harder than a normal same-level mob is worth a **Frost Nova** even when it is nearly dead: the rule that skips it only reflects the mobs the fight lab models.
- Against a caster mob, **Counterspell** it when it starts a cast, as every mage list in the fight lab does.
