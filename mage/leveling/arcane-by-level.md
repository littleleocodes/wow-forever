# Arcane Mage — leveling, level by level

[Mage](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Arcane](arcane.md) > Level by level

What an Arcane mage presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Arcane levels on **Arcane Missiles**, a channel you keep going even when the mob is on you. From 20 two **Arcane Blast** casts on the walk-in make every spell hit harder, and from 25 **Arcane Wrath** opens the pull. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 30/0/21 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/mage/60/05100520310031153--2323013031200001-12)), the one on the [Arcane leveling page](arcane.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Fireball** is your only attack. |
| 4-5 | **Frostbolt** replaces Fireball until the Missiles arrive at 8. It slows the mob, so you get more casts before it arrives. |
| 6-7 | **Fire Blast** for the moment the mob reaches you, and to finish it. |
| 8-9 | **Arcane Missiles** replaces Frostbolt: a channel that keeps hitting while the mob walks in and while it hits you. |
| 10-19 | **Frost Nova** roots the mob at your feet: step back and keep channeling. Talent points start. |
| 20-24 | **Arcane Blast** (talent): two casts on the walk-in add spell damage to all your spells. Free, fast Missiles arrive at 21. |
| 25-29 | **Arcane Wrath** (talent): a big 3.5-second cast that opens each pull and goes out again whenever it fits. **Counterspell** for casters. |
| 30-49 | **Presence of Mind** (talent): every 3 minutes the opening **Arcane Wrath** goes out instantly. **Ice Armor** replaces Frost Armor. |
| 50-60 | **Ice Lance** (talent): an instant that deals four times its damage to a frozen mob, after every **Frost Nova**. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, conjuring, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes, so you never relearn a key. Bold marks what arrives in that stage: drag it onto the slot when you train it. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [mage keybinds page](keybinds.md); this spec's macro is in the notes below the tables.

## Levels 1-3: Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Arcane Intellect (1), **Fireball** (1), Frost Armor (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Fireball; Middle mouse = Wand.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Fireball.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Fireball** until the mob is dead.
2. If it reaches you, keep casting. Nothing hits hard at these levels.

- Keep **Frost Armor** and **Arcane Intellect** up at all times, from here to 60.
- The fight lab does not simulate these three levels: its Arcane rotation starts with **Frostbolt** at 4.

## Levels 4-5: Frostbolt replaces Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Conjure Water (4), **Frostbolt** (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Frostbolt.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can, again and again.
2. Your wand finishes a mob that is almost dead, once you own one.

- **Conjure Water** (4): drink between fights instead of waiting for your mana.
- Take **Fireball** off your bar.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 22 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Frostbolt 4.8, Wand 0.8.*

## Levels 6-7: Fire Blast, your instant

**The trainer teaches:** Conjure Food (6), **Fire Blast** (6).

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Fire Blast.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can.
2. **Fire Blast** when the mob is on you, and whenever it will kill.
3. Your wand finishes it.

- **Conjure Food** (6) saves you buying bread.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 27 at level 6. Presses per kill at level 6: Frostbolt 5.0, Fire Blast 1.0, Wand 0.7.*

## Levels 8-9: Arcane Missiles, your main spell

**The trainer teaches:** **Arcane Missiles** (8), Polymorph (8).

**Keys to set:** Q = Arcane Missiles; 2 = Polymorph.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Arcane Missiles, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Arcane Missiles** from as far away as you can, again and again, also when the mob is on you.
2. **Fire Blast** when it will kill the mob.

- **Polymorph** (8): when a second mob joins, sheep it, kill the first, then fight the sheep.
- Take **Frostbolt** off your bar: the rotation casts it only when you cannot afford the Missiles, and the fight lab never ran that low.
- A hit while you channel pushes the Missiles back. From 11 your talents start to stop that.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 24 at level 8. Presses per kill at level 8: Arcane Missiles 2.2, Fire Blast 0.4.*

## Levels 10-19: Frost Nova: freeze, step back, channel

**The trainer teaches:** **Frost Nova** (10), Dampen Magic (12), Slow Fall (12), Arcane Explosion (14), Flamestrike (16), Amplify Magic (18), Remove Lesser Curse (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Missiles at 16; Fire Blast at 14.

**Talents:** 10 Arcane Resilience, 11-14 Improved Channeling, 15-19 Arcane Concentration.

**Keys to set:** E = Frost Nova; Shift+E = Arcane Explosion (at 14); Shift+3 = Remove Lesser Curse (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Arcane Missiles, E Frost Nova, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Arcane Missiles** from maximum range, and whenever nothing below applies.
2. **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
3. With **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep channeling while it hits you.
4. **Fire Blast** when it will kill the mob.

- **Improved Channeling** (11-14) makes hits push your Missiles back less, and not at all with the fifth point at 23.
- **Arcane Concentration** (15-19) makes about one cast in ten free. **Arcane Resilience** (10) adds armor from your Intellect.
- **Arcane Explosion** (14) and **Flamestrike** (16) are for packs. One mob at a time never needs them.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 26 at level 10, 36 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Arcane Missiles 2.5, Fire Blast 0.5, Frost Nova 0.4.*

## Levels 20-24: Arcane Blast stacks

**The trainer teaches:** Blink (20), Blizzard (20), Evocation (20), Fire Ward (20), Mana Shield (20), Frost Ward (22), Scorch (22), Counterspell (24).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Missiles at 24; Fire Blast at 22.

**Talents:** 20 Arcane Blast, 21-22 Missile Barrage, 23 Improved Channeling, 24 Arcane Impact.

**Keys to set:** F = Blink; 1 = Arcane Blast; 3 = Mana Shield; 4 = Evocation; Shift+R = Blizzard; Shift+Mouse 5 = Fire Ward; Shift+Mouse 4 = Frost Ward (at 22); Mouse 4 = Counterspell (at 24).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Arcane Missiles, E Frost Nova, F Blink, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, 1 Arcane Blast, 3 Mana Shield.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Arcane Blast** twice in a row while the mob walks in, each only if its cast ends before the mob reaches you and it costs no more than a quarter of the mana you have left. Not on a mob that is almost dead.
2. **Arcane Missiles** after your two stacks, and whenever nothing else applies. From 21, when free Missiles light up, cast them at once after your stacks, or at once if the mob is on you.
3. **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
4. With **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep channeling while it hits you.
5. **Fire Blast** when it will kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- **Missile Barrage** (21-22): an **Arcane Blast** that lands can make your next **Arcane Missiles** free, half as long and twice as dense.
- Each **Arcane Blast** makes the next one cost more; the stacks last 18 seconds from the first, and the fight lab stops at two.
- **Arcane Impact** (24) adds crit to your Arcane spells. **Counterspell** (24): a mob that starts casting stops standing back and walks to you.
- **Evocation** (20) is for between fights. **Blink** (20) and **Mana Shield** (20) are for a pull that goes wrong. **Blizzard** (20) is for packs.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 35 at level 20. Presses per kill at level 20: Arcane Missiles 2.5, Arcane Blast 2.0, Frost Nova 0.6, Fire Blast 0.4.*

## Levels 25-29: Arcane Wrath opens the pull

**The trainer teaches:** Cone of Cold (26), Conjure Mana Agate (28).

**New ranks to buy:** Frost Nova at 26.

**Talents:** 25 Arcane Wrath, 26-28 Arcane Meditation, 29 Arcane Impact.

**Keys to set:** Shift+Q = Arcane Wrath; C = Cone of Cold (at 26).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Arcane Missiles, E Frost Nova, F Blink, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, Mouse 4 Counterspell, 1 Arcane Blast, 3 Mana Shield, Shift+Q Arcane Wrath.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting: it stops standing back and walks to you.
2. Open with **Arcane Wrath** when its cast will end before the mob reaches you.
3. **Arcane Blast** twice while the mob walks in, each only if its cast ends before the mob reaches you and it costs no more than a quarter of the mana you have left. Not on a mob that is almost dead.
4. **Arcane Missiles** after your two stacks, and whenever nothing else applies. When the free Missiles of **Missile Barrage** light up, cast them at once after your stacks, or at once if the mob is on you.
5. **Arcane Wrath** again whenever it is ready and its cast will end before the mob reaches you, or while the mob stands frozen.
6. **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
7. With **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep channeling while it hits you.
8. **Fire Blast** when it will kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- **Cone of Cold** (26) only when the mob is on you, **Frost Nova** is down and the mob is below a fifth of its health (a tuned rule); step back after it. It comes up rarely.
- **Arcane Meditation** (26-28) keeps half your mana regeneration going while you cast. **Arcane Impact** (29) adds more crit.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 31 at level 25. Presses per kill at level 25: Arcane Blast 1.9, Arcane Missiles 1.7, Frost Nova 0.6, Arcane Wrath 0.5, Fire Blast 0.3, Counterspell 0.2.*

## Levels 30-49: Presence of Mind on the pull

**The trainer teaches:** Ice Armor (30), Mage Armor (34), Conjure Mana Jade (38), Frostfire Bolt (40), Conjure Mana Citrine (48).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Blast at 30, 40; Arcane Missiles at 32, 40, 48; Arcane Wrath at 36, 48; Fire Blast at 30, 38, 46; Frost Nova at 40.

**Talents:** 30 Presence of Mind, 31 Arcane Impact, 32-34 Arcane Mind, 35-37 Arcane Instability, 38-39 Arcane Mind, 40-41 Precision, 42-44 Improved Frostbolt, 45-47 Ice Shards, 48 Improved Frost Nova, 49 Frost Warding.

**Keys to set:** Shift+Q = Arcane Wrath, macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Arcane Missiles, E Frost Nova, F Blink, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, Mouse 4 Counterspell, 1 Arcane Blast, 3 Mana Shield, Shift+Q Arcane Wrath.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting.
2. Open with **Arcane Wrath**: the Shift+Q macro makes it instant whenever Presence of Mind is ready; without it, only when its cast will end before the mob reaches you.
3. **Arcane Blast** twice while the mob walks in, each only if its cast ends before the mob reaches you and it costs no more than a quarter of the mana you have left. Not on a mob that is almost dead.
4. **Arcane Missiles** after your two stacks, and whenever nothing else applies. When the free Missiles of **Missile Barrage** light up, cast them at once after your stacks, or at once if the mob is on you.
5. **Arcane Wrath** again whenever it is ready and its cast will end before the mob reaches you, or while the mob stands frozen.
6. **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
7. With **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep channeling while it hits you.
8. **Fire Blast** when it will kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- **Presence of Mind** (30) makes your next cast instant; the fight lab spends it on the opening **Arcane Wrath**.
- **Ice Armor** (30) replaces **Frost Armor** as your armor spell.
- **Arcane Mind** (32-34, 38-39) adds Intellect and bigger Arcane crits, **Arcane Instability** (35-37) damage and crit.
- From 40 the last 21 points go to Frost: **Precision** (40-41) for hit, then **Improved Frostbolt** (42-44, which this list never uses: it casts no **Frostbolt**), **Ice Shards** (45-47), **Improved Frost Nova** (48) and **Frost Warding** (49). Nothing new to press until 50; **Frostfire Bolt** (40) is not part of this list.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 33 at level 30, 32 at level 40, 32 at level 49. Presses per kill at level 30: Arcane Missiles 1.9, Arcane Blast 1.8, Arcane Wrath 0.8, Frost Nova 0.7, Fire Blast 0.6, Counterspell 0.2.*

## Levels 50-60: Ice Lance into the freeze

**The trainer teaches:** Arcane Brilliance (56), Conjure Mana Ruby (58).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Blast at 50, 60; Arcane Missiles at 56; Arcane Wrath at 60; Fire Blast at 54; Frost Nova at 54; Ice Lance at 56.

**Talents:** 50 Ice Lance, 51 Frost Warding, 52-54 Improved Blizzard, 55-56 Arctic Reach, 57-59 Permafrost, 60 Fingers of Frost.

**Keys to set:** R = Ice Lance.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Arcane Missiles, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, F Blink, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, Mouse 4 Counterspell, 1 Arcane Blast, 3 Mana Shield, Shift+Q Arcane Wrath.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting.
2. Open with **Arcane Wrath**: the Shift+Q macro makes it instant whenever Presence of Mind is ready; without it, only when its cast will end before the mob reaches you.
3. **Arcane Blast** twice while the mob walks in, each only if its cast ends before the mob reaches you and it costs no more than a quarter of the mana you have left. Not on a mob that is almost dead.
4. **Arcane Missiles** after your two stacks, and whenever nothing else applies. When the free Missiles of **Missile Barrage** light up, cast them at once after your stacks, or at once if the mob is on you.
5. **Arcane Wrath** again whenever it is ready and its cast will end before the mob reaches you, or while the mob stands frozen.
6. **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back, and **Ice Lance** it while it stands frozen. Skip the Nova on a mob that is almost dead.
7. With **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep channeling while it hits you.
8. **Fire Blast** or an **Ice Lance** when it will kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- **Improved Blizzard** (52-54) only matters in packs. **Arctic Reach** (55-56) widens **Frost Nova**, **Permafrost** (57-59) makes your chills last longer.
- **Fingers of Frost** (60): a chill (your armor slows a mob that hits you) can make your next 2 spells count as cast on a frozen mob. **Ice Lance** then hits as hard as into a **Frost Nova**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 30 at level 50, 29 at level 60. Presses per kill at level 50: Arcane Blast 1.8, Arcane Missiles 1.6, Frost Nova 0.7, Ice Lance 0.7, Arcane Wrath 0.5, Fire Blast 0.5, Counterspell 0.1.*

## Between fights

- Drink your conjured water when you are below about a third of your mana, and eat below about two thirds of your health. The tuned rules set no thresholds for this list, so the fight lab rests at its defaults (35% and 65%).
- **Evocation** (from 20) whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- Conjure both food and water yourself.
- Visit the trainer every even level. **Arcane Missiles** alone has seven ranks.

## Other ways to level this class

- [Arcane-Frost](arcane-frost-by-level.md) and [Frost](frost-by-level.md) are the recommended mage leveling specs in the fight lab: both freeze the mob and hit it with **Frostbolt** and **Ice Lance**.
- Packs: **Frost Nova** when two or more reach you, then **Arcane Explosion** while they stand around you.
