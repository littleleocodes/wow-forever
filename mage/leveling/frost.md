## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30, **Mage Armor** from 34) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Ice Barrier** (the page build's, from 40) before each pull when it is ready, and again in a fight once it has run out. Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in (it slows it) and whenever nothing below applies, but only while the mob has more than a tenth of its health; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends, wand it until it arrives (the tuned rules keep **Fire Blast** for the finish).
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee; if your build has **Cold Snap**, it resets **Frost Nova** when more than 8 seconds of cooldown are left and the mob still has 40% of its health.
3. **Ice Lance** into the freeze whenever it is ready: it has an 8-second cooldown, shared with **Fire Blast**, so one per **Frost Nova**, **Frostbite** freeze or **Fingers of Frost** charge, not a stream of them.
4. In the last fifth of the mob's health: **Fire Blast** when it will kill or the mob is on you, or an **Ice Lance** that will kill; below a tenth, only **Ice Lance** or your wand. No Cone of Cold at all.
5. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana (the tuned rule), **Evocation** in place of a drink when it is ready and you are under 15% mana, eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rule). Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. **Evocation** is not a sit and brings no food.

## Why this rotation

Frost levels by slowing, freezing and shattering: **Frostbolt** slows the mob as it walks in, **Frost Nova** freezes it when it arrives, you step back, and **Ice Lance** hits the frozen target for four times its damage with **Shatter**'s crit chance; from 40 **Ice Barrier** goes up before each pull. Frost points first (**Ice Lance** at 20, **Shatter** complete by 29, **Fingers of Frost** at 30-31, **Cold Snap** at 37, **Ice Barrier** at 40 and **Improved Frost Nova**'s second point at 41), then the Arcane mana talents from 42 (**Arcane Concentration** by 54, **Arcane Meditation** at 58-60), with one point in **Incineration** (57). In the 20-60 race of the redo of 2026-11-21 (your own regeneration running while you eat and drink) this build takes about 26.9 seconds per kill, level-weighted, within the noise of the Arcane page's build (26.3) and the Arcane-Frost page's (26.9). The page's numbers table has each level.

**Why it works**

- **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can and whenever nothing else applies, while the mob has more than a tenth of its health (a fifth before the re-tune of 2026-11-20): 2.5 seconds with **Improved Frostbolt** at 5 points, 290 mana at top rank less **Frost Channeling**'s 15%, and a 40% slow for 9 seconds that buys extra casts before the mob reaches you (**Permafrost** lengthens it). From 30 every chill has a 15% chance to light up **Fingers of Frost** (your next 2 spells count as cast on a frozen target), and from 33 **Frostbite** freezes the mob on the same roll: the fight lab rolls the two together. If the mob would reach you before the cast ends and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives; if it is already on you with **Frost Nova** down, keep casting.
- **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee: it freezes the mob for up to 8 seconds (damage can break it), and out of reach it cannot hit you or push your casts back. **Improved Frost Nova** takes 4 seconds off its 25-second cooldown.
- **Ice Lance** into the freeze whenever it is ready: it deals 300% more damage to a frozen target, crits 50% more often there with **Shatter**, and crits for double with **Ice Shards**. It has an 8-second cooldown, so you get one per freeze, not a stream. In the open search's talent values at level 60 (2026-11-20), taking out **Ice Lance** costs about 3.4 seconds per kill, **Shatter** 3.3, **Ice Barrier** 2.5, **Fingers of Frost** 2.4, **Ice Shards** 1.8 and **Improved Frostbolt** 1.5.
- **Fire Blast** is the finish and nothing else: the tuned rules cast it only while the mob is between a tenth and a fifth of its health, when it will kill or the mob is on you, and since 2026-11-20 they keep **Frostbolt** going down to a tenth (a fifth before). Below a tenth an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or the wand, finishes cheaper than a long cast. It is not the answer to a mob that reaches you with health to spare. **Fire Blast** and **Ice Lance** share one cooldown, and **Incineration** (one point) adds 2% crit to both.
- The Arcane points are for mana: **Arcane Concentration** gives a 10% chance after a damage spell hits that your next one is free, and **Arcane Meditation** (which needs all 5 **Arcane Concentration** points) keeps another half of your regeneration going while you cast, all of it with **Mage Armor** (about 1.5 seconds per kill at 60 in the open search of 2026-11-20). **Wand Specialization** makes the finishing wand hits 25% stronger.
- **Counterspell** (level 24) a mob that casts spells: it stops the cast and locks that school for 10 seconds.

**Caveats**

- This build takes **Cold Snap** (37) and **Ice Barrier** (40): from 40, **Ice Barrier** before each pull whenever it is ready, and again mid-fight when its minute of extra Frost damage runs out, as the fight lab plays it. **Frostbite** (33, 36) and **Winter's Chill** (38-39) fill the Frost tree to the 30 points **Ice Barrier** needs: **Winter's Chill** does nothing in a list without **Cone of Cold**, and **Frostbite**'s second point measured nothing (its 10% is under the 15% of **Fingers of Frost**, and the fight lab rolls the two together).
- This is the open leveling search's 19/0/32 winner of 2026-11-20, 1.7% faster per kill at levels 20-60 than the hand-made Frost build under Other builds. The leveling filler passes of 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21 moved eight points that measured nothing or only held a tier open (Ice Block, Arcane Wrath, Arcane Blast, a point each of **Precision**, **Winter's Chill** and **Missile Barrage**, two of **Permafrost**) to **Improved Frost Nova**, **Frostbite**, **Arctic Reach**, **Incineration**, **Arcane Resilience** and **Magic Absorption**.
- **Cone of Cold** stays out of single pulls: the tuned rules switch it off (kills measured faster without).
- Drink below 15% of your mana (the tuned rule); **Evocation** in place of that drink, whenever it is ready (under 15% mana); eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rules). Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).
- Armor: **Mage Armor** from 34 (the tuned rules of 2026-11-15). It keeps half your mana regeneration going while you cast, so you drink less: 4-5% faster per kill at 34-40 in the fight lab, 2-3% at 46-50, 1.4-2.5% at 56-60, though you eat a little more without armor. Before 34, **Frost Armor** and **Ice Armor** chill a mob that hits you, and from 30 that chill can light up **Fingers of Frost**; **Mage Armor** does not.
- Pulls of several mobs are a different game: the AoE build under Other builds gathers about four, freezes them with **Frost Nova** and uses **Blizzard** and **Cone of Cold**, in **Ice Armor** (in pulls of four, Mage Armor measured 9-20% slower per kill, with more deaths).

**When to change it**

- If **Frost Nova** is on cooldown when the mob arrives, **Cold Snap** (from 37) resets it when it has more than 8 seconds left and the mob still has 40% of its health; otherwise keep casting **Frostbolt**.
- Low on mana in a fight: let an **Ice Lance** into the freeze or the wand finish a nearly dead mob rather than start a **Frostbolt**.
