## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Cold Snap**, **Ice Barrier** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Ice Barrier** before each pull once you have it. Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in (it slows it) and whenever nothing below applies, but only while the mob has more than a fifth of its health; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends, **Fire Blast** it instead (or wand it until it arrives if **Fire Blast** is on cooldown and **Frost Nova** is ready).
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee; **Cold Snap** resets **Frost Nova** if it has more than 8 seconds of cooldown left and the mob still has 40% of its health; with both down, **Fire Blast**.
3. **Ice Lance** again and again while the mob is frozen (**Frost Nova**, **Frostbite** or **Fingers of Frost**).
4. Below a fifth of the mob's health: **Fire Blast** (not below a tenth), an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand. No Cone of Cold at all.
5. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below a third of your mana, **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

Frost levels by slowing, freezing and shattering: **Frostbolt** slows the mob as it walks in, **Frost Nova** freezes it when it arrives, and you step back and **Ice Lance** the frozen target. In the fight lab at level 60, **Ice Lance** is about 58% of this build's damage, **Frostbolt** 29% and **Fire Blast** 11%.

**Why it works**

- **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can and whenever nothing else applies: its 40% slow buys extra casts before the mob reaches you, and every chill can freeze it (**Frostbite**, 15%) or light up **Fingers of Frost** (15%: your next 2 spells count as cast on a frozen target). If the mob would reach you before the cast ends, **Fire Blast** it instead, or wand it while it closes if **Fire Blast** is on cooldown and **Frost Nova** is ready.
- **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee: it roots the mob, and once you are out of reach it cannot hit you or push back your casts. **Improved Frost Nova** (levels 55-56) brings it back 4 seconds sooner.
- **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen (**Frost Nova**, a **Frostbite** freeze or a **Fingers of Frost** charge): 200% more damage to a frozen target, 50% more crit chance from **Shatter** and bigger crits from **Ice Shards**. It is instant, so cast it again and again while the freeze lasts. In the fight lab a kill at level 60 takes about 5.6 seconds longer without **Ice Lance**, 4.2 without **Shatter** and 2.4 without **Ice Shards**.
- **Fire Blast** is the instant for the moments a **Frostbolt** does not fit: the mob about to reach you, the mob on you with **Frost Nova** down, or a kill it will finish. **Incineration** (levels 50-52) adds 6% crit chance to it and to **Ice Lance**.
- Finishing: the tuned rules (2026-10-22) start no **Frostbolt** once the mob is below a fifth of its health and no **Fire Blast** below a tenth; an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or the wand, finishes it.
- The Arcane points (levels 32-49) are for mana: **Arcane Concentration** makes a damage spell free now and then (10% per hit) and **Arcane Meditation** keeps half your mana regeneration going while you cast; in the fight lab each is worth about a second per kill at level 60.
- **Counterspell** (level 24) a mob that casts spells: it stops the cast and locks that school for 10 seconds.

**Caveats**

- This build takes neither **Cold Snap** nor **Ice Barrier**: skip the list's lines for them. The hand-made build under Other builds has both (**Cold Snap** at 37, **Ice Barrier** at 40).
- **Cone of Cold** stays out of single pulls: the tuned rules never cast it (about 2% faster kills in the fight lab). **Winter's Chill**, the build's last point, only helps **Cone of Cold**.
- Drink below a third of your mana; **Evocation** is for between fights, whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- **Frost Armor** and **Ice Armor** can chill a mob that hits you, another roll for **Frostbite** to freeze it.
- Pulls of several mobs are a different game: the AoE build under Other builds gathers about four mobs, freezes them with **Frost Nova** and uses **Blizzard** and **Cone of Cold**.

**When to change it**

- If **Frost Nova** is on cooldown when the mob arrives, **Fire Blast** it and keep casting; a build with **Cold Snap** resets Nova instead when it has more than 8 seconds left and the mob still has 40% of its health.
- Low on mana in a fight: let an **Ice Lance** into the freeze or the wand finish a nearly dead mob rather than start a **Frostbolt**.
