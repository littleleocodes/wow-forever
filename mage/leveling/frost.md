## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Ice Barrier** before each pull once you have it. Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in (it slows it) and whenever nothing below applies, but only while the mob has more than a fifth of its health; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives.
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee; **Cold Snap** resets **Frost Nova** if it has more than 8 seconds of cooldown left and the mob still has 40% of its health.
3. **Ice Lance** again and again while the mob is frozen (**Frost Nova**, **Frostbite** or **Fingers of Frost**).
4. Below a fifth of the mob's health: an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand. No Fire Blast or Cone of Cold at all.
5. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana, **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

Frost levels by slowing, freezing and shattering: **Frostbolt** slows the mob as it walks in, **Frost Nova** freezes it when it arrives, and you step back and **Ice Lance** the frozen target. In the fight lab, **Ice Lance** is about two thirds of this build's damage at level 60 and **Frostbolt** most of the rest.

**Why it works**

- **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can: its 40% slow buys extra casts before the mob reaches you, and every chill can freeze it (**Frostbite**, 15%) or light up **Fingers of Frost** (15%: your next 2 spells count as cast on a frozen target). If the mob would reach you before the cast ends and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it while it closes instead.
- **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee: it roots the mob, and once you are out of reach it cannot hit you or push back your casts.
- **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen (**Frost Nova**, a **Frostbite** freeze or a **Fingers of Frost** charge): 200% more damage to a frozen target, 50% more crit chance from **Shatter** and bigger crits from **Ice Shards**. It is instant, so cast it again and again while the freeze lasts. Without **Ice Lance** a kill at level 60 takes about 6 seconds longer, without **Shatter** about 4.5.
- Finishing: the tuned rotation (2026-10-19) stops **Frostbolt** once the mob is below a fifth of its health (about 1.5% faster kills) and never casts **Fire Blast** (about 1.3% faster): no Frost talent helps it. An **Ice Lance** that will kill, or the wand, finishes the mob.
- **Ice Barrier** (level 40) before each pull: besides the shield, hits do not push back your casts while it holds.
- **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells: it stops the cast and locks that school.

**Caveats**

- Drink below 15% of your mana, not a third: the tuned rotation measured kills slightly faster that way. **Evocation** is for between fights, whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- **Cone of Cold** stays out of single pulls: the fight lab measured kills about 1.7% faster without it.
- **Frost Armor** and **Ice Armor** can chill a mob that hits you, another roll for **Frostbite** to freeze it.
- Pulls of several mobs are a different game: the AoE grinding build under Other builds gathers about four mobs, freezes them with **Frost Nova** and uses **Blizzard** and **Cone of Cold**.

**When to change it**

- If **Frost Nova** is on cooldown when the mob arrives, **Cold Snap** (level 39) resets it; the fight lab does this when Nova has more than 8 seconds left and the mob still has 40% of its health.
- Low on mana in a fight: let an **Ice Lance** into the freeze or the wand finish a nearly dead mob rather than start a **Frostbolt**.
