## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up. Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Your bolt: **Frostbolt** until level 40, to the end of the fight; **Frostfire Bolt** from 40, while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health. Cast it while the mob walks in and whenever nothing below applies; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends and **Frost Nova** is ready, wand it until it arrives.
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back.
3. **Ice Lance** into the freeze whenever it is ready (an 8-second cooldown).
4. From 40, below about a third of the mob's health: no bolt; no Fire Blast and no Cone of Cold at any level (the tuned rules switch both off); an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand, finishes it.
5. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana (the tuned rule), **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rule).

## Why this rotation

The Frostfire leveling build is a Frost mage whose bolt changes at level 40: **Frostbolt** before, **Frostfire Bolt** after, with **Frost Nova**, a step back and **Ice Lance** into the freeze throughout. Frost points come first (**Ice Lance** at 20, **Shatter** by 27, **Fingers of Frost** at 30-31), then **Accelerant** (35-39) and the Fire points that make the bolt hit harder: **Ignite**, **Burning Soul**, **Incineration**, **Master of Elements**, **Critical Mass** and one point of **Impact** at 60. In the open leveling search's 20-60 race of 2026-11-06 it took about 36 seconds per kill level-weighted, behind the Frost page's 30.5; the page's numbers table has each level.

**Why it works**

- **Frostfire Bolt** (trained at level 40) counts as Frost and Fire, so both trees' talents apply (**Ice Shards**, **Frost Channeling**, **Ignite**, **Critical Mass**, **Master of Elements**), and it slows like **Frostbolt** (40%), so its chill rolls **Frostbite** (15% to freeze) and **Fingers of Frost** (15%: your next 2 spells count as cast on a frozen target). **Accelerant** cuts its 3-second cast to 2.5. The tuned rules (2026-11-06) cast it only while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health.
- Before level 40 **Frostbolt** does the same job, to the end of the fight (the rules veto only **Frostfire Bolt**); finish with an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or the wand.
- **Ice Lance** and **Shatter** are the build's two most valuable talents in the open search (about 10.3 and 7.6 seconds per kill at 60, then **Ice Shards** 3.7, **Fingers of Frost** 3.3, **Frostbite** 2.2 and **Frost Channeling** 2.1): a frozen mob takes 300% more damage from **Ice Lance**, with 50% more crit chance and **Ice Shards**' bigger crits. It has an 8-second cooldown, one per freeze.
- **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back out of melee. **Improved Frost Nova** (32, 55) brings it back 4 seconds sooner with both points.
- No **Fire Blast** and no **Cone of Cold**: the tuned rules switch both off for this list (kills measured faster without), so the finish is an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or the wand.
- **Master of Elements** refunds 30% of a spell's base cost on each Fire or Frost crit, which **Shatter** makes common; **Critical Mass** adds 6% crit to your Fire spells, **Frostfire Bolt** included, and **Incineration** adds crit to **Ice Lance**.

**Caveats**

- **Burning Soul** keeps hits from pushing a Fire cast back (70% at 3 points). The leveling filler pass of 2026-11-09 moved five points that measured nothing or only held a tier open (Blast Wave, Fire Power, Arcane Resilience and both Frost Warding) to **Improved Frostbolt**, **Improved Frost Nova** and **Impact**: 2/25/24, with no Blast Wave for packs.
- The signature rule: the Frostfire spec must do at least 30% of its damage with **Frostfire Bolt** from level 40. The page shows the build at 37% at level 60 in the fight lab; the rules that keep it there cost a little speed against a plain Frost list.
- Mana: **Frostfire Bolt** costs 370 at top rank. Drink below 15% of your mana and eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rules); use **Evocation** between fights when it is ready and you are under 40% mana.

**When to change it**

- On a nearly dead mob (below about a third of its health), start no bolt: an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or the wand.
- Mob on you with **Frost Nova** down: keep casting the bolt (**Burning Soul** keeps most hits from pushing it back); with a pack, **Cone of Cold** is back in play.
