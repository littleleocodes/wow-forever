# Frost Mage — leveling, level by level

[Mage](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Frost](frost.md) > Level by level

What a Frost mage presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Frost levels by slowing the mob, freezing it at your feet, stepping back and hitting it while it cannot reach you. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 18/3/30 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/mage/60/2331050010003-3-1525321301203002-12)), the one on the [Frost leveling page](frost.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Fireball** is your only attack. |
| 4-5 | **Frostbolt** replaces Fireball. It slows the mob, so you get more casts before it arrives. |
| 6-9 | **Fire Blast** to finish a mob between a tenth and a fifth of its health. |
| 10-19 | **Frost Nova** roots the mob at your feet: step back and keep casting. Talent points start. |
| 20-23 | **Ice Lance** (talent): an instant that deals four times its damage to a frozen mob. |
| 24-29 | **Counterspell** for mobs that cast. **Shatter** makes spells crit far more often on a frozen mob. |
| 30-39 | **Fingers of Frost** (talent): a chill can make your next spells hit as if the mob were frozen. **Ice Armor** replaces Frost Armor. |
| 40-60 | Nothing new to press. The remaining points go to Arcane for mana. Buy every new rank. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, conjuring, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes, so you never relearn a key. Bold marks what arrives in that stage: drag it onto the slot when you train it. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and macros: the [mage keybinds page](keybinds.md).

- Level 4 is the one swap on bar 1: **Frostbolt** takes Q and **Fireball** leaves the bar.
- Levels 1-9 fight with three keys: Q, Mouse 5 and the middle mouse button. Add a key when its spell arrives.
- No key: **Frost Armor** and **Ice Armor**, **Arcane Intellect**, **Conjure Water** and **Conjure Food**, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone. Put them on a side bar and click them between fights. The eat-and-drink macro on the keybinds page is a click too.
- **Evocation** on 4 is the one between-fights spell with a key: it has a cooldown and a mana rule, so it is a decision, not a chore. Click it instead if you want 4 free.
- Key 1 and Shift+Q are for the Arcane-Frost build: **Arcane Missiles** once it has **Missile Barrage** (37 on its page) and the **Presence of Mind** macro at 53. With the Frost build of this page they stay free.
- The single-target rotation never casts **Cone of Cold**, **Arcane Explosion** or **Blizzard**. They are there for a pull that brings friends, and **Cone of Cold** on C is the slow for it.

## Levels 1-3: Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Arcane Intellect (1), **Fireball** (1), Frost Armor (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Fireball; Middle mouse = Wand; Shift+C = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Fireball.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Fireball** until the mob is dead.
2. If it reaches you, keep casting. Nothing hits hard at these levels.

- Keep **Frost Armor** and **Arcane Intellect** up at all times, from here to 60.
- The fight lab does not simulate these three levels: its Frost rotation starts with **Frostbolt** at 4.

## Levels 4-5: Frostbolt replaces Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Conjure Water (4), **Frostbolt** (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Frostbolt.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can, again and again.
2. Your wand finishes a mob that is almost dead, once you own one.

- **Conjure Water** (4): drink between fights instead of waiting for your mana.
- Take **Fireball** off your bar. A slowed mob reaches you later, and that is worth more than Fireball's damage.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 21 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Frostbolt 4.5, Wand 1.1.*

## Levels 6-9: Fire Blast, your instant

**The trainer teaches:** Conjure Food (6), **Fire Blast** (6), Arcane Missiles (8), Polymorph (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Frostbolt at 8.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Fire Blast; 2 = Polymorph (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies. Start no new one once the mob is below a fifth of its health.
2. **Fire Blast** on a mob between a tenth and a fifth of its health. The tuned rules keep it for the finish, not for the moment the mob arrives.
3. Your wand finishes it.

- **Polymorph** (8): when a second mob joins, sheep it, kill the first, then fight the sheep.
- **Arcane Missiles** (8) is not part of the Frost rotation. **Conjure Food** (6) saves you buying bread.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 28 at level 6. Presses per kill at level 6: Frostbolt 5.8, Fire Blast 0.7, Wand 0.4.*

## Levels 10-19: Frost Nova: the kite begins

**The trainer teaches:** **Frost Nova** (10), Dampen Magic (12), Slow Fall (12), Arcane Explosion (14), Flamestrike (16), Amplify Magic (18), Remove Lesser Curse (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Fire Blast at 14; Frostbolt at 14.

**Talents:** 10-11 Precision, 12-14 Improved Frostbolt, 15-17 Frostbite, 18-19 Improved Frostbolt.

**Keys to set:** E = Frost Nova; Shift+E = Arcane Explosion (at 14); Shift+3 = Remove Lesser Curse (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in.
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
3. **Frostbolt** while it stands rooted. If it reaches you again with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting.
4. Below a fifth of its health: **Fire Blast** (not below a tenth) or your wand. No new **Frostbolt**: a long cast on a dying mob is wasted.

- **Improved Frostbolt** (12-14, 18-19) takes half a second off every **Frostbolt** cast.
- **Frostbite** (15-17) sometimes freezes a mob your chill hits. Treat it as a free **Frost Nova**: step back and cast.
- **Arcane Explosion** (14) and **Flamestrike** (16) are for packs. One mob at a time never needs them.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 28 at level 10, 37 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Frostbolt 4.8, Wand 1.2, Frost Nova 0.6, Fire Blast 0.6.*

## Levels 20-23: Ice Lance into every freeze

**The trainer teaches:** Blink (20), Blizzard (20), Evocation (20), Fire Ward (20), Mana Shield (20), Frost Ward (22), Scorch (22).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Explosion at 22; Fire Blast at 22; Frostbolt at 20; Polymorph at 20.

**Talents:** 20 Ice Lance, 21-23 Frost Channeling.

**Keys to set:** R = Ice Lance; F = Blink; 3 = Mana Shield; 4 = Evocation; Shift+R = Blizzard; Shift+Mouse 5 = Fire Ward; Shift+Mouse 4 = Frost Ward (at 22).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a fifth of its health.
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
3. **Ice Lance** the moment the mob is frozen, by **Frost Nova** or by **Frostbite**. It has an 8-second cooldown, so one per freeze.
4. If the mob reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting: the tuned rules save **Fire Blast** for the finish.
5. Below a fifth of its health: **Fire Blast** (not below a tenth), an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand.

- **Evocation** (20) is for between fights: channel it whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- **Blink** (20) and **Mana Shield** (20) are escapes for a pull that goes wrong, not rotation. **Blizzard** (20) is for packs.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 28 at level 20. Presses per kill at level 20: Frostbolt 3.9, Ice Lance 1.2, Wand 0.8, Frost Nova 0.5, Fire Blast 0.5.*

## Levels 24-29: Counterspell and Shatter

**The trainer teaches:** **Counterspell** (24), Cone of Cold (26), Conjure Mana Agate (28).

**New ranks to buy:** Blizzard at 28; Frost Nova at 26; Frostbolt at 26; Ice Lance at 28; Mana Shield at 28.

**Talents:** 24 Ice Shards, 25 Shatter, 26-27 Ice Shards, 28-29 Shatter.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Counterspell; C = Cone of Cold (at 26).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, Mouse 4 Counterspell.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting: it stops standing back and walks to you.
2. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a fifth of its health.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
4. **Ice Lance** the moment the mob is frozen. One per freeze.
5. If the mob reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting: the tuned rules save **Fire Blast** for the finish.
6. Below a fifth of its health: **Fire Blast** (not below a tenth), an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand.

- **Shatter** (25, 28-29) and **Ice Shards** (24, 26-27) are why the **Ice Lance** into a freeze is your biggest hit from here on.
- **Cone of Cold** (26) goes on C for packs. The tuned rotation never casts it on a single mob; kills measured faster without.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 32 at level 24, 34 at level 29. Presses per kill at level 24: Frostbolt 4.7, Ice Lance 1.5, Wand 0.9, Frost Nova 0.7, Fire Blast 0.6, Counterspell 0.2.*

**Keys to set:** 1 = Arcane Missiles (at 37).

- **Ice Armor** (30) replaces **Frost Armor** as your armor spell.

- **Improved Frost Nova** (36-37) brings **Frost Nova** back 4 seconds sooner.

## Between fights

- Drink your conjured water when you are below about 15% of your mana. That is the tuned rule: topping up earlier cost more time in the fight lab than the wand hits it saved.
- **Evocation** (from 20) whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- Eat when you are below two thirds of your health. Conjure both food and water yourself.
- Visit the trainer every even level. **Frostbolt** alone has eleven ranks.

## Other ways to level this class

- [Arcane-Frost](arcane-frost-by-level.md) is the recommended mage leveling spec in the fight lab. It plays this same loop and adds **Arcane Missiles** whenever **Missile Barrage** lights up.
- Pulling packs is a different game: the AoE Frost build on the Frost page gathers about four mobs, freezes them with **Frost Nova** and channels **Blizzard**.
