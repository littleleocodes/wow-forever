# Frost Mage — leveling, level by level

[Mage](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Frost](frost.md) > Level by level

What a Frost mage presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Frost levels by slowing the mob, freezing it at your feet, stepping back and hitting it while it cannot reach you. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 18/1/32 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/mage/60/2132151000003-1-052522130120301221-12)), the one on the [Frost leveling page](frost.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Fireball** is your only attack. |
| 4-5 | **Frostbolt** replaces Fireball. It slows the mob, so you get more casts before it arrives. |
| 6-9 | **Fire Blast** to finish a mob between a tenth and a fifth of its health. |
| 10-19 | **Frost Nova** roots the mob at your feet: step back and keep casting. Talent points start. |
| 20-23 | **Ice Lance** (talent): an instant that deals four times its damage to a frozen mob. |
| 24-29 | **Counterspell** for mobs that cast. **Shatter** makes spells crit far more often on a frozen mob. |
| 30-39 | **Fingers of Frost** (talent): a chill can make your next spells hit as if the mob were frozen. **Ice Armor** replaces Frost Armor, and **Mage Armor** replaces Ice Armor at 34. |
| 40-60 | **Ice Barrier** (talent): a shield before each pull that adds 15% to your Frost damage for a minute. The remaining points go to Arcane for mana. Buy every new rank. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, conjuring, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes, so you never relearn a key. Bold marks what arrives in that stage: drag it onto the slot when you train it. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and macros: the [mage keybinds page](keybinds.md).

- Level 4 is the one swap on bar 1: **Frostbolt** takes Q and **Fireball** leaves the bar.
- Levels 1-9 fight with three keys: Q, Mouse 5 and the middle mouse button. Add a key when its spell arrives.
- No key: your armor (**Frost Armor**, **Ice Armor**, **Mage Armor**), **Arcane Intellect**, **Conjure Water** and **Conjure Food**, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone. Put them on a side bar and click them between fights. The eat-and-drink macro on the keybinds page is a click too.
- **Evocation** on 4 is the one between-fights spell with a key: it has a cooldown and a mana rule, so it is a decision, not a chore. Click it instead if you want 4 free.
- Key 1 and Shift+Q are for the Arcane-Frost build: **Arcane Missiles** once it has **Missile Barrage** (34 on its page) and the **Presence of Mind** macro at 53. With the Frost build of this page they stay free.
- The single-target rotation never casts **Cone of Cold**, **Arcane Explosion** or **Blizzard**. They are there for a pull that brings friends, and **Cone of Cold** on C is the slow for it.

## Levels 1-3: Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Arcane Intellect (1), **Fireball** (1), Frost Armor (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Fireball; Middle mouse = Wand; Shift+C = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Fireball.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Fireball** until the mob is dead.
2. If it reaches you, keep casting. Nothing hits hard at these levels.

- Keep your armor and **Arcane Intellect** up at all times, from here to 60: **Frost Armor** now, **Ice Armor** from 30, **Mage Armor** from 34.
- The fight lab does not simulate these three levels: its Frost rotation starts with **Frostbolt** at 4.

## Levels 4-5: Frostbolt replaces Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Conjure Water (4), **Frostbolt** (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Frostbolt.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from as far away as you can, again and again.
2. Your wand finishes a mob that is almost dead, once you own one.

- **Conjure Water** (4): drink between fights instead of waiting for your mana.
- Take **Fireball** off your bar. A slowed mob reaches you later, and that is worth more than Fireball's damage.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Frostbolt 4.8, Wand 0.8.*

## Levels 6-9: Fire Blast, your instant

**The trainer teaches:** Conjure Food (6), **Fire Blast** (6), Arcane Missiles (8), Polymorph (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Frostbolt at 8.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Fire Blast; 2 = Polymorph (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies. Start no new one once the mob is below a tenth of its health.
2. **Fire Blast** in the mob's last fifth of health, not below a tenth: when it will kill, or when the mob is on you. The tuned rules keep it for the finish, not for the moment the mob arrives.
3. Your wand finishes it.

- **Polymorph** (8): when a second mob joins, sheep it, kill the first, then fight the sheep.
- **Arcane Missiles** (8) is not part of the Frost rotation. **Conjure Food** (6) saves you buying bread.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 23 at level 6. Presses per kill at level 6: Frostbolt 5.8, Fire Blast 0.7, Wand 0.4.*

## Levels 10-19: Frost Nova: the kite begins

**The trainer teaches:** **Frost Nova** (10), Dampen Magic (12), Slow Fall (12), Arcane Explosion (14), Flamestrike (16), Amplify Magic (18), Remove Lesser Curse (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Fire Blast at 14; Frostbolt at 14.

**Talents:** 10-11 Precision, 12-16 Improved Frostbolt, 17-19 Ice Shards.

**Keys to set:** E = Frost Nova; Shift+E = Arcane Explosion (at 14); Shift+3 = Remove Lesser Curse (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in.
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
3. **Frostbolt** while it stands rooted. If it reaches you again with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting.
4. Below a fifth of its health: **Fire Blast** when it will kill or the mob is on you. Below a tenth: your wand, and no new **Frostbolt** (a long cast on a dying mob is wasted) or **Fire Blast**.

- **Improved Frostbolt** (12-16) takes half a second off every **Frostbolt** cast.
- **Ice Shards** (17-19) makes your Frost crits hit harder.
- **Arcane Explosion** (14) and **Flamestrike** (16) are for packs. One mob at a time never needs them.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 26 at level 10, 32 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Frostbolt 4.9, Wand 1.4, Frost Nova 0.7, Fire Blast 0.4.*

## Levels 20-23: Ice Lance into every freeze

**The trainer teaches:** Blink (20), Blizzard (20), Evocation (20), Fire Ward (20), Mana Shield (20), Frost Ward (22), Scorch (22).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Explosion at 22; Fire Blast at 22; Frostbolt at 20; Polymorph at 20.

**Talents:** 20 Ice Lance, 21-23 Frost Channeling.

**Keys to set:** R = Ice Lance; F = Blink; 3 = Mana Shield; 4 = Evocation; Shift+R = Blizzard; Shift+Mouse 5 = Fire Ward; Shift+Mouse 4 = Frost Ward (at 22).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a tenth of its health.
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
3. **Ice Lance** the moment **Frost Nova** has frozen the mob. It has an 8-second cooldown, so one per freeze.
4. If the mob reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting: the tuned rules save **Fire Blast** for the finish.
5. Below a fifth of its health: **Fire Blast** when it will kill or the mob is on you, or an **Ice Lance** that will kill. Below a tenth: no **Fire Blast**, only the **Ice Lance** or your wand.

- **Evocation** (20) is for between fights: channel it in place of a drink, whenever it is ready and you are under 15% mana.
- **Blink** (20) and **Mana Shield** (20) are escapes for a pull that goes wrong, not rotation. **Blizzard** (20) is for packs.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 27 at level 20. Presses per kill at level 20: Frostbolt 4.6, Ice Lance 0.9, Frost Nova 0.7, Wand 0.6, Fire Blast 0.1.*

## Levels 24-29: Counterspell and Shatter

**The trainer teaches:** **Counterspell** (24), Cone of Cold (26), Conjure Mana Agate (28).

**New ranks to buy:** Blizzard at 28; Frost Nova at 26; Frostbolt at 26; Ice Lance at 28; Mana Shield at 28.

**Talents:** 24 Ice Shards, 25 Shatter, 26 Improved Frost Nova, 27 Ice Shards, 28-29 Shatter.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Counterspell; C = Cone of Cold (at 26).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, Mouse 4 Counterspell.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting: it stops standing back and walks to you.
2. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a tenth of its health.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
4. **Ice Lance** the moment the mob is frozen. One per freeze.
5. If the mob reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting: the tuned rules save **Fire Blast** for the finish.
6. Below a fifth of its health: **Fire Blast** when it will kill or the mob is on you, or an **Ice Lance** that will kill. Below a tenth: no **Fire Blast**, only the **Ice Lance** or your wand.

- **Shatter** (25, 28-29) and **Ice Shards** (24, 27) are why the **Ice Lance** into a freeze is your biggest hit from here on. **Improved Frost Nova** (26) brings **Frost Nova** back 2 seconds sooner.
- **Cone of Cold** (26) goes on C for packs. The tuned rotation never casts it on a single mob; kills measured faster without.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 30 at level 24, 32 at level 29. Presses per kill at level 24: Frostbolt 5.5, Ice Lance 1.2, Frost Nova 0.9, Wand 0.9, Counterspell 0.2, Fire Blast 0.1.*

## Levels 30-39: Fingers of Frost

**The trainer teaches:** Ice Armor (30), Mage Armor (34), Conjure Mana Jade (38).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Explosion at 30, 38; Arcane Missiles at 32; Blizzard at 36; Cone of Cold at 34; Fire Blast at 30, 38; Fire Ward at 30; Frost Ward at 32; Frostbolt at 32, 38; Ice Lance at 34; Mana Shield at 36.

**Talents:** 30-31 Fingers of Frost, 32 Arctic Reach, 33 Frostbite, 34 Permafrost, 35 Arctic Reach, 36 Frostbite, 37 Cold Snap, 38-39 Winter's Chill.

**Keys to set:** 1 = Arcane Missiles (at 34); Shift+2 = Cold Snap (at 37).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, Mouse 4 Counterspell.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting.
2. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a tenth of its health.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
4. **Ice Lance** whenever the mob is frozen or **Fingers of Frost** lights up, as soon as its 8-second cooldown allows.
5. If the mob reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting: the tuned rules save **Fire Blast** for the finish.
6. Below a fifth of its health: **Fire Blast** when it will kill or the mob is on you, or an **Ice Lance** that will kill. Below a tenth: no **Fire Blast**, only the **Ice Lance** or your wand.

- **Ice Armor** (30) replaces **Frost Armor** as your armor spell until **Mage Armor** (34): half your mana regeneration then keeps going while you cast, so you drink less. Mage Armor gives no armor and no chill, so you eat a little more, and it still kills faster: 5% per kill at 34 and 4% at 40 in the fight lab (drinking 6.5 to 3.5 seconds a kill at 34).
- **Arctic Reach** (32, 35) lets you pull from farther away: one more **Frostbolt** before the mob arrives.
- **Frostbite** (33, 36) sometimes freezes a mob your chill hits. Treat it as a free **Frost Nova**: step back and cast.
- **Permafrost** (34) makes your chills last longer. **Cold Snap** (37, talent): it finishes the cooldown on your other Frost spells, so **Frost Nova** is ready again. Press it when a mob reaches you with more than 8 seconds left on **Frost Nova** and 40% of its health or more, as the fight lab's list does; it comes back every 10 minutes. **Ice Barrier** (40) needs it and 30 points in the Frost tree: **Winter's Chill** (38-39) only fills those points, and does nothing in a list that never casts **Cone of Cold**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 31 at level 30, 31 at level 39. Presses per kill at level 30: Frostbolt 4.5, Ice Lance 1.5, Wand 1.2, Frost Nova 1.0, Fire Blast 0.3, Counterspell 0.2.*

## Levels 40-60: The finished rotation

**The trainer teaches:** Frostfire Bolt (40), Conjure Mana Citrine (48), Arcane Brilliance (56), Conjure Mana Ruby (58).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Explosion at 46, 54; Arcane Missiles at 40, 48, 56; Blizzard at 44, 52, 60; Cone of Cold at 42, 50, 58; Fire Blast at 46, 54; Fire Ward at 40, 50, 60; Frost Nova at 40, 54; Frost Ward at 42, 52, 60; Frostbolt at 44, 50, 56, 60; Ice Barrier at 46, 52, 58; Ice Lance at 42, 48, 56; Mana Shield at 44, 52, 60; Polymorph at 40, 60.

**Talents:** 40 Ice Barrier, 41 Improved Frost Nova, 42-43 Wand Specialization, 44-45 Arcane Resilience, 46 Improved Channeling, 47-49 Arcane Concentration, 50 Arcane Subtlety, 51 Missile Barrage, 52 Arcane Resilience, 53-54 Arcane Concentration, 55 Arcane Subtlety, 56 Magic Absorption, 57 Incineration, 58-60 Arcane Meditation.

**Keys to set:** Shift+1 = Ice Barrier; Shift+Q = Presence of Mind macro (at 53).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Frostbolt, E Frost Nova, R Ice Lance, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, Mouse 4 Counterspell, Shift+1 Ice Barrier.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Ice Barrier** before the pull whenever it is ready (a 30-second cooldown) and your last shield is used up, and again mid-fight when its minute of extra Frost damage is about to run out.
2. **Counterspell** a mob that starts casting.
3. **Frostbolt** from maximum range while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a tenth of its health.
4. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of its reach.
5. **Ice Lance** whenever the mob is frozen or **Fingers of Frost** lights up, as soon as its 8-second cooldown allows.
6. If the mob reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown, keep casting: the tuned rules save **Fire Blast** for the finish.
7. Below a fifth of its health: **Fire Blast** when it will kill or the mob is on you, or an **Ice Lance** that will kill. Below a tenth: no **Fire Blast**, only the **Ice Lance** or your wand.

- **Arcane Concentration** (47-49, 53-54) makes about one cast in ten free, and **Arcane Meditation** (58-60) keeps another half of your mana regeneration going while you cast: with **Mage Armor**, all of it.
- **Mage Armor** stays on to 60: 2-3% faster per kill than **Ice Armor** at 46-50 in the fight lab, 1.4-2.5% at 56-60.
- **Ice Barrier** (40) absorbs damage and adds 15% to your Frost damage for a minute, 5% more while the shield holds, and while it holds a hit costs your cast no time: in the open search of 2026-11-20 it was worth about 2.5 seconds per kill at 60. **Improved Frost Nova** (41) gets its second point: **Frost Nova** comes back 4 seconds sooner. **Incineration** (57) adds 2% crit chance to **Ice Lance** and **Fire Blast**. **Improved Channeling** (46), **Missile Barrage** (51) and **Magic Absorption** (56) fill the Arcane tree to the 15 points **Arcane Meditation** needs: this list never casts **Arcane Missiles**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 29 at level 40, 25 at level 50, 24 at level 60. Presses per kill at level 40: Frostbolt 4.2, Ice Lance 1.5, Wand 1.5, Frost Nova 0.8, Ice Barrier 0.2, Counterspell 0.1, Fire Blast 0.1.*

## Between fights

- Drink your conjured water when you are below about 15% of your mana. That is the tuned rule: topping up earlier cost more time in the fight lab than the wand hits it saved.
- **Evocation** (from 20) in place of a drink: whenever it is ready and you are under 15% mana, channel it instead of drinking (it gives back 70% to more than all of your mana, so at 40% part of it was lost: 0.6-1.1% faster per kill in the fight lab, 2026-11-16).
- Eat when you are below 40% of your health (the tuned rule). Conjure both food and water yourself.
- Whenever you sit down to drink, eat as well if your health is not full, and drink while you eat if your mana is not full: the other bar fills in the same seconds at no extra time. Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. **Evocation** is not a sit and brings no food. It was 4.0% faster per kill for this build in the fight lab (2026-11-16), and the tuned rules play it.
- Visit the trainer every even level. **Frostbolt** alone has eleven ranks.

## Other ways to level this class

- [Arcane-Frost](arcane-frost-by-level.md) plays this same loop and adds **Arcane Missiles** whenever **Missile Barrage** lights up. It and [Arcane](arcane-by-level.md) are a little faster in the fight lab; all three are the recommended mage leveling specs (within 3% of each other).
- Pulling packs is a different game: the AoE Frost build on the Frost page gathers about four mobs, freezes them with **Frost Nova** and channels **Blizzard**. It wears **Ice Armor**: in pulls of four, Mage Armor measured 9-20% slower per kill, with more deaths.
