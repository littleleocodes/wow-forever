**Signature spells:** 70% of the damage at level 60 in the fight lab comes from Fireball, Pyroblast, Fire Blast, Scorch, Ignite, Flamestrike (the spec's rule: at least 60%; **Fireball**, **Pyroblast** cast in every fight).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Combustion** at the pull when it is ready. Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Fireball** while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health.
2. **Fire Blast** when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** lands (**Scorch** if **Fire Blast** is on cooldown and there is time), once it is on you, or when it will kill.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back; **Blast Wave** if **Frost Nova** is on cooldown.
4. **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen; **Pyroblast** only at 3 stacks of **Heating Up**.
5. Mob on you and no control left: **Scorch** (a short cast, pushed back less), while the mob has more than about a third of its health.
6. Below about a third of the mob's health, no **Fireball** or **Scorch**: finish with **Fire Blast**, an **Ice Lance** or your wand.
7. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana (the tuned rule), **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

## Why this rotation

Fire levels with **Fireball** at range and **Fire Blast** up close, and borrows the Frost tree's freeze: **Frost Nova**, a step back, and **Ice Lance** into the frozen mob; **Heating Up** makes some **Pyroblast** casts fast. In the fight lab at level 60 (2026-11-04 run), **Fire Blast** is about 28% of this build's damage, **Ice Lance** 21%, **Fireball** 19% and **Pyroblast** 12%; about 33 seconds per kill level-weighted in the audit, the slowest mage leveling page (this hand-made build has no talent order yet, so the fight lab plays all 51 points from level 10, which flatters the low levels).

**Why it works**

- The 19 Frost points: **Precision** (hit), **Frostbite**, **Ice Lance**, **Ice Shards**, **Shatter**, a point of **Permafrost** and an **Ice Block** for emergencies. A frozen mob takes 300% more damage from **Ice Lance**, which crits 50% more often there with **Shatter** and for double with **Ice Shards**; **Ice Lance** has an 8-second cooldown, one per freeze. The stats model rates **Ice Lance** this build's most valuable talent by far.
- **Fireball** from as far away as you can, and as the filler whenever nothing else applies, while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health. Below that the tuned rules (2026-11-03) start no **Fireball**, **Scorch** or **Pyroblast**: a long cast overkills. **Accelerant** takes half a second off its cast at 5 points.
- **Fire Blast** is your instant: when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** lands, when it is on you and **Frost Nova** is down, and to finish a mob it will kill. **Improved Fire Blast** takes 2 seconds off its cooldown, **Critical Mass** adds crit, and every hit adds a **Heating Up** stack.
- **Heating Up** (**Pyroblast** first, then the talent): crits with **Fireball**, **Fire Blast** and **Scorch**, and every **Fire Blast** hit, each cut your next **Pyroblast**'s cast by 25%, up to 3 stacks. At 3 stacks its 6-second cast takes 1.5 seconds: cast it then, never without them.
- **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you, then step back; **Ice Lance** while it is frozen; **Scorch** when the mob is on you and your control is down, above a third of its health (its 1.5-second cast is pushed back less than a **Fireball**), and during the walk-in when **Fire Blast** is on cooldown and a **Fireball** would not land in time.
- **Ignite** (40% of every crit's damage over 4 seconds), **Master of Elements** (30% of the cost back on every Fire or Frost crit) and **Fire Power** (3% per Fire effect on the mob, up to 12%) are the Fire tree's passive half (**Ignite** about 5% of the damage at 60 in the fight lab). **Combustion** goes at the pull once you have it.

**Caveats**

- This build takes **Blast Wave**, a 50% daze for 6 seconds on everything around you: the list casts it, like **Frost Nova**, for distance when the mob is on you. **Ice Block** is yours for emergencies, outside the list.
- Mana: **Fireball** costs 410 at top rank against 290 for a **Frostbolt**. Drink below 15% of your mana (the tuned rule) and use **Evocation** between fights when it is ready and you are under 40% mana; eat below two thirds of your health.
- Pulls of several mobs: **Flamestrike** hits them all, but the fight lab only measured single pulls for Fire.
- The open search's 2026-11-03 winner (0/31/20, 8% faster at levels 20-60 in its race) never casts **Pyroblast** at level 40 in the fight lab, which fails the Fire signature rule (half a cast per kill there), so it was not written: the page keeps this hand-made build. It has no talent order yet; the queue's order step races three for it.

**When to change it**

- On a nearly dead mob (below about a third), finish with an instant that will kill (**Fire Blast**, an **Ice Lance** into a freeze) or the wand rather than start a **Fireball** or **Scorch**.
- If you run low on mana in a fight, keep **Fire Blast** and **Ice Lance** for the freeze and let the wand finish.

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-11-04; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
