## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Blast Wave** is a talent this build does not take: where a line below names it, that part does not apply. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Combustion** at the pull when it is ready (once you have the talent, at 59). Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. From level 20 (the **Pyroblast** talent), open every pull with a hard-cast **Pyroblast**; before that, and after it, **Fireball** while the mob walks in and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health and you have more than 15% of your mana.
2. **Fire Blast** when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** lands, once it is on you, or when it will kill. No Scorch: the tuned rules never cast it.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back; **Blast Wave** if **Frost Nova** is on cooldown and the mob has more than about a third of its health.
4. **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen; **Pyroblast** again only at 3 stacks of **Heating Up** (from level 44).
5. Below about a third of the mob's health, or under 15% of your mana, no **Fireball**: finish with **Fire Blast**, an **Ice Lance** or your wand.
6. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana and eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rules), **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana.

## Why this rotation

Fire levels with **Fireball** at range and **Fire Blast** up close, opens every pull from level 20 with a hard-cast **Pyroblast**, and borrows the Frost tree's freeze: **Frost Nova**, a step back, and **Ice Lance** into the frozen mob; **Heating Up** (level 44) makes some later **Pyroblast** casts fast. The page's order is **Accelerant** and **Ignite** to 19, **Pyroblast** at 20, 18 Frost points and one Arcane point from 21 to 39 (**Ice Lance** at 31, **Shatter** by 38, **Wand Specialization** at 39), then Fire again from 40 (**Improved Fire Blast**, **Burning Soul**, **Heating Up**, **Improved Scorch**, **Master of Elements**, **Critical Mass**, **Incineration**, **Fire Power**, **Combustion** at 59, the last **Incineration** point at 60). In the open leveling search's 20-60 race of 2026-11-15 it took about 36 seconds per kill level-weighted, the slowest of the five mage leveling pages; the page's numbers table has each level.

**Why it works**

- The 18 Frost points: **Precision** (hit), **Frost Warding**, **Improved Frost Nova** (**Frost Nova** back 4 seconds sooner), **Frostbite**, **Ice Shards**, **Ice Lance** and **Shatter**. A frozen mob takes 300% more damage from **Ice Lance**, which crits 50% more often there with **Shatter** and for double with **Ice Shards**; **Ice Lance** has an 8-second cooldown, one per freeze. In the open search of 2026-11-15 the build loses about 4.2 seconds per kill at 60 without **Ice Lance** and 3.7 without **Shatter**.
- **Fireball** from as far away as you can, and as the filler whenever nothing else applies, while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health and you have more than 15% of your mana. Below that health the tuned rules (2026-11-15) start no **Fireball** or **Pyroblast**: a long cast overkills. Under 15% mana they start no **Fireball** either: **Fire Blast** and the wand end the fight, and you drink after it (the drink rule is 15% too). **Accelerant** takes half a second off its cast at 5 points.
- **Pyroblast** opens every pull (a tuned rule that makes the Fire signature spell part of every fight): a 6-second cast the mob runs in during, so finish it and go on. After that it is cast only at 3 stacks of **Heating Up**, while the mob has more than a third of its health.
- **Fire Blast** is your instant: when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** lands, when it is on you and **Frost Nova** is down, to finish a mob it will kill, and whenever it is ready once the rules start no **Fireball** (the mob's last third, or under 15% of your mana). **Improved Fire Blast** (level 40-41) takes 2 seconds off its 8-second cooldown, **Critical Mass** and **Incineration** (10% on **Fire Blast** and **Ice Lance** at 5 points) add crit, and every hit adds a **Heating Up** stack.
- **Heating Up**: crits with **Fireball**, **Fire Blast** and **Scorch**, and every **Fire Blast** hit, each cut your next **Pyroblast**'s cast by 25%, up to 3 stacks. At 3 stacks its 6-second cast takes 1.5 seconds. On one mob the 3 stacks rarely come together before it dies, so the opener stays the main **Pyroblast**.
- **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you with more than about a tenth of its health left, at any mana: the rules of 2026-11-15 dropped the old floor of 30% of your mana. Then step back; **Ice Lance** while it is frozen; with **Frost Nova** down, **Fire Blast** and keep casting. The tuned rules never cast **Scorch**, so the page's **Improved Scorch** (45) does nothing in this list.
- **Ignite** (40% of every crit's damage over 4 seconds), **Master of Elements** (30% of the base cost back on every Fire or Frost crit) and **Fire Power** (up to 12% more Fire damage with several Fire effects on the mob) are the Fire tree's passive half. **Combustion** goes at the pull once you have it at level 59.

**Caveats**

- This build has no Ice Block: a pull that goes wrong is **Blink**, **Mana Shield** or a healing potion.
- Mana: **Fireball** costs 410 at top rank against 290 for a **Frostbolt**. Drink below 15% of your mana and eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rules); use **Evocation** between fights when it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- Pulls of several mobs: **Flamestrike** hits them all, but the fight lab only measured single pulls for Fire.
- This is the open leveling search's 1/32/18 winner of 2026-11-15: the talents of the build it replaced in a new order (**Improved Frost Nova** complete at 27, **Shatter** by 38, **Master of Elements** (46-48) and **Critical Mass** (49-51) before most of **Incineration**), 0.6% faster per kill at levels 20-60. The talents come from the leveling filler pass of 2026-11-09, which moved eight points that measured nothing or only held a tier open (Blast Wave among them) to **Incineration**, **Improved Frost Nova** and **Wand Specialization**.

**When to change it**

- On a nearly dead mob (below about a third), finish with an instant that will kill (**Fire Blast**, an **Ice Lance** into a freeze) or the wand rather than start a **Fireball**.
