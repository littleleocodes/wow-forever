## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30, **Mage Armor** from 34) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Combustion** at the pull when it is ready (once you have the talent, at 59). Pull from as far away as you can.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. From level 20 (the **Pyroblast** talent), open every pull with a hard-cast **Pyroblast**, and cast it again whenever its whole cast ends before the mob reaches you; before that, and after it, **Fireball** while the mob walks in and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health and you have more than 15% of your mana.
2. **Fire Blast** when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** lands, once it is on you, or when it will kill. No Scorch: the tuned rules never cast it.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back; Blast Wave, in a build that takes it, if **Frost Nova** is on cooldown and the mob has more than about a third of its health.
4. **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen; **Pyroblast** again at 3 stacks of **Heating Up** (from level 44), or hard-cast when its cast ends before the mob can hit you (in practice inside a **Frost Nova** freeze), both while the mob has more than 35% of its health.
5. Below about a third of the mob's health, or under 15% of your mana, no **Fireball**: finish with **Fire Blast**, an **Ice Lance** or your wand.
6. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

**Between fights:** Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana and eat below 80% of your health (the tuned rules of 2026-11-20), **Evocation** in place of a drink when it is ready and you are under 15% mana. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. **Evocation** is not a sit and brings no food.

## Why this rotation

Fire levels with **Fireball** at range and **Fire Blast** up close, opens every pull from level 20 with a hard-cast **Pyroblast**, and borrows the Frost tree's freeze: **Frost Nova**, a step back, and **Ice Lance** into the frozen mob; **Heating Up** (level 44) shortens later **Pyroblast** casts enough to fit some into a freeze. The page's order is **Accelerant** and **Ignite** to 19, **Pyroblast** at 20, 18 Frost points and one Arcane point from 21 to 39 (**Ice Lance** at 31, **Shatter** by 38, **Wand Specialization** at 39), then Fire again from 40 (**Improved Fire Blast**, **Burning Soul**, **Incineration**, **Heating Up**, **Master of Elements**, **Critical Mass**, **Fire Power**, **Combustion** at 59 and **Improved Fire Blast**'s second point at 60). In the 20-60 race of the redo of 2026-11-21 (your own regeneration running while you eat and drink) it takes about 32.8 seconds per kill level-weighted, the slowest of the mage leveling pages, about 25% behind the Arcane page's build (26.3). The page's numbers table has each level.

**Why it works**

- The 18 Frost points: **Precision** (hit), **Frost Warding**, **Improved Frost Nova** (**Frost Nova** back 4 seconds sooner), **Ice Shards**, **Ice Lance**, **Frost Channeling** (Frost spells 10% cheaper) and **Shatter**; since the open search of 2026-11-17 no Frostbite, so only **Frost Nova** freezes the mob. A frozen mob takes 300% more damage from **Ice Lance**, which crits 50% more often there with **Shatter** and for double with **Ice Shards**; **Ice Lance** has an 8-second cooldown, one per freeze, and shares it with **Fire Blast**. In the open search of 2026-11-20, taking out **Shatter** costs about 3.1 seconds per kill at 60 and **Ice Lance** 2.2.
- **Fireball** from as far away as you can, and as the filler whenever nothing else applies, while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health and you have more than 15% of your mana. Below that health the tuned rules (2026-11-15) start no **Fireball** or **Pyroblast**: a long cast overkills. Under 15% mana they start no **Fireball** either: **Fire Blast** and the wand end the fight, and you drink after it (the drink rule is 15% too). **Accelerant** takes half a second off its cast at 5 points.
- **Pyroblast** opens every pull (a tuned rule that makes the Fire signature spell part of every fight): a 6-second cast the mob runs in during, so finish it and go on. Since the tune of 2026-11-20 it goes out again whenever its whole cast will end before the mob can hit you, and at once at 3 stacks of **Heating Up**, both only while the mob has more than a third of its health. A 6-second cast rarely fits; from 44 one or two **Heating Up** stacks (4.5 or 3 seconds) make it fit into a **Frost Nova** freeze.
- **Fire Blast** is your instant: when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** lands, when it is on you and **Frost Nova** is down, to finish a mob it will kill, and whenever it is ready once the rules start no **Fireball** (the mob's last third, or under 15% of your mana). **Improved Fire Blast** takes 1 second off its 8-second cooldown at 40 and 2 from 60, **Critical Mass** and **Incineration** (10% on **Fire Blast** and **Ice Lance** at 5 points) add crit, and every hit adds a **Heating Up** stack. A **Fire Blast** puts **Ice Lance** on cooldown too, and the other way round.
- **Heating Up**: crits with **Fireball**, **Fire Blast** and **Scorch**, and every **Fire Blast** hit, each cut your next **Pyroblast**'s cast by 25%, up to 3 stacks. At 3 stacks its 6-second cast takes 1.5 seconds. On one mob the 3 stacks rarely come together before it dies; one or two already let a **Pyroblast** fit into a freeze, and the opener stays the main one.
- **Frost Nova** when the mob reaches you with more than about a tenth of its health left, at any mana: the rules of 2026-11-15 dropped the old floor of 30% of your mana. Then step back; **Ice Lance** while it is frozen; with **Frost Nova** down, **Fire Blast** and keep casting. The tuned rules never cast **Scorch**.
- **Ignite** (40% of every crit's damage over 4 seconds), **Master of Elements** (30% of the base cost back on every Fire or Frost crit) and **Fire Power** (up to 12% more Fire damage with several Fire effects on the mob) are the Fire tree's passive half. **Combustion** goes at the pull once you have it at level 59.

**Caveats**

- This build has no Ice Block: a pull that goes wrong is **Blink**, **Mana Shield** or a healing potion.
- Mana: **Fireball** costs 410 at top rank against 290 for a **Frostbolt**. Drink below 15% of your mana and eat below 80% of your health (the tuned rules; 80% since 2026-11-20); use **Evocation** between fights in place of a drink, when it is ready and you are under 15% mana. Whenever you sit down to drink or eat, do both while the other bar is not full: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17).
- Armor: **Mage Armor** from 34, as on the other mage leveling pages: the tune of 2026-11-17, once you eat while you drink, found it about 1.2% faster per kill. The rules of 2026-11-15 had kept **Ice Armor** to 60 (Mage Armor was no faster then).
- Pulls of several mobs: **Flamestrike** hits them all, but the fight lab only measured single pulls for Fire.
- This is the open leveling search's 1/32/18 winner of 2026-11-20, 0.7% faster per kill at levels 20-60 than the build it replaced. The leveling filler pass of the same day moved two points that measured nothing or only held a tier open, Improved Scorch and Improved Fire Ward, to **Burning Soul** (45) and **Improved Fire Blast** (60); **Precision**'s third point measured nothing too, and stays because it holds **Ice Shards**' row open.

**When to change it**

- On a nearly dead mob (below about a third), finish with an instant that will kill (**Fire Blast**, an **Ice Lance** into a freeze) or the wand rather than start a **Fireball**.
