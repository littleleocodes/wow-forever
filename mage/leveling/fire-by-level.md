# Fire Mage — leveling, level by level

[Mage](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Fire](fire.md) > Level by level

What a Fire mage presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Fire levels with **Fireball** at range and **Fire Blast** up close, opens every pull from 20 with a hard-cast **Pyroblast**, and borrows the Frost tree's freeze: **Frost Nova**, a step back and, from 31, **Ice Lance** into the frozen mob. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 1/32/18 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/mage/60/1-55500201210133031-2034320001003-12)), the one on the [Fire leveling page](fire.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | **Fireball** is your attack, and it stays on Q all the way to 60. |
| 6-9 | **Fire Blast** for the moment the mob reaches you, and to finish it. |
| 10-19 | **Frost Nova** roots the mob at your feet: step back and keep casting. Talent points start. |
| 20-23 | **Pyroblast** (talent): a long, heavy cast that opens every pull. |
| 24-30 | **Counterspell** for mobs that cast. From 27 **Frostbite** sometimes freezes a mob that hits you. |
| 31-43 | **Ice Lance** (talent): an instant that deals four times its damage to a frozen mob. |
| 44-58 | **Heating Up** (talent): Fire crits and every **Fire Blast** add stacks; at 3 your next **Pyroblast** takes a quarter of the time. |
| 59-60 | **Combustion** (talent): every 3 minutes, more Fire crit for the opening **Pyroblast** and the hits after it. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, conjuring, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes, so you never relearn a key. Bold marks what arrives in that stage: drag it onto the slot when you train it. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [mage keybinds page](keybinds.md); this spec's macro is in the notes below the tables.

## Levels 1-5: Fireball

**The trainer teaches:** Arcane Intellect (1), **Fireball** (1), Frost Armor (1), Conjure Water (4), Frostbolt (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Fireball; Middle mouse = Wand; Shift+C = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Fireball.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Fireball** while the mob has more than about a third of its health. If it reaches you, keep casting.
2. Below a third: your wand, once you own one.

- Keep **Frost Armor** and **Arcane Intellect** up at all times, from here to 60.
- **Frostbolt** (4) is not part of the Fire list: skip it. **Conjure Water** (4): drink between fights instead of waiting for your mana.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 12 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Fireball 2.2, Wand 0.8.*

## Levels 6-9: Fire Blast, your instant

**The trainer teaches:** Conjure Food (6), **Fire Blast** (6), Arcane Missiles (8), Polymorph (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Fireball at 6.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Fire Blast; 2 = Polymorph (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Fireball, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Fireball** from as far away as you can, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than about a third of its health.
2. **Fire Blast** when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** finishes, when it is on you, and when it will kill.
3. Below about a third of its health: **Fire Blast** whenever it is ready, else your wand. No new **Fireball**: a long cast on a dying mob is wasted.

- **Polymorph** (8): when a second mob joins, sheep it, kill the first, then fight the sheep.
- **Arcane Missiles** (8) is not part of the Fire list. **Conjure Food** (6) saves you buying bread.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 6. Presses per kill at level 6: Fireball 2.3, Wand 1.8, Fire Blast 1.0.*

## Levels 10-19: Frost Nova: freeze and step back

**The trainer teaches:** **Frost Nova** (10), Dampen Magic (12), Slow Fall (12), Arcane Explosion (14), Flamestrike (16), Amplify Magic (18), Remove Lesser Curse (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Fire Blast at 14; Fireball at 12, 18.

**Talents:** 10-14 Accelerant, 15-19 Ignite.

**Keys to set:** E = Frost Nova; Shift+E = Arcane Explosion (at 14); Shift+R = Flamestrike (at 16); Shift+3 = Remove Lesser Curse (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Fireball, E Frost Nova, Mouse 5 Fire Blast.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Fireball** while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than about a third of its health.
2. **Fire Blast** when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** finishes. With **Fire Blast** on cooldown and **Frost Nova** ready, wand it until it arrives.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, while you have more than 30% of your mana, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
4. If it reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown: **Fire Blast**, then keep casting **Fireball**.
5. Below about a third of its health: **Fire Blast** whenever it is ready, else your wand. No new **Fireball**: a long cast on a dying mob is wasted.

- **Accelerant** (10-14) takes half a second off your **Fireball** cast. **Ignite** (15-19): your Fire crits burn the mob for more.
- **Arcane Explosion** (14) and **Flamestrike** (16) are for packs. One mob at a time never needs them.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 28 at level 10, 35 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Fireball 3.1, Wand 2.1, Fire Blast 1.5, Frost Nova 0.5.*

## Levels 20-23: Pyroblast opens every pull

**The trainer teaches:** Blink (20), Blizzard (20), Evocation (20), Fire Ward (20), Mana Shield (20), Frost Ward (22), Scorch (22).

**New ranks to buy:** Arcane Explosion at 22; Fire Blast at 22; Polymorph at 20.

**Talents:** 20 Pyroblast, 21-22 Precision, 23 Frost Warding.

**Keys to set:** F = Blink; 1 = Pyroblast; 3 = Mana Shield; 4 = Evocation; Shift+Mouse 5 = Fire Ward; Shift+Mouse 4 = Frost Ward (at 22).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Fireball, E Frost Nova, F Blink, Mouse 5 Fire Blast, 1 Pyroblast, 3 Mana Shield.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Open every pull with a hard-cast **Pyroblast** from maximum range. The mob runs at you during the 6-second cast: finish it, then go on.
2. **Fireball** while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than about a third of its health.
3. **Fire Blast** when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** finishes. With **Fire Blast** on cooldown and **Frost Nova** ready, wand it until it arrives.
4. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, while you have more than 30% of your mana, then step back out of its reach. Skip it on a mob that is almost dead.
5. If it reaches you with **Frost Nova** on cooldown: **Fire Blast**, then keep casting **Fireball**.
6. Below about a third of its health: **Fire Blast** whenever it is ready, else your wand. No new **Fireball**: a long cast on a dying mob is wasted.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Mob on you, **Frost Nova** on cooldown and your health low: **Blink** out of its reach and keep casting; **Mana Shield** if it still hits you.

- The opener is a tuned rule: **Pyroblast** is the Fire signature spell, so every pull starts with one. The fight lab lets the mob run at you from the first second of the cast; a mob that only notices you when the **Pyroblast** lands gives you more time.
- **Scorch** (22): the tuned rules never cast it. Leave it off your bar. **Precision** (21-22) adds hit.
- **Evocation** (20) is for between fights: channel it whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana. **Blink** (20) and **Mana Shield** (20) are for a pull that goes wrong.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 35 at level 20. Presses per kill at level 20: Fireball 2.0, Wand 1.8, Fire Blast 1.4, Pyroblast 1.0, Frost Nova 0.7.*

## Between fights

- Drink your conjured water when you are below about 15% of your mana, and eat below 40% of your health. Those are the tuned rules: resting earlier cost more time in the fight lab than it saved.
- **Evocation** (from 20) whenever it is ready and you are under 40% mana.
- Conjure both food and water yourself.
- Visit the trainer every even level. **Fireball** alone has eleven ranks.

## Other ways to level this class

- Packs: **Frost Nova** when two or more reach you, then **Arcane Explosion** around you or **Flamestrike** on the group. The fight lab measured single pulls only for Fire.
- [Arcane-Frost](arcane-frost-by-level.md) is the recommended mage leveling spec in the fight lab; [Frost](frost-by-level.md) is close behind.
