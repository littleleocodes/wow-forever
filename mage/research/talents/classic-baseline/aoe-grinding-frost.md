## Summary

The community AoE-farming build (Icy Veins' 1-60 AoE grinding build): control first — Elemental Precision so nothing resists, Improved Frost Nova, Permafrost and Improved Blizzard so a pack stays snared through two Blizzards, Cold Snap/Ice Block/Ice Barrier for bad pulls and for gathering — then damage (Piercing Ice, Ice Shards, Improved Cone of Cold) and Arcane mana talents. No Frostbite (freezes scatter the clump) and no Shatter (mobs are only frozen during the Nova, and the points buy more control elsewhere). Warcraft Tavern's 7/0/44 variant spends the Arcane points in Frost instead.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Improved Blizzard** — The essential talent: Blizzard kills the pack while its snare keeps the pack off you.
2. **Permafrost** — Makes the Blizzard chill last long enough for the second Blizzard to land before it wears off.
3. **Improved Frost Nova** — Nova every 21s instead of 25s: a second Nova is up when the pack closes, and there is margin for a resist.
4. **Elemental Precision** — One resist in a pack of 8 means melee damage; hit is control here, not DPS.
5. **Arctic Reach** — Blizzard from further away (less melee damage) and a bigger Nova/Cone of Cold radius.
6. **Cold Snap** — A second Nova and Ice Block when a pull goes wrong.
7. **Ice Block** — Pull-and-block gathering; the emergency button.
8. **Ice Barrier** — Gather on the mount without being dazed off it; an escape when running.
9. **Improved Cone of Cold** — Cone of Cold is cast every pack when the mobs close.

## Notes

- Never take Frostbite in this build: random freezes scatter the clump and mobs drift out of Blizzard.
- Warcraft Tavern's guide keeps Improved Blizzard at 2/3 so the Blizzard chill does not overwrite Cone of Cold's stronger snare; Icy Veins takes 3/3.
- Loop: gather (mount / Ice Barrier / pull-and-Ice Block), Frost Nova, Blink to range, two Blizzards, Cone of Cold as they close, kite until Nova is back, repeat; Arcane Explosion / wand for the last slivers.
