1. **Improved Frostbolt** — Faster Frostbolt from level 10.
2. **Shatter** — Frost Nova -> crit at 30 makes every pull a kite.
3. **Ice Block** — The 'oops' button at 34.
4. **Ice Barrier** — Shield at 43; no more pushback.
