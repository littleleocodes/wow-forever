# Mage / forever: respec plan for `leveling-st`

> Generated by `_reference/data/scorer/respec.py`. Plans are optimizer builds for a target level, bought in optimizer order and extended greedily to 60; the schedule minimises total leveling hours including respec gold converted to time. Assumptions (edit in `respec.py`): hours per level by decade {10: 0.7, 20: 1.2, 30: 1.6, 40: 2.2, 50: 3.0}, gold/hour {10: 0.3, 20: 0.8, 30: 1.5, 40: 3.0, 50: 6.0}, respec 1g then +5g each (cap 50g), at most 4 respecs. Scores are the leveling profile's 'fraction of time per kill saved' vs a talentless character of the same model; models are compared through their `abs_dps_factor`.

Talentless baseline: 90 h for levels 10-60 under these assumptions.

## Reading it

- A respec only pays when the level-optimal build differs from the 60-build's path *and* the gap, summed over the levels until the next switch, beats the respec fee in hours. Small gaps (a few %) rarely do before 40; large ones (a different tree, e.g. Fire vs Frost, or a two-hander build vs a dual-wield build) can.
- 'Extended greedily' plans (target < 60) show what happens if you stop planning after the target level; if the schedule ends on one, the 60-build path was not worth its early sacrifices.
- Everything downstream of the effect encodings inherits their uncertainty; treat differences under ~1 h as noise.
