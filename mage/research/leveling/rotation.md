## arcane-leveling

Played by the page build of [Arcane](../../leveling/arcane.md) ([`arcane-open-leveling`](builds/arcane-open-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30, **Mage Armor** from 34) and **Arcane Intellect** up. Pull from as far away as you can; **Presence of Mind** at the pull (your first cast goes out instantly); **Arcane Power** at the pull when it is ready and you have more than half your mana (once you have the talent, at 42).

1. With **Arcane Wrath**: cast it first on the pull if it will land before the mob reaches you, and again whenever it is ready and there is time.
2. **Arcane Blast** while the mob walks in: at most two in a row, only if the cast ends before the mob reaches you and costs no more than a quarter of your mana. Each stack gives all your spells spell damage (12% of your Intellect) for 18 seconds.
3. **Arcane Missiles** as your channel, also once the mob is on you (with **Improved Channeling**, hits do not cut it short), and at once when **Missile Barrage** lights up (after your two stacks, or when the mob is on you).
4. Mob on you: **Frost Nova** while it has more than about a tenth of its health, then step back and keep casting; **Cone of Cold** only when the mob is on you and below a fifth of its health, then step back; **Ice Lance** if the mob is frozen.
5. When you cannot afford **Arcane Missiles**: **Fire Blast** if the mob is on you, else **Frostbolt**; finish with a **Fire Blast** that will kill, or your wand.
6. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

Between fights: Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana and eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rules), **Evocation** in place of a drink when it is ready and you are under 15% mana. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. **Evocation** is not a sit and brings no food.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-15, on top of the priority below): drink below 15% mana; MAGE_ARMOR = 1; eat below 40% health. Together +2.1% time per kill saved for `arcane-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`arcane-open-leveling`](builds/arcane-open-leveling.md): not recorded (its times per kill come from the race records of the search that found it, which keep times only; the table above has them).

## frost-leveling

Played by the page build of [Frost](../../leveling/frost.md) ([`frost-open-leveling`](builds/frost-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level: pull at max range, the mob walks in at 7 yd/s (slowed by chills), swings every 2 s once in melee (pushing casts back), Frost Nova when it arrives, a 2-second step back, Ice Lance/Frostbolt into the freeze, Fire Blast and wand to finish without overkill. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Evocation when low, conjured water below 35% mana, food below 65% health; a death costs 90 s. Every hit, miss, crit, partial resist, chill, freeze, proc and pushback is rolled.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30, **Mage Armor** from 34) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Ice Barrier** (the page build's, from 40) before each pull when it is ready, and again in a fight once it has run out. Pull from as far away as you can.

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in (it slows it) and whenever nothing below applies, but only while the mob has more than a tenth of its health; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends, wand it until it arrives (the tuned rules keep **Fire Blast** for the finish).
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee; if your build has **Cold Snap**, it resets **Frost Nova** when more than 8 seconds of cooldown are left and the mob still has 40% of its health.
3. **Ice Lance** into the freeze whenever it is ready: it has an 8-second cooldown, shared with **Fire Blast**, so one per **Frost Nova**, **Frostbite** freeze or **Fingers of Frost** charge, not a stream of them.
4. In the last fifth of the mob's health: **Fire Blast** when it will kill or the mob is on you, or an **Ice Lance** that will kill; below a tenth, only **Ice Lance** or your wand. No Cone of Cold at all.
5. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

Between fights: Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana (the tuned rule), **Evocation** in place of a drink when it is ready and you are under 15% mana, eat below 40% of your health (the tuned rule). Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. **Evocation** is not a sit and brings no food.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-20, on top of the priority below): Frostbolt only while the mob has more than 10% health. Together +0.5% time per kill saved for `frost-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
