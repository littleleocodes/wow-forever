## barrage-frost-leveling

Played by the page build of [Arcane-Frost](../../leveling/arcane-frost.md) ([`arcane-frost-open-leveling`](builds/arcane-frost-open-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up. Pull from as far away as you can.

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in (it slows it) and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than a tenth of its health; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends, **Fire Blast** it instead.
2. **Arcane Missiles** whenever **Missile Barrage** lights up (your Frostbolts give it): free and twice as fast; not while the mob hits you in melee unless it is frozen.
3. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee; **Cold Snap** resets **Frost Nova** if it has more than 8 seconds of cooldown left and the mob still has 40% of its health; with both down, **Fire Blast**. No Cone of Cold.
4. **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen (**Frost Nova**, **Frostbite** or **Fingers of Frost**).
5. To finish: a **Fire Blast** (not below a tenth of the mob's health) or **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand.
6. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

Between fights: Drink your conjured water below 15% of your mana, **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below 40% of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-23, on top of the priority below): Frostbolt only while the mob has more than 10% health; Fire Blast only while the mob has more than 10% health; Cone of Cold only while the mob has more than 35% health; drink below 15% mana; never cast Cone of Cold; eat below 40% health. Together +4.5% time per kill saved for `opt-frost-leveling-leveling-st` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`arcane-frost-open-leveling`](builds/arcane-frost-open-leveling.md): not recorded (its times per kill come from the race records of the search that found it, which keep times only; the table above has them).

## frost-leveling

Played by the page build of [Frost](../../leveling/frost.md) ([`frost-open-leveling`](builds/frost-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level: pull at max range, the mob walks in at 7 yd/s (slowed by chills), swings every 2 s once in melee (pushing casts back), Frost Nova when it arrives, a 2-second step back, Ice Lance/Frostbolt into the freeze, Fire Blast and wand to finish without overkill. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Evocation when low, conjured water below 35% mana, food below 65% health; a death costs 90 s. Every hit, miss, crit, partial resist, chill, freeze, proc and pushback is rolled.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Ice Barrier** before each pull once you have it. Pull from as far away as you can.

1. **Frostbolt** while the mob walks in (it slows it) and whenever nothing below applies, but only while the mob has more than a fifth of its health; if the mob would reach you before the cast ends, **Fire Blast** it instead (or wand it until it arrives if **Fire Blast** is on cooldown and **Frost Nova** is ready).
2. **Frost Nova** when it reaches you, then step back out of melee; **Cold Snap** resets **Frost Nova** if it has more than 8 seconds of cooldown left and the mob still has 40% of its health; with both down, **Fire Blast**.
3. **Ice Lance** again and again while the mob is frozen (**Frost Nova**, **Frostbite** or **Fingers of Frost**).
4. Below a fifth of the mob's health: **Fire Blast** (not below a tenth), an **Ice Lance** that will kill, or your wand. No Cone of Cold at all.
5. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

Between fights: Drink your conjured water below a third of your mana, **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below two thirds of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-22, on top of the priority below): never cast Cone of Cold; Frostbolt only while the mob has more than 20% health; Fire Blast only while the mob has more than 10% health. Together +3.7% time per kill saved for `leveling-frost` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## arcane-leveling

Played by the page build of [Arcane](../../leveling/arcane.md) ([`arcane-open-leveling`](builds/arcane-open-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up. Pull from as far away as you can; **Presence of Mind** at the pull (your first cast goes out instantly), and **Arcane Power** too while you have more than half your mana.

1. With **Arcane Wrath**: cast it first on the pull if it will land before the mob reaches you, and again whenever it is ready and there is time.
2. **Arcane Blast** while the mob walks in: at most two in a row, only if the cast ends before the mob reaches you and costs no more than a quarter of your mana. Each stack gives all your spells spell damage (15% of your Intellect) for 15 seconds.
3. **Arcane Missiles** as your channel, also once the mob is on you (with **Improved Channeling**, hits do not cut it short), and at once when **Missile Barrage** lights up (after your two stacks, or when the mob is on you).
4. No Frost Nova: let the mob come and keep channeling. **Cone of Cold** only when the mob is on you and below a fifth of its health, then step back; **Ice Lance** if the mob is frozen.
5. When you cannot afford **Arcane Missiles**: **Fire Blast** if the mob is on you, else **Frostbolt**; finish with a **Fire Blast** that will kill, or your wand.
6. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

Between fights: Drink your conjured water below a third of your mana, **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below 40% of your health.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-22, on top of the priority below): Cone of Cold only when the mob is below 20% health; never cast Frost Nova; eat below 40% health. Together +4.0% time per kill saved for `mage/research/leveling/fightlab/candidates/arcane-blast-leveling-start.json` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`arcane-open-leveling`](builds/arcane-open-leveling.md): not recorded (its times per kill come from the race records of the search that found it, which keep times only; the table above has them).

Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Evocation when low, conjured water below 35% mana, food below 65% health; a death costs 90 s. Every hit, miss, crit, partial resist, chill, freeze, proc and pushback is rolled.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Frost Armor** (**Ice Armor** from level 30) and **Arcane Intellect** up; **Combustion** at the pull when it is ready. Pull from as far away as you can.

1. **Fireball** while the mob walks in, and whenever nothing below applies, while the mob has more than about a third (35%) of its health.
2. **Fire Blast** when the mob would reach you before a **Fireball** lands (**Scorch** if **Fire Blast** is on cooldown and there is time), once it is on you, or when it will kill.

4. **Ice Lance** while the mob is frozen; **Pyroblast** only at 3 stacks of **Heating Up**.
5. Mob on you and no control left: **Scorch** (a short cast, pushed back less), while the mob has more than about a third of its health.
6. Below about a third of the mob's health, no **Fireball** or **Scorch**: finish with **Fire Blast**, an **Ice Lance** or your wand.
7. **Counterspell** a mob that casts spells.

Between fights: Drink your conjured water below a third of your mana, **Evocation** when it is ready and you are under 40% mana, eat below two thirds of your health.
