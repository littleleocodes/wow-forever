## How to play

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Scorch** when the target has fewer than 5 stacks of Fire Vulnerability
2. **Evocation** when your mana is below 10%
3. **Pyroblast** when Hot Streak has 3 stacks
4. **Fire Blast**
5. **Frostfire Bolt**
6. **Fireball**

**Fireball** is your filler: cast it whenever nothing above is ready. This is the simulator's rotation for this build, simplified (potions, trinkets, racials and fine timing left out); the exact list: [research rotation page](../research/raid/rotation.md).
