## How to play

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Scorch** when the target has fewer than 5 stacks of Fire Vulnerability
2. **Evocation** when your mana is below 10%
3. **Pyroblast** when Hot Streak has 2 stacks
4. **Combustion**
5. **Frostfire Bolt**
6. **Fire Blast**
7. **Arcane Missiles** when Missile Barrage is active
8. **Fireball**

**Fireball** is your filler: cast it whenever nothing above is ready.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Ignite**: part of the rotation (about 29 times a fight)
- Use your trinkets, your racial and a potion together with your big cooldowns.
