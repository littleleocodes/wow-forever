## How to play

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Fire Blast**
2. **Arcane Missiles** when Missile Barrage is active
3. **Frostbolt** when Arcane Blast has 2 stacks
4. **Arcane Blast**
5. **Fireball**

**Fireball** is your filler: cast it whenever nothing above is ready.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Ignite**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 6 times a fight)
- **Arcane Power**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 1 time a fight)
- **Presence of Mind**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 1 time a fight)
- Use your trinkets, your racial and a potion together with your big cooldowns.
