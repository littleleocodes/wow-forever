# Warrior

> Classic's raid melee DPS (Fury) and default raid tank (Protection). Forever turns Arms into a raid spec around Bloodthrill,
> Overpower and Slam, folds the weapon specializations into Weaponmaster, makes Tactical Mastery baseline, and gives Protection
> Vanguard, Focused Rage and Bastion. Rage is normalized to weapon speed. Playable by 9 races including the new Skyborne.

## Forever headline changes (beta client build 1.60.1.70753)

Forever's warrior has 53 talents (17 Arms, 18 Fury, 18 Protection), as refreshed on build 1.60.1.70755 (the live game removed Raging Blows, which the 70753 export still had). Classic talents are gone or renamed, and new ones take their places.

- **Baseline now:** Tactical Mastery is a passive trained at 14 that keeps 10 rage through a stance change (Improved Tactical Mastery, 3 ranks, adds up to 15 more).
- **Removed talents:** Tactical Mastery, Axe, Mace, Sword and Polearm Specialization (Arms); Improved Demoralizing Shout, Improved Battle Shout (Fury); Boundless Rage, Precision (Fury, removed in 70755); Toughness, Improved Shield Block, Improved Taunt, One-Handed Weapon Specialization (Protection).
- **New talents:** Improved Tactical Mastery, Spearing Strike, Bloodthrill, Weaponmaster (Arms); Reckless Swings, Gore Drinker, Improved Whirlwind (Fury); Iron Will (it shortens stuns and fears), Master of Defense, Vanguard, Focused Rage, Bastion (Protection).
- **Renamed:** Improved Cleave is Furious Precision (now +10% off-hand hit); Classic's Iron Will slot is Lingering Rage (now 10 sec longer before rage decays out of combat); Raging Blows is Reckless Swings (now: your next 4 melee attacks generate 5 additional rage, 8 sec).
- **Moved or reworked:** Improved Slam to Arms (2 ranks: up to 0.5 sec off Slam's cast and global cooldown, up to 5 sec off its cooldown, no swing delay); Bloodthrill is 2 ranks (10% and 20% chance per main-hand hit on a target with your Rend); Improved Thunder Clap to Protection. Prerequisites: Anger Management needs 3 points in Improved Tactical Mastery, Gore Drinker needs 3 points in Blood Craze, Mortal Strike needs Sweeping Strikes, Impale needs Deep Wounds, Master of Defense needs Shield Specialization, Shield Slam needs Concussion Blow. Unbridled Wrath is a 60% chance for 1 rage with any weapon; Dual Wield Specialization +25% off-hand damage and +50% off-hand rage; Deep Wounds 60% of the weapon's average damage over 12 sec; Weaponmaster by weapon: axe or polearm +5% crit, mace or staff 15% of armor ignored, sword a chance for an extra attack.
- **Spells:** Battle Shout 40% less attack power but lasts 3 minutes and, since 70425, reaches party or raid members within 20 yards; Bloodthirst 45% of attack power plus a flat amount and 10% movement speed (no healing); Death Wish +5% damage taken instead of lower armor and resistances; Demoralizing Shout 204 attack power for 45 sec; Last Stand on a 3-minute cooldown (a talent in Protection); Retaliation 15 minutes; Shield Block 2 blocks in 7 sec; Shield Slam 640-670 plus block value; Shield Wall 60% less damage for 12 sec on a 15-minute cooldown; Slam a 1.5 sec cast on a 15-second cooldown; Taunt 8-second cooldown; Thunder Clap slows attacks by 20% on a 6-second cooldown; Victory Rush (new: 15% of attack power, heals 10%, for 20 sec after a kill); Whirlwind strikes with both weapons; Concussion Blow 10 rage.
- **Beta notes (to 2026-10-08):** white swings give rage by weapon speed alone (normalized), and a critical strike gives 100% more; rage from damage taken is measured against a same-level creature's expected health (2026-10-08); out of combat 1.25 rage decays per second; a queued Heroic Strike no longer raises off-hand hit; Rend and Sunder Armor tag a mob at once. Unknown until launch: Sunder Armor's threat, and the attack power shares Forever adds to Rend, Revenge and Thunder Clap (the community simulator fits them from level-20 beta logs).
- **Beta notes (16 Oct, build 70425):** Battle Shout raid-wide; Slam no longer suffers spell pushback; Overpower can trigger against much
  lower-level enemies; Lingering Rage no longer stops your health regeneration after combat; Improved Berserker Rage removes movement
  impairing effects; Weaponmaster's sword extra attack no longer triggers from Shield Bash and Shield Slam. Bloodthrill's tooltip now
  reads "allow an activation of Overpower" (the 6-second window it named is gone; Overpower's own window applies).
