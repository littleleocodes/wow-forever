# Warrior: start here

[All classes](../START-HERE.md)

The Classic raid melee DPS king (Fury) and the default raid tank (Protection). Forever keeps that identity but removes Tactical Mastery and the weapon specializations, moves Improved Slam into Arms, and hands Protection a set of new threat/stamina talents. Playable by 9 races including the new Skyborne.
