## Summary

The threat-and-mitigation raid tank. Shield Slam, Defiance and Improved Sunder Armor carry threat; Shield Specialization, Anticipation, Toughness and Last Stand carry survival. Arms is taken to Tactical Mastery and Anger Management for stance dancing, Fury for Cruelty.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Defiance** — +15% threat in Defensive Stance — the biggest single threat talent.
2. **Shield Slam** — Highest-threat ability in the kit.
3. **Shield Specialization** — +5% block and rage on block.
4. **Anticipation** — +10 Defense: reduces crit/crushing exposure and adds avoidance.
5. **Last Stand** — 30% max-health emergency button.
6. **Improved Shield Block** — One point is enough for the extra block charge; the duration ranks are marginal.
7. **Improved Sunder Armor** — Cheaper Sunders = more threat per rage.
8. **One-Handed Weapon Specialization** — +10% damage with one-handers — threat.
9. **Tactical Mastery** — Charge/Berserker Rage/Intercept swaps without losing rage; unlocks Anger Management.
10. **Cruelty** — +5% crit = more Heroic Strike/Revenge threat and Deep Wounds if taken.

## Notes

- In late Classic (AQ40/Naxx gear) many main tanks ran Fury-Prot or even 17/34/0 dual-wield in Defensive Stance for threat; this 11/5/35 build is the safe baseline for progression.
- Improved Shield Wall 1/2 vs 2/2 is a matter of how often Shield Wall is the plan.
