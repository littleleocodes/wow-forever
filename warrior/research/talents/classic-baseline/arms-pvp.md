## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Mortal Strike** — -50% healing on the target and a hard-hitting instant.
2. **Improved Hamstring** — 15% chance to root for 5s — the PvP kill-setup talent.
3. **Tactical Mastery** — Stance dance with rage: Charge -> Battle, Intercept -> Berserker, Defensive for Disarm.
4. **Improved Charge** — Opening rage for an immediate Hamstring + Mortal Strike.
5. **Enrage** — +25% damage after being crit — very frequent in PvP.
6. **Piercing Howl** — AoE snare; the anti-kite tool.
7. **Sweeping Strikes** — Cleaves onto pets/second targets; prerequisite for Mortal Strike.
8. **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** — +5% damage with the two-hander.

## Notes

- Weapon specialization is chosen by the weapon you actually have; Sword Spec (extra attacks) and Axe Spec (crit) are the usual picks and replace Improved Heroic Strike/Improved Overpower.
