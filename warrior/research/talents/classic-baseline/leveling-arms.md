## Summary

Two-handed Arms is the standard Classic leveling path: Improved Rend/Deep Wounds early, Mortal Strike at 40, then Fury for Cruelty, Unbridled Wrath, Piercing Howl and Enrage. Ends at a full 31/20/0 PvP-ready build with no respec needed.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Mortal Strike** — Big instant hit at 40; the leveling milestone.
2. **Deep Wounds** — Bleed on crit; excellent while Rend is your main opener.
3. **Two-Handed Weapon Specialization** — Flat 5% damage.
4. **Sweeping Strikes** — Cleave on multi-pulls; needed for Mortal Strike.

## Notes

- Rend -> Charge -> Heroic Strike/Overpower until 40; after Mortal Strike, MS on cooldown with Heroic Strike as the dump. Improved Overpower is skipped so Mortal Strike lands exactly at 40.
- Respec to 17/34/0 Fury at 60 for raiding once you have a second one-hander and ~9% hit.
