## Raid sim (community simulator, level 60)

[`raidsim/`](raidsim/) runs every raid build through the ElliotWood/Forever simulator (the community's wowsims fork for the beta; commit 40be98e) with that simulator's own gear preset and rotation, 3000 iterations per build, next to the simulator's preset talent builds. **DPS specs are ranked on a boss-level training dummy with only your own buffs** (the class's self-buffs and the spec's standard consumables; no raid or party buffs, no debuffs from others): raw personal DPS decides the raid DPS builds. Tanks are measured in the raid setting (full buffs, boss melee). Same gear and rotation for every build, so only the talents differ. OOM s = seconds per fight out of mana: on the dummy a spec that lives on Blessing of Wisdom and Mana Spring in a raid runs dry, and its mana talents weigh more there than in a raid.
