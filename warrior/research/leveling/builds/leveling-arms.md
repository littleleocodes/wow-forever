## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Mortal Strike** — Worth +40% time per kill at 60 in the sim (the rotation has nothing else to spend rage on); reaching 30 Arms points by 40 is the whole point of the order.
2. **Cruelty** — +5% crit: +9-11% per kill in the sim through Deep Wounds, Overpower and Enrage procs.
3. **Bloodthrill** — Overpower on a Rended mob without waiting for a dodge: +9-10% in the sim.
4. **Deep Wounds** — Crit bleeds: +7-9% in the sim.
5. **Enrage** — +10% damage after being hit, which happens constantly solo: +7-10% in the sim.
6. **Improved Overpower** — Overpower crits: +5-6%.
7. **Weaponmaster** — +3% crit with an axe or polearm: +5%.
8. **Unbridled Wrath** — +2 rage per white hit with a two-hander: +4%.
9. **Deflection** — +5% parry: +3% per kill and fewer deaths; also the cheapest way to 30 Arms points.
10. **Improved Rend** — +35% Rend: +3%.

## Leveling rotation (fight lab)

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a two-hander in Battle Stance: Bloodrage and Charge on the pull (the mob is stunned 1 s), Battle Shout kept up, Victory Rush after a kill, Mortal Strike on cooldown, Overpower after a dodge or a Bloodthrill proc, Rend once, Execute under 20% with all the rage, Heroic Strike with spare rage, white swings that generate rage by Classic's formula (the mob dodges 5%, parries 5%, 10% of swings glance for 95%). Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, a bandage below 60% health (First Aid, 60 s cooldown), food below 50%, rage decays; a death costs 90 s.
