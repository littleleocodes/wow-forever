## How the fights go

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level. Arms (a two-hander) and Fury (a weapon in each hand) fight in Battle Stance: Bloodrage and Charge on the pull (the mob is stunned 1 s; Death Wish too when the build has it), Battle Shout kept up, Victory Rush after a kill, Mortal Strike or Bloodthirst on cooldown, Overpower after a dodge or a Bloodthrill proc, Rend once, Execute under 20% with all the rage, Heroic Strike with spare rage, Reckless Swings (when talented) while health allows; spells that need Berserker Stance (Whirlwind, Berserker Rage) are never cast. Protection fights sword and board in Defensive Stance: Charge from Battle Stance, then Shield Block while the mob hits, Revenge, Shield Slam, Rend and Heroic Strike. White swings generate rage by Forever's normalized rule (weapon speed, half for the off-hand, double on a crit; tables.RAGE_MODEL) (the mob dodges 5%, parries 5%, 10% of swings glance for 95%). Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, a bandage below 60% health (First Aid, 60 s cooldown), food below the eating threshold (65%; 40% under the tuned Arms and Fury rules), rage decays; a death costs 90 s.

The rotation in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Battle Stance**, a two-handed weapon.

1. **Bloodrage** (above 45% health), then **Charge** in; open with **Victory Rush** when it is up and you are below 90% health.
2. **Battle Shout**: keep it up (it lasts 3 minutes).
3. **Execute** first once the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Overpower** whenever it lights up: after a dodge, or from **Bloodthrill** while **Rend** ticks.
5. **Reckless Swings** (if your build has it) whenever it is ready while you are above 80% health and the mob above 30%: your next 4 hits give 5 more rage each.
6. **Mortal Strike** whenever ready.
7. **Rend** once, while the mob has more than a third of its health.
8. Spare rage: **Slam** (only with **Improved Slam**), then **Spearing Strike** (if your build has it), then **Heroic Strike**; keep 15 for **Overpower**, and 30 more when **Mortal Strike** is almost ready.
9. Two or more mobs: **Sweeping Strikes**, then **Thunder Clap** and **Cleave**.

Between fights: Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40% (the tuned rule).

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-03, on top of the priority below): eat below 40% health. Together +1.8% time per kill saved for `leveling-arms` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
