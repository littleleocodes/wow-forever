# Protection Warrior — leveling, level by level

[Warrior](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Protection](protection.md) > Level by level

What a Protection warrior presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. A warrior has no mana: every attack costs rage, and rage comes from hitting and being hit; with a shield, blocks give rage too. Protection levels with a one-handed weapon and a shield. From level 10 you fight in Defensive Stance and step into Battle Stance only to Charge. It kills more slowly than Arms (the Protection leveling page has the numbers), but you almost never stop to eat, and you arrive at 60 as a tank in your raid talents. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 17/3/31 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/warrior/60/35300103002-03-050530020330001351-12)), the one on the [Protection leveling page](protection.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | A one-handed weapon and a shield from the start. **Heroic Strike** is for the end of the fight. |
| 4-9 | **Charge** opens every fight with free rage and a stun. **Rend** is a bleed you apply once. |
| 10-11 | **Defensive Stance**: you take 10% less damage (and deal 10% less) and fight there. **Charge** still needs **Battle Stance**, so every pull starts with a swap. |
| 12-13 | **Shield Bash** interrupts a caster and locks that school of spells for 6 seconds. |
| 14-15 | **Revenge** lights up after you block, dodge or parry: cheap and hard-hitting. **Tactical Mastery** keeps 10 rage through a stance change. |
| 16-32 | **Shield Block**: 75% more chance to block for 7 seconds or 2 blocks. Each block opens **Revenge** and gives rage. |
| 33-39 | **Concussion Blow** (talent): a 5-second stun, your emergency button below half your health. |
| 40-60 | **Shield Slam** (talent): your main attack from here to 60, whenever it is ready. It works in both stances. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. A warrior's bar 1 is one bar per stance: WoW shows the Battle Stance bar while you are in Battle Stance and the Defensive Stance bar while you are in Defensive Stance, so the tables below give bar 1 once per stance; bar 2 stays the same in every stance. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape or the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the wider or rarer version of the key under it. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the opener macro: the [warrior keybinds page](keybinds.md), laid out for Arms; the notes below say what Protection changes.

- Bar 1 is two bars from level 10: set up the Defensive Stance bar the first time you enter the stance. A key keeps its job on both: Q strikes, E engages, R bleeds, F escapes.
- Every pull from 10: E (**Battle Stance**), Mouse 4 (**Bloodrage**), E again (**Charge**, on the bar you now see), R (**Rend**; from 40 Q, **Shield Slam**) while you have the rage, Mouse 5 (**Defensive Stance**). The fight then runs on the Defensive Stance bar: Mouse 4 **Shield Block**, Mouse 5 **Revenge**, Q **Shield Slam** from 40.
- What changes from the Arms keys: on the Defensive Stance bar the thumbs hold the shield's reactions, **Revenge** where Arms has **Overpower** and **Shield Block** where Arms has **Bloodrage** (which stays on the Battle Stance bar, where you press it); 2, 3 and 4 hold **Concussion Blow**, **Shield Bash** and **Shield Wall**, because **Execute**, **Hamstring** and **Retaliation** need Battle Stance. On the Battle Stance bar, Mouse 5 takes **Defensive Stance** and 2, 4 and C stay free. On bar 2, Shift+R takes **Demoralizing Shout** and Shift+Mouse 4 **Taunt**; Shift+Q stays free.
- Q is the macro from the [keybinds page](keybinds.md) on both stance bars: it starts your swings. This rotation presses **Heroic Strike** only below 20% of the mob's health, so before 40 press Q to start swinging and then only at the end of the fight. At 40 change the macro's two names to **Shield Slam**; plain **Heroic Strike** goes to C on the Defensive Stance bar. The [macros page](../macros.md) has a macro that puts the swap and **Charge** on one key, and one that adds **Shield Block** to Q.
- A mob that runs: E (**Battle Stance**), then 3 (**Hamstring**), which needs Battle Stance.
- No key: **Battle Shout** (3 minutes: click it between fights), food, bandages, the mount and the Hearthstone.
- The single-target rotation never casts **Thunder Clap**, **Demoralizing Shout** or **Cleave**. They are there for a pull that brings friends. **Taunt** is for a group.

## Levels 1-3: Heroic Strike, with a shield

**The trainer teaches:** **Battle Shout** (1), Battle Stance (1), **Heroic Strike** (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Heroic Strike.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Heroic Strike.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Keep **Battle Shout** up.
2. **Heroic Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health.

- You start in **Battle Stance**; **Defensive Stance** comes at 10.
- This rotation's tuned rule (2026-11-06) holds **Heroic Strike** until the mob is below 20% of its health, at every level: before that the rage is worth more on your other attacks. At 1-3 that means: let the rage build, then spend it at the end.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 13 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Melee 2.2, Heroic Strike 0.1, Battle Shout 0.1.*

## Levels 10-11: Defensive Stance: charge, then swap

**The trainer teaches:** **Bloodrage** (10), **Defensive Stance** (10), Sunder Armor (10), Taunt (10).

**New ranks to buy:** Rend at 10.

**Talents:** 10-11 Shield Specialization.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Defensive Stance; Mouse 4 = Bloodrage; Q (defensive) = Heroic Strike; E (defensive) = Battle Stance; R (defensive) = Rend; Shift+Mouse 4 = Taunt.

**Your keys in this stage:** E Charge, Mouse 5 Defensive Stance, Mouse 4 Bloodrage, Q (defensive) Heroic Strike, E (defensive) Battle Stance, R (defensive) Rend.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Battle Stance**, then **Bloodrage** while you are above 45% of your health, then **Charge** in.
2. **Rend** if you have the rage, then **Defensive Stance**. Until 14 a stance change loses all your rage, so spend it first.
3. Keep **Battle Shout** up.
4. **Rend** once, if the mob has none yet and has more than half its health.
5. **Heroic Strike** once the mob is below 20% of its health.

- Press **Bloodrage** after the swap to **Battle Stance**, not before: a stance change keeps none of your rage until 14, and 10 of it after. Its second 10 rage comes over 10 seconds, in **Defensive Stance** too.
- **Shield Specialization** (10-14): more blocks, and rage from each block. Nothing new to press. **Taunt** and **Sunder Armor** (10) are for groups.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 23 at level 10. Presses per kill at level 10: Melee 5.6, Rend 1.1, Battle Stance 1.0, Charge 1.0, Defensive Stance 1.0, Heroic Strike 1.0, Bloodrage 0.3, Battle Shout 0.1.*

## Between fights

- Bandage when you are below 60% of your health and eat below 65%: the fight lab's defaults, which the 2026-11-06 tuning of this rotation did not change. With the shield you rarely need either.
- Visit the trainer every even level. **Revenge**, **Rend** and **Heroic Strike** have many ranks.

## Other ways to level this class

- Arms with a two-hander and Fury with a weapon in each hand kill faster and have their own pages in this folder. Protection is for arriving at 60 as a tank, and for tanking dungeons on the way.
- **Vanguard**, a Protection talent this build does not take, lets **Charge** work in **Defensive Stance**: with it the swap goes away.
- Two or three mobs: open with **Demoralizing Shout** and **Thunder Clap**, and use **Cleave** for spare rage. On pulls of 3 the fight lab measured fewer deaths with **Demoralizing Shout**.
