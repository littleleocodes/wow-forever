## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Battle Stance**, a weapon in each hand.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Bloodrage** (above 45% health) and **Death Wish** when it is ready, then **Charge** in; open with **Victory Rush** when it is up and you are below 90% health.
2. **Battle Shout**: keep it up (it lasts 3 minutes).
3. **Execute** first once the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Overpower** whenever it lights up (after a dodge).
5. **Bloodthirst** whenever ready.
6. **Rend** once, while the mob has more than a third of its health.
7. **Heroic Strike** with spare rage: keep 15 for **Overpower**.
8. Two or more mobs: **Thunder Clap** and **Cleave** (**Whirlwind** needs **Berserker Stance**).

**Between fights:** Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40%.

## Why this rotation

Fury levels with a weapon in each hand in **Battle Stance**. Open with **Bloodrage** and **Charge** (and **Death Wish** when it is ready), hit **Bloodthirst** on cooldown, **Overpower** after a dodge and **Execute** below 20%, and spend the rest on **Heroic Strike**.

**Why it works**

- The opening matches Arms: **Bloodrage** (above 45% health) and **Charge** fill your rage before the first swing. The fight lab also presses **Death Wish** (level 30) on the pull whenever it is ready (3-minute cooldown): 20% more physical damage for 30 seconds covers a whole fight.
- Below 20%, **Execute** comes first: it spends all your rage, and **Improved Execute** (level 25, 1 point) cuts its cost by 3. **Overpower** comes next when the mob dodges (this build has no **Bloodthrill**): 5 rage, with 50% more crit chance once **Improved Overpower** arrives at 59-60.
- **Bloodthirst** (from 40: 30 rage, 6-second cooldown, 45% of your attack power) is your main attack. Before 40, **Heroic Strike** is where your rage goes.
- **Rend** goes on once per mob while it has more than a third of its health: the fight lab casts it about once a kill, and your build takes **Improved Rend**.
- **Heroic Strike** takes the spare rage once 15 are kept for an **Overpower**. Your rage comes from landed white swings of both hands and doubles on a crit, which is why the build leans on **Flurry** (faster swings after a crit), **Cruelty** and **Enrage**.
- **Battle Shout** lasts 3 minutes in Forever, so one shout covers several pulls, and **Victory Rush** (free, heals 10%) opens the next fight whenever you are below 90% health.

**Caveats**

- **Furious Precision** (levels 22-24: 10% more off-hand hit) and **Dual Wield Specialization** (26-29 and 31: 25% more off-hand damage and 50% more off-hand rage) are why this build wants a weapon in each hand; **Gore Drinker** (43) makes your next 3 hits after **Bloodrage**, **Death Wish** or **Bloodthirst** heal 1% of your health each.
- **Improved Tactical Mastery** (50-53 and 57) only keeps rage through a stance change, which this list never makes: its 5 points are the prerequisite for **Anger Management** (58: 1 rage every 3 seconds in combat) and fill the Arms tiers for **Deep Wounds** (54-56).
- Between fights the fight lab bandages below 60% health and eats below 40%.
- Several mobs: **Whirlwind** needs **Berserker Stance**. In **Battle Stance**, use **Cleave** instead of **Heroic Strike** and **Thunder Clap** to slow them (20% slower attacks).

**When to change it**

- Fighting in **Berserker Stance** instead (3% more crit, 10% more damage taken) gives you **Whirlwind**, **Berserker Rage** and **Pummel** but costs **Overpower**; the fight lab has not measured that trade.
- A mob that runs at low health: **Hamstring** it, or finish it with **Execute**.
