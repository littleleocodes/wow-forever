## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Battle Stance**, a weapon in each hand.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Bloodrage** (above 45% health) and **Death Wish** when it is ready, then **Charge** in; open with **Victory Rush** when it is up and you are below 90% health.
2. **Battle Shout**: keep it up (it lasts 3 minutes).
3. **Execute** first once the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Overpower** whenever it lights up (after a dodge).
5. **Reckless Swings** (if your build has it) whenever it is ready while you are above 80% health and the mob above 30%: your next 4 hits give 5 more rage each.
6. **Bloodthirst** whenever ready.
7. **Rend** once, while the mob has more than a third of its health.
8. **Heroic Strike** with spare rage: keep 15 for **Overpower**.
9. Two or more mobs: **Thunder Clap** and **Cleave** (**Whirlwind** needs **Berserker Stance**).

**Between fights:** Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40% (the tuned rule).

## Why this rotation

Fury levels with a weapon in each hand in **Battle Stance**. Open with **Bloodrage** and **Charge** (and **Death Wish** when it is ready), hit **Bloodthirst** on cooldown, **Overpower** after a dodge and **Execute** below 20%, and spend the rest on **Heroic Strike**, with **Reckless Swings** (43) for more rage; bandage below 60% health and eat only below 40%.

**Why it works**

- The opening matches Arms: **Bloodrage** (above 45% health) and **Charge** fill your rage before the first swing. The fight lab also presses **Death Wish** (level 30) on the pull whenever it is ready (3-minute cooldown): 20% more physical damage for 30 seconds covers a whole fight.
- Below 20%, **Execute** comes first: it spends all your rage, and it is about an eighth of your damage at 60. **Overpower** comes next when the mob dodges (this build has no **Bloodthrill**): 5 rage, with 25% more crit chance from **Improved Overpower** at 58 and 50% at 60.
- **Bloodthirst** (from 40: 30 rage, 6-second cooldown, 45% of your attack power) is your main attack, about a fifth of your damage. Before 40, **Heroic Strike** is where your rage goes (about a third of your damage at 20, a quarter at 30).
- **Rend** goes on once per mob while it has more than a third of its health: the fight lab casts it about once a kill, and the build takes **Improved Rend** at 44-46.
- **Heroic Strike** takes the spare rage once 15 are kept for an **Overpower**. Your rage comes from landed white swings of both hands and doubles on a crit, which is why the build opens with **Cruelty** (10-14), **Unbridled Wrath** (15-19) and **Enrage** (20-24) and takes **Flurry** (30% faster swings for 3 swings after a crit, at 35-37); in the fight lab **Cruelty**, **Gore Drinker**, **Dual Wield Specialization**, **Flurry** and **Enrage** are its most valuable talents at 60.
- **Reckless Swings** (43; 15-second cooldown) costs a quarter of your base health and gives 5 more rage on each of your next 4 swings within 8 seconds. The fight lab casts it in melee while you are above 80% of your health and the mob above 30% of its own, off the global cooldown (an assumption: the tooltip names none): about 0.7 times a kill at 50-60, worth about 1 second per kill at 60.
- **Battle Shout** lasts 3 minutes in Forever, so one shout covers several pulls, and **Victory Rush** (free, heals 10%) opens the next fight whenever you are below 90% health.

**Caveats**

- **Furious Precision** (levels 25 and 32-33: 10% more off-hand hit) and **Dual Wield Specialization** (26-29 and 31: 25% more off-hand damage and 50% more off-hand rage) are why this build wants a weapon in each hand; **Gore Drinker** (41-42, both ranks) makes your next 3 hits after **Bloodrage**, **Death Wish**, **Bloodthirst** or an **Enrage** heal 1% of your health each.
- From 44 the points go to Arms (**Improved Rend**, **Improved Heroic Strike**, **Improved Charge**, **Improved Tactical Mastery**, **Anger Management**, **Deep Wounds**, **Improved Overpower**, **Impale**): small gains each, none of them changes the list.
- Between fights the fight lab bandages below 60% health and eats below 40% (the rule tuned on 2026-11-15, under 1% faster per kill than the default 65%); with **Victory Rush** and the bandage it almost never needs food.
- Several mobs: **Whirlwind** needs **Berserker Stance**. In **Battle Stance**, use **Cleave** instead of **Heroic Strike** and **Thunder Clap** to slow them (20% slower attacks).

**When to change it**

- Fighting in **Berserker Stance** instead (3% more crit, 10% more damage taken) gives you **Whirlwind**, **Berserker Rage** and **Pummel** but costs **Overpower**; the fight lab has not measured that trade.
- A mob that runs at low health: **Hamstring** it, or finish it with **Execute**.
