## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Battle Stance**, a two-handed weapon.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Bloodrage** (above 45% health), then **Charge** in; open with **Victory Rush** when it is up and you are below 90% health.
2. **Battle Shout**: keep it up (it lasts 3 minutes).
3. **Execute** first once the mob is below 20% health.
4. **Overpower** whenever it lights up: after a dodge, or from **Bloodthrill** while **Rend** ticks.
5. **Mortal Strike** whenever ready.
6. **Rend** once, while the mob has more than a third of its health.
7. Spare rage: **Slam** (only with **Improved Slam**), then **Spearing Strike**, then **Heroic Strike**; keep 15 for **Overpower**, and 30 more when **Mortal Strike** is almost ready.
8. Two or more mobs: **Sweeping Strikes**, then **Thunder Clap** and **Cleave**.

**Between fights:** Bandage below 60% health, eat below 50%.

## Why this rotation

Arms levels with a two-hander in **Battle Stance**. Open with **Bloodrage** and **Charge**, keep **Rend** on the mob so **Bloodthrill** lights up **Overpower**, hit **Mortal Strike** on cooldown, spend spare rage on **Slam** and **Spearing Strike**, and finish with **Execute**; bandage between pulls and eat only below half health.

**Why it works**

- **Bloodrage** then **Charge** puts rage in your bar before the first swing: **Bloodrage** gives 10 at once and 10 more over 10 seconds, **Charge** 9 to 15 by rank plus 6 with **Improved Charge**, and the mob is stunned for a second. The fight lab uses **Bloodrage** only above 45% health, on about every third pull (1-minute cooldown).
- Once the mob is below 20%, **Execute** goes before everything else in the fight lab: it spends all your rage (600 damage plus 15 per extra point at rank 5) and usually ends the fight. It is about an eighth of your damage at 40-60.
- **Overpower** comes next whenever it lights up: 5 rage, 50% more crit chance with **Improved Overpower**, and a window of only 5 seconds. With **Bloodthrill** (20% with all 5 points, levels 30-34), main-hand hits on a mob with your **Rend** light it up too, so you press it more than once a kill; it is about a seventh of your damage at 60.
- **Mortal Strike** (from 40) on cooldown is your biggest attack, about a fifth of your damage at 40-60. **Rend** goes on once per mob, early: the fight lab casts it only while the mob has more than a third of its health and you have 40 rage or **Mortal Strike** is cooling down, so it never delays **Mortal Strike**.
- Rage left over goes to **Slam** first, but only with **Improved Slam** (levels 35-36): it cuts the cast to 1 second, takes 3 seconds off the cooldown and lets your swing keep running. Without the talent **Slam** stops and restarts your swing, which cost 3-6% per kill in the fight lab, so a build without **Improved Slam** skips it. **Spearing Strike** (level 25: 40% weapon damage, 15 rage, 20-second cooldown) comes next, then **Heroic Strike**.
- **Heroic Strike** only takes rage you will not need: the fight lab keeps 15 for an **Overpower** and, when **Mortal Strike** is 3 seconds or less from ready, 30 more for it. Before 40 it is your main rage dump (about two fifths of your damage at 20, a fifth at 30); after **Mortal Strike** it almost disappears.
- **Battle Shout** lasts 3 minutes in Forever, so one shout covers several pulls. **Victory Rush** is free for 20 seconds after a kill and heals 10% of your health: it opens your next fight whenever you are below 90%.

**Caveats**

- The fight lab's numbers are for one same-level mob at a time in quest greens, with an axe or polearm for **Weaponmaster** (+5% crit); it does not model runners, adds or stance swaps.
- **Sweeping Strikes** is in the build only because **Mortal Strike** requires it: on a single mob it does nothing. **Improved Tactical Mastery** and **Anger Management** fill the Arms tiers; this list never changes stance.
- Between fights the fight lab bandages below 60% health and eats below 50% (the rules re-tuned on 2026-10-19: about 3% faster per kill than eating at 65%). With **Victory Rush** and the bandage it almost never needs food.
- Casters stand back and cast at you. **Charge** reaches them at the pull; in **Battle Stance** you have no interrupt (**Pummel** needs **Berserker Stance**), so just close in and hit.

**When to change it**

- Two mobs: open with **Sweeping Strikes** (30 rage; your next 5 attacks also hit the second mob), slow both with **Thunder Clap** (20% slower attacks) and use **Cleave** instead of **Heroic Strike**. The single-mob numbers above do not cover this.
- **Spearing Strike** deals an extra 80% weapon damage to Giants, Dragonkin and mounted targets: against those, press it on cooldown ahead of **Slam**.
- A mob that runs at low health: **Hamstring** it before it gets away, or finish it with **Execute** (the fight lab does not model runners).
