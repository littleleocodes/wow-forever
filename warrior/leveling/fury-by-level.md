# Fury Warrior — leveling, level by level

[Warrior](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Fury](fury.md) > Level by level

What a Fury warrior presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. A warrior has no mana: every attack costs rage, and rage comes from hitting and being hit. Fury fights with a weapon in each hand: two weapons swing more often, and every landed swing gives rage. You stay in Battle Stance from 1 to 60, as Arms does; what Fury adds is Death Wish at 30 and Bloodthirst at 40. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 18/33/0 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/warrior/60/20323213002-050530355010110031--12)), the one on the [Fury leveling page](fury.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Heroic Strike** turns rage into a harder next swing. |
| 4-9 | **Charge** opens every fight with free rage and a stun. **Rend** is a bleed you apply once. |
| 10-11 | **Bloodrage** gives you rage before you **Charge**. Talent points start, all in Fury until 42. |
| 12-19 | **Overpower** lights up when the mob dodges: a cheap hit that cannot be dodged. Keep rage for it. |
| 20-23 | **Victory Rush** is a free strike after each kill. Open the next fight with it when you are hurt. |
| 24-29 | **Execute** finishes a mob below 20% health. From then on it comes first. |
| 30-39 | **Death Wish** (talent): 20% more physical damage for 30 seconds, every 3 minutes. Press it before the Charge whenever it is ready. |
| 40-60 | **Bloodthirst** (talent): your main attack from here to 60, whenever it is ready. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape or the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the wider or rarer version of the key under it. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the opener macro: the [warrior keybinds page](keybinds.md), laid out for Arms; the notes below say what Fury changes.

- These are the Arms keys, so the two specs share muscle memory. Three changes: **Bloodthirst** takes Q at 40 (change the macro's two names) and **Heroic Strike**, the spare-rage strike, moves to C, as **Mortal Strike** does for Arms; **Death Wish** (30) goes on Shift+Mouse 4, the Shift version of the **Bloodrage** key, because you press both before the Charge; Shift+Q and Shift+R stay free, since this build takes neither **Improved Slam** nor **Sweeping Strikes**.
- The pull from 30: Mouse 4 (**Bloodrage**), Shift+Mouse 4 (**Death Wish**, when it is ready), E (**Charge**). **Bloodrage** first, because **Death Wish** costs 10 rage. The [macros page](../macros.md) has a macro that puts the two cooldowns on one key.
- No key: **Battle Shout** (3 minutes: click it between fights), **Battle Stance** (you never leave it), food, bandages, the mount and the Hearthstone.
- Levels 1-3 fight with Q alone. Q is a macro on the [keybinds page](keybinds.md) that also starts your swings, so the middle mouse button stays free. Add a key when its spell arrives.
- The single-target rotation never casts **Thunder Clap** or **Cleave**. They are there for a pull that brings friends. **Whirlwind** needs **Berserker Stance** and has no key.

## Levels 1-3: Heroic Strike

**The trainer teaches:** **Battle Shout** (1), Battle Stance (1), **Heroic Strike** (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Heroic Strike.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Heroic Strike.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Keep **Battle Shout** up.
2. **Heroic Strike** whenever you have the rage for it.

- Stay in **Battle Stance**. The fight lab gives you a one-handed weapon in each hand from level 1. The Forever data here names no level for Dual Wield (in Classic Era the trainer taught it at 20): until you can wield two weapons, fight with a two-hander. The fight lab has not measured that stretch.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 13 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Melee 1.6, Melee (off-hand) 1.4, Heroic Strike 0.5, Battle Shout 0.1.*

## Levels 4-9: Charge and Rend

**The trainer teaches:** **Charge** (4), **Rend** (4), Thunder Clap (6), Hamstring (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Heroic Strike at 8.

**Keys to set:** E = Charge; R = Rend; Shift+E = Thunder Clap (at 6); 3 = Hamstring (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Heroic Strike, E Charge, R Rend.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Charge** to open every fight.
2. Keep **Battle Shout** up.
3. **Rend** once, while the mob has more than a third of its health.
4. **Heroic Strike** with the rage you have left.

- **Thunder Clap** (6) is for two or more mobs. **Hamstring** (8) stops a mob that runs away.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 15 at level 4, 18 at level 9. Presses per kill at level 4: Melee (off-hand) 2.9, Melee 2.3, Rend 1.0, Heroic Strike 0.9, Charge 0.6, Battle Shout 0.1.*

## Levels 10-11: Bloodrage, and talents begin

**The trainer teaches:** **Bloodrage** (10), Defensive Stance (10), Sunder Armor (10), Taunt (10).

**New ranks to buy:** Rend at 10.

**Talents:** 10-11 Cruelty.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Bloodrage.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Heroic Strike, E Charge, R Rend, Mouse 4 Bloodrage.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

2. Keep **Battle Shout** up.
3. **Rend** once, while the mob has more than a third of its health.

- **Defensive Stance**, **Taunt** and **Sunder Armor** (10) are for tanking. Fury levels in **Battle Stance**.
- **Cruelty** (10-14) adds critical strikes. A critical swing gives double rage, so it feeds every button.

**New ranks to buy:** Battle Shout at 22; Rend at 20.

## Between fights

- Bandage when you are below 60% of your health. Keep First Aid trained: it is a warrior's main way to recover.
- Eat when you are below 65% of your health, about two thirds. That is the fight lab's default, and the 2026-11-03 tuning of the Fury rotation kept it.
- Visit the trainer every even level. **Heroic Strike** and **Rend** have many ranks.

## Other ways to level this class

- Arms with a two-hander, and Protection for a warrior who wants to arrive at 60 as a tank, have their own pages in this folder.
- Several mobs: **Thunder Clap** and **Cleave** in **Battle Stance**. **Whirlwind** needs **Berserker Stance**; the fight lab has not measured Fury fighting there.
