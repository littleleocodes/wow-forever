**Signature spells:** 95% of the damage at level 60 in the fight lab comes from melee, Shield Slam, Revenge, Heroic Strike, Thunder Clap (the spec's rule: at least 70%; **Shield Slam** cast in every fight).

**Leveling as Protection or as Arms?** This build takes 26 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Arms](../leveling/arms.md) 27 s: Protection is 2% faster. Your first Protection point comes at level 10. Level this way if you want to tank dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Arms and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** A one-handed weapon and a shield, **Defensive Stance**.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Battle Stance**, **Charge** in, spend the rage **Tactical Mastery** cannot keep on **Shield Slam** or **Rend**, then back to **Defensive Stance** (with **Vanguard**, **Charge** straight from **Defensive Stance**).
2. **Shield Bash** a mob that is casting.
3. **Shield Block** whenever the mob is hitting you.
4. **Battle Shout**: keep it up (it lasts 3 minutes).
5. **Concussion Blow** when you drop below half health; **Victory Rush** after a kill when you are below 90%.
6. **Revenge** whenever it lights up (after a block, dodge or parry), then **Shield Slam** whenever ready.
7. **Rend** once, while the mob has more than half its health.
8. **Heroic Strike** with spare rage, keeping enough for **Shield Slam** and **Revenge**; two or more mobs: **Thunder Clap**, **Demoralizing Shout** and **Cleave**.

**Between fights:** You rarely need to eat or bandage: the shield soaks most of the damage.

## Why this rotation

Protection levels sword and board in **Defensive Stance**, with almost no time spent eating or bandaging. **Charge** in, keep **Shield Block** up while the mob hits you, and spend rage on **Revenge**, **Shield Slam** and **Heroic Strike**.

**Why it works**

- **Charge** needs **Battle Stance** until level 27: swap for it and come back. **Tactical Mastery** (trained at 14) keeps 10 rage through the swap, and the fight lab spends anything above that on **Shield Slam** or **Rend** before it switches. From 28, **Vanguard** lets you **Charge** straight from **Defensive Stance**, so there is no swap at all.
- **Shield Block** goes up whenever the mob is hitting you (10 rage, 5-second cooldown, off the global cooldown): 75% more block chance for 7 seconds or 2 blocks. Each block gives 5 rage with **Shield Specialization** and opens **Revenge**, so it pays for itself; the fight lab measured 2.0 seconds per kill lost at 60 without **Shield Specialization**.
- **Revenge** (2 rage with **Focused Rage**) whenever it lights up and **Shield Slam** (from 40) on cooldown are your damage. **Shield Slam** is the build's most valuable talent in the fight lab (5.5 seconds per kill at 60 without it), then **Improved Revenge** (2.5) and **Shield Specialization**; **Bastion** adds 10% to everything you deal while a shield is equipped.
- **Heroic Strike** takes the spare rage after the fight lab keeps enough for **Shield Slam** (when it is 2 seconds or less from ready) and for **Revenge**. **Rend** goes on once while the mob has more than half its health.
- From 41 the points go to Fury: **Cruelty** for crit, **Unbridled Wrath** for rage and **Enrage** (10% more physical damage after you are hit, which a tank is all the time).
- **Thunder Clap**, **Demoralizing Shout** and **Sunder Armor** stay off on a single mob: the fight lab measured **Thunder Clap** at +1.2 seconds and **Demoralizing Shout** at +1.0 second per kill with no fewer deaths (a 2026-09-30 test), and **Sunder Armor** never beat **Heroic Strike** for the rage.
- **Concussion Blow** (level 33: a 5-second stun, 45-second cooldown, no damage) is your emergency button below half health, and **Victory Rush** heals 10% at the start of the next fight. **Shield Bash** is for casters: it interrupts and locks that school for 6 seconds, and **Improved Shield Bash** adds a 3-second silence.

**Caveats**

- The comparison with Arms above shows what the shield costs in time per kill; your damage jumps at 40 with **Shield Slam**.
- **Improved Sunder Armor**, **Improved Thunder Clap**, **Improved Disarm** and **Improved Shield Wall** do nothing on a single mob in the fight lab: they fill tiers on the way to **Bastion** and **Shield Slam**.

**When to change it**

- Two or three mobs: open with **Demoralizing Shout** and **Thunder Clap** (up to 4 targets, 20% slower attacks) and use **Cleave** for spare rage. On pulls of 3, the fight lab measured fewer deaths with **Demoralizing Shout** (1.1 to 0.75 per 100 kills).
- In real trouble, **Shield Wall** (60% less damage for 12 seconds, 15-minute cooldown) buys time, and an early **Concussion Blow** keeps a hard hitter off you for 5 seconds.
