**Leveling as Protection or as Arms?** This build takes 37 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Arms](../leveling/arms.md) 28 s: Protection is 33% slower. Its talent order takes damage talents first and puts its first point into Protection at level 30, so it only becomes a real Protection build in the 40s and 50s. Level this way if you want to tank dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Arms and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** A one-handed weapon and a shield, **Defensive Stance**.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Battle Stance**, **Charge** in, then back to **Defensive Stance**.
2. **Battle Shout**: keep it up.
3. **Shield Block** whenever the mob is hitting you.
4. **Revenge** whenever it lights up (after a block, dodge or parry).
5. **Shield Slam** whenever ready.
6. **Rend** once.
7. **Shield Bash** a mob that casts spells.
8. **Heroic Strike** with spare rage; **Victory Rush** after a kill; **Concussion Blow** when you are hurt.

**Between fights:** You rarely need to eat or bandage: the shield soaks most of the damage.
