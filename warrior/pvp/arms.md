## How to play

PvP has no fixed rotation: it depends on who you fight. No simulator plays PvP, so this build rests on the talents' descriptions and the community's PvP builds.

**Why these talents:** Mortal Strike, Improved Hamstring, Weaponmaster and Improved Tactical Mastery for stance dancing, with Cruelty, Unbridled Wrath, Piercing Howl and the reworked Enrage from Fury. Improved Overpower is dropped to fit Weaponmaster; Spearing Strike (dismount) is a tempting one-point swap.
