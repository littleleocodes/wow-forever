## How to play

**Before the pull:** **Battle Shout**.

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Shield Wall**
2. **Revenge** after you block, dodge or parry
3. **Shield Slam**
4. **Thunder Clap**
5. **Heroic Strike** when you have 45+ rage to spare
6. **Rend**: keep it on the target
