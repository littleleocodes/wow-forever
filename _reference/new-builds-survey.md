Every spell the Forever beta spellbook (build 1.60.1.70753) marks new against Classic Era, what the community simulator (with this project's patches, `raidsim/patches/`; the ElliotWood/Forever fork for runs before 2026-10-18, the official wowsims/forever since: each report names its commit) and the fight lab measured it doing, and the builds searched around the new spells: each target has its own rotation (a rotation-only entry of `raidsim/specs.json`, tuned by `raidsim/apl_opt.py`, whose pool offers every new spell) and its talents found by `raidsim/talent_opt.py` (a local search over legal 51-point builds in the simulator, keeping every talent the rotation reads). Raid numbers are the last races: the boss-level training dummy with your own buffs and consumables, and the same build in a fully buffed raid. Models of the beta, not measurements.
