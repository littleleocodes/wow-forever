# New spells and new builds (generated)

> Generated by `_reference/data/render_new_builds.py` on 2026-09-29 from the beta spellbook, the raid races, the fight-lab reports and the survey notes (`_reference/data/new-builds.json`, 2026-09-28). Do not hand-edit.

Every spell the Forever beta spellbook (build 1.60.1.70009) marks new against Classic Era, what the community simulator (ElliotWood/Forever, with this project's Frostfire Bolt, `raidsim/patches/`) and the fight lab measured it doing, and the builds searched around the new spells: each target has its own rotation (a rotation-only entry of `raidsim/specs.json`, tuned by `raidsim/apl_opt.py`, whose pool offers every new spell) and its talents found by `raidsim/talent_opt.py` (a local search over legal 51-point builds in the simulator, keeping every talent the rotation reads). Raid numbers are the last races: the boss-level training dummy with your own buffs and consumables, and the same build in a fully buffed raid. Models of the beta, not measurements.
