> Generated by `_reference/data/raidsim/unused_abilities.py` on 2026-11-09 from the last race of every raid entry and a removal run of each talent (12,000 fights). Do not hand-edit.

A talent that grants an ability is only worth its point when the build uses it. *Automatic*: the simulator applies it without a rotation line (removing it costs DPS), so a player must cast it before the pull. *Prerequisite*: the price of the talent named. *Situational*: a defensive spell cast when needed. *Wasted*: none of these; `filler_audit.py` moves such points in our hand and derived builds (the scorer's `opt-*` builds are its optimizer's own).
