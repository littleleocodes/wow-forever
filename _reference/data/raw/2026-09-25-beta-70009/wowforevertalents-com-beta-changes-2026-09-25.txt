1.60.1.70009
In use
Read from 25 September 2026, published 24 September 2026
The first build that moved talents: two gone, two renamed, two swapped rows, and a round of retuning.
42 of the 90 tables changed. In the trait tables the talent count went from 469 to 467: Improved Holy Strike (Holy, first row) and Crusade (Retribution, 15 points) are no longer in the trees, both of them talents Forever had added rather than Classic ones. Two druid nodes carry new names, Mangle as Primal Bite and Primal Fury as Blood Frenzy, and every talent that named Mangle (Ferocity, Berserk) now names Primal Bite. In the shaman Elemental tree, Elemental Alacrity moved from 25 points to 10 and Elemental Fury from 10 to 25, and the arrows moved with them. Seventeen talents were retuned or reworded: Bloodthrill, Improved Slam, Holy Power, Light's Vigil, Sacred Arbiter, Two-Handed Weapon Specialization, Vengeance, Twist of Light, Strider Kick, Devouring Contagion, Lava Burst, Rage of the Farseer, Wake of Fire, Hot Streak, Ferocity, Primal Bite and Berserk. In the spellbooks, Wrath's damage rose on all eight ranks, Holy Strike's weapon damage rose on ranks 2 to 8 and its cooldown fell from 12 to 10 seconds, Slam's cooldown rose from 15 to 18 seconds, Righteous Fury's threat bonus fell from 90% to 60%, Lightning Bolt ranks 3 and 4 and Lava Burst rank 2 gained damage, and Dark Sacrifice and Life Tap now state their Mana in terms of Spirit. Because talents moved, every build link written before this build is read against the frozen 1.60.1.69977 layout and placed by talent name, so it opens as the build it meant; a link whose only points sat in Improved Holy Strike or Crusade now opens empty. Beyond talents and spellbooks: the achievement tables grew by about 7,000 rows (Achievement 233 to 433, Criteria 1,353 to 3,276, CriteriaTree 1,951 to 6,603), 189 of the 200 new achievements being zero-point counters such as total gold acquired or duels lost, with only twelve worth points (ten 5-point dungeon and raid boss trackers, two 10-point cooking and fishing ones); a whole prototype encounter left the tables (about 90 spells, an Adaptive gear system of eight named armour sets, a slime boss kit and a Contribute Reagent mechanic, with its dev-only map, Development Land); Deadmines gained an encounter row for Miner Johnson; five quest ids, 66 newly named quest items, four zone item sets and a Spawn of Grubthor companion arrived; the Nightclaw Druids faction went from an empty stub to a wired reputation track; the Gnome racial Eureka! now discounts Energy by 10% instead of 20%; and, with no note anywhere, 1,053 spell effects had their bonus coefficient zeroed and some 270 items had their sheathe type reclassified.
Every raw table hash-compared between the builds; the talent pipeline run against both and diffed node by node across all 469 talents; every spellbook rank compared text for text and cooldown for cooldown; 9,230 stored build codes re-read against the frozen layout, with the four that spend nothing all naming only Improved Holy Strike.
