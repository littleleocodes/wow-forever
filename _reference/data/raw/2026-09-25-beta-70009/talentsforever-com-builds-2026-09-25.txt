WoW Forever beta changes
Every WoW Forever beta build checked against the talent trees, racials and Legacy perks the day it lands: what is new, gone, moved or reworded, build by build, in plain words with every detail behind it.
Beta build 1.60.1.70009, checked 2026-09-24: Fifth beta build
Blizzard pushed 70009 on the afternoon of 24 Sep with development notes. The first build since 69876 that changes talents and spells: two talents gone, two Druid talents renamed, two Shaman talents swapped rows, and a run of number changes. Blizzard's notes also say Bastion and Focused Rage swap rows for Protection Warriors, but this build's files still have them where they were, so the site shows them as the files have them.
28 changes on the site.
Paladin: Improved Holy Strike. Gone from Holy.
Paladin: Crusade. Gone from Retribution.
Shaman: Elemental Alacrity. Moved from Elemental row 6 to Elemental row 3.
Shaman: Elemental Fury. Moved from Elemental row 3 to Elemental row 6.
Shaman: Call of Thunder. Now needs Elemental Alacrity maxed first.
Shaman: Elemental Alacrity. No longer needs Call of Thunder.
Shaman: Elemental Fury. Now needs Call of Thunder maxed first.
Druid: Blood Frenzy. Replaces Primal Fury in Feral Combat: Gives you a 50% chance to gain an additional 5 Rage any time you get a critical strike while in Bear Form or Dire Bear Form. In addition, your non-periodic critical strikes from Cat Form abilities that generate Combo Points have a 50% chance to add an additional Combo Point.
Druid: Primal Bite. Replaces Mangle in Feral Combat: Bite the target for 100% normal damage plus 26.
Beta build 1.60.1.69977, checked 2026-09-22: Fourth beta build
Blizzard pushed 69977 on the evening of 22 Sep. Compared file by file with 69913: 25 client files changed, and none of the 73 tables that hold talent, spell, racial or Legacy data is among them. Two settings tables did change: the game rules table gained eight rows (the files do not name them) and three region dates shifted.
No data changes.
Beta build 1.60.1.69913, checked 2026-09-18: Third beta build
Compared file by file with 69893: 407 client files changed, and none of the 73 tables that hold talent, spell, racial or Legacy data is among them. One game rules table (cfg_gamerules) did change.
No data changes.
Beta build 1.60.1.69893, checked 2026-09-17: Second beta build
Blizzard pushed 69893 on the evening of 16 Sep. Compared file by file with 69876: 18 client files changed and none of them hold talent, spell, racial or Legacy data.
No data changes.
Beta build 1.60.1.69876, checked 2026-09-17: First beta build
The first beta client. Everything the site showed before this came from BlizzCon demo footage; this is what the client's own files changed.
252 changes on the site.
Warrior: Vitality. Gone from Protection.
Warrior: Bastion. Moved from Protection row 6 to Protection row 5.
Warrior: Focused Rage. Moved along row 6 in Protection.
Paladin: Improved Holy Strike. Gone from Holy.
Paladin: Crusade. Gone from Retribution.
Rogue: Flawless Execution. Replaces Restless Blades in Combat: Reduces the Energy cost of your Eviscerate ability by 10.
Rogue: Aggression. No longer needs Hack and Slash.
Priest: Shadow. The Shadow Magic tab is called Shadow now.
Shaman: Elemental. The Elemental Combat tab is called Elemental now.
Shaman: Elemental Alacrity. Moved from Elemental Combat row 6 to Elemental row 3.
Shaman: Elemental Fury. Moved from Elemental Combat row 3 to Elemental row 6.
Shaman: Tidal Mastery. Moved from Restoration row 1 to Restoration row 4.
Shaman: Totemic Focus. Moved from Restoration row 4 to Restoration row 1.
Shaman: Call of Thunder. Now needs Elemental Alacrity maxed first.
Shaman: Elemental Alacrity. No longer needs Call of Thunder.
Shaman: Elemental Fury. Now needs Call of Thunder maxed first.
Warlock: Wrack. Replaces Drain Hope in Affliction: Tears the target apart from within, dealing 36 Shadow damage every 1 sec and increasing the damage they take from your other Shadow damage over time effects by 10%. Lasts 6 sec.
Warlock: Conflagrate. No longer needs Shadowburn.
Druid: Blood Frenzy. Replaces Primal Fury in Feral Combat: Gives you a 50% chance to gain an additional 5 Rage any time you get a critical strike while in Bear Form or Dire Bear Form. In addition, your non-periodic critical strikes from Cat Form abilities that generate Combo Points have a 50% chance to add an additional Combo Point.
Druid: Primal Bite. Replaces Mangle in Feral Combat: Bite the target for 100% normal damage plus 26.
Druid: Balance of Nature. Gone from Balance.
Legacy: Bartering. New perk in Professions: Reduces the gold price of items from all vendors by 5%.
Legacy: Performance Bonus. 5 ranks before, 3 now.
