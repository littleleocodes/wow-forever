# The 70425 re-run (started 2026-10-16)

Hand-kept. User, 2026-10-16: "I think we should maybe just start the rerun with the new build now" (the decision of the same morning had
been to wait for the end of the beta on 2026-10-22); of the speed-ups offered, the user chose **scope by what changed**: full searches where
client 70425 changed a class's talents for that mode, a confirm pass elsewhere (the certificates around the current winners; a full search
only when they find a lead). Not chosen: skipping the per-class page regen, a power-plan change, cloud sessions.

## What moved

- **Simulator** pinned at b7083fa (client 70425; 2ce8265, 70291, before). Upstream since our pin: crit rage and Bear Form threat (our two
  edits retired), pet crit from the hunter's ranged crit, Blood Craze off Bloodthirst, Reckoning charges, Retribution Aura and Thorns spell
  power, Mutilate and Cold Blood, Siphon Life crits, Malediction and Hellfire, Fire Nova and Elemental Devastation, Ice Lance +200% on frozen
  targets, Fire Blast and Heating Up, racials reworked, two item database updates (6,200 items get our armor fill).
- **Our patches**: pets also inherit the hunter's ranged hit and expertise (70425's UI); Champion of the Light 10% of Intellect a rank (the
  fork still reads 20%); Resourcefulness procs from ability crits only (`patches/README.md`).
- **Talent data** 70291 -> 70425 (talentsforever.com's export; thewowdb answered 503, so `refresh_forever_talents.py` used the export's
  positions for trees the old snapshot disagrees on, and every fetch now fails soft).

1. `deep-runs/sweep_70425.sh`: the five classes above (raid confirm, tanks, leveling certificates, race-neutral races, pages).
2. The four classes' fight-lab and healer-lab modules brought to 70425, then their open leveling searches, order repair, the priest's

3. When the fork implements the 70425 trees: re-pin, then the four classes' raid open searches and ML pipeline.

warrior and rogue raid confirm, the paladin re-check, ML builds, race-neutral races, rankings and pages.

### Checked against Blizzard's 16 Oct notes (2026-10-17)

The notes (us.forums.blizzard.com t/2360696 post 6) against our fight lab and healer lab: **Winter's Chill was wrong** (we read the
tooltip's 40% a stack from any Frost spell, never spent; the notes and the client rows: Frostbolt, Frost Blast and a Fingers of Frost

`fightlab/mage.py` before part 2 reaches the mage (Frost AoE at 60: 32.0 -> 33.7 s per kill; single target unchanged). Already right:
Missile Barrage (Arcane Missiles hits), Arcane Concentration (one roll a cast), Arcane Wrath, Elemental Mastery, Call of Flame,
Dark Inspiration, Improved Mind Flay, Divine Aegis (non-periodic crits, cap), Consecration (4.2% a tick from the spellbook), Furor
(reworded only). Not modelled, small: Auto Shot needs 0.5 s standing still (the hunter's step back delays one shot), Lingering Rage,
Cone of Cold's extra slow; the healer lab's Water Shield globe timer is 3.0 s (the fork: 3.5 s).
