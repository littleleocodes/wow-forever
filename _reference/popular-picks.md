Pick rate = share of the builds made on the site, among those that lead in the same tree as ours, that take the talent. Values are the scorer's in the build's own scenario (calibrated to the fight lab, the community raid simulator and the healer lab where they measure the talent). A screen, not a result: swaps are decided by races. Do not hand-edit.

Consensus we skip: picked by at least 70% of that group. Rarely taken: picked by at most 20%. *Value if added* is the score gained by adding the talent's full ranks on top of the build (points over 51), per point next to it; *weakest* is the cheapest talent the build could give up (removal value per point, gating fillers excluded), so a skipped talent whose per-point value beats it is worth a race.
