## Not simulated (per class)

- **mage**: runners, Ice Block, Mana Shield, Arcane Missiles (outside the arcane-leveling rotation), gear upgrades within a level bracket (adds and Blizzard only in the AoE variant, tables.MOBS_PER_PULL).
- **priest**: runners, adds, Mind Soothe, Fade, Holy Nova, Penance, Prayer of Mending, Power Infusion, gear upgrades within a level bracket; racial priest spells (Desperate Prayer is a Dwarf's: off on the race-neutral pages).
- **warlock**: runners, adds, soul shard economy (Drain Soul, Shadowburn shards), Curse of Exhaustion kiting, Sacrifice, Demonic Sacrifice, Succubus/Felhunter pets, gear upgrades within a level bracket.
- **hunter**: runners, adds (Volley only in the AoE variant), the ranged hunter's traps (the pet holds the mob away from them), kiting (Concussive Shot / Wing Clip and running), Scatter Shot, Disengage, the pet's movement speed (Bestial Swiftness), pet happiness and feeding, gear upgrades within a level bracket.
- **warrior**: runners, stance dancing (Whirlwind, Berserker Rage, Intercept and Pummel need Berserker Stance, which the Battle Stance playstyles never take), Hamstring kiting, Retaliation, weapon skill, Lingering Rage and Anger Management's slower rage loss out of combat (the engine decays rage between fights with no module hook), gear upgrades within a level bracket.
- **rogue**: runners, adds, the stealth approach time, Expose Armor, Rupture by default (Eviscerate is used; the RUPTURE_HP knob lets rotation_opt try it at 5 points), Garrote's attack power bonus (its tooltip gives no number), Gouge-and-bandage, Vanish, Sprint, weapon skill, maces (Hack and Slash's armor share), gear upgrades within a level bracket, Death Mark's storage rate (an assumption, DEATH_MARK_STORE) and its cap, whether Shank is on the global cooldown (1.0 s assumed, like every rogue ability) and whether a dodged or parried Shank still gives its energy (assumed not), Ghostly Strike's stealthless Ambush (needs the mob's back).
- **paladin**: runners, adds, Exorcism / Holy Wrath (Undead and Demons), Repentance, Retribution Aura (Devotion Aura is assumed up), Seal of Wisdom / Seal of Light twisting, Blessing of Kings, shields, gear upgrades within a level bracket.
- **shaman**: runners, adds, Strength of Earth / Stoneskin / Grace of Air totems (5-minute totems left behind between mobs), Frost Shock kiting, Totemic Call, Ghost Wolf travel, gear upgrades within a level bracket, where a Fire totem stands (Fire Nova and Magma Totem reach the mobs around you).
- **druid**: levels 1-9 (Wrath / Moonfire), Moonfire / Wrath pulls in bear levels, Faerie Fire, Swipe, Growl, Dash, Innervate, Barkskin, Hurricane, Lacerate's stacks, adds, gear upgrades within a level bracket.
