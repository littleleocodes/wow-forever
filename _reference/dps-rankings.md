# Raid DPS rankings by gear tier

> Generated by `_reference/data/raidsim/ml_builds.py` on 2026-10-03 from the machine-learning pipeline's records (`_reference/data/raidsim/ml/`). Do not hand-edit.

Every raid damage spec's DPS in two gear tiers, like WoWSims' Pre-BiS and BiS presets: **Pre-BiS** (dungeons, crafted, quest, world drops and reputation; no raid items) and **Phase 1 BiS** (adds Molten Core, Onyxia and the Forever launch items). Each spec gets its own set per tier (raidsim/gear_tiers.py: the simulator's stat weights for the spec, the best item per slot, set pieces and trinket pairs raced), and its talents are searched separately for each tier and setting (raidsim/ml_talents.py: a random forest trained on simulated builds guides a genetic search over every legal build of the spec; the finalists are raced on fresh seeds and the winner is checked against every one-move and prerequisite-chain neighbour). *Training dummy*: a boss-level dummy with only your own buffs (the project's ranking setting). *Full raid*: every raid buff and debuff. 3-minute fights; each number is the winner's average over 100,000 fights (standard error about ±0.1 DPS; differences under 2-3% are within the model's assumptions). These numbers use each spec's own best gear, so they differ a little from the spec pages' ranking, which races every build in the project's standard set per spec. Simulations of the community's model of the beta (ElliotWood/Forever 40be98e with this project's patches), not in-game measurements.
