## What talentsforever.com shows

The site publishes a **Popular builds** list per class (the fold under the talent trees on each class page), and it is readable: the page loads it from a static script, `https://talentsforever.com/popular.js` (`window.POPULAR`), no browser needed. Per class it holds the top 5 level-60 builds people made, shared, saved or opened on the site, ranked by a score of shares, saves and opens ("builds within a few points of each other count as one"), with the counts, the share of each tree as the lead, and per-talent pick rates. It is what people *planned* on a talent calculator during the level-20 beta, not what anyone played at 60, and it has no scenario tag: a raid, a leveling and a PvP plan all count the same. Paladin, shaman and druid count only builds made since the 70009 patch (2026-09-24); the other six count everything since 2026-09-13, earlier codes translated by talent name.

## How to read this

- Every popular build ran in every raid DPS entry of its class (Holy and Protection builds too); the table shows its best entry, so a Fire build is played by the Fire rotation. Our tuned rotations were searched on our builds and the simulator's presets, not on these: a popular build that needs a different priority is at a small disadvantage.
- Builds the site shows with fewer than 51 points (its row is a cluster of near-identical builds and the representative can be short) were raced as published; the split column gives their point count.
- Leveling orders for the popular builds are the scorer's (the same order search our optimizer builds get); people who planned these builds for level 60 may level differently. Most popular builds are raid or PvP plans, so a leveling gap is expected, not a flaw.
- "(= `id`)" marks a popular build identical to one of ours. Builds that differ only in talents the simulator does not model (e.g. the Survival hunters' Survival Tactics, Clever Traps, Survivalist's Discipline under the melee rotation) read the same DPS: a tie, not a result.
- Popularity is what visitors of a talent calculator planned during a level-20 beta. It says nothing about what performs.
