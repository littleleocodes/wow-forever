# Forever stats: the rules gear scoring needs

Hand-kept research of 2026-11-13. The user asked for it before an all-class gear add-on: "make sure all the stats are scored ... keep in mind caps", and then "Do full extensive research on everything you're missing". Each row gives its source and how sure it is. "Client" means Forever's own game files or UI text. "Simulator" means the official wowsims/forever code at the pin (ff9babba5).

| # | Rule | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ratings are the same at every level, 1 to 70.** Per 1%: hit 10, crit 14, haste 10, expertise 10, dodge 12, parry 15, block 5. Defense is 1 rating per skill point. Unlike the Burning Crusade, a rating point is worth as much at 20 as at 60. | Client CombatRatings game table, as shipped in the simulator: [assets/db_inputs/basestats/combatratings.txt](https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/master/assets/db_inputs/basestats/combatratings.txt) (read by tools/base_stats_parser.py). [foreverdb.net/stats](https://foreverdb.net/stats) says the same ("at every level", client 1.60.1.70938). | Client data |
| 2 | **Hit and crit are each one stat** for melee, ranged and spells. The table's three columns are equal. | Blizzard, [Deep Dive Panel Recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313): "Spell, melee, and ranged hit chance are combined, as are spell, melee, and ranged critical chance." | Blizzard |
| 3 | **Hit caps.** Raid bosses: 8% melee/ranged, 17% spells, 27% dual-wield white swings. Your own level: 5% melee/ranged, 4% spells. There is no 1% minimum miss: 17% is a boss's whole spell miss chance. Spell miss by level difference: 4/5/6/17% at +0/+1/+2/+3. Guides that say 16% and 9% are using Classic's numbers. Hit from talents and race counts toward the cap. | Client tooltips CR_*_HIT_CAP_TOOLTIP (archived in raw/2026-10-08-beta-70291/client-tables/GlobalStrings-hit-caps-and-pets.csv); the simulator's sim/core/target.go spellMissChance and spell_result.go ("Forever has no 1% minimum miss"). | Client text |
| 4 | **Three spell stats.** Spell Damage raises damage only. Spell Healing raises healing only. Spell Power raises both. A "+N Spell Power" item counts as N damage and N healing. | Client STAT_SPELLPOWER_TOOLTIP / STAT_SPELLHEALING_TOOLTIP: "Increased by Spell Damage and Spell Power" / "Increased by Spell Healing and Spell Power". Item data: 1,156 items carry equal damage and healing. | Client text + data |
| 5 | **Healing gear also adds damage,** about a third as much. The item already lists it as its own "+N Spell Damage" line, so count the lines as listed and don't add a third on top. Robes of Insight has 33 healing and 11 damage; Staff of Westfall 48 and 16; Rotmender's Garb 11 and 4. About 530 items have a ratio of 0.30-0.38. | Blizzard recap: "Bonus healing now also includes one third as much bonus damage". The simulator's item database (assets/database/db.json). | Blizzard + data |
| 6 | **Spell power per rank and downranking.** Each rank has its own coefficient; Forever's low ranks keep more than Classic's (Fireball rank 1: 43% against 12.3%). Ranks far below your level get less, by an amount Blizzard hasn't published. Wands get nothing from spell damage. | Client tooltip (STAT_SPELLPOWER_TOOLTIP's last line); Kaivax, Beta Development Notes of 2026-09-24 and 2026-10-01 (via [RankedBoost](https://rankedboost.com/world-of-warcraft/forever/stats/spell-power/)). | Client text + Blizzard |
| 7 | **Haste:** 10 rating = 1% faster casts and swings (rule 1). Whether haste shortens the **global cooldown** is open. The simulator assumes it does for spells, down to 1.0 s (sim/core/cast.go hastedGCD, GCDMin), but not for physical abilities (IgnoreHaste). The client suggests it doesn't: Nature's Grace's proc (spell 16886) has a cast-speed aura (65) *and* its own global-cooldown modifier (aura 108, SpellModOp 21), and Blizzard describes it as "grants spellcasting haste and reduces your global cooldown". That second effect would be pointless if haste already shortened the cooldown. | Simulator code; client SpellEffect (1.60.1.70888); Blizzard's druid deep dive. | **Open**: simulator and client disagree |
| 8 | **Weapon skill stays on items,** "less of it per item". Skill above the target's defense lowers miss, glancing and crit suppression, under Classic's rules. | Blizzard recap; simulator target.go (miss 5% + 0.1-0.2% per point of skill gap; glancing; crit suppression 0.2% per point). | Blizzard + simulator |
| 9 | **Expertise is on items** ("reduce the chance for attacks to be parried or dodged"). A boss dodges 6.5% and parries 14% from the front. | Blizzard recap; simulator target.go. | Blizzard + simulator |
| 10 | **Defense:** 0.04% less chance to be hit or crit per point, and 0.04% more dodge, parry and block. Against bosses, 440 at level 60 makes a tank crit-immune. | Simulator constants.go (MissDodgeParryBlockCritChancePerDefense); foreverdb.net, Warcraft Tavern. | Simulator + guides |
| 11 | **Crit from Agility and Intellect** varies by class and level and was retuned for seven of nine classes (hunter 0.0189 per Agility where Classic said 0.0301). | Client PlayerExpectedStat (fightlab/player_expected_stat.json); simulator tools/base_stats_parser.py. | Client data |
| 12 | **Spirit regen:** the client doesn't ship a formula, so both our tools use Classic's. The five-second rule exists: Blizzard says Reverence "allows Paladins to regenerate Mana from Spirit while casting". mp5 works while casting. | foreverdb.net ("can't be datamined"); Blizzard recap. | Assumption (Classic) |
| 13 | **Spell penetration** ("Spell Piercing") past the target's resistance is bonus damage: on average P / (P + K) of a spell's damage, K = 8 x the caster's level + 19 (the logs' fit over levels 20-51; 9L fits only 20-30), rolled in 10% bins; a DoT's ticks take a tenth, channels all of it; gear and talent piercing (Arcane Subtlety) count the same. Mobs of your level have no resistance, so while leveling all of it is bonus (2026-11-13; before, this row said it only offsets resistance, and the fight lab scored it and Arcane Subtlety at 0). | wowsims/forever issue #76 (138 Forever combat logs, 2026-10-04 to 11-12); sim/core/spell_resistances.go | Simulator; fight lab (tables.pierce_bonus) since 2026-11-13 |
| 14 | **Pawn 2.14.2 on Forever:** "+N Spell Power" is read but valued at 0. The Spell Power row isn't flagged for Forever in UIStrings.lua, PawnCorrectScaleErrors deletes the value, and every Forever weight list in ClassicHawsJon.lua has SpellPower=0. Hit, crit and haste rating points are multiplied by Classic's per-percent factor (8): roughly 8x too much against rule 1. No caps. | Pawn's own files (D:/World of Warcraft/_forever_/Interface/AddOns/Pawn). | Read from code, not yet seen in game |
