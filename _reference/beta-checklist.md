# Beta checklist (level cap 30)

> Generated by `_reference/data/build_beta_checklist.py` from the Forever talent snapshot (beta client build 1.60.1.70009), the scorer's manual effect files, the spec models and the respec plans. The beta opened 2026-09-17 with a level-20 cap that Blizzard raises to 30 later in the test (2026-09-17 to 2026-10-21); until then only rows 1-3 and the first 11 points are testable. Only what a level-30 character can verify: talent rows 1-5, spells learned by 30, the first 21 talent points. Capstones, raid crit/hit baselines and level-60 rotations stay open until the cap lifts.

Global checks: respec cost schedule in Forever (the planner assumes 1g then +5g, cap 50g); whether DoTs can crit (the Forever models assume yes); pet talent behaviour; whether Forever changed any ability learn level (section 5 of each class).
