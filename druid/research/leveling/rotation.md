## feral-cat-dps

Played by the page builds of [Feral Combat](../../leveling/feral-combat.md) ([`feral-combat-open-leveling`](builds/feral-combat-open-leveling.md)), [Restoration](../../leveling/restoration.md) ([`restoration-leveling`](builds/restoration-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level. Levels 10-19 in Bear Form: walk up, Enrage, Demoralizing Roar, Maul on every swing the rage allows, Bash when low or against a casting mob. From 20 in Cat Form: prowl up behind the mob and open with Ravage from 32 (Shred from 22, Claw before; Pounce comes at 36, after Ravage), Tiger's Fury (from 24), Rake once, Claw to five points (the mob faces you, so Shred is only for the opener), Ferocious Bite whenever it kills (from 32), Rip at five points on a mob above half health (before Ferocious Bite: Rip at 2 points on a mob above 40% of its health), Berserk on the pull; when low, shift out for a Regrowth (Nature's Swiftness + Healing Touch in an emergency) and shift back at 55% of base mana, Furor carrying the energy. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, Rejuvenation / Regrowth in caster form when hurt (only with the mana to shift back) then Cat Form again, water below 35% mana (the tuned rule drinks lower).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: Levels 10-19: **Bear Form**. From 20: **Cat Form**, then **Prowl** (stealth) up behind the mob.

1. Levels 10-19 (bear): **Enrage**, **Demoralizing Roar**, then **Maul** whenever you have the rage; **Bash** a mob that starts casting.
2. From 20 (cat), open from **Prowl** with **Ravage** (from 32; **Shred** from 22, **Claw** before that), then Tiger's Fury (from 24).
3. **Rake** once.
4. **Claw** to build combo points.
5. **Ferocious Bite** (from 32) whenever it will kill the mob, at any number of points.
6. **Rip** at 5 points on a mob above half its health. Before **Ferocious Bite** (levels 20-31): **Rip** at 2 points while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. **Berserk** on the pull whenever it is ready.
8. Under 30% health while the mob has more than a quarter of its health: leave **Cat Form**, cast **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again.

Between fights: Low on health: leave **Cat Form** and cast **Rejuvenation** and **Regrowth**, then shift back (only with the mana to shift back). Drink when your mana is below 15%: it only pays for shifts and heals.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-09, on top of the priority below): drink below 15% mana. Together +0.8% time per kill saved for `leveling-feral` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Report: [fightlab/feral-cat-dps.md](fightlab/feral-cat-dps.md).

## balance-dps

Played by the page build of [Balance](../../leveling/balance.md) ([`balance-open-leveling`](builds/balance-open-leveling.md)).

Balance: Moonfire and Insect Swarm up, Wrath while the mob walks in (Eclipse charges), Starfire on Eclipse charges or when the mob is rooted / stunned, Wrath otherwise (pushback in melee), Regrowth below 35% health with Nature's Swiftness when it is up; Entangling Roots when low and the mob is not rooted. A heal leaves Moonkin Form; the druid goes back in while the mob still has half its health (else after the fight). Out of mana it waits: druids cannot use wands.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Moonkin Form** once you have it.

1. **Moonfire** on a mob above a third of its health, **Insect Swarm** above 40%.
2. **Wrath** while the mob walks in and whenever nothing below applies: each one stores **Eclipse** charges.
3. **Starfire** while **Eclipse** has charges, or while the mob is rooted or stunned.
4. A nearly dead mob: finish it with **Moonfire** or **Wrath**.
5. Low on health: **Entangling Roots** on the mob, step out of melee, then **Regrowth** (it drops **Moonkin Form**; go back in while the mob still has half its health).

Between fights: Stay in **Moonkin Form** and eat when your health is below 80%. Drink when your mana is below 60%.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-09, on top of the priority below): drink below 60% mana; eat below 80% health. Together +4.5% time per kill saved for `opt-balance-dps-leveling-st` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
