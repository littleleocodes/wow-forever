## Summary

The full Restoration tree: Swiftmend burst, Gift of Nature (+10% healing), Improved Regrowth (+50% Regrowth crit), Nature's Swiftness, Improved Rejuvenation/Healing Touch, Tranquil Spirit and Reflection, with Subtlety for threat. Five leftover points go to Improved Wrath for questing. The first build to run at 60.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Swiftmend** — Consume a Rejuvenation/Regrowth for an instant heal — tank-saving burst.
2. **Gift of Nature** — +10% healing.
3. **Improved Regrowth** — +50% Regrowth crit chance.
4. **Nature's Swiftness** — Instant Healing Touch every 3 minutes.
5. **Improved Healing Touch** — -0.5s Healing Touch cast.
6. **Improved Rejuvenation** — +15% Rejuvenation.
7. **Tranquil Spirit** — -10% Healing Touch/Tranquility mana.
8. **Reflection** — +15% mana regen while casting.
9. **Nature's Focus** — 70% pushback resistance.
10. **Subtlety** — -20% healing threat.
11. **Improved Mark of the Wild** — +35% Mark/Gift of the Wild for the raid.
12. **Insect Swarm** — Prerequisite for Gift of Nature; a fine DoT while soloing.
