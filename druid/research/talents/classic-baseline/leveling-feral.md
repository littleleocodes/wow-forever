## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Ferocity** — Cheaper Claw from the moment you get Cat Form.
2. **Sharpened Claws** — +6% crit at 20.
3. **Heart of the Wild** — +20% Strength at 40.
4. **Leader of the Pack** — Crit aura at 41.
5. **Furor** — Powershifting from 46.
