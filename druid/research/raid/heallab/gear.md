Items from the community simulator's item database in the damage tiers' pools (`raidsim/gear_tiers.py`: pre-BiS = no raid drops; BiS = phase 1: dungeons, Molten Core, Onyxia, world, crafted, quest), best item per slot by the weights, healer enchants from `raidsim/fill_enchants.py`. Not valued: set bonuses, on-use and proc effects (most healing trinkets and relics count only their static stats), Stamina (the lab never damages the healer), haste. Do not hand-edit.

## Assumptions

- The lab's assumed gear (tables.HEALER_GEAR, druid): intellect 230, spirit 190, healing 370, mp5 12, crit_gear 0.02; raid buffs, Kings and talents on top (engine.Healer.setup_stats), as for every lab report.
- Perturbations: healing ±50, intellect ±25, spirit ±25, mp5 ±8, crit_gear ±0.02; weights are local slopes, refreshed once at the chosen set.
- Item stats are the database's level-60 scaling; generic crit rating counts for heals (Forever pays gear crit to every attack, sim/core/forever_rules.go); spell damage without healing power heals for nothing (the simulator's HealingPower stat).
- Weapon skills are Classic's (`PROFICIENCY`); items needing a profession or a faction are left out; rings and trinkets are two different items, not of one unique-equip category.
