## Forever headline changes (beta client build 1.60.1.70425, 2026-10-16)

Forever's druid has 52 talents (16 Balance, 20 Feral Combat, 16 Restoration): 18 are new, none is a Classic talent under a new name, and 13 Classic talents are gone.

- **Baseline now:** Omen of Clarity (a passive trained at 20; it procs from spells and attacks, and Wrath does not use it), Nature's Grasp (trained at 10, one charge, castable in forms), Faerie Fire in Cat, Bear and Dire Bear Form (free, 6 sec cooldown), Lacerate, Revive (an out-of-combat resurrection). Tiger's Fury is gone: Blizzard's notes of 2026-10-01 say it "has been removed" and the 70425 spellbook has no rank of it; the community simulator still reads it from the client as granted (15% more physical damage for 6 sec, 30 sec cooldown), so its numbers include it.
- **Removed talents:** Nature's Grasp, Improved Nature's Grasp, Natural Weapons, Improved Thorns, Omen of Clarity (Balance); Feral Aggression, Feline Swiftness, Improved Shred, Primal Fury, Faerie Fire (Feral) (Feral Combat); Improved Mark of the Wild, Improved Healing Touch, Improved Enrage (Restoration).
- **New talents:** Genesis, Nature's Majesty, Nature's Splendor, Overgrowth, Eclipse (Balance); Feral Swiftness, Shredding Attacks, Shifting Power, Improved Shifting Power, Primal Bite (bear only), Natural Instinct (named Predatory Instincts until 70291; it also adds a share of your Intellect to spell healing), Natural Reaction, Rend and Tear, Berserk (Feral Combat); Naturalist, Gift of the Earthmother, Living Spirit, Wild Growth (Restoration).
- **Moved or reworked:** Heart of the Wild to Feral tier 1 (Intellect, plus Strength in Cat Form and Stamina in Bear Form); Insect Swarm to Balance tier 4; Natural Shapeshifter to Restoration; Swiftmend from Classic's capstone to Restoration tier 4; Nature's Swiftness needs 5 points in Naturalist. Feral Swiftness and Shredding Attacks (18 energy off Shred, 3 rage off Lacerate) take the places of Feline Swiftness and Improved Shred; Blood Frenzy does what Classic's Blood Frenzy and Primal Fury did between them. Furor restores the Cat Form energy you left with (up to 100%) plus 10 a second out of form, instead of a chance at 40 (since 70425 also through Bear Form, which still stops energy regeneration); Nature's Grace is 10% faster casts and global cooldown for 3 sec after a critical hit; Nature's Focus covers every Arcane and Nature spell; Moonglow cheapens damaging spells only (25%); Moonkin Form doubles Omen of Clarity's chance and gives party or raid members within 45 yards 3% spell crit, and Leader of the Pack 3% melee and ranged crit: since client 70425 both are raid-wide and they stack (before, each gave all crit to the party and they were exclusive).
- **Spells:** Shred is 155% weapon damage (Classic 225%) and Claw 110% weapon damage plus a bonus; in forms your equipped weapon's DPS sets your damage at a 1.0 sec (cat) or 2.5 sec (bear) swing (Blizzard's druid deep dive, 2026-09-30), weapon enchants work in forms and you can use items and potions without shifting out; Barkskin no longer slows casts or attacks; Entangling Roots works indoors, on one target at a time; Mark of the Wild and Gift of the Wild give 385 armor, 16 to all attributes and 27 resistances for an hour; Enrage gives 10 rage at once; Frenzied Regeneration turns each point of rage into 1% health.
- **Beta notes (to 2026-10-08):** the Bear and Dire Bear Form threat bonus is 50% (from 30%, notes of 2026-10-08).
- **Beta notes (16 Oct, build 70425):** Moonkin Aura only spell crit and Leader of the Pack only melee and ranged crit, both raid-wide;
  Furor keeps your Cat Form energy through Bear Form; Faerie Fire generates threat in Bear and Dire Bear Form and no longer triggers Omen of
  Clarity in forms (it costs nothing there); Shred rank 1 at level 20 (was 22); several Insect Swarms no longer stack their hit reduction;
  a high-rank Rejuvenation no longer blocks another druid's.
