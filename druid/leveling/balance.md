## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Mark of the Wild** and **Thorns** up; **Moonkin Form** once you have it (40).

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Moonfire** on a mob above a third of its health.
2. **Wrath** while the mob walks in and whenever nothing below applies: each one stores **Eclipse** charges.
3. **Starfire** while **Eclipse** has charges, or while the mob is rooted or stunned.
4. A nearly dead mob: finish it with **Moonfire** or **Wrath**.
5. Low on health: **Entangling Roots** on the mob, step out of melee, then **Regrowth** (it drops **Moonkin Form**; go back in while you have half your health and the mob still has half of its own).

**Between fights:** In **Moonkin Form**, stay in it and eat when your health is below 40%. Before 40: **Rejuvenation** below 85% health, **Regrowth** below 70%. Drink when your mana is below about a third (35%).

## Why this rotation

Pull with an instant, then cast: **Moonfire** on the mob, **Wrath** while it runs in, then **Starfire** whenever **Eclipse** has shortened it or the mob cannot hit you, **Wrath** otherwise. Finish with an instant, and heal from range when you must.

**Why it works**

- **Moonfire** first: it is instant, so it ticks while the mob is still running at you. The fight lab puts it only on a mob above about a third of its health: a mob below that dies before the burn pays off. This build skips Insect Swarm (a Balance tier 4 talent) and Genesis, so **Moonfire** is the only damage over time you cast.
- **Wrath** is the default nuke and, until level 26, almost the only one: with **Improved Wrath** (levels 10-14) it is a 1.5-second cast for half the mana, and a short cast loses little to pushback once the mob is hitting you.
- **Eclipse** (levels 30-32) is the build's most valuable talent in the fight lab: each **Wrath** takes up to 0.5 seconds off your next 2 **Starfire** casts (0.17 s at 1 point, 0.33 at 2, 0.5 at 3; up to 4 charges, 15 seconds). So the fight lab weaves: **Wrath**, then **Starfire** while charges last, about two **Starfire** casts for each **Wrath**.
- **Starfire** is your hardest hit. It takes 3 seconds with **Improved Starfire** (2.5 on a full **Eclipse** charge), so it waits for a charge or a safe moment: when the mob is rooted or stunned (**Improved Starfire** stuns for 3 seconds 15% of the time). **Nature's Focus** (levels 48-50 and 59-60) lets your casts ignore pushback 42% of the time at 3 points and 70% at 5.
- **Moonglow** (levels 15, 17 and 19: damage spells cost 25% less), **Moonfury** (10% more Arcane and Nature damage at 5 points) and **Moonkin Form** (40) are the next most valuable talents in the fight lab.
- Keep **Thorns** and **Mark of the Wild** up between fights (no key).
- Low on health (below 35%): **Entangling Roots** holds the mob in place but it still hits you if you stand next to it, so step away, then cast **Regrowth** from range. A direct hit can break the roots.

**Caveats**

- **Moonkin Form** blocks healing spells: to cast **Regrowth** you must leave it, and going back costs 35% of your base mana. So stay in the form between fights and eat; leave it only for an emergency heal, and go back in while you have half your health and the mob still has half of its own (the fight lab plays it that way).
- Mana: **Moonglow** cuts your damage spells' cost by 25% and **Reflection** (levels 51, 57-58) keeps up to half your regeneration going while you cast. The tuned fight lab drinks below about a third of your mana (35%) and eats below 40% of your health (the tuned rule of 2026-11-06). Druids cannot use a wand, so an empty bar means waiting.
- Until **Moonkin Form** arrives at level 40, the fight lab heals between fights instead of eating: **Rejuvenation** below 85% health, **Regrowth** below 70%.

**When to change it**

- When the mob is nearly dead, finish it with an instant **Moonfire** or a quick **Wrath** rather than a **Starfire** it will not live through: the fight lab does exactly that.
