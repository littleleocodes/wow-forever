## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Mark of the Wild** and **Thorns** up. Levels 10-19: **Bear Form**. From 20: **Cat Form**, then **Prowl** (stealth) up behind the mob.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Levels 10-19 (bear): **Enrage**, **Demoralizing Roar**, then **Maul** whenever you have the rage; **Bash** a mob that starts casting.
2. From 20 (cat), open from **Prowl** with **Ravage** (from 32; **Claw** before that).
3. **Rake** whenever it is not on the mob and the mob has more than 30% of its health.
4. **Claw** to build combo points.
5. **Ferocious Bite** (from 32) whenever it will kill the mob, at any number of points.
6. **Rip** at 5 points on a mob above half its health. Before **Ferocious Bite** (levels 20-31): **Rip** at 2 points while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. **Berserk** on the pull whenever it is ready.
8. Under 30% health while the mob has more than a quarter of its health: leave **Cat Form**, cast **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again.

**Between fights:** Below 70% health: leave **Cat Form**, cast **Rejuvenation** (and **Regrowth** if still below 85%), then shift back; only with the mana to shift back. Drink when your mana is below 15%: it only pays for shifts and heals. Eat below two thirds of your health when there was no mana to heal.

## Why this rotation

Pull with instants, then cast: **Moonfire** and **Insect Swarm** on the mob, **Wrath** while it runs in, then **Starfire** whenever **Eclipse** has shortened it or the mob cannot hit you, **Wrath** otherwise. Finish with an instant, and heal from range when you must.

**Why it works**

- **Moonfire** and **Insect Swarm** (25) first: both are instant, so they tick while the mob is still running at you, and **Genesis** adds up to 5% to their ticks. The fight lab puts **Moonfire** only on a mob above 35% health and **Insect Swarm** above 40%: a mob below that dies before they pay off. **Moonfire** is about a fifth of your damage at 60, **Insect Swarm** about a tenth.
- **Wrath** is the default nuke and, until level 26, almost the only one (about 10 a kill at 20): with **Improved Wrath** (levels 10-14) it is a 1.5-second cast for half the mana, and a short cast loses little to pushback once the mob is hitting you.
- **Eclipse** (levels 30-32) is the build's most valuable talent in the fight lab (about 6 seconds per kill at 60 without it): each **Wrath** takes 0.5 seconds off your next 2 **Starfire** casts (up to 4 charges, 15 seconds). So the fight lab weaves: **Wrath**, then **Starfire** while charges last, about two **Starfire** casts for each **Wrath**.
- **Starfire** is more than half your damage at 60. It takes 3 seconds with **Improved Starfire** (2.5 on an **Eclipse** charge), so it waits for a charge or a safe moment: when the mob is rooted or stunned (**Improved Starfire** stuns for 3 seconds 15% of the time). From 46, **Nature's Focus** lets you ignore 70% of the pushback.
- **Moonglow** (levels 17-19: 25% cheaper damage spells), **Moonfury** and **Moonkin Form** (40) are the next most valuable talents in the fight lab (2.6 to 3.6 seconds per kill at 60 each).
- **Thorns** stays up all the time (a few percent of your damage, from mobs hitting you), with **Mark of the Wild**.
- Low on health (below 35%): **Entangling Roots** holds the mob in place but it still hits you if you stand next to it, so step away, then cast **Regrowth** from range. A direct hit can break the roots.

**Caveats**

- **Moonkin Form** blocks healing spells: to cast **Regrowth** you must leave it, and going back costs 35% of your base mana. So stay in the form between fights and eat; leave it only for an emergency heal, and go back in while you have half your health and the mob still has half of its own (the fight lab plays it that way).
- Mana: **Moonglow** cuts your damage spells' cost by 25% and **Reflection** (levels 56-58) keeps half your regeneration going while you cast. The tuned fight lab drinks below about a third of your mana and eats below half your health (eating at half measured about 0.6% faster per kill than at two thirds). Druids cannot use a wand, so an empty bar means waiting.
- Before **Moonkin Form** (levels 10-39) the fight lab heals between fights instead of eating: **Rejuvenation** below 85% health, **Regrowth** below 70%.

**When to change it**

- When the mob is nearly dead, finish it with an instant **Moonfire** or a quick **Wrath** rather than a **Starfire** it will not live through: the fight lab does exactly that.
