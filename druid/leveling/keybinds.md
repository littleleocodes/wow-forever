# Druid leveling keybinds (hand-written)

[Druid](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > Keybinds

A personal layout, written 2026-11-06 on the player's own two action bars, for a player of League of Legends and Deadlock with a mouse
that has two side buttons (WoW shows them as Button 4 and Button 5). Same scheme as the [mage](../../mage/leveling/keybinds.md)
and [paladin](../../paladin/leveling/keybinds.md) layouts: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the
escape, the two thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the forms and the
rarer tools. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; Mark of the Wild, Thorns, Omen of Clarity,
the tracking, food, water, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key and are clicked between fights. It is built on the
[Feral Combat leveling page](feral-combat.md): the cat, the druid's fastest leveling build in the fight lab.

**Which spell is on each key at your level:** the "Keybinds by level" tables of the
[Feral Combat level-by-level guide](feral-combat-by-level.md). This page is the layout at 60, the setup and the macros.

This file is hand-written: the regeneration that rewrites the rest of this folder leaves it alone. Edit it by hand.

## Set up first (Esc, Key Bindings and Interface)

- The two action bars: bar 1 is bound to Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 to the same keys
  with Shift, with Shift+X in the last slot. The tables below list the slots in that order.
- **Bar 1 is three bars in one.** When you shift, WoW swaps bar 1 to the form's own bar: one for Bear Form, one for Cat Form, and (as
  Classic did) one more while you prowl. Bar 2 never swaps. So bar 1 is set up three times, once per form, and the same key keeps its job
  in each: Q attacks, E controls, R pays off, F saves. Set the Prowl bar up as a copy of the Cat Form bar with Q and E changed.
- Movement: Strafe Left on A and Strafe Right on D, turning with the right mouse button held, as in Deadlock.
- Interface, Combat: Auto Self Cast on, Self Cast Key Alt, so the heals land on you with a mob targeted.
- A side bar without keybinds for the things you click: Mark of the Wild, Thorns, Omen of Clarity, Track Humanoids, food, water, the
  mount, the Hearthstone.

## Macros (Esc, Macros, New; pick the question-mark icon so the button shows the spell)

Your form's attack with the start attack line, on Q on the Bear Form bar from 10 and on the Cat Form bar from 20. They start your swings
as well, so no key needs the plain Attack button. The start line runs first, so a press still starts your swings when you are short
of rage or energy. At levels 1-9, right-click the mob to start swinging.

```
#showtooltip Maul
/startattack [harm,nodead]
/cast Maul
```

```
#showtooltip Claw
/startattack [harm,nodead]
/cast Claw
```

`/startattack` is a command of the Classic client, and Forever macro guides use it
([RankedBoost](https://rankedboost.com/world-of-warcraft/forever/macros/),
[Kami Labs](https://kami-labs.fr/en/wow-forever/macros-wow-forever-9-classes/)). It is not tested in Forever's client here; if it fails,
put plain Maul and Claw back on Q and right-click the mob to start swinging.

The emergency heal is three presses: Shift+Q to leave Cat Form, Mouse 5 for Regrowth on the bar you now see, Shift+Q for Cat Form again.
A macro on a free slot (Shift+Mouse 5) makes it two, if Forever's client takes `/cancelform` as Classic Era's does:

```
#showtooltip Regrowth
/cancelform
/cast Regrowth
```

Then Shift+Q to go back in. Keep the mana for the way back: Cat Form costs 55% of your base mana each time (10% less with the one point in
Natural Shapeshifter at 52), and the tuned rotation only shifts out when it has the mana for the Regrowth and the shift back.

## No key: click these between fights

Mark of the Wild, Thorns, Omen of Clarity (once, it stays on for 30 minutes), Track Humanoids, food, water, your mount (40 in Classic)
and the Hearthstone. Shred needs the mob's back and the tuned rotation never casts it; Starfire, Insect Swarm, Hurricane and Moonkin Form
are for the Balance build, which has its own page; Primal Bite and Shifting Power are the Feral tree's last row, which this build does
not reach. Tab and Space stay on WoW's defaults.
