# Feral Combat Druid — leveling, level by level

[Druid](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Feral Combat](feral-combat.md) > Level by level

What a Feral druid presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. The cat is the druid's fastest leveling build in the fight lab: Wrath and Moonfire to 9, Bear Form from 10 to 19, and from 20 a cat that prowls up behind the mob, claws, bleeds it and bites. The page build (11/29/11) is a Feral Combat build with eleven points in Balance for crit, hit and bleed damage and eleven in Restoration for Heart of the Wild and Naturalist. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 11/29/11 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/druid/60/15002201-51201302123002124-05501-12)), the one on the [Feral Combat leveling page](feral-combat.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Wrath** as the mob comes, melee once it is on you. |
| 4-9 | **Moonfire** once at the pull, **Wrath** while the mob walks in, melee after. |
| 10-13 | **Bear Form**: walk up, **Demoralizing Roar** once, **Maul** on every swing you have the rage for. |
| 14-19 | **Enrage** at the pull, **Bash** a mob that casts. |
| 20-23 | **Cat Form**: **Prowl** up behind the mob, **Claw** to 2 points, **Rip**. |
| 24-31 | **Rake** once, **Claw** to build, **Ferocious Bite** as soon as it kills. |
| 32-39 | **Ravage** from **Prowl**: the opener that takes a third of the mob. |
| 40-60 | Nothing new to press. **Dire Bear Form** arrives; the deep Feral talents (**Primal Bite**, **Shifting Power**) are not in this build. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. A druid's bar 1 is three bars in one: WoW shows the form's own bar while you are in Bear Form or Cat Form, and the Prowl bar while you prowl, so the tables below give bar 1 once per form. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; Mark of the Wild, Thorns, Omen of Clarity, the tracking, food, water, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class and in every form: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the forms and the rarer tools. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [druid keybinds page](keybinds.md).

## Levels 1-3: Wrath, then your weapon

**The trainer teaches:** Healing Touch (1), Mark of the Wild (1), **Wrath** (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Wrath; 1 = Healing Touch.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Wrath, 1 Healing Touch.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Wrath** as the mob comes at you, and again as long as your mana lasts.
2. Melee once it reaches you: an early Wrath is slow, and the mob's hits push the cast back.

**If the fight goes wrong:** **Healing Touch** below a third of your health. The mob hits you through the cast, so step back first when you can.

- The fight lab does not model levels 1-9 (it only swings the weapon there), so this stage and the next carry no numbers.
- **Mark of the Wild** (1) is a buff: keep it on, no key.

## Levels 4-9: Moonfire

**The trainer teaches:** **Moonfire** (4), Rejuvenation (4), Thorns (6), Entangling Roots (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Wrath at 6.

**Keys to set:** R = Moonfire; Mouse 4 = Rejuvenation; E = Entangling Roots (at 8).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Wrath, R Moonfire, Mouse 4 Rejuvenation, 1 Healing Touch.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Moonfire** once as the mob comes: the hit and the bleed.
2. **Wrath** while it walks in.
3. Melee once it reaches you. **Wrath** between hits only while you have the mana: at these levels your water is the limit.

**If the fight goes wrong:** **Rejuvenation** on yourself at the first hits, **Healing Touch** below a third of your health.

- **Thorns** (6) is a buff: keep it on, no key.
- **Entangling Roots** (8): root the mob, step back, cast. Damage can break it.
- Levels 1-9 are not in the fight lab: no numbers.

## Levels 10-13: Bear Form

**The trainer teaches:** **Bear Form** (10), **Demoralizing Roar** (10), Growl (10), **Maul** (10), Nature's Grasp (10), Enrage (12), Regrowth (12), Revive (12).

**Talents:** 10-13 Ferocity.

**Keys to set:** Q (bear) = Maul; R (bear) = Demoralizing Roar; Mouse 4 (bear) = Growl; Shift+E = Bear Form; Shift+F = Nature's Grasp; Mouse 5 = Regrowth (at 12); Mouse 5 (bear) = Enrage (at 12).

**Your keys in this stage:** Mouse 4 Rejuvenation, 1 Healing Touch, Q (bear) Maul, R (bear) Demoralizing Roar, Shift+E Bear Form.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Bear Form** before the pull and stay in it. Walk up to the mob: a bear has no stealth.
2. **Demoralizing Roar** once the mob is on you.
3. **Maul** whenever you have the rage: it replaces your next swing. White swings fill the rest.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 30% of your health while the mob has more than a quarter of its own: leave **Bear Form**, **Healing Touch** or **Rejuvenation**, then **Bear Form** again. At these levels the mob usually dies first.

- From 12, **Enrage** at the pull: 10 rage now and 20 more over 10 s, for 27% of your armor. The fight lab presses it on a mob above 40% of its health.
- **Regrowth** (12) is your fight heal from here: a big heal plus a heal over time.
- **Nature's Grasp** (10) can be cast in a form: the next mob that hits you is rooted. Walk away and heal.
- **Growl** (10) is a taunt: for groups.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 26 at level 10. Presses per kill at level 10: Melee 5.4, Maul 2.0, Demoralizing Roar 1.0.*

## Levels 14-19: Bash

**The trainer teaches:** **Bash** (14), Cure Poison (14), Aquatic Form (16), Swipe (16), Faerie Fire (18), Hibernate (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Maul at 18.

**Talents:** 14 Ferocity, 15-16 Feral Swiftness, 17-19 Thick Hide.

**Keys to set:** 3 = Cure Poison; E (bear) = Bash; 1 (bear) = Swipe (at 16); 2 = Hibernate (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Mouse 5 Regrowth, Q (bear) Maul, E (bear) Bash, R (bear) Demoralizing Roar, Mouse 5 (bear) Enrage, Shift+E Bear Form.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Enrage** at the pull while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
2. **Demoralizing Roar** once the mob is on you.
3. **Bash** a mob that starts casting, or whenever you drop below 40% of your health.
4. **Maul** whenever you have the rage. White swings fill the rest.

**If the fight goes wrong:** **Bash** buys you 2 seconds. Below 30% of your health: leave **Bear Form**, **Regrowth**, then **Bear Form** again.

- **Swipe** (16) hits three mobs for little: for pulls of two or three, never one.
- **Hibernate** (18) sleeps a second beast or dragonkin for up to 20 s: cast it from caster form before the pull.
- **Faerie Fire** (18) is not worth the shift out; the fight lab never casts it.
- **Cat Form** comes at 20 and changes everything.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 29 at level 14, 33 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 14: Melee 4.8, Maul 3.7, Demoralizing Roar 1.0, Enrage 0.5, Bash 0.1.*

## Levels 20-23: Cat Form

**The trainer teaches:** **Cat Form** (20), **Claw** (20), Omen of Clarity (20), **Prowl** (20), Rebirth (20), **Rip** (20), Shred (20), Starfire (20), Soothe Animal (22).

**Talents:** 20-21 Sharpened Claws, 22-23 Savage Fury.

**Keys to set:** Q (cat) = Claw; Mouse 4 (cat) = Rip; 1 (cat) = Prowl; Shift+Q = Cat Form.

**Your keys in this stage:** Mouse 5 Regrowth, Q (cat) Claw, Mouse 4 (cat) Rip, 1 (cat) Prowl, Shift+Q Cat Form.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Cat Form** before the pull. **Prowl**, and walk up behind the mob.
2. **Claw** to 2 combo points.
3. **Rip** at 2 points while the mob has more than 40% of its health: the bleed finishes it while you claw.
4. **Claw** until it is dead.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 30% of your health while the mob has more than a quarter of its own: leave **Cat Form**, **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again. Keep the mana for that: Cat Form costs 55% of your base mana each time.

- **Shred** (20) needs the mob's back. A mob facing you cannot be Shredded, and the tuned rules never cast it.
- **Omen of Clarity** (20) is a buff: keep it on, no key. It makes an ability free now and then.
- Energy, not mana, limits you now. **Furor** (24 in this order) gives you part of your energy back when you shift back in.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 20. Presses per kill at level 20: Melee 10.6, Claw 4.3, Rip 0.9.*

## Levels 24-31: Ferocious Bite and Rake

**The trainer teaches:** **Ferocious Bite** (24), **Rake** (24), Remove Curse (24), Abolish Poison (26), Dash (26), Challenging Roar (28), Cower (28), Tranquility (30), Travel Form (30).

**New ranks to buy:** Claw at 28.

**Talents:** 24 Furor, 25-27 Predatory Strikes, 28-29 Blood Frenzy, 30 Leader of the Pack, 31 Genesis.

**Keys to set:** 4 = Remove Curse; R (cat) = Ferocious Bite; Mouse 5 (cat) = Rake; 3 = Abolish Poison (at 26); F (cat) = Dash (at 26); 3 (bear) = Challenging Roar (at 28); 2 (cat) = Cower (at 28); Shift+R = Travel Form (at 30).

**Your keys in this stage:** Mouse 5 Regrowth, Q (cat) Claw, R (cat) Ferocious Bite, Mouse 5 (cat) Rake, 1 (cat) Prowl, Shift+Q Cat Form.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Prowl** up behind the mob; **Claw** is the first hit.
2. **Rake** once while the mob has more than 30% of its health: it bleeds for 9 s.
3. **Claw** to build combo points.
4. **Ferocious Bite** at any number of points as soon as it will kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 30% of your health while the mob has more than a quarter of its own: leave **Cat Form**, **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again.

- **Rip** stays for a mob above half its health at 5 points. One on one, Claw and Bite get there first: the fight lab casts it about once in ten fights from 52 and never here.
- **Dash** (26): 50% faster for 15 s in Cat Form, prowling or not. The escape, and the way to the next mob.
- **Travel Form** (30) is the road between mobs.
- **Cower** (28) drops your threat and **Challenging Roar** (28) is a bear taunt: groups only.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 24, 22 at level 30, 22 at level 31. Presses per kill at level 24: Melee 10.5, Claw 3.4, Rake 1.3, Ferocious Bite 0.5.*

## Levels 32-39: Ravage

**The trainer teaches:** Poised (32), **Ravage** (32), Track Humanoids (32), Frenzied Regeneration (36), Pounce (36).

**New ranks to buy:** Claw at 38; Ferocious Bite at 32; Rake at 34.

**Talents:** 32-35 Genesis, 36-37 Nature's Reach, 38-39 Nature's Majesty.

**Keys to set:** Q (prowling) = Ravage; F (bear) = Frenzied Regeneration (at 36); E (prowling) = Pounce (at 36).

**Your keys in this stage:** Mouse 5 Regrowth, Q (cat) Claw, R (cat) Ferocious Bite, Mouse 5 (cat) Rake, 1 (cat) Prowl, Q (prowling) Ravage, Shift+Q Cat Form.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Prowl** up behind the mob and open with **Ravage**: 435% weapon damage from stealth, the biggest hit you have.
2. **Rake** while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
3. **Claw** to build combo points.
4. **Ferocious Bite** as soon as it will kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 30% of your health while the mob has more than a quarter of its own: leave **Cat Form**, **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again.

- **Poised** (32): rested to full energy out of combat, your next ability costs 10 less. Wait for full energy before you prowl in, and Ravage costs 50 instead of 60.
- **Pounce** (36) is the other stealth opener: a 2 s stun and a bleed for 1 combo point. With full energy the fight lab always has the 60 for Ravage, which hits harder; Pounce is for a caster you want quiet for the first seconds.
- **Frenzied Regeneration** (36) turns rage into health in Bear Form.
- **Track Humanoids** (32) is a tracking: clicked, no key.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 32, 23 at level 39. Presses per kill at level 32: Melee 10.6, Claw 2.1, Rake 1.3, Ravage 1.0, Ferocious Bite 0.8.*

## Levels 40-60: The same play to 60

**The trainer teaches:** Dire Bear Form (40), Feline Grace (40), Hurricane (40), Innervate (40), Barkskin (44), Gift of the Wild (50).

**New ranks to buy:** Claw at 48, 58; Ferocious Bite at 40, 48, 56, 60; Prowl at 40, 60; Rake at 44, 54; Ravage at 42, 50, 58.

**Talents:** 40-41 Natural Instinct, 42-46 Heart of the Wild, 47-51 Naturalist, 52 Natural Shapeshifter, 53 Brutal Impact, 54 Feral Charge, 55-58 Rend and Tear, 59 Improved Wrath, 60 Nature's Splendor.

**Keys to set:** C = Innervate; Shift+E = Dire Bear Form; F = Barkskin (at 44); E (cat) = Feral Charge (at 54).

**Your keys in this stage:** Mouse 5 Regrowth, Q (cat) Claw, R (cat) Ferocious Bite, Mouse 5 (cat) Rake, 1 (cat) Prowl, Q (prowling) Ravage, Shift+Q Cat Form.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Prowl** up behind the mob and open with **Ravage**.
2. **Rake** while the mob has more than 30% of its health.
3. **Claw** to build combo points.
4. **Ferocious Bite** as soon as it will kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 30% of your health while the mob has more than a quarter of its own: leave **Cat Form**, **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again.

- **Dire Bear Form** (40) is Bear Form with more armor and attack power: for dungeons.
- **Primal Bite** and **Shifting Power** are the Feral tree's last row: this build stops at Rend and Tear and never has them.
- From 54, **Feral Charge** (the talent): at a caster that stands and casts at 8-25 yards, you leap behind it and interrupt it for 4 s. The fight lab meets few casters at range, so it rarely fires.
- **Barkskin** (44) needs caster form: 20% less physical damage for 15 s, before a hard pull.
- **Innervate** (40): mana for a group's healer, or for you after a run of heals.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 23 at level 40, 23 at level 50, 24 at level 60. Presses per kill at level 40: Melee 12.9, Claw 2.5, Rake 1.4, Ravage 1.0, Ferocious Bite 1.0.*

## Between fights

- Below 70% of your health after a fight: leave Cat Form, **Rejuvenation** (and **Regrowth** if you are still below 85%), then Cat Form again, only with the mana to shift back.
- Drink when your mana is below about a third (35%): mana only pays for shifts and heals. Eat below two thirds of your health when there was no mana to heal.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Claw**, **Rake**, **Rip**, **Ferocious Bite** and **Ravage** all have many ranks.
