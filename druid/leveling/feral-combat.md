## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Mark of the Wild** and **Thorns** up. Levels 10-19: **Bear Form**. From 20: **Cat Form**, then **Prowl** (stealth) up behind the mob.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Levels 10-19 (bear): **Enrage**, **Demoralizing Roar**, then **Maul** whenever you have the rage; **Bash** a mob that starts casting.
2. From 20 (cat), open from **Prowl** with **Ravage** (from 32; **Claw** before that).
3. **Rake** whenever it is not on the mob and the mob has more than 30% of its health.
4. **Claw** to build combo points.
5. **Ferocious Bite** (from 32) whenever it will kill the mob, at any number of points.
6. **Rip** at 5 points on a mob above half its health. Before **Ferocious Bite** (levels 20-31): **Rip** at 2 points while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. **Berserk** on the pull whenever it is ready.
8. Under 30% health while the mob has more than a quarter of its health: leave **Cat Form**, cast **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again.

**Between fights:** Below 70% health: leave **Cat Form**, cast **Rejuvenation** (and **Regrowth** if still below 85%), then shift back; only with the mana to shift back. Drink when your mana is below 15%: it only pays for shifts and heals. Eat below two thirds of your health when there was no mana to heal.

## Why this rotation

Level 10-19 as a bear, then as a cat: open every pull from **Prowl** behind the mob, bleed it with **Rake**, build combo points with **Claw** and finish with **Ferocious Bite** when it will kill. Heal in caster form between fights.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-19: no **Cat Form** yet, so **Bear Form**'s armor and health carry you. You start each fight with no rage: **Enrage** first (10 rage at once, 20 more over 10 seconds), then **Demoralizing Roar** to cut the mob's attack power, then **Maul** on every swing you can pay for.
- From 20, **Prowl** up behind the mob and open with your hardest stealth hit: **Ravage** from 32 (350% weapon damage plus a bonus, about 14% of all your damage at 60), **Claw** before that. **Pounce** only arrives at 36, after **Ravage**, so the fight lab never opens with it.
- The 22-31 opener from behind used to be Shred. The re-tuned fight lab (2026-10-19) never casts it: opening with **Claw** instead measured about 0.3-0.4% faster per kill. Shred costs 60 energy against **Claw**'s 40 with **Ferocity**, and once the mob turns to face you it cannot be used anyway.
- Tiger's Fury was removed (Blizzard's notes of 1 Oct; the 70425 spellbook lacks it); the fight lab no longer casts it since 2026-10-17.
- **Rake**: a cheap bleed (35 energy with **Ferocity**) that ticks while you claw, and while the mob bleeds **Rend and Tear** (levels 35-39) adds 10% to your attacks. The fight lab puts it back whenever it has run out and the mob has more than 30% of its health, about twice a kill.
- **Claw** builds your combo points because the mob faces you. With **Ferocity** it costs 40 energy; **Sharpened Claws** and **Blood Frenzy** (an extra point on a critical **Claw**) are among the most valuable talents at 60.
- **Ferocious Bite** (from 32) ends most fights: the fight lab bites whenever the bite would kill, at any number of points, about once a kill. **Rip** only goes on a mob with more than half its health at 5 points, under one kill in two at 60: on a mob that dies sooner, most of its 12 seconds are wasted.

**Caveats**

- Before 32 there is no **Ferocious Bite**, so **Rip** is your only finisher: put it on at 2 or more points while the mob has more than 40% of its health. The fight lab measured that 3% faster per kill at 30 than waiting for 5 points on a mob above half health, which almost never came.
- Leaving **Cat Form** costs you: going back costs 55% of your base mana (less with **Natural Shapeshifter**), and without **Furor** your energy starts from zero. Heal between fights rather than in them when you can.
- Your mana only pays for shifts and heals, so drink late: the tuned fight lab drinks below 15% mana, about 0.5-0.7% faster per kill than drinking at about a third. It heals with **Rejuvenation** and **Regrowth** below 70% health between fights and eats only when there was no mana for that.
- **Berserk**, once you have it (level 40 in this order), goes on the pull whenever it is ready: 15 seconds of sure critical strikes on your combo point attacks, every 3 minutes.

**When to change it**

- Under 30% health with the mob still above a quarter of its health: shift out, cast **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again. If the mob is lower than that, finish it instead.
- In the bear levels, **Bash** a mob that starts casting, or when you are low: the fight lab does both.
