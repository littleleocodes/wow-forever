# Balance Druid — leveling, level by level

[Druid](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Balance](balance.md) > Level by level

What a Balance druid presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Balance stays a caster the whole way: **Wrath** from level 1, **Moonfire** at the pull from 4, **Starfire** as your nuke from 20, woven with **Eclipse** charges from 33 and **Moonkin Form** from 40; you eat between fights, and **Regrowth** (12) is for a pull that goes wrong. The page build (36/0/15) puts 36 points in Balance and 15 in Restoration for Intellect, pushback, mana and damage; it skips **Insect Swarm**. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 36/0/15 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/druid/60/5332200105501351--51531-12)), the one on the [Balance leveling page](balance.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Wrath** is your only attack. |
| 4-11 | **Moonfire** at the pull, **Wrath** after it. |
| 12-19 | **Regrowth** is your emergency heal: the tuned rules eat between fights and heal in no fight. Hurt in a fight: root the mob and step out of its reach. |
| 20-30 | **Starfire** (20) becomes your nuke: the fight lab casts it on every mob, and **Wrath** finishes a mob a **Starfire** would overkill. |
| 31-32 | **Eclipse** (31-32, talent): its first two points change nothing you press. The list keeps **Starfire** as your nuke until all three are in (33): one or two take too little off a charged **Starfire** to pay for the **Wrath** casts (the fight lab, 2026-11-23: 2.7-2.9% faster per kill at 31 without the weave). |
| 33-39 | **Eclipse** 3/3: each **Wrath** speeds up your next two **Starfire** casts by half a second. About two **Starfire** to each **Wrath**. |
| 40-45 | **Moonkin Form**: fight in it, and eat instead of healing between fights. |
| 46-60 | Nothing new to press: your last points go to Restoration for mana and damage, and to **Vengeance**. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Balance fights in caster form and, from 40, in Moonkin Form, both on the same bar 1. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; Mark of the Wild, Thorns, Omen of Clarity, food, water, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the forms and the rarer tools. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [druid keybinds page](keybinds.md), its Balance section says what changes for you.

## Levels 1-3: Wrath

**The trainer teaches:** Healing Touch (1), Mark of the Wild (1), **Wrath** (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Wrath; 1 = Healing Touch; Shift+C = Healing potion macro; Shift+X = Mana potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Wrath, 1 Healing Touch.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Wrath** until the mob is dead.
2. Keep casting when it reaches you: its hits push each cast back a little.

**If the fight goes wrong:** **Healing Touch** below a third of your health. The mob hits you through the cast, so step back first when you can.

- **Mark of the Wild** (1) is a buff: keep it on, no key.
- Druids cannot use a wand, so mana is your limit: drink after a fight that left you below a quarter.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 14 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Wrath 3.6.*

## Levels 4-11: Moonfire

**The trainer teaches:** **Moonfire** (4), Rejuvenation (4), Thorns (6), Entangling Roots (8), Bear Form (10), Demoralizing Roar (10), Growl (10), Maul (10), Nature's Grasp (10).

**New ranks to buy:** Healing Touch at 8; Moonfire at 10; Rejuvenation at 10; Wrath at 6.

**Talents:** 10-11 Improved Wrath.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Rejuvenation; Middle mouse = Moonfire; E = Entangling Roots (at 8); Shift+F = Nature's Grasp (at 10).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Wrath, 1 Healing Touch, Middle mouse Moonfire.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Moonfire** as the mob comes, and again when it runs out, while the mob has more than a third of its health: the hit, then the burn.
2. **Wrath** while it walks in, and after it reaches you.
3. A nearly dead mob: **Moonfire** when its hit will kill it, else **Wrath**.

**If the fight goes wrong:** **Healing Touch** below a third of your health. At these levels the mob usually dies first.

- **Rejuvenation** (4) is for a pull that goes wrong: the tuned rules cast no heal between fights (eating while you drink measured 2.1-2.3% faster per kill).
- **Thorns** (6) is a buff: keep it on, no key. Every mob that hits you takes its damage.
- **Entangling Roots** (8) is for the emergency of the next stage: root the mob, step out of its reach, heal. A direct hit can break it.
- From 10, **Improved Wrath**, one point a level: by 14 **Wrath** is a 1.5-second cast for half the mana.
- **Nature's Grasp** (10) roots the next mob that hits you: your way out. **Bear Form** (10) is for the Feral build; this one stays a caster.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 4, 26 at level 10, 28 at level 11. Presses per kill at level 4: Wrath 5.5, Moonfire 1.1.*

**Talents:** 20-21 Nature's Majesty, 22 Nature's Splendor, 23-24 Genesis, 25-29 Improved Starfire, 30 Nature's Grace.

**Keys to set:** R = Starfire; 4 = Remove Curse (at 24); 3 = Abolish Poison (at 26); Shift+R = Travel Form (at 30).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Wrath, E Entangling Roots, R Starfire, Middle mouse Moonfire.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Moonfire** as the mob comes, and again when it runs out, while the mob has more than a third of its health: the hit, then the burn.
2. **Starfire** while the mob has more than about half a **Starfire** hit of health left, from the pull on.
3. A nearly dead mob: **Moonfire** when its hit will kill it, else **Wrath**.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below 35% of your health while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Entangling Roots** on it and step out of its reach: a rooted mob still hits you if you stand next to it. While the roots hold, a **Starfire**. A mob with less left: finish it instead.

- **Starfire** (20) is your biggest hit, a 3.5-second cast (3 seconds with all five points of **Improved Starfire**, 29) that the mob's hits push back. From 20 it does three quarters of your damage in the fight lab, **Wrath** almost none (2026-11-23).
- **Improved Starfire** (25-29): **Starfire** casts 0.5 s faster and stuns the mob for 3 s 15% of the time.
- **Nature's Majesty** (20-21): 4% more critical strike chance. **Nature's Splendor** (22): **Moonfire** burns 3 s longer.
- **Nature's Grace** (30): a spell critical hit makes your casts and your global cooldown 10% faster for 3 s.
- **Genesis** (23-24): **Moonfire**'s burn and your heals over time do 2% more a point. Small; its two points open the rows above.
- **Omen of Clarity** (20) is a buff: keep it on, no key. **Cat Form** (20) is for the Feral build.
- **Travel Form** (30) is the road between mobs.
- **Insect Swarm** is a talent this build skips.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 30 at level 20, 34 at level 30. Presses per kill at level 20: Starfire 3.2, Moonfire 1.1, Wrath 0.5.*

## Between fights

- Eat below 40% of your health and drink below a quarter of your mana, both at once: no heals (the re-tune of 2026-11-21 measured eating 2.1-2.3% faster per kill than **Rejuvenation** and **Regrowth** before 40). From 40, stay in **Moonkin Form** to eat: leaving it costs 35% of your base mana to go back in.
- Drink when your mana is below a quarter (25%). Eat below 40% of your health when there was no mana to heal.
- Whenever you sit down to drink, eat as well if your health is not full, and drink while you eat if your mana is not full: the other bar fills in the same seconds at no extra time. Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. It was 7.1% faster per kill for this build in the fight lab (2026-11-16), and the tuned rules play it.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Wrath**, **Moonfire** and **Starfire** have many ranks.
