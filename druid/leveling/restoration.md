**Leveling as Restoration or as Feral Combat?** This build takes 28 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Feral Combat](../leveling/feral-combat.md) 23 s: Restoration is 22% slower. Your first Restoration point comes at level 19. Level this way if you want to heal dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Feral Combat and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Mark of the Wild** and **Thorns** up. Levels 10-19: **Bear Form**. From 20: **Cat Form**, then **Prowl** (stealth) up behind the mob.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Levels 10-19 (bear): **Enrage**, **Demoralizing Roar**, then **Maul** whenever you have the rage; **Bash** a mob that starts casting.
2. From 20 (cat), open from **Prowl** with **Ravage** (from 32; **Claw** before that).
3. **Rake** whenever it is not on the mob and the mob has more than 30% of its health.
4. **Claw** to build combo points.
5. **Ferocious Bite** (from 24) whenever it will kill the mob, at any number of points.
6. **Rip** at 5 points on a mob above half its health. Before **Ferocious Bite** (levels 20-23): **Rip** at 2 points while the mob has more than 40% of its health.
7. Under 30% health while the mob has more than a quarter of its health: leave **Cat Form**, cast **Regrowth**, then **Cat Form** again.

**Between fights:** Below 70% health: leave **Cat Form**, cast **Rejuvenation** (and **Regrowth** if still below 85%), then shift back; only with the mana to shift back. Drink when your mana is below about a third (35%): it only pays for shifts and heals. Eat below two thirds of your health when there was no mana to heal.

## Why this rotation

You level as a cat with your healer's talents: the Feral Combat steps, slower per kill than the Feral Combat build (the comparison above). Your points make your heals and shifts cheaper and stronger more than your claws, and the cat has no **Ferocity** or **Furor**.

**Why it works**

- Levels 10-19 are the bear steps (**Enrage** from 12, **Demoralizing Roar**, **Maul**). Your first points go to Balance and do little for a bear: **Improved Wrath** (10) is a gate point, **Genesis** (11-14, 29) adds 2% a point to periodic damage and healing (10% at 5), **Improved Moonfire** (15-16) another gate point, **Nature's Majesty** (17-18) +4% critical strike chance. **Genesis** later strengthens **Rake** and **Rip** and your heals over time.
- **Heart of the Wild** (19-23, 5 points: +10% Strength in **Cat Form**, +10% Intellect, +20% Stamina in **Bear Form**) and **Naturalist** (24-28, +10% to all your damage at 5 points) are the points that pay in the cat.
- From 20 the cat steps follow the Feral Combat page: open from **Prowl** (**Ravage** from 32, **Claw** before), then **Rake**, **Claw** to 5 points, **Ferocious Bite** when it will kill and **Rip** on a mob above half health (before 24, **Rip** at 2 points while the mob has more than 40% of its health). Without **Ferocity**, **Claw** and **Rake** cost their full energy.
- This build takes no **Furor** and no **Natural Shapeshifter**: each shift back into **Cat Form** starts at no energy and costs the full 55% of your base mana.
- The rest is the raid healer: **Nature's Splendor** (30) stretches **Rejuvenation** and **Regrowth**, **Nature's Focus** (31-33) and **Reflection** (34-35, 45) keep your casts and mana going, **Swiftmend** comes at 36, **Tranquil Spirit**, **Living Spirit**, **Gift of Nature**, **Improved Rejuvenation** and **Improved Regrowth** fill 37-59, and **Bloom** is the 60th point.

**Caveats**

- Each shift into **Cat Form** costs 55% of your base mana: heal between fights rather than during them when you can.
- The fight lab does not cast **Swiftmend** while leveling: it heals with **Rejuvenation** and **Regrowth** between fights (below 70% health) and with **Regrowth** in an emergency (with **Nature's Swiftness** and **Healing Touch**, once you have the talent at 54).
- Drink late: mana only pays for shifts and heals, and the tuned cat rotation keeps the default, below about a third of your mana.

**When to change it**

- Under 30% health with the mob still above a quarter of its health: shift out and cast **Regrowth**, then back into **Cat Form**.
- In a dungeon group, heal instead: that is the reason to level with these talents, and the times on this page are solo kills.
