## Summary

One Moonkin per raid for the +3% spell crit aura: Moonkin Form, Moonfury, Nature's Grace, Vengeance, Improved Starfire/Wrath/Moonfire and Moonglow for mana, with Improved Mark of the Wild, Reflection and Insect Swarm from Restoration. Mana-limited in Classic; damage is below mages and warlocks.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Moonkin Form** — +3% spell crit to the group, +360% armor.
2. **Moonfury** — +10% Starfire/Moonfire/Wrath damage.
3. **Vengeance** — +100% crit damage on Starfire/Moonfire/Wrath.
4. **Nature's Grace** — -0.5s cast after a crit.
5. **Improved Starfire** — -0.5s Starfire cast.
6. **Moonglow** — -9% mana on Moonfire/Starfire/Wrath — mana is the limiting factor.
7. **Improved Moonfire** — +10% Moonfire damage/crit; prerequisite for Vengeance.
8. **Reflection** — Mana regen while casting.
9. **Insect Swarm** — DoT and -2% hit debuff on the boss.
10. **Improved Wrath** — -0.5s Wrath cast.

## Notes

- Starfire spam with Moonfire/Insect Swarm if debuff slots allow; Innervate yourself or the priest.
- Bring one for the crit aura; a second Moonkin is rarely worth a raid slot in Classic Era.
