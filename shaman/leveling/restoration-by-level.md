# Restoration Shaman — leveling, level by level

[Shaman](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Restoration](restoration.md) > Level by level

What a Restoration shaman presses from level 1 to 60 while leveling into the raid healer's talents. Restoration has no leveling rotation of its own, so it plays the Enhancement list without the talents it never takes (**Stormstrike**, **Maelstrom Weapon**, **Rage of the Farseer**): a slow two-handed weapon, a weapon imbue, a shield, **Searing Totem** at the pull and **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready. It is slower per kill than Enhancement: the price of arriving at 60 ready to heal. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 0/10/41 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/shaman/60/-055-0530503315513151-12)), the one on the [Restoration leveling page](restoration.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | Your weapon and **Rockbiter Weapon** do the work. |
| 4-7 | **Earth Shock**, an instant on a short cooldown. From here on, skip the opening Lightning Bolt. |
| 8-9 | **Lightning Shield** on yourself: mobs that hit you take damage. **Stoneclaw Totem** is your first panic button. |
| 10-19 | **Searing Totem** at the pull adds free damage for the whole fight. Talent points start. |
| 20-27 | Nothing new in the fight. **Lesser Healing Wave** is a fast heal, **Ghost Wolf** gets you between mobs sooner. |
| 28-29 | **Water Shield** (talent): a shield that gives you mana back when you are hit. It takes Lightning Shield's place when your mana is low. |
| 30-34 | **Windfury Weapon** replaces Rockbiter on your weapon. **Mana Tide Totem** (talent) is a long cooldown for mana. |
| 35-60 | **Nature's Swiftness** (talent) makes your next **Healing Wave** instant: your emergency heal. Nothing else new in the fight: buy every new rank. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape or the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the wider or rarer version of the key under it. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [shaman keybinds page](keybinds.md), written for Enhancement; the notes under these tables say which keys differ here.

## Levels 1-3: Swing

**The trainer teaches:** Healing Wave (1), **Lightning Bolt** (1), Rockbiter Weapon (1).

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Healing Wave; 1 = Lightning Bolt; Shift+3 = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** 1 Lightning Bolt.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. One **Lightning Bolt** while the mob runs at you, if you like. Mana is no worry yet.
2. Then swing until it is dead.

- Keep **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon at all times. Recast it when it runs out.
- Heal with **Healing Wave** between fights.

## Levels 4-7: Earth Shock

**The trainer teaches:** **Earth Shock** (4), Stoneskin Totem (4), Earthbind Totem (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Healing Wave at 6.

**Keys to set:** Q = Earth Shock; Shift+4 = Mana potion macro (at 5); Shift+F = Earthbind Totem (at 6).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Earth Shock.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Walk in and swing. No opening Lightning Bolt: its mana costs more drinking than the damage saves.
2. **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready.

- **Earth Shock** also interrupts. Use it on a caster's spell and the mob walks to you.
- **Stoneskin Totem** (4) and **Earthbind Totem** (6) are not part of a fight against one mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 15 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Melee 2.3, Earth Shock 1.2.*

## Levels 8-9: Lightning Shield

**The trainer teaches:** **Lightning Shield** (8), Stoneclaw Totem (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Earth Shock at 8; Lightning Bolt at 8.

**Keys to set:** F = Stoneclaw Totem.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Earth Shock, F Stoneclaw Totem, Mouse 5 Healing Wave.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Lightning Shield** up before the pull.
2. **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Under 30% of your health: drop **Stoneclaw Totem**. The mob turns on the totem while you cast **Healing Wave**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 16 at level 8. Presses per kill at level 8: Melee 2.4, Earth Shock 1.4, Lightning Shield 0.5.*

## Levels 10-19: Searing Totem, and talents begin

**The trainer teaches:** Flame Shock (10), Flametongue Weapon (10), **Searing Totem** (10), Strength of Earth Totem (10), Ancestral Spirit (12), Fire Nova (12), Purge (12), Cure Poison (16), Tremor Totem (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Earth Shock at 14; Flame Shock at 18; Healing Wave at 12, 18; Lightning Bolt at 14; Lightning Shield at 16; Stoneclaw Totem at 18.

**Talents:** 10-14 Tidal Focus, 15-17 Mindfulness, 18-19 Thundering Strikes.

**Keys to set:** 2 = Searing Totem; Shift+Q = Flame Shock; Shift+E = Fire Nova (at 12); Shift+2 = Purge (at 12); Shift+Mouse 4 = Tremor Totem (at 18).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Earth Shock, F Stoneclaw Totem, Mouse 5 Healing Wave, 2 Searing Totem.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Searing Totem** as you pull, while you have more than half your mana.
2. **Lightning Shield** up before the pull.
3. **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Under 30% of your health: drop **Stoneclaw Totem** and cast **Healing Wave**.

- The first points: **Tidal Focus** (10-14) for 5% more chance to hit, **Mindfulness** (15-17) to keep half your mana regeneration going while you cast.
- **Flame Shock** (10) shares its cooldown with **Earth Shock**. Against one mob the fight lab never casts it.
- Stay on **Rockbiter Weapon** until level 30. Use a slow two-handed weapon as soon as you can: your swings are most of your damage.
- **Strength of Earth Totem** (10) and **Fire Nova** (12) cost more mana than they return against one mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 16 at level 10, 23 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Melee 2.3, Earth Shock 1.4, Searing Totem 1.0, Lightning Shield 0.5.*

## Between fights

- Heal back to about 80% of your health with **Lesser Healing Wave** from level 20, or **Healing Wave** for a big gap and before 20.
- Eat if you are still under 80% and out of mana for heals. Drink only below about 15% of your mana.
- Recast your weapon imbue when it runs out, and your shield before each pull: **Lightning Shield**, or **Water Shield** from 28 when you are under 60% of your mana.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of **Earth Shock**, **Lightning Shield** and your heals.

## Other ways to level this class

- Enhancement plays the same list with **Stormstrike** and levels faster per kill in the fight lab. If you only quest, level as Enhancement and respec at 60.
- Elemental fights from range with **Lightning Bolt**; it has its own page and guide in this folder.
