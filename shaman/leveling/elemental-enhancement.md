## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Elemental Mastery** is a talent this build does not take: skip the lines that use it. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Lightning Shield** up, **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon (**Windfury Weapon** from 30): you swing it whenever nothing else is ready. Drop **Searing Totem** as you pull while you have more than half your mana, and pull from range with **Lightning Bolt**.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Elemental Mastery** when it is ready, at the start of a fight on a fresh mob (if your build has it): **Lightning Bolt** and **Lava Burst** cast 0.5 s faster for 15 s.
2. **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, as long as the cast ends before it reaches you.
3. **Earth Shock** when it would kill the mob, or to interrupt a caster.
4. **Flame Shock** while it is not on the mob and the mob has more than 30% health.
5. **Lava Burst** whenever it is ready (20% more damage with **Flame Shock** on the mob).
6. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free.
7. **Lightning Bolt** in between while you keep the mana for the next **Earth Shock** and **Flame Shock**; otherwise let your weapon swing.
8. Low on health at any point: under 30% drop **Stoneclaw Totem**, under 40% cast **Lesser Healing Wave**.

**Between fights:** Heal back to 80% health with **Lesser Healing Wave** (eat if you cannot), drink below 15% of your mana.

## Why this rotation

Elemental-Enhancement levels with the Elemental list: **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, then the shocks on cooldown and bolts between them while your mana holds. The Enhancement half (levels 10-31) turns your Intellect into spell damage and attack power (**Mental Quickness**, **Mental Dexterity**), so your spells and your weapon both hit harder; the Elemental talents come last (40-60), **Lava Burst** only at 60.

**Why it works**

- **Shamanistic Focus** (level 20) makes your shocks and **Lightning Shield** 45% cheaper: at 60 the fight lab measured 3.9 seconds per kill without it, the build's most valuable talent.
- **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, as long as the cast ends before it reaches you; **Elemental Alacrity** (levels 45-47) cuts its cast by half a second. Once the mob hits you each hit pushes a cast back until **Eye of the Storm** (55-57): bolt in melee only while you keep the mana for the next shocks.
- The shocks share one cooldown: **Flame Shock** while it is not ticking and the mob is above 30% health, **Earth Shock** on every other free shock and first whenever it would kill. **Reverberation** (levels 52-54) shortens the shared cooldown by 0.6 seconds.
- **Mental Quickness** (levels 30-31) adds 30% of your Intellect as spell damage and **Mental Dexterity** (19 and 23-24) all of it as attack power; **Ancestral Knowledge** (15-18) raises that Intellect. Without Mental Quickness the fight lab measured 2.7 seconds more per kill at 60.
- Before every pull: **Lightning Shield** (**Improved Lightning Shield** from 25), **Rockbiter Weapon** (**Windfury Weapon** from 30), and **Searing Totem** while you have more than half your mana.

**Caveats**

- No **Elemental Mastery**, and **Lava Burst** only at 60: skip those steps until then.
- In the fight lab it takes 35 seconds per kill (levels 20-60): about 15% slower than Enhancement (30.5) and about 5% faster than Elemental (37).
- This list is for one mob at a time; the fight lab does not model area pulls for this rotation.
- Drink only below 15% mana and heal or eat back to 80% health between pulls: the tuned rules the fight lab plays.

**When to change it**

- A nearly dead mob: an instant **Earth Shock** finishes it before another bolt could land.
- A caster mob that stays back: **Earth Shock** interrupts it and locks that school for 2 seconds, which brings it to you.
- A bad pull (you under 30% health): **Stoneclaw Totem** taunts the mob onto itself, then heal with **Lesser Healing Wave**.
- A mob that flees: **Frost Shock** slows it by 50% for 8 seconds (it shares the shock cooldown).
