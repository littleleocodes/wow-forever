## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Lightning Shield** up, **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon (**Windfury Weapon** from 30): you swing it whenever nothing else is ready. Drop **Searing Totem** as you pull while you have more than half your mana, and pull from range with **Lightning Bolt**.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, as long as the cast ends before it reaches you.
2. **Earth Shock** when it would kill the mob, or to interrupt a caster.
3. **Flame Shock** while it is not on the mob and the mob has more than 30% health.
4. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free.
5. **Lightning Bolt** in between while you keep the mana for the next **Earth Shock** and **Flame Shock**; otherwise let your weapon swing.
6. Low on health at any point: under 30% drop **Stoneclaw Totem**, under 40% cast **Lesser Healing Wave**.

**Between fights:** Heal back to 80% health with **Lesser Healing Wave** (eat if you cannot), drink below 15% of your mana.

## Why this rotation

Elemental-Enhancement levels with the Elemental list: **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, then the shocks on cooldown and bolts between them while your mana holds. The talents come in blocks: Elemental first for cheaper, faster bolts (levels 10-20), hit and then crit and Intellect from Restoration and Enhancement (21-35), the deep Elemental talents (36-47), and Enhancement for the last points (48-58), which turn your Intellect into attack power and spell damage (**Mental Dexterity**, **Mental Quickness**).

**Why it works**

- **Shamanistic Focus** (level 35) makes your shocks and **Lightning Shield** 45% cheaper: more bolts between the shocks, and one of the most valuable talents of the Elemental rotation in the fight lab.
- **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, as long as the cast ends before it reaches you; **Elemental Alacrity** (levels 15-17) cuts its cast by half a second. Once the mob hits you each hit pushes a cast back until **Eye of the Storm** (39-41): bolt in melee only while you keep the mana for the next shocks.
- The shocks share one cooldown: **Flame Shock** while it is not ticking and the mob is above 30% health, **Earth Shock** on every other free shock and first whenever it would kill. **Reverberation** (levels 38 and 42-43) shortens the shared cooldown by 0.6 seconds.
- **Ancestral Knowledge** (31-34) raises your Intellect, **Mental Dexterity** (30, 48-49) turns 150% of it into attack power and **Mental Quickness** (57-58) turns 30% of it into spell damage, so the Enhancement points make both your weapon and your spells hit harder.
- Before every pull: **Lightning Shield** (**Improved Lightning Shield** from 50), **Rockbiter Weapon** (**Windfury Weapon** from 30), and **Searing Totem** while you have more than half your mana.

**Caveats**

- **Toughness** (52-55) and **Improved Lightning Shield** (50-51, 56) mostly fill the tier points on the way to **Mental Quickness**: buy them in this order anyway.
- In the fight lab it takes 29 seconds per kill (levels 20-60, level-weighted): about 18% slower than Enhancement (25) and level with Elemental (29).
- This list is for one mob at a time; the fight lab does not model area pulls for this rotation.
- Drink only below 15% mana and heal or eat back to 80% health between pulls: the tuned rules the fight lab plays.

**When to change it**

- A nearly dead mob: an instant **Earth Shock** finishes it before another bolt could land.
- A caster mob that stays back: **Earth Shock** interrupts it and locks that school for 2 seconds, which brings it to you.
- A bad pull (you under 30% health): **Stoneclaw Totem** taunts the mob onto itself, then heal with **Lesser Healing Wave**.
- A mob that flees: **Frost Shock** slows it by 50% for 8 seconds (it shares the shock cooldown).
