## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Nature's Swiftness** is a talent this build does not take: where a line below names it, that part does not apply. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Lightning Shield** up, **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon (**Windfury Weapon** from 30, **Rockbiter Weapon** again from 40), a slow two-handed weapon. Walk in without an opening **Lightning Bolt** (its mana costs more drinking than it saves) and drop **Searing Totem** as you pull while you have more than half your mana.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Rage of the Farseer** at the start of a fight whenever it is ready.
2. **Earth Shock** a caster mob's spell: it interrupts and brings the mob to you.
3. With 5 stacks of **Maelstrom Weapon**: an instant **Lightning Bolt**, once the mob is under 35% health (not on a mob one bolt would overkill).
4. **Stormstrike** whenever it is ready.
5. From 30 to 49, **Flame Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free and its burn is not on the mob (the tuned rules of 2026-11-22); **Earth Shock** on every other shock cooldown: it takes **Stormstrike**'s 20%.
6. Low on health at any point: under 30% drop **Stoneclaw Totem** (or **Nature's Swiftness** and **Healing Wave** if you have it), under 40% cast **Lesser Healing Wave**.

**Between fights:** Heal back to 80% health with **Lesser Healing Wave** (**Healing Wave** for a big gap); eat if you are still under 90%; drink below 15% of your mana. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

## Why this rotation

Melee with a slow two-hander and your weapon imbue (**Windfury Weapon** from 30, **Rockbiter Weapon** again from 40), and use your spells to finish mobs rather than to open on them: every spell costs mana, and mana costs drinking time.

**Why it works**

- Before every pull: **Lightning Shield** and your imbue: **Rockbiter Weapon** until 30, **Windfury Weapon** from 30, **Rockbiter Weapon** again from 40 (its attack power counts on every swing and **Stormstrike**: 0.4% faster per kill than Windfury at 40 and 1.2% at 50 in the rules re-tuned on 2026-11-16, after the hotfix that cut **Mental Dexterity** to 100% of your Intellect). At 60 in the fight lab your swings do about 30% of your damage, and **Lightning Shield** with **Improved Lightning Shield** (levels 18, 19 and 21) about 12%, because a mob hits you all fight.
- **Searing Totem** at the pull while you have more than half your mana: it shoots the mob all fight, a few percent of your damage at 60.
- **Stormstrike** (level 25) whenever it is ready: a weapon hit (100% weapon damage since the 2026-11-10 hotfix, 150% before) that makes your next **Earth Shock** or **Lightning Bolt** 20% stronger, and with **Improved Stormstrike** (38-39) half your mana regeneration continues while you cast. At 60 it is worth about 3 seconds per kill in the fight lab, second only to **Shamanistic Focus**.
- One tuned rule shapes the fight: no **Lightning Bolt** until the mob is under 35% health, so no opening bolt, and a 5-stack bolt finishes the mob. The fight lab measured it about 10% faster per kill (re-tuned 2026-10-20).
- **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free: about a third of your damage at 60. From 30 to 49 **Flame Shock** takes the shock cooldowns its burn is off (the tuned rules of 2026-11-22, 0.6% faster per kill over levels 20-60), and **Earth Shock** the others; from 50 the rules leave **Flame Shock** out again.
- **Maelstrom Weapon** (levels 40-44) stacks as you hit: since the 2026-11-10 hotfix each stack takes 20% off your next **Lightning Bolt**'s cast time and mana at every rank, and more points bring the stacks faster, so 5 stacks make it instant and free. **Rage of the Farseer** (45) goes at the start of a fight whenever it is ready.

**Caveats**

- **Shamanistic Focus** (at 20) makes shocks and **Lightning Shield** 45% cheaper; at 60 it is worth about 4 seconds per kill in the fight lab, the most of any talent in this build.
- Before 25 you have no **Stormstrike** and before 40 no **Maelstrom Weapon**: until then it is weapon, shield, **Earth Shock** and **Searing Totem**.
- **Anticipation** (22 and 24) and **Elemental Weapons** (23) mostly fill the 15 Enhancement points that **Stormstrike**'s row asks for before you can take it at 25.
- Between pulls heal back to 80% health, eat if you are still under 90% and drink only below 15% mana, and whenever you sit down for one, take the other too: it costs no extra time (the tuned rules of 2026-11-17). Below level 24 eat only under 80%: there the 90% line sits you down after almost every kill, 9-10% slower per kill at 20-22 in the fight lab; from 26 on it is faster.

**When to change it**

- A caster mob that stays back: **Earth Shock** interrupts it and locks that school for 2 seconds, which brings it to you.
- Do not spend a 5-stack **Lightning Bolt** on a mob your next swing will kill: the stacks last 30 seconds and may carry over to the next pull.
- A mob that flees at low health: **Earth Shock** finishes it, or **Frost Shock** slows it by 50%.
