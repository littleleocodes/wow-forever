## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Nature's Swiftness** is a talent this build does not take: skip the lines that use it. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Lightning Shield** up, **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon (**Windfury Weapon** from 30), a slow two-handed weapon. Walk in without an opening **Lightning Bolt** (its mana costs more drinking than it saves) and drop **Searing Totem** as you pull while you have more than half your mana.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Rage of the Farseer** at the start of a fight whenever it is ready.
2. **Earth Shock** a caster mob's spell: it interrupts and brings the mob to you.
3. With 5 stacks of **Maelstrom Weapon**: an instant **Lightning Bolt**, once the mob is under 35% health (not on a mob one bolt would overkill).
4. **Stormstrike** whenever it is ready.
5. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free: it takes **Stormstrike**'s 20%. It shares that cooldown with **Flame Shock**, which the fight lab therefore never casts on one mob.
6. Low on health at any point: under 30% drop **Stoneclaw Totem** (or **Nature's Swiftness** and **Healing Wave** if you have it), under 40% cast **Lesser Healing Wave**.

**Between fights:** Heal back to 80% health with **Lesser Healing Wave** (**Healing Wave** for a big gap), drink below 15% of your mana.

## Why this rotation

Melee with a slow two-hander and **Windfury Weapon**, and use your spells to finish mobs rather than to open on them: every spell costs mana, and mana costs drinking time.

**Why it works**

- Before every pull: **Lightning Shield** and your imbue (**Rockbiter Weapon** until 30, then **Windfury Weapon**). At 60 in the fight lab your swings and Windfury attacks do over 40% of your damage, and **Lightning Shield** with **Improved Lightning Shield** (levels 17, 19 and 21) about 15%, because a mob hits you all fight.
- **Searing Totem** at the pull while you have more than half your mana: it shoots the mob all fight, about 6% of your damage at 60.
- **Stormstrike** (level 25) whenever it is ready: a weapon hit that makes your next **Earth Shock** or **Lightning Bolt** 20% stronger, and with **Improved Stormstrike** (35-36) half your mana regeneration continues while you cast. It is this build's most valuable talent at 60 (4.5 seconds per kill without it).
- One tuned rule shapes the fight: no **Lightning Bolt** until the mob is under 35% health, so no opening bolt, and a 5-stack bolt finishes the mob. The fight lab measured it about 10% faster per kill (2026-10-09).
- **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free: about a quarter of your damage at 60. It shares that cooldown with **Flame Shock**, so the fight lab never casts **Flame Shock** on one mob.
- **Maelstrom Weapon** (from 37) stacks as you hit; with all 5 points (from 42), 5 stacks make **Lightning Bolt** instant and free. **Rage of the Farseer** (from 40) goes at the start of a fight whenever it is ready.

**Caveats**

- **Shamanistic Focus** (at 20) makes shocks and **Lightning Shield** 45% cheaper; at 60 it is worth 4 seconds per kill in the fight lab.
- Before 25 you have no **Stormstrike** and before 37 no **Maelstrom Weapon**: until then it is weapon, shield, **Earth Shock** and **Searing Totem**.
- Drink only below 15% mana and heal or eat back to 80% health between pulls: the tuned rules the fight lab plays.

**When to change it**

- A caster mob that stays back: **Earth Shock** interrupts it and locks that school for 2 seconds, which brings it to you.
- Do not spend a 5-stack **Lightning Bolt** on a mob your next swing will kill: the stacks last 30 seconds and may carry over to the next pull.
- A mob that flees at low health: **Earth Shock** finishes it, or **Frost Shock** slows it by 50%.
