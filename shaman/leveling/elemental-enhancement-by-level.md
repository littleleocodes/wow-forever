# Elemental-Enhancement Shaman — leveling, level by level

[Shaman](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Elemental-Enhancement](elemental-enhancement.md) > Level by level

What an Elemental-Enhancement shaman presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. The hybrid plays the Elemental list: **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, then the shocks on their shared cooldown, more bolts between them while your mana holds, and your weapon swinging in every gap. Its talents swap trees in blocks: Elemental first for cheaper, faster bolts (10-20), then hit from Restoration (21-23), Enhancement for crit and Intellect (25-35), Elemental again for the deep bolt talents (36-47), and Enhancement for most of the last points. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 26/22/3 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/shaman/60/520333100301131-054033010400002-03-12)), the one on the [Elemental-Enhancement leveling page](elemental-enhancement.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | **Lightning Bolt** is your attack: from range as the mob walks in, and again in melee while your mana lasts. |
| 4-7 | **Earth Shock**, an instant on a short cooldown. It also interrupts a caster. |
| 8-9 | **Lightning Shield** on yourself: mobs that hit you take damage. **Stoneclaw Totem** is your first panic button. |
| 10-19 | **Flame Shock** burns the mob over time and **Searing Totem** shoots it from the pull. Talent points start, in Elemental. |
| 20-29 | Nothing new in the fight. **Lesser Healing Wave** is a fast heal, **Ghost Wolf** gets you between mobs sooner. |
| 30-34 | **Windfury Weapon** replaces Rockbiter on your weapon. **Mental Dexterity** (talent) turns your Intellect into attack power for its swings, and **Ancestral Knowledge** raises that Intellect. |
| 35-38 | **Shamanistic Focus** (talent) makes your shocks and **Lightning Shield** 45% cheaper: more bolts between the shocks. |
| 39-60 | **Eye of the Storm** (talent): at 3 points (41), 70% of a mob's hits no longer push your **Lightning Bolt** back. Nothing else new in the fight: buy every new rank. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape or the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the wider or rarer version of the key under it. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [shaman keybinds page](keybinds.md), written for Enhancement; the notes under these tables say which keys differ here.

- Three spells sit on other keys than in the [Enhancement guide](enhancement-by-level.md): **Lightning Bolt**, the spell you press most here, takes Q; **Earth Shock** moves to R (Stormstrike's key, a talent this build lacks); **Flame Shock**, cast once per mob, moves up from Shift+Q to 1, the key Lightning Bolt leaves. Key 4 stays free: no Rage of the Farseer here. The heals, the totems and **Frost Shock** keep their keys, so a shaman who switches specs finds them where they were.
- Q is plain **Lightning Bolt**, no macro: a spell never starts your swings. Right-click the mob once it reaches you, and your weapon swings between casts (the fight lab's weapon does a part of your damage at every level). The middle mouse button stays free.
- Two swaps on bar 1: **Lesser Healing Wave** takes the thumb at 20 and **Healing Wave** moves to C as the big heal.
- No key: **Lightning Shield**, the weapon imbue (**Rockbiter Weapon**, **Windfury Weapon** from 30), **Ghost Wolf**, food, water, the mount and the Hearthstone. **Strength of Earth Totem** and the other buff totems cost more than they return against one mob.
- The single-target rotation never casts **Fire Nova**, **Chain Lightning** or **Magma Totem**. They are there for a pull that brings friends.

## Levels 1-3: Lightning Bolt

**The trainer teaches:** Healing Wave (1), **Lightning Bolt** (1), Rockbiter Weapon (1).

**Keys to set:** Q = Lightning Bolt; Mouse 5 = Healing Wave; Shift+3 = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Lightning Bolt.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Lightning Bolt** from as far as it reaches, and again while the mob walks in.
2. In melee, **Lightning Bolt** again while you have the mana; then let your weapon swing until the mob is dead.

- Keep **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon. Your swings fill every gap between casts, from here to 60.
- Right-click the mob once it reaches you, so your weapon swings between casts.
- Heal with **Healing Wave** between fights.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 12 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Lightning Bolt 2.4, Melee 1.0.*

## Levels 4-7: Earth Shock

**The trainer teaches:** **Earth Shock** (4), Stoneskin Totem (4), Earthbind Totem (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Healing Wave at 6.

**Keys to set:** R = Earth Shock; Shift+4 = Mana potion macro (at 5); Shift+F = Earthbind Totem (at 6).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Lightning Bolt, R Earth Shock.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, as long as the cast ends before it reaches you. The first one from as far as it reaches.
2. **Earth Shock** a caster mob's spell: it interrupts, and the mob walks to you.
3. In melee: **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready, first of all when it would kill the mob.
4. **Lightning Bolt** between shocks while you keep the mana for the next **Earth Shock**; otherwise let your weapon swing. Skip it on a mob your next swing or shock will finish.

- In melee the bolts now stop where your mana would no longer cover the next **Earth Shock**: the shock is instant, and a mob's hits cannot push it back.
- **Stoneskin Totem** (4) and **Earthbind Totem** (6) are not part of a fight against one mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 15 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Lightning Bolt 3.0, Melee 1.3, Earth Shock 1.0.*

## Levels 10-19: Flame Shock and Searing Totem, and talents begin

## Between fights

- Heal back to about 80% of your health with **Lesser Healing Wave** from level 20, or **Healing Wave** for a big gap and before 20.
- Eat if you are still under 80% and out of mana for heals. Drink only below about 15% of your mana.
- Recast your weapon imbue when it runs out, and **Lightning Shield** before each pull.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of **Lightning Bolt**, **Earth Shock**, **Flame Shock** and your heals.
