# Enhancement Shaman — leveling, level by level

[Shaman](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Enhancement](enhancement.md) > Level by level

What an Enhancement shaman presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Enhancement is the shaman's fastest leveling spec in the fight lab: you walk in, swing a slow weapon, and add a shock and a strike on short cooldowns. Mana goes to shocks, not to Lightning Bolts. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 12/33/6 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/shaman/60/5501001-050033312041012251-050001-12)), the one on the [Enhancement leveling page](enhancement.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | Your weapon and **Rockbiter Weapon** do the work. |
| 4-7 | **Earth Shock**, an instant on a short cooldown. From here on, skip the opening Lightning Bolt. |
| 8-9 | **Lightning Shield** on yourself: mobs that hit you take damage. **Stoneclaw Totem** is your first panic button. |
| 10-19 | **Searing Totem** at the pull adds free damage for the whole fight. Talent points start. |
| 20-24 | Nothing new in the fight. **Lesser Healing Wave** is a fast heal, **Ghost Wolf** gets you between mobs sooner. |
| 25-29 | **Stormstrike** (talent): an instant weapon strike that also makes your next **Earth Shock** or **Lightning Bolt** hit 20% harder. |
| 30-34 | **Windfury Weapon** replaces Rockbiter on your weapon: a chance on every hit for extra swings. **Flame Shock** joins the list from 30 to 49: the tuned rules of 2026-11-22 cast it whenever the shock cooldown is free and its burn is not on the mob (0.6% faster per kill over levels 20-60). |
| 35-39 | Nothing new to press. **Improved Stormstrike** (38-39): after a **Stormstrike**, half your mana regeneration keeps going while you cast, and a dodge or parry can reset its cooldown. |
| 40-44 | **Maelstrom Weapon** (talent): your hits build stacks, and at 5 your **Lightning Bolt** is instant and free. |
| 45-49 | **Rage of the Farseer** (talent) opens a fight whenever it is ready. Nothing else new: buy every new rank. |
| 50-60 | From 50 the tuned rules drop **Flame Shock** again: **Earth Shock** takes every shock cooldown. Pressed on to 60 it measured 0.8-1.4% slower a kill at 50 and 0.5-1.1% faster at 55-60 (the fight lab, 2026-11-23): no clear gain either way. The rest stays. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; buffs, food, drink, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the escape or the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the wider or rarer version of the key under it. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [shaman keybinds page](keybinds.md).

- Two swaps on bar 1: **Lesser Healing Wave** takes the thumb at 20 and **Healing Wave** moves to C as the big heal.
- **Lightning Shield** and your weapon imbue (**Rockbiter Weapon**, **Windfury Weapon** from 30, **Rockbiter Weapon** again from 40) are buffs: click them between fights. The fight lab puts the shield back before a pull, about every second kill, and never in the fight.
- No key: **Lightning Shield**, the weapon imbue, **Ghost Wolf**, food, water, the mount and the Hearthstone. **Strength of Earth Totem** and the other buff totems cost more than they return against one mob.
- Right-click the mob to start swinging at levels 1-3. From 4, Q is a macro on the [keybinds page](keybinds.md) that also starts your swings, so the middle mouse button stays free.
- From 30 to 49 the single-target rotation casts **Flame Shock** (Shift+Q) on the shock cooldowns its burn is off. It never casts **Fire Nova**, **Chain Lightning** or **Magma Totem**: they are there for a pull that brings friends.

## Levels 1-3: Swing

**The trainer teaches:** Healing Wave (1), **Lightning Bolt** (1), Rockbiter Weapon (1).

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Healing Wave; 1 = Lightning Bolt; Shift+3 = Healing potion macro.

**Your keys in this stage:** 1 Lightning Bolt.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. One **Lightning Bolt** while the mob runs at you, if you like. Mana is no worry yet.
2. Then swing until it is dead.

- Keep **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon at all times. Recast it when it runs out.
- Heal with **Healing Wave** between fights.

## Levels 4-7: Earth Shock

**The trainer teaches:** **Earth Shock** (4), Stoneskin Totem (4), Earthbind Totem (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Healing Wave at 6.

**Keys to set:** Q = Earth Shock; Shift+4 = Mana potion macro (at 5); Shift+F = Earthbind Totem (at 6).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Earth Shock.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Walk in and swing. No opening Lightning Bolt: its mana costs more drinking than the damage saves.
2. **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready.

- **Earth Shock** also interrupts. Use it on a caster's spell and the mob walks to you.
- **Stoneskin Totem** (4) and **Earthbind Totem** (6) are not part of a fight against one mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 15 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Melee 2.4, Earth Shock 1.3.*

## Levels 8-9: Lightning Shield

## Between fights

- Heal back to about 80% of your health with **Lesser Healing Wave** from level 20, or **Healing Wave** for a big gap and before 20.
- Eat if you are still under 90% after the heals: the tuned rules of 2026-11-17 (80% before), faster from 26 on (3.6% per kill at 40). Below level 24 eat only under 80%: there the 90% line sits you down after almost every kill, 9-10% slower per kill at 20-22 in the fight lab (the seconds on this page play 90% from 20). Drink only below about 15% of your mana.
- Whenever you sit down to drink, eat as well if your health is not full, and drink while you eat if your mana is not full: the other bar fills in the same seconds at no extra time. Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full. It saves a little for this build (1.0% per kill in the fight lab, 2026-11-16), and the tuned rules play it.
- Recast your weapon imbue when it runs out, and **Lightning Shield** before each pull.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of **Earth Shock** and **Lightning Shield**.

## Other ways to level this class

- Elemental is about 9% slower per kill in the fight lab. It plays **Lightning Bolt** from range and is on its own page in this folder.
- A shaman who wants to heal at 60 levels fastest as Enhancement and respecs, or levels into the Restoration build shown on its page.
