# Enhancement Shaman — leveling, level by level

[Shaman](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Enhancement](enhancement.md) > Level by level

What an Enhancement shaman presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Enhancement is the shaman's fastest leveling spec in the fight lab: you walk in, swing a slow weapon, and add a shock and a strike on short cooldowns. Mana goes to shocks, not to Lightning Bolts. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 12/32/7 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/shaman/60/55000011-050033211051012251-05002-7)), the one on the [Enhancement leveling page](enhancement.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-3 | Your weapon and **Rockbiter Weapon** do the work. |
| 4-7 | **Earth Shock**, an instant on a short cooldown. From here on, skip the opening Lightning Bolt. |
| 8-9 | **Lightning Shield** on yourself: mobs that hit you take damage. **Stoneclaw Totem** is your first panic button. |
| 10-19 | **Searing Totem** at the pull adds free damage for the whole fight. Talent points start. |
| 20-24 | Nothing new in the fight. **Lesser Healing Wave** is a fast heal, **Ghost Wolf** gets you between mobs sooner. |
| 25-29 | **Stormstrike** (talent): an instant weapon strike that also makes your next shocks hit 20% harder. |
| 30-34 | **Windfury Weapon** replaces Rockbiter on your weapon: a chance on every hit for extra swings. |
| 35-39 | **Maelstrom Weapon** (talent): your hits build stacks, and at 5 your **Lightning Bolt** is instant. |
| 40-60 | **Rage of the Farseer** (talent) opens a fight whenever it is ready. Nothing else new: buy every new rank. |

## Levels 1-3: Swing

**The trainer teaches:** Healing Wave (1), **Lightning Bolt** (1), Rockbiter Weapon (1).

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. One **Lightning Bolt** while the mob runs at you, if you like. Mana is no worry yet.
2. Then swing until it is dead.

- Keep **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon at all times. Recast it when it runs out.
- Heal with **Healing Wave** between fights.

## Levels 4-7: Earth Shock

**The trainer teaches:** **Earth Shock** (4), Stoneskin Totem (4), Earthbind Totem (6).

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Walk in and swing. No opening Lightning Bolt: its mana costs more drinking than the damage saves.
2. **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready.

- **Earth Shock** also interrupts. Use it on a caster's spell and the mob walks to you.
- **Stoneskin Totem** (4) and **Earthbind Totem** (6) are not part of a fight against one mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 15 at level 4. Presses per kill at level 4: Melee 2.4, Earth Shock 1.3.*

## Levels 8-9: Lightning Shield

**The trainer teaches:** **Lightning Shield** (8), Stoneclaw Totem (8).

**New ranks to buy:** Earth Shock at 8.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. Keep **Lightning Shield** up.
2. **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Under 30% of your health: drop **Stoneclaw Totem**. The mob turns on the totem while you cast **Healing Wave**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 16 at level 8. Presses per kill at level 8: Melee 2.4, Earth Shock 1.5, Lightning Shield 0.5.*

## Levels 10-19: Searing Totem, and talents begin

**The trainer teaches:** Flame Shock (10), Flametongue Weapon (10), **Searing Totem** (10), Strength of Earth Totem (10), Ancestral Spirit (12), Fire Nova (12), Purge (12), Cure Poison (16), Tremor Totem (18).

**New ranks to buy:** Earth Shock at 14; Lightning Shield at 16.

**Talents:** 10-14 Thundering Strikes, 15-17 Mental Dexterity, 18-19 Improved Lightning Shield.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Searing Totem** as you pull, while you have more than half your mana.
2. Keep **Lightning Shield** up.
3. **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Under 30% of your health: drop **Stoneclaw Totem** and cast **Healing Wave**.

- **Flame Shock** (10) shares its cooldown with **Earth Shock**. Against one mob the fight lab never casts it.
- Stay on **Rockbiter Weapon** until level 30. **Flametongue Weapon** (10) is not the leveling imbue.
- Use a slow two-handed weapon as soon as you can: every strike and proc scales with the weapon's hit.
- **Strength of Earth Totem** (10) and **Fire Nova** (12) cost more mana than they return against one mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 16 at level 10, 24 at level 19. Presses per kill at level 10: Melee 2.4, Earth Shock 1.4, Searing Totem 1.0, Lightning Shield 0.5.*

**New ranks to buy:** Earth Shock at 24; Lightning Shield at 24; Searing Totem at 20.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

3. **Earth Shock** whenever it is ready.

- **Ghost Wolf** (20) between pulls and on every walk back from the graveyard.
- **Frost Shock** (20) is for a mob that runs away, or to slow one you want to escape. It shares the shock cooldown.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 21 at level 20. Presses per kill at level 20: Melee 3.1, Earth Shock 2.0, Searing Totem 0.8, Lightning Shield 0.6.*

## Levels 25-29: Stormstrike

**The trainer teaches:** Far Sight (26), Magma Totem (26), Mana Spring Totem (26), Fire Resistance Totem (28), Flametongue Totem (28), Water Walking (28).

**Talents:** 25 Stormstrike, 26-29 Flurry.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Searing Totem** as you pull, while you have more than half your mana.
2. Keep **Lightning Shield** up.
3. **Stormstrike** whenever it is ready.
4. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free. Cast after a **Stormstrike**, it takes the 20% bonus.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Under 40% of your health: **Lesser Healing Wave**. Under 30%: drop **Stoneclaw Totem** first.

- **Flurry** (26-29, 32) makes you swing faster after every critical hit. Nothing to press.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 18 at level 25. Presses per kill at level 25: Melee 2.1, Stormstrike 1.4, Earth Shock 1.1, Searing Totem 0.7, Lightning Shield 0.4.*

## Levels 35-39: Maelstrom Weapon

**The trainer teaches:** Windwall Totem (36), Disease Cleansing Totem (38).

**New ranks to buy:** Earth Shock at 36; Lightning Bolt at 38.

**Talents:** 35-39 Maelstrom Weapon.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Searing Totem** as you pull, while you have more than half your mana.
2. Keep **Lightning Shield** up.
3. With 5 stacks of **Maelstrom Weapon**: an instant **Lightning Bolt**, once the mob is under 35% of its health.
4. **Stormstrike** whenever it is ready.
5. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Under 40% of your health: **Lesser Healing Wave**. Under 30%: drop **Stoneclaw Totem** first.

- The stacks come faster with every point of **Maelstrom Weapon** (35-39). Never cast **Lightning Bolt** with its cast time.
- Do not spend the instant bolt on a mob it would overkill.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 24 at level 35. Presses per kill at level 35: Melee 3.0, Stormstrike 2.0, Windfury 1.6, Earth Shock 1.6, Searing Totem 0.6, Lightning Shield 0.6, Lightning Bolt 0.1.*

## Levels 40-60: Rage of the Farseer, the finished rotation

**The trainer teaches:** Call of the Spirits (40), Chain Heal (40), Grace of Air Totem (42).

**New ranks to buy:** Earth Shock at 48, 60; Lightning Bolt at 44, 50, 56; Lightning Shield at 40, 48, 56; Searing Totem at 40, 50, 60.

**Talents:** 40 Rage of the Farseer, 41 Improved Stormstrike, 42-46 Tidal Focus, 47-51 Concussion, 52-56 Convection, 57 Elemental Focus, 58 Elemental Warding, 59-60 Totemic Focus.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Rage of the Farseer** at the start of a fight whenever it is ready.
2. **Searing Totem** as you pull, while you have more than half your mana.
3. Keep **Lightning Shield** up.
4. With 5 stacks of **Maelstrom Weapon**: an instant **Lightning Bolt**, once the mob is under 35% of its health.
5. **Stormstrike** whenever it is ready.
6. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Under 40% of your health: **Lesser Healing Wave**. Under 30%: drop **Stoneclaw Totem** first.

- The remaining points go to Elemental for cheaper, harder shocks: **Convection** (52-56) and **Concussion** (47-51).

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 25 at level 40, 26 at level 50, 26 at level 60. Presses per kill at level 40: Melee 3.2, Stormstrike 2.0, Windfury 1.7, Earth Shock 1.4, Lightning Bolt 0.7, Searing Totem 0.6, Lightning Shield 0.6.*

## Between fights

- Heal back to about 80% of your health with **Lesser Healing Wave** from level 20, or **Healing Wave** for a big gap and before 20.
- Eat if you are still under 80% and out of mana for heals. Drink only below about 15% of your mana.
- Recast your weapon imbue when it runs out, and **Lightning Shield** before each pull.
- Visit the trainer every even level for new ranks of **Earth Shock** and **Lightning Shield**.

## Other ways to level this class

- Elemental is about 16% slower per kill in the fight lab. It plays **Lightning Bolt** from range and is on its own page in this folder.
- A shaman who wants to heal at 60 levels fastest as Enhancement and respecs, or levels into the Restoration build shown on its page.
