## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Lightning Shield** up, **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon (**Windfury Weapon** from 30): you swing it whenever nothing else is ready. Drop **Searing Totem** as you pull while you have more than half your mana, and pull from range with **Lightning Bolt**.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, as long as the cast ends before it reaches you.
2. **Earth Shock** when it would kill the mob, or to interrupt a caster.
3. **Flame Shock** while it is not on the mob and the mob has more than 30% health.
4. **Lava Burst** whenever it is ready (20% more damage with **Flame Shock** on the mob).
5. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free.
6. **Lightning Bolt** in between while you keep the mana for the next **Earth Shock** and **Flame Shock**; otherwise let your weapon swing.
7. Low on health at any point: under 30% drop **Stoneclaw Totem**, under 40% cast **Lesser Healing Wave**.

**Between fights:** Heal back to 80% health (eat if you cannot), drink below a third of your mana.

## Why this rotation

Kill as much as you can with **Lightning Bolt** before the mob reaches you, then use your shocks whenever they are ready and bolt between them while your mana holds. Mana is the limit: about a third of each kill is drinking and eating in the fight lab.

**Why it works**

- **Lightning Shield** before each pull: it strikes a mob each time it hits you, one ball every few seconds. With **Improved Lightning Shield** and **Shamanistic Focus** it was about an eighth of your damage at 60 in the fight lab.
- **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in: nothing pushes those casts back. Once the mob hits you, each hit pushes a cast back (about 3 times per kill in the fight lab); **Eye of the Storm** (from 46) cuts that by 70%.
- The shocks share one cooldown and cannot be pushed back. **Flame Shock** goes first while it is not ticking and the mob is above 30% health; **Earth Shock** takes every other shock, and comes first whenever it would kill. **Shamanistic Focus** (at 20) makes both 45% cheaper: at 60 it is worth 5 seconds per kill, this build's most valuable talent.
- **Lava Burst** comes with your last talent point at 60. Cast it whenever it is ready, with **Flame Shock** on the mob for its 20% bonus.
- Bolt between shocks only while you keep the mana for the next **Earth Shock** and **Flame Shock**; otherwise let your weapon swing. The fight lab keeps **Rockbiter Weapon** (from 30, **Windfury Weapon**) on, and your weapon does about a fifth of your damage at 60.
- Drop **Searing Totem** at each pull while you have more than half your mana: it does about 8% of your damage at 60.

**Caveats**

- From 40, **Elemental Focus** sometimes makes your next damage spell free: nothing to change, the next spell in the list takes it.
- This list is for one mob at a time; the fight lab does not model area pulls for Elemental. **Chain Lightning** hits up to 3 targets if you pull more.
- The build spends its first 20 points outside Elemental (the leveling search found that faster), so your first Elemental talent comes at 30.

**When to change it**

- A nearly dead mob: an instant **Earth Shock** finishes it before another bolt could land.
- A caster mob that stays back: **Earth Shock** interrupts it and locks that school for 2 seconds, which brings it to you.
- A bad pull (you under 30% health): **Stoneclaw Totem** taunts the mob onto itself, then heal with **Lesser Healing Wave**; the fight lab does this for every shaman.
- A mob that flees: **Frost Shock** slows it by 50% for 8 seconds (it shares the shock cooldown).

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-09; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
