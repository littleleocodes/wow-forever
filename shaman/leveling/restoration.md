**Leveling as Restoration or as Enhancement?** This build takes 34 s per kill (level-weighted, 20-60), [Enhancement](../leveling/enhancement.md) 33 s: Restoration is 2% slower. Its talent order takes damage talents first and puts its first point into Restoration at level 31, so it only becomes a real Restoration build in the 40s and 50s. Level this way if you want to heal dungeons later on the way; otherwise level as Enhancement and switch at 60 (a respec at a class trainer).

## How to play

**Before the fight:** **Lightning Shield** up, **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon (**Windfury Weapon** from 30), a slow two-handed weapon. Drop **Searing Totem** as you pull.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in.
2. **Stormstrike** whenever ready, then **Earth Shock**.
3. **Flame Shock** while your mana allows.
4. **Lightning Bolt** when Maelstrom Weapon reaches 5 stacks (it is instant).
5. Low on health: **Stoneclaw Totem** and **Lesser Healing Wave**.

**Between fights:** Heal back to 80% health, drink below a third of your mana.
