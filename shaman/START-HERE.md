# Shaman: start here

> For new players: what to play, which talents to take and what to press. Generated by `_reference/data/render_start_here.py` on 2026-09-29 from this project's builds and simulations of the WoW: Forever beta (client build 1.60.1.70009). The numbers are simulations, not measurements in the game; the class's other pages hold the evidence. Do not hand-edit.

Classic's Horde raid healer, with Elemental as a late-game caster and Enhancement as a Windfury-support hybrid. Forever gives Elemental Lava Burst and Lightning Overload (but removes Lightning/Elemental Mastery), turns Enhancement into a real melee spec (Maelstrom Weapon, Rage of the Farseer) by removing the totem-improvement talents, and gives Restoration Riptide and Water Shield. Dwarf shamans bring the class to the Alliance; Skyborne on both sides.

What to press: Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a slow two-hander: Lightning Shield (Water Shield when talented and mana is low) and the weapon imbue up (Rockbiter's attack power until 30, Windfury's extra attacks from 30), Searing Totem dropped at the pull, a Lightning Bolt while the mob walks in, then Stormstrike, Earth Shock (buffed by Stormstrike), Flame Shock while mana allows and instant Maelstrom Lightning Bolts between white swings; Stoneclaw Totem and a Lesser Healing Wave when low, Nature's Swiftness + Healing Wave in an emergency; a death with Reincarnation ready costs 30 s instead of 90. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, heals back to 80% health, water below 35% mana.

What to press with the top build, One slow weapon (the first line that is ready is the one you cast):

Before the pull: cast Strength of Earth Totem; cast Grace of Air Totem; cast Searing Totem.

1. If time left on the totem ≤ 0s: cast Strength of Earth Totem
2. If time left on the totem ≤ 0s: cast Grace of Air Totem
3. Use every other cooldown when ready
4. Cast Stormstrike
5. If stacks of Maelstrom Weapon ≥ 5: cast Lightning Bolt
6. If time left on the totem ≤ 0s and time left in the fight ≥ 20s: cast Searing Totem
7. If not Flame Shock is on the target: cast Flame Shock
8. Cast Earth Shock
