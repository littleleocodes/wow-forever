## How to play

**Before the pull:** **Strength of Earth Totem**, **Grace of Air Totem**, **Searing Totem**.

**Keep up all fight:** **Strength of Earth Totem**, **Grace of Air Totem**, **Magma Totem**, **Searing Totem**, **Rockbiter Weapon**.

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Flame Shock**: keep it on the target, until the last 10 seconds of the fight
2. **Stormstrike**
3. **Lightning Bolt** when Maelstrom Weapon has 5 or more stacks
4. **Earth Shock**
5. **Fire Nova** when you have at least 10% mana

Between these, your white swings do the rest.

## Why this rotation

This hybrid is a melee shaman: Enhancement's **Stormstrike**, **Flurry** and **Maelstrom Weapon** with Elemental's **Elemental Devastation**, **Call of Flame** and **Trial by Fire**. It plays the Enhancement raid list: **Flame Shock**, **Stormstrike**, an instant **Lightning Bolt** at 5 **Maelstrom Weapon** stacks, **Earth Shock** and **Fire Nova** between your swings, which with **Windfury Weapon** do about half the damage.

**Why it works**

- Before the pull, drop **Strength of Earth Totem**, **Grace of Air Totem** and **Searing Totem**; the first two last 5 minutes, so one cast covers the fight.
- **Stormstrike** is your main button: 8% of your base mana, a weapon hit and 20% more on your next **Lightning Bolt**, **Chain Lightning** or **Earth Shock**; with **Improved Stormstrike** each one keeps half your mana regeneration going while you cast for 15 seconds.
- **Flame Shock** whenever the burn is off the target, **Earth Shock** on every other free shock. Your Elemental points shape them: 2 points in **Reverberation** bring the shared cooldown to 5.6 seconds, **Concussion** adds 5% to **Earth Shock** and your bolts, **Shamanistic Focus** halves their cost, and **Call of Flame** adds 15% to **Flame Shock**, **Fire Nova** and **Searing Totem**.
- **Lightning Bolt** only at 5 stacks of **Maelstrom Weapon**, when it is instant and free. This build takes 4 of the 5 points, so the stacks come a little slower than in the Enhancement build.
- **Fire Nova** last: it erupts around your Fire totem, so keep **Searing Totem** within 10 yards of the boss and replace it when it expires. **Trial by Fire** makes it 20% stronger on a 6-second cooldown; adding Fire Nova to the Enhancement list measured about 7% more damage in the rotation search.
- **Elemental Devastation** turns every spell crit into 9% more melee crit chance for 10 seconds, which feeds **Flurry**: another reason to keep a shock going between swings.
- Put **Windfury Weapon** on your weapon with a sharpening stone on top: in Forever an imbue and a stone stack, and **Elemental Weapons** makes the Windfury attacks 40% stronger.

**Caveats**

- No **Rage of the Farseer** in this build: use your trinkets and potion at the pull.
- Mana: **Improved Stormstrike** and **Shamanistic Focus** pay for the shocks; with only your own buffs the simulator's build still runs out of mana for part of a 3-minute fight. Use a mana potion when low, and do not hold shocks back.
- Several targets: **Fire Nova** hits every enemy within 10 yards of your Fire totem. You can have only one Fire totem out; **Totemic Call** brings it to you, with your other totems.
- For a **Lightning Bolt** caster, play Elemental: this split is a melee build.

**When to change it**

- In the last 10 seconds, skip **Flame Shock** (its burn would not finish) and use **Earth Shock**.
- In a party of warriors and rogues, ask whether they want **Windfury Totem** instead of **Grace of Air Totem**: a party gets only one, and it does nothing for you while you use **Windfury Weapon**.
