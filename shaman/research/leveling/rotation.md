## enhancement-dps

Played by the page builds of [Enhancement](../../leveling/enhancement.md) ([`enhancement-open-leveling`](builds/enhancement-open-leveling.md)), [Restoration](../../leveling/restoration.md) ([`restoration-leveling`](builds/restoration-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a slow two-hander: Lightning Shield (Water Shield when talented and mana is low) and the weapon imbue up (Rockbiter's attack power until 30, Windfury's extra attacks from 30), Searing Totem dropped at the pull, a Lightning Bolt while the mob walks in, then Stormstrike, Earth Shock (buffed by Stormstrike), Flame Shock while mana allows and instant Maelstrom Lightning Bolts between white swings; Stoneclaw Totem and a Lesser Healing Wave when low, Nature's Swiftness + Healing Wave in an emergency; a death with Reincarnation ready costs 30 s instead of 90. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, heals back to 80% health, water below 35% mana.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Lightning Shield** up, **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon (**Windfury Weapon** from 30, **Rockbiter Weapon** again from 40), a slow two-handed weapon. Walk in without an opening **Lightning Bolt** (its mana costs more drinking than it saves) and drop **Searing Totem** as you pull while you have more than half your mana.

1. **Rage of the Farseer** at the start of a fight whenever it is ready.
2. **Earth Shock** a caster mob's spell: it interrupts and brings the mob to you.
3. With 5 stacks of **Maelstrom Weapon**: an instant **Lightning Bolt**, once the mob is under 35% health (not on a mob one bolt would overkill).
4. **Stormstrike** whenever it is ready.
5. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free: it takes **Stormstrike**'s 20%. It shares that cooldown with **Flame Shock**, which the fight lab therefore never casts on one mob.
6. Low on health at any point: under 30% drop **Stoneclaw Totem** (or **Nature's Swiftness** and **Healing Wave** if you have it), under 40% cast **Lesser Healing Wave**.

Between fights: Heal back to 80% health with **Lesser Healing Wave** (**Healing Wave** for a big gap); eat if you are still under 90%; drink below 15% of your mana. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-17, on top of the priority below): eat below 90% health. Together +1.0% time per kill saved for `enhancement-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## elemental-dps

Played by the page build of [Elemental](../../leveling/elemental.md) ([`elemental-open-leveling`](builds/elemental-open-leveling.md)).

Elemental: Lightning Mastery on a fresh mob when ready, Lightning Bolts from range (the pull and while the mob walks in), then Flame Shock and Earth Shock on cooldown with Lightning Bolts between them while mana lasts (Chain Lightning on a single mob only as CL_SINGLE allows); a bolt in melee eats pushback (Eye of the Storm resists it); the weapon swings whenever nothing is cast. Lava Burst left the game on 2026-11-02 (it was cast on cooldown here).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Lightning Shield** up, **Rockbiter Weapon** on your weapon (**Windfury Weapon** from 30, **Rockbiter Weapon** again from 50): you swing it whenever nothing else is ready. Drop **Searing Totem** as you pull while you have more than half your mana, and pull from range with **Lightning Bolt**.

1. **Lightning Bolt** while the mob walks in, as long as the cast ends before it reaches you.
2. **Earth Shock** when it would kill the mob, or to interrupt a caster.
3. **Flame Shock** while it is not on the mob and the mob has more than 30% health.
4. **Earth Shock** whenever the shock cooldown is free.
5. **Lightning Bolt** in between while you keep the mana for the next **Earth Shock** and **Flame Shock**; otherwise let your weapon swing.
6. Low on health at any point: under 30% drop **Stoneclaw Totem**, under 40% cast **Lesser Healing Wave**.

Between fights: Heal back to 80% health with **Lesser Healing Wave**; eat if you are still under 90%; drink below 15% of your mana. Whenever you sit down to drink, also eat if your health is not full, and whenever you sit down to eat, also drink if your mana is not full: no extra time (the tuned rules do it). Stand up once the bar you sat down for is full.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-17, on top of the priority below): eat below 90% health; IMBUE_TOP = rockbiter. Together +1.2% time per kill saved for `elemental-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
