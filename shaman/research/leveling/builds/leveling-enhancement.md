## Summary

Classic Enhancement leveling reached Stormstrike at 40; in Forever it is the 16-point talent, so it lands at 25. Thundering Strikes (now crit with everything) first, Mental Dexterity (AP from Intellect, and the Flurry prerequisite), Shamanistic Focus at 20 for cheap shocks, Elemental Weapons, Stormstrike, Flurry, Improved Stormstrike (mana regen), Mental Quickness, Maelstrom Weapon (instant Lightning Bolts) and Rage of the Farseer at 40. Then Concussion/Reverberation for shocks and Tidal Mastery/Tidal Focus for hit. Ends in the Forever Enhancement raid build.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Thundering Strikes** — +5% crit with all spells and attacks — better than Classic's melee-only version, so it stays first.
2. **Mental Dexterity** — New: AP from Intellect (33%/rank read — scaling unclear) and the Flurry prerequisite.
3. **Shamanistic Focus** — New 1-pointer at 20: shocks and Lightning Shield cost 45% less — mana was the Classic Enhancement leveling problem.
4. **Elemental Weapons** — Windfury Weapon +13%/rank (est. 40%) from 30 when Windfury is learned; Rockbiter before.
5. **Stormstrike** — 16-pt at 25 (Classic: 40): weapon strike + 20% Nature damage taken for shocks.
6. **Flurry** — Haste after crits (demo rank 1 = 5%; est. 25%).
7. **Improved Stormstrike** — New: 50% chance for 50% mana regen while casting after Stormstrike — the sustain talent.
8. **Maelstrom Weapon** — New: melee hits stack a cheaper/faster Lightning Bolt (5 stacks) — instant bolts to open or finish.
9. **Rage of the Farseer** — Capstone at 40: +30% attack and cast speed for 25s every 3 minutes.
10. **Tidal Focus** — +5% hit with everything (est.) — last because it needs 5 Restoration filler points first.

## Leveling rotation (fight lab)

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with a slow two-hander: Lightning Shield (Water Shield when talented and mana is low) and the weapon imbue up (Rockbiter's attack power until 30, Windfury's extra attacks from 30), Searing Totem dropped at the pull, a Lightning Bolt while the mob walks in, then Stormstrike, Earth Shock (buffed by Stormstrike), Flame Shock while mana allows and instant Maelstrom Lightning Bolts between white swings; Stoneclaw Totem and a Lesser Healing Wave when low, Nature's Swiftness + Healing Wave in an emergency; a death with Reincarnation ready costs 30 s instead of 90. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, heals back to 80% health, water below 35% mana.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-20, on top of the priority below): Lightning Bolt only when the mob is below 35% health; drink below 15% mana; eat below 80% health. Together +11.7% time per kill saved for `leveling-enhancement` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
