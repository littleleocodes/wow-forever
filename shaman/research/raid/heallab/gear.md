## Assumptions

- The lab's assumed gear (tables.HEALER_GEAR, shaman): intellect 250, spirit 150, healing 360, mp5 20, crit_gear 0.02; raid buffs, Kings and talents on top (engine.Healer.setup_stats), as for every lab report.
- Perturbations: healing ±50, intellect ±25, spirit ±25, mp5 ±8, crit_gear ±0.02; weights are local slopes, refreshed once at the chosen set.
- Item stats are the database's level-60 scaling; generic crit rating counts for heals (Forever pays gear crit to every attack, sim/core/forever_rules.go); spell damage without healing power heals for nothing (the simulator's HealingPower stat).
- Weapon skills are Classic's (`PROFICIENCY`); items needing a profession or a faction are left out; rings and trinkets are two different items, not of one unique-equip category.
