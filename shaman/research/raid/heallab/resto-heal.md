## Mechanics simulated

- **Nature's Swiftness**: instant Healing Wave on a tank under 35%, every 3 min
- **Mana Tide Totem**: 290 mana every 3 s for 12 s when mana is under 50%, every 5 min
- **Ancestral Healing**: every critical heal: +8/17/25% armor on the target for 15 s (the engine's armor buff is a fixed +25%, so ranks 1-2 apply it on 32%/68% of crits: the same average armor)
- **Water Shield**: 3 globes, cast before the pull: a critical direct heal spends one for 2% of maximum mana (one globe every 3.5 s; client 70425: any spell crit, and the lab casts only heals); recast (a global cooldown, no mana) when the globes run out and the tank is not in danger (2026-10-17: the lab had unlimited globes every 3 s)
- **Totemic Focus**: totem mana only: nothing for heals
- **Elemental Focus**: clearcasting from damage spells, for the next damage spell: nothing for heals
- **Riptide**: a 15 s HoT (805 at rank 3, a tick every 3 s) on a 12 s cooldown for 231 mana (70753: no initial heal, about 40% less mana, 6 s before): the tank's HoT, kept up while the cooldown allows; a Chain Heal cast on a target with your Riptide heals 25% more (every jump: the cast's effectiveness), and Chain Heal starts on a hurt member with your Riptide
- **Healing Stream Totem**: placed at the pull: 11 (+Restorative Totems) every 2 s on the group

Everything else the build takes is either a plain tooltip number the lab applies (healing, mana cost, crit, cast time, regen, stats) or not simulated.
