Talents, gear and rotation were tuned in turns until none of them moved; every winner was then checked against every build one or two talent moves away and raced against all our builds of its spec over 200,000 fights. The community simulator's model of the beta (ElliotWood/Forever 40be98e with this project's patches), not in-game measurements. No search over this many builds can prove a global optimum; what the checks below rule out is a better build near the winner, and the independent runs show how often the search reaches the same top from different starts.
