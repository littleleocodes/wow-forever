## Summary

The maximum-healing build: Improved Healing Wave, Tidal Focus/Mastery, Purification, Healing Way, Nature's Swiftness and Mana Tide Totem, with Ancestral Knowledge from Enhancement for mana. Chain Heal and Healing Wave carry the raid; support totems are not improved.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Purification** — +10% healing.
2. **Mana Tide Totem** — Group mana every 5 minutes.
3. **Nature's Swiftness** — Instant Healing Wave / Chain Heal every 3 minutes.
4. **Tidal Mastery** — +5% healing crit (feeds Ancestral Healing).
5. **Improved Healing Wave** — -0.5s Healing Wave cast.
6. **Healing Way** — Healing Wave stacks +18% healing on the tank.
7. **Tidal Focus** — -5% healing mana cost.
8. **Totemic Focus** — -25% totem mana cost.
9. **Ancestral Healing** — +25% armor on the target after a crit heal.
10. **Healing Focus** — 70% pushback resistance.
11. **Restorative Totems** — +25% Healing Stream/Mana Spring; prerequisite for Mana Tide.
12. **Ancestral Knowledge** — +5% Intellect/mana.
13. **Totemic Mastery** — 30-yard totem radius.

## Notes

- Chain Heal is the raid heal, Healing Wave (with Healing Way stacks) the tank heal, Lesser Healing Wave the emergency heal.
- Alternative 'Enhancing Totems' and 'Improved Weapon Totems' support builds move points into Enhancement for melee groups (see Enhancement build).
