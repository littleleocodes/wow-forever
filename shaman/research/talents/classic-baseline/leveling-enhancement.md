## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Two-Handed Axes and Maces** — Big slow two-hander + Windfury Weapon from 30 = huge white crits.
2. **Flurry** — Haste after crits at 29.
3. **Weapon Mastery** — +10% weapon damage.
4. **Stormstrike** — The 40 milestone; extra Nature damage from shocks.
