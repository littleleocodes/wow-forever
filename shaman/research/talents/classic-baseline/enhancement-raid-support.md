## Summary

Stormstrike, Flurry and Weapon Mastery for melee damage, Improved Weapon Totems (Windfury +30% AP bonus) and Enhancing Totems for the melee group, Restorative Totems and Totemic Focus from Restoration. Mostly played to supercharge a warrior/rogue group with Windfury and Strength of Earth.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Improved Weapon Totems** — Windfury Totem +30% attack power bonus for the melee group — the raid reason for Enhancement.
2. **Enhancing Totems** — Strength of Earth / Grace of Air +15%.
3. **Stormstrike** — Instant weapon attack that makes the next 2 sources of Nature damage on the target deal +20% — feeds your own shocks.
4. **Flurry** — 30% haste after crits.
5. **Weapon Mastery** — +10% weapon damage.
6. **Thundering Strikes** — +5% melee crit.
7. **Elemental Weapons** — +40% Rockbiter/Flametongue/Frostbrand; prerequisite for Stormstrike.
8. **Two-Handed Axes and Maces** — Enables two-handers (Nightfall!).
9. **Nature's Guidance** — +3% hit (melee and spell).
10. **Restorative Totems** — Mana Spring/Healing Stream +25% for the group.
11. **Ancestral Knowledge** — Mana.

## Notes

- Nightfall (two-handed sword from Blackrock Spire) with Two-Handed Axes and Maces is the classic 'support Enhancement' weapon: its proc gives +15% spell damage taken.
- Personal DPS is well below rogues/warriors; the value is the totems.
