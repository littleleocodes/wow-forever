## Summary

The classic PvP shaman: Lightning Mastery burst, Elemental Fury crits, Eye of the Storm, Earth's Grasp for totem control, plus Nature's Swiftness (instant Chain Lightning or Healing Wave) and Tidal Mastery from Restoration. Elemental Mastery is not reachable.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Nature's Swiftness** — Instant Chain Lightning burst or instant Healing Wave.
2. **Lightning Mastery** — -1.0s casts.
3. **Elemental Fury** — Double crit damage.
4. **Call of Thunder** — +6% crit.
5. **Eye of the Storm** — Casting through melee damage.
6. **Earth's Grasp** — Stoneclaw health and Earthbind radius.
7. **Tidal Mastery** — +5% crit on lightning and heals.
8. **Nature's Guidance** — +3% hit.
