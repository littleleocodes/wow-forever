# Rogue: start here

[All classes](../START-HERE.md)

Classic's pure melee DPS: Combat swords or daggers in raids, Seal Fate / Hemorrhage builds in PvP. Forever removes the weapon specializations and Improved Backstab (replaced by Hack and Slash / Puncturing Wounds), adds Restless Blades to Combat, and turns Assassination into a real Mutilate/Venom spec. Playable by 8 races including Skyborne.
