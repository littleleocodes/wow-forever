## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Boot Knife**, **Preparation** are talents this build does not take: where a line below names them, that part does not apply. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** Daggers in both hands, **Instant Poison** on both. Stealth up behind the mob at full energy: **Poised** (level 18) makes your opener 10 energy cheaper. **Premeditation** for two free combo points.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Open with **Ambush** in short fights; **Garrote** once its bleed outlasts the fight (with the Subtlety build the fight lab opens with **Ambush** on most pulls up to level 25 and at 35, with **Garrote** at 30 and on every pull from 40; the Combat-Subtlety build opens with **Ambush** from level 18, with **Garrote** on about half the pulls at 23-25, and with **Ambush** on every pull from 30).
2. **Ghostly Strike** whenever it is ready (it also makes you dodge more). With **Dirty Deeds**, follow it with a **Garrote** when none is bleeding and the mob has more than half its health.
3. **Shank** whenever it is ready (level 24), unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum; with **Boot Knife** also to build combo points.
4. **Sinister Strike** to build combo points; once you have **Hemorrhage**, it replaces **Sinister Strike**.
5. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder; earlier when it would kill the mob.
6. Do not interrupt a caster mob: let it cast and walk in.
7. **Evasion** below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health; if it is on cooldown, **Preparation** resets it.

**Between fights:** Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40%.

## Why this rotation

Combat-Subtlety levels on Subtlety's openers and Combat's finishers: **Premeditation** (24) and a stealth opener, **Ambush** on most pulls, start the fight at up to 4 points, **Sinister Strike** builds until **Hemorrhage** (32) takes over, **Ghostly Strike** (25) goes in on its cooldown, and **Eviscerate** finishes.

**Why it works**

- From stealth, **Premeditation** (level 24) adds 2 points (a 2-minute cooldown, so about one pull in six or seven) and **Initiative** (1 rank at 23, all 3 by 29) makes your opener add a second one: up to 4 points before your first builder.
- Open with **Ambush** from level 18: **Opportunity** (5 ranks by 18) and **Improved Ambush** (19, 30-31) make it hit harder and crit more, and the fight lab opens with it on every pull at 20 and from 30. In between, until **Improved Ambush** takes its last two points, **Garrote** rank 2 (22) outweighs **Ambush** on the mobs that will take a while to kill: at 23-25 the fight lab opens with **Garrote** on about half the pulls. **Garrote** is also the opener before 18 (14-17), and the fight lab casts it after a **Ghostly Strike**, whose next **Garrote** needs no stealth (**Dirty Deeds** lets it land from the front), when no bleed is on the mob and it has more than half its health.
- **Sinister Strike** builds to 31. **Hemorrhage** (32: it needs 20 points in Subtlety and 3 ranks of **Serrated Blades**) takes over: 35 energy for 145% weapon damage with a dagger, and your **Garrote** bleeds 15% harder for 15 seconds after it. **Efficient Strikes** (44-45) cut its cost by 5.
- **Ghostly Strike** (level 25) goes in on its 20-second cooldown: 180% weapon damage with a dagger, 15% more dodge for 7 seconds. With **Setup** (12-14, 45% at 3 ranks) a dodge can add a combo point.
- At 5 points, wait for the energy for **Eviscerate** rather than press a builder whose point would be lost, and cast it earlier when it would kill the mob. **Coup de Grace** (33-35) makes it hit 20% harder.
- Pull the next mob within 20 seconds of a kill: **Remorseless Attacks** (26-27) gives your next **Ambush**, **Sinister Strike** or **Ghostly Strike** 40% more crit chance; **Hemorrhage** does not use it.

**Caveats**

- Skip **Slice and Dice** (about 6% faster per kill without it). **Kidney Shot** is not in the list: the tuned rules never cast it (under 1% faster per kill). Keep it for an add or a pull that goes wrong.
- Keep a dagger in your main hand: **Ambush** needs one, and **Ghostly Strike** and **Hemorrhage** hit much harder with it.
- This build has no Riposte, Preparation, Death Mark, Boot Knife or Relentless Strikes: the list drops the lines that lead with them, and where it still names Boot Knife or Preparation, that part does not apply. **Evasion** is your only cooldown when a pull goes wrong.
- Eat only below 40% health and bandage below 60%: the tuned rules measured eating that late about 4.3% faster per kill.
- The tuned rules never **Kick** (2026-11-15: about 0.6% to 0.9% faster per kill without it): a caster mob that stays back walks in once it has cast. Keep **Kick** for a caster that hits too hard.

**When to change it**

- Below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health, press **Evasion** (50% more dodge for 15 seconds, and with **Setup** sometimes a combo point).
- If a pull goes wrong, **Vanish** gets you out; the fight lab does not model adds or escapes.
