## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Preparation** is a talent this build does not take: skip the lines that use it. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** Daggers in both hands: **Instant Poison** on your main hand, **Deadly Poison** on your off hand. Stealth up behind the mob. **Premeditation** for two free combo points.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Open with **Ambush** while fights are short; from the 30s on, **Garrote** (its bleed ignores armor and the mob lives through it).
2. **Ghostly Strike** whenever it is ready (it also makes you dodge more).
3. **Sinister Strike** to build combo points.
4. Once you have **Hemorrhage**, it replaces **Sinister Strike** as the builder.
5. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder; earlier when it would kill the mob.
6. **Riposte** after you parry.
7. Do not **Kick** a caster mob: let it cast and walk in.
8. **Evasion** when your health gets low.
9. If **Evasion** is on cooldown when you need it, **Preparation** resets it.

**Between fights:** Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40%.

## Why this rotation

Combat-Subtlety levels on Subtlety's openers and Combat's defenses: **Premeditation** and **Garrote** start the fight at up to 4 points, **Hemorrhage** builds, **Eviscerate** finishes, and **Setup**, **Ghostly Strike** and **Riposte** turn the mob's swings into combo points and free hits.

**Why it works**

- From stealth, **Premeditation** adds 2 points (a 2-minute cooldown, so about every fifth pull) and **Initiative** makes your opener add a second one: up to 4 points before your first builder.
- Open with **Ambush** at low levels, where fights are short and its burst wins (**Improved Ambush** makes it crit more); once fights last longer, **Garrote** is worth more, because its bleed ignores armor.
- **Hemorrhage** is your builder from level 32: 35 energy for 145% weapon damage with a dagger in your main hand (**Sinister Strike** before that).
- **Ghostly Strike** (level 20) goes in on its 20-second cooldown: 180% weapon damage with a dagger and 15% more dodge for 7 seconds. With **Setup** (levels 15-17) every dodge adds a combo point; the fight lab measured **Setup** the most valuable talent of this build, about 2 seconds per kill at 60.
- At 5 points, wait for the energy for **Eviscerate** rather than press a builder whose point would be lost, and cast it earlier when it would kill the mob. At 5 points **Relentless Strikes** always refunds 25 energy and **Ruthlessness** often adds a point back.
- **Riposte** after a parry is your cheapest hit: 10 energy for 150% weapon damage and a 6-second disarm.

**Caveats**

- Skip **Slice and Dice**: the fight lab (re-tuned 2026-10-15) measured leveling without it about 3% faster per kill. **Kidney Shot** is not in the list: the tuned rules never cast it (about 1% faster per kill). Keep it for an add or a pull that goes wrong.
- Keep a dagger in your main hand: **Ambush** needs one, and **Hemorrhage** and **Ghostly Strike** hit much harder with it.
- This build has no **Preparation**: when **Evasion** is on cooldown, there is no second one.
- Eat only below 40% health and bandage below 60%: the fight lab measured eating that late about 4.4% faster than eating at 65%.
- The tuned rules never **Kick** (about 0.5% faster per kill): a caster mob that stays back walks in once it has cast.

**When to change it**

- Below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health, press **Evasion** (50% more dodge for 15 seconds, and with **Setup** more combo points).
- If a pull goes wrong, **Vanish** gets you out; the fight lab does not model adds or escapes.
