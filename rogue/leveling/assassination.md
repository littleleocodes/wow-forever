## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Adrenaline Rush**, **Blade Flurry** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** **Instant Poison** on your main hand, **Deadly Poison** on your off hand. Stealth up behind the mob.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Open with **Garrote**.
2. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
3. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**; earlier when it would kill the mob.
4. **Cold Blood** right before an **Eviscerate** at 5 points.
5. **Riposte** after you parry.
6. **Blade Flurry** and **Adrenaline Rush** at the start of a fight.
7. Do not interrupt a caster mob: let it cast and walk in.
8. **Evasion** below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health.

**Between fights:** Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40%.

## Why this rotation

Assassination levels with two daggers: **Garrote** from stealth, **Sinister Strike** to 5 points until **Mutilate** (level 32 in this build) takes over as the builder, then **Eviscerate**. **Mutilate**, **Seal Fate** and the poison talents are what set it apart.

**Why it works**

- A mob you fight alone turns to face you after the opener, and **Backstab** needs you behind it, so the builder is **Sinister Strike** until you have **Mutilate**.
- **Mutilate** hits with both daggers for 75% weapon damage plus a flat amount each, 20% more on a poisoned mob, and gives 2 combo points for 60 energy: wait for the energy rather than fall back to **Sinister Strike**. The fight lab casts it about 3 times per kill at 60 and measured it the most valuable talent of this build, about 1.7 seconds per kill.
- Open with **Garrote** from stealth: a combo point and an 18-second bleed. The tuned rules never open with **Cheap Shot** (about 10% faster per kill): it gives 2 points and a stun but no damage, for 60 energy.
- **Eviscerate** at 5 points, or earlier when it would kill the mob: points left on a dead mob are lost. Press **Cold Blood** (level 27) right before an **Eviscerate** at 5 points; otherwise your next builder uses it up.
- Keep **Instant Poison** on the main hand and **Deadly Poison** on the off hand: every weapon strike can apply them, **Deadly Poison** turns on the **Mutilate** bonus, and **Improved Poisons** and **Vile Poisons** make both stronger.
- **Seal Fate** comes last (levels 56-60): every builder crit then adds a second point, and **Malice**, **Lethality** and **Remorseless Attacks** make those crits frequent. Pull the next mob within 20 seconds of a kill so the 40% crit chance from **Remorseless Attacks** is not wasted.
- Skip **Slice and Dice**: fights end before it pays back (about 1.3% faster per kill without it).

**Caveats**

- Keep daggers in both hands: **Mutilate** needs them.
- **Venom** is not in the list: every way the fight lab tried to keep it up was slower than spending the points on **Eviscerate**.
- **Kidney Shot** is not in the list: the tuned rules never cast it (about 1.3% faster per kill). Keep it for an add or a pull that goes wrong.
- **Kick** a caster mob's spell: with daggers the tuned rules keep it.
- Without **Blade Flurry** or **Adrenaline Rush**, **Evasion** is your only cooldown when a pull goes wrong.
- Eat only below 40% health and bandage below 60%: the tuned rules measured eating that late about 4.2% faster per kill.

**When to change it**

- Below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health, press **Evasion** (50% more dodge for 15 seconds).
- In a dungeon, where the tank holds the mob, stand behind it: the raid list builds with **Backstab** and **Mutilate**; the fight lab only plays solo.
