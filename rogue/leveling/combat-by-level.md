# Combat Rogue — leveling, level by level

[Rogue](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Combat](combat.md) > Level by level

What a Combat rogue presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Combat with swords is the rogue's fastest leveling spec in the fight lab, and the simplest: build combo points with **Sinister Strike**, spend them on **Eviscerate**, open from stealth once you have an opener worth the walk. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 21/30/0 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/rogue/60/02531320201101-321033001201232025--12)), the one on the [Combat leveling page](combat.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | **Sinister Strike** builds combo points, **Eviscerate** spends them. |
| 6-7 | **Gouge** stuns the mob for a few seconds: a breather. |
| 8-9 | **Evasion**: for half a minute the mob can barely hit you. |
| 10-13 | Talent points start with **Coup de Grace**. Nothing new in the fight itself. |
| 14-20 | **Garrote**: open from stealth with a bleed. From here every fight starts in **Stealth**. |
| 21-23 | **Riposte** (talent) lights up after you parry: a free strike that disarms. |
| 24-29 | **Shank**, a strike on a cooldown that also gives energy. Press it whenever it is ready. |
| 30-39 | Nothing new to press. **Deadly Poison** goes on your off hand; **Hack and Slash** adds free swings. |
| 40-54 | The rotation is complete. The remaining points go to crit and energy; buy every new rank. |
| 55-60 | **Cold Blood** (talent): a guaranteed critical **Eviscerate** every 3 minutes. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; poisons, food, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the rarer tools. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup: the [rogue keybinds page](keybinds.md).

## Levels 1-5: Sinister Strike and Eviscerate

**The trainer teaches:** **Eviscerate** (1), **Sinister Strike** (1), Stealth (1), Backstab (4), Pick Pocket (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Sinister Strike; R = Eviscerate.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, R Eviscerate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
2. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

- **Backstab** (4) is for daggers. This build plays swords: never Backstab.
- **Stealth** (1) has nothing to open with yet and is not worth the walk: mobs die in a few hits. It gets its key at 14, with **Garrote**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 9 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Sinister Strike 1.4, Melee 1.0, Melee (off-hand) 0.2.*

**The trainer teaches:** Sap (10), Slice and Dice (10), Sprint (10), Kick (12).

**Talents:** 10-12 Coup de Grace, 13 Lightning Reflexes.

**Keys to set:** 2 = Sap; 4 = Sprint; Shift+E = Kick (at 12).

**What to press**, top to bottom:

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather.

- **Slice and Dice** (10): leave it off your bar. The tuned rotation never casts it: a mob dies before the extra swings pay for the combo points.
- **Sap** (10): a second mob next to your target, from stealth, before the pull.
- **Kick** (12): the tuned rotation never interrupts. A caster that stands and casts walks to you when you close in, and the kill is faster without the detour.
- **Coup de Grace** (10-12) makes every **Eviscerate** hit harder. **Precision** (15-17) makes your hits land.

## Between fights

- Bandage when you are below 60% of your health, eat when you are below 40%. That is the tuned rule.
- Reapply your poisons when they run out, and **Stealth** before each pull from 14.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Sinister Strike** and **Eviscerate** have many ranks.

## Other ways to level this class

- The Combat-Subtlety split is about 1% behind in the fight lab and plays **Ghostly Strike** and the stealth openers; Assassination with daggers plays **Mutilate** from 40. Both have pages in this folder.
