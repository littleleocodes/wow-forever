# Subtlety Rogue — leveling, level by level

[Rogue](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Subtlety](subtlety.md) > Level by level

What a Subtlety rogue presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Subtlety levels with a dagger in each hand on its openers: **Premeditation** and a stealth opener put combo points on the mob before your first builder, **Ghostly Strike** and **Hemorrhage**, the spec's signature strikes, build the rest, and from 40 **Death Mark** stores part of your damage and deals it again. The three come as early as the tree allows: **Ghostly Strike** at 25, **Hemorrhage** at 30, **Death Mark** at 40. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 0/20/31 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/rogue/60/-32230310010122-335201131301001151-12)), the one on the [Subtlety leveling page](subtlety.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | **Sinister Strike** builds combo points, **Eviscerate** spends them. |
| 6-7 | **Gouge** stuns the mob for a few seconds: a breather. |
| 8-9 | **Evasion**: for half a minute the mob can barely hit you. |
| 10-11 | Talent points start with **Opportunity**: your openers hit harder. Nothing new in the fight itself. |
| 12-13 | **Kick** interrupts a caster mob's spell. This rotation uses it. |
| 14-17 | **Garrote**: open from stealth with a bleed. From here every fight starts in **Stealth**. |
| 18-21 | **Ambush** from stealth: one big hit with your dagger, your usual opener for now. |
| 22-23 | **Garrote** rank 2: from here its bleed beats **Ambush** on most mobs. |
| 24 | **Premeditation** (talent): 2 free combo points before the opener. **Shank** strikes on a cooldown and gives energy. |
| 25-29 | **Ghostly Strike** (talent) on its 20-second cooldown: a big dagger strike and more dodge. A **Garrote** can follow it. |
| 30-34 | **Hemorrhage** (talent) takes over from **Sinister Strike** as your builder. |
| 35-39 | **Boot Knife** (talent): a landed **Shank** can add 2 combo points, so it is a builder too. |
| 40-60 | **Death Mark** (talent): a finisher that stores part of your damage for 6 seconds, then deals it. Cast it early. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; poisons, food, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the rarer tools. Every spell this spec shares with Combat sits on the Combat key, so your hands do not relearn anything when you switch specs. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the macros: the [rogue keybinds page](keybinds.md).

## Levels 1-5: Sinister Strike and Eviscerate

**The trainer teaches:** **Eviscerate** (1), **Sinister Strike** (1), Stealth (1), Backstab (4), Pick Pocket (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Sinister Strike; R = Eviscerate.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, R Eviscerate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
2. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

- Carry a dagger in each hand from the start: **Ambush** (18) needs one in your main hand, and **Ghostly Strike** and **Hemorrhage** hit much harder with it.
- **Backstab** (4) needs the mob's back, and a mob you fight alone turns to face you after your first hit: never Backstab.
- **Stealth** (1) has nothing to open with yet and is not worth the walk: mobs die in a few hits. It gets its key at 14, with **Garrote**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 10 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Sinister Strike 1.6, Melee 1.3, Melee (off-hand) 1.0, Eviscerate 0.2.*

## Levels 14-17: Garrote from stealth

**The trainer teaches:** Expose Armor (14), **Garrote** (14), Feint (16).

**Talents:** 14 Setup, 15-16 Dirty Deeds, 17 Master of Deception.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, 1 Garrote, Shift+E Kick, Shift+1 Stealth.

2. Open with **Garrote**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 14. Presses per kill at level 14: Melee 6.4, Melee (off-hand) 5.9, Sinister Strike 2.5, Garrote 1.0, Eviscerate 0.6, Kick 0.1.*

## Levels 18-21: Ambush

**The trainer teaches:** **Ambush** (18), Poised (18), Crippling Poison (20), Instant Poison (20), Rupture (20).

**New ranks to buy:** Gouge at 18; Stealth at 20.

**Talents:** 18-19 Master of Deception, 20-21 Serrated Blades.

**Keys to set:** C = Ambush.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, 1 Garrote, C Ambush, Shift+E Kick, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. Open with **Ambush**. On a mob that will take a while to kill (a lot of health or armor), **Garrote** instead: its bleed ignores armor but pays only while the mob lives.
3. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
4. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
5. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather.

- **Ambush** needs a dagger in your main hand and you behind the mob.
- **Poised** (18) makes the opener 10 energy cheaper.
- **Rupture** (20) is never cast: the points are worth more as **Eviscerate**.
- **Serrated Blades** (20-22): your attacks ignore part of the mob's armor.
- Put **Instant Poison** on your main hand once you have it (20).

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 18, 19 at level 20. Presses per kill at level 18: Melee 6.4, Melee (off-hand) 5.8, Sinister Strike 2.7, Eviscerate 0.7, Ambush 0.7, Garrote 0.3, Kick 0.2.*

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.

- **Stealth** and **Premeditation** are off the global cooldown: Stealth, Premeditation, then the opener. Use the points within 20 seconds.
- **Mind-numbing Poison** (24) is for casters in groups.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 20 at level 24. Presses per kill at level 24: Melee 6.7, Melee (off-hand) 6.3, Sinister Strike 3.2, Shank 1.5, Garrote 0.9, Eviscerate 0.9, Kick 0.3, Premeditation 0.2, Ambush 0.1.*

3. Open with **Garrote**.
4. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.

2. **Premeditation** whenever it is ready (every 2 minutes): 2 combo points on the mob before you open.
3. Open with **Garrote**.
4. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
5. **Death Mark** as soon as you have a combo point, while the mob has more than 40% of its health: right after the opener, and again whenever it is ready while the mob is still above 40%.
6. **Shank** whenever it is ready, unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum. With 3 combo points or fewer, press it even when the energy would go over: **Boot Knife** may add 2.
7. **Ghostly Strike** whenever it is ready, unless you have 5 points. Right after it, **Garrote** if none is bleeding on the mob and it has more than half its health: **Ghostly Strike** lets **Garrote** go without **Stealth**, and **Dirty Deeds** lets it go from the front.
8. **Hemorrhage** until you have 5 combo points.
9. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather. **Vanish** is the emergency exit. If **Evasion** is on cooldown, **Preparation** resets it.

## Between fights

- Bandage when you are below 60% of your health, eat when you are below 40%. That is the tuned rule.
- Reapply your poisons when they run out, and **Stealth** before each pull from 14.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Garrote**, **Ambush** and **Eviscerate** have many ranks.
