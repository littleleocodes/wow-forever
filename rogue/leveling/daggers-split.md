**Signature spells:** 75% of the damage at level 60 in the fight lab comes from melee, Backstab, Mutilate, Sinister Strike, Eviscerate (the spec's rule: at least 60%).

## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Mutilate**, **Venom** are talents this build does not take: skip the lines that use them. The fight lab played the build without them.

**Before the fight:** Daggers in both hands: **Instant Poison** on your main hand, **Deadly Poison** on your off hand. Stealth up behind the mob at full energy: **Poised** (level 18) makes your opener 10 energy cheaper.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Open with **Garrote**.
2. **Shank** whenever it is ready (level 24), unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum; with **Venom** it also strengthens your poisons for 6 seconds.
3. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points; once your build has **Mutilate** (from about level 40), it replaces **Sinister Strike**: 2 points a press, so wait for its 60 energy. The mob faces you after the opener, so **Backstab** is out.
4. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**; earlier when it would kill the mob.
5. **Cold Blood** right before an **Eviscerate** at 5 points.
6. **Riposte** after you parry.
7. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
8. **Evasion** below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health.

**Between fights:** Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40%.

## Why this rotation

Daggers (split) plays the dagger leveling list: **Garrote** from stealth, **Sinister Strike** to 5 points until **Mutilate** (level 30) takes over, then **Eviscerate**. Its last Subtlety points buy **Setup** and **Initiative**, which add combo points from dodges and from the opener.

**Why it works**

- A mob you fight alone turns to face you after the opener, and **Backstab** needs you behind it, so the builder is **Sinister Strike** until you have **Mutilate**.
- **Mutilate** hits with both daggers and gives 2 combo points for 60 energy, 20% more damage on a mob with your **Deadly Poison**: wait for the energy rather than fall back to **Sinister Strike**. The fight lab measured it about 1.2 seconds per kill at 60.
- Open with **Garrote** from stealth: a combo point (2 with **Initiative**) and an 18-second bleed. The tuned rules never open with **Cheap Shot** (about 10% faster per kill): a stun deals no damage.
- **Setup** (levels 54-56) adds a combo point every time you dodge; the fight lab measured it the most valuable talent of this build at 60, about 2.4 seconds per kill. **Riposte** after a parry is your cheapest hit (10 energy for 150% weapon damage).
- **Eviscerate** at 5 points, or earlier when it would kill the mob. At 5 points **Relentless Strikes** always refunds 25 energy and **Ruthlessness** often adds a point back; press **Cold Blood** right before an **Eviscerate**, since your next builder would use it up otherwise.
- Skip **Slice and Dice** (about 1.3% faster per kill without it). **Kidney Shot** is not in the list: the tuned rules never cast it (about 1.3% faster per kill). Keep it for an add or a pull that goes wrong. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
- Pull the next mob within 20 seconds of a kill: **Remorseless Attacks** gives your next builder 40% more crit chance.

**Caveats**

- **Master of Deception**, **Improved Ambush** and **Elusiveness** are only there to reach **Setup** and **Initiative**: this list never opens with **Ambush**.
- Keep daggers in both hands: **Mutilate** needs them.
- This build has no **Blade Flurry** or **Adrenaline Rush**: **Evasion** is your only cooldown for a bad pull.
- Eat only below 40% health and bandage below 60%: the tuned rules measured eating that late about 4.2% faster per kill.

**When to change it**

- Below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health, press **Evasion** (50% more dodge for 15 seconds, and with **Setup** more combo points).
- In a dungeon, where the tank holds the mob, stand behind it and build with **Backstab**; the fight lab only plays solo.

*This guide was written for the rotation of 2026-10-26; the list above was re-tuned since, so follow the list where the two differ.*
