## How to play

**Not in this build:** **Adrenaline Rush** is a talent this build does not take: skip the lines that use it. The fight lab played the build without it.

**Before the fight:** **Instant Poison** on your main hand, **Deadly Poison** on your off hand. Stealth up behind the mob.

**In the fight**, top to bottom:

1. Open with **Cheap Shot** (**Garrote** before level 26).
2. **Slice and Dice** at 1-2 combo points when it is not up.
3. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
4. At 5 points: **Eviscerate** (**Kidney Shot** instead if you are below half health).
5. **Riposte** after you parry.
6. **Adrenaline Rush** at the start of a fight; **Evasion** when your health gets low.

**Between fights:** Bandage below 60% health, eat below half.

## Why this rotation

Daggers (split) plays the dagger leveling list: **Garrote** from stealth, **Sinister Strike** to 5 points, **Eviscerate**. Its Subtlety points buy **Setup** and **Initiative**, which add combo points from dodges and from the opener.

**Why it works**

- A mob you fight alone turns to face you after the opener, and **Backstab** needs you behind it, so **Sinister Strike** is your builder even with daggers.
- Open with **Garrote** from stealth: with **Initiative** it gives 2 points, plus an 18-second bleed. The fight lab measured a **Cheap Shot** opener about 10% slower per kill: a stun deals no damage.
- **Setup** adds a combo point every time you dodge; the fight lab measured it the most valuable talent of this build, about 1.8 seconds per kill at 60. **Riposte** after a parry is your cheapest hit (10 energy for 150% weapon damage).
- **Eviscerate** at 5 points, or earlier when it would kill the mob. At 5 points **Relentless Strikes** always refunds 25 energy and **Ruthlessness** often adds a point back; press **Cold Blood** right before an **Eviscerate**, since your next **Sinister Strike** would use it up otherwise.
- Skip **Slice and Dice** and **Kidney Shot**: the fight lab measured leveling without them about 3% and 1% faster per kill.
- Pull the next mob within 20 seconds of a kill: **Remorseless Attacks** gives your next **Sinister Strike** 40% more crit chance.

**Caveats**

- **Improved Ambush**, **Camouflage** and **Master of Deception** are only there to reach **Initiative**: this list never opens with **Ambush**, and the fight lab measured all three at zero.
- This build has no **Blade Flurry** or **Adrenaline Rush**: **Evasion** is your only cooldown for a bad pull.
- Eat only below 40% health and bandage below 60%: the fight lab measured eating that late about 4% faster than eating at 65%.

**When to change it**

- Below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health, press **Evasion** (50% more dodge for 15 seconds, and with **Setup** more combo points).
- In a dungeon, where the tank holds the mob, stand behind it and build with **Backstab**; the fight lab only plays solo.
