# Combat-Subtlety Rogue — leveling, level by level

[Rogue](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Combat-Subtlety](combat-subtlety.md) > Level by level

What a Combat-Subtlety rogue presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. This split plays Subtlety's rotation with a dagger in each hand: **Premeditation** and a stealth opener, **Ambush** on most pulls, put combo points on the mob before your first builder, **Ghostly Strike**, its signature strike, goes in on its cooldown, and Combat's talents add hit, dodge and cheaper finishers. **Sinister Strike** stays your builder until **Hemorrhage** takes over at 32. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 3/27/21 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/rogue/60/021-323023000201232022-3051030313010001-12)), the one on the [Combat-Subtlety leveling page](combat-subtlety.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | **Sinister Strike** builds combo points, **Eviscerate** spends them. |
| 6-7 | **Gouge** stuns the mob for a few seconds: a breather. |
| 8-9 | **Evasion**: for half a minute the mob can barely hit you. |
| 10-13 | Talent points start with **Opportunity**: your openers hit harder. Nothing new in the fight itself. |
| 14-17 | **Garrote**: open from stealth with a bleed. From here every fight starts in **Stealth**. |
| 18-23 | **Ambush** from stealth: one big hit with your dagger, your usual opener from here. |
| 24 | **Premeditation** (talent): 2 free combo points before the opener. **Shank** strikes on a cooldown and gives energy. |
| 25-29 | **Ghostly Strike** (talent) on its 20-second cooldown: a big dagger strike and more dodge. A **Garrote** can follow it. |
| 30-31 | **Improved Ambush** takes its last two points, and **Ambush** opens every pull again. **Instant Poison** stays on both weapons and **Kidney Shot** (30) stays off. |
| 32-39 | **Hemorrhage** (talent) takes over from **Sinister Strike** as your builder. |
| 40-60 | The rotation stays as it is until 60. The points go to Combat's energy and damage talents; buy every new rank. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; poisons, food, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the rarer tools. Every spell this spec shares with Combat sits on the Combat key, so your hands do not relearn anything when you switch specs. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the macros: the [rogue keybinds page](keybinds.md).

- The Combat layout for every spell the two share, and the Subtlety layout's keys for the rest: Mouse 4 holds **Ghostly Strike** (no **Riposte** in this build), C **Ambush** (no **Cold Blood**), Shift+Q **Premeditation**, and Q takes **Hemorrhage** at 32. **Garrote** keeps 1 as in every rogue layout: the opener before 18 and on a long fight at 22-29, and from 25 the bleed that follows **Ghostly Strike**. **Kick** stays on Shift+E, although this rotation never casts it.
- WoW switches bar 1 to a second page while you are in **Stealth** (the stealth bar, as in Classic). Put the same spells in the same slots on it, so every key does the same thing in and out of Stealth.
- Before each pull: Shift+1 (**Stealth**), Shift+Q (**Premeditation**, when it is ready), then C (**Ambush**) or 1 (**Garrote**: before 18, and at 22-29 on a mob that will take a while to kill). Stealth and Premeditation are off the global cooldown, so the three go one after the other.
- No key: the poisons, **Slice and Dice** and **Kidney Shot** (the tuned rotation never casts them), **Cheap Shot** and **Backstab** (not in this list), food, bandages, the mount and the Hearthstone.
- Levels 1-5 fight with Q and R. Q is a macro on the [keybinds page](keybinds.md) that also starts your swings, so the middle mouse button stays free; at 32 change its spell to **Hemorrhage**. Add a key when its spell arrives.

## Levels 1-5: Sinister Strike and Eviscerate

**The trainer teaches:** **Eviscerate** (1), **Sinister Strike** (1), Stealth (1), Backstab (4), Pick Pocket (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Sinister Strike; R = Eviscerate.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, R Eviscerate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
2. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

- Carry a dagger in each hand from the start: **Ambush** (18) needs one in your main hand, and **Ghostly Strike** (25) and **Hemorrhage** (32) hit much harder with it.
- **Backstab** (4) needs the mob's back, and a mob you fight alone turns to face you after your first hit: never Backstab.
- **Stealth** (1) has nothing to open with yet and is not worth the walk: mobs die in a few hits. It gets its key at 14, with **Garrote**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 9 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Sinister Strike 1.6, Melee 1.1, Melee (off-hand) 0.8, Eviscerate 0.2.*

## Levels 6-7: Gouge

**The trainer teaches:** Gouge (6).

**New ranks to buy:** Sinister Strike at 6.

**Keys to set:** E = Gouge.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

2. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 14 at level 6. Presses per kill at level 6: Melee 3.6, Sinister Strike 3.3, Melee (off-hand) 3.0, Eviscerate 0.3.*

**Keys to set:** F = Evasion.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

## Levels 10-13: Talents begin; Sprint and Sap

**The trainer teaches:** Sap (10), Slice and Dice (10), Sprint (10), Kick (12).

**Talents:** 10-11 Opportunity, 12-13 Setup.

**Keys to set:** 2 = Sap; 4 = Sprint; Shift+E = Kick (at 12).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
2. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather.

- **Slice and Dice** (10): leave it off your bar. The tuned rotation never casts it: a mob dies before the extra swings pay for the combo points.
- **Sap** (10): a second mob next to your target, from stealth, before the pull.
- **Kick** (12): the tuned rotation never interrupts. A caster that stands and casts walks to you when you close in, and the kill is faster without the detour.
- **Setup** (12-14): a dodge can add a combo point. Nothing to press.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 18 at level 10. Presses per kill at level 10: Melee 5.6, Melee (off-hand) 5.0, Sinister Strike 3.9, Eviscerate 0.6.*

## Levels 14-17: Garrote from stealth

**The trainer teaches:** Expose Armor (14), **Garrote** (14), Feint (16).

**New ranks to buy:** Eviscerate at 16; Sinister Strike at 14.

**Talents:** 14 Setup, 15 Dirty Deeds, 16-17 Opportunity.

**Keys to set:** 1 = Garrote; Shift+1 = Stealth.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, 1 Garrote, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. Open with **Garrote**.
3. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
4. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather.

- **Dirty Deeds** (15) makes **Garrote** 10 energy cheaper and lets it go from the front.
- **Expose Armor** (14) is never cast. **Opportunity** (16-18) takes its last three points: **Garrote** and **Ambush** hit 20% harder.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 14. Presses per kill at level 14: Melee 5.9, Melee (off-hand) 5.4, Sinister Strike 2.8, Garrote 1.0, Eviscerate 0.7.*

## Levels 18-23: Ambush

**The trainer teaches:** **Ambush** (18), Poised (18), Crippling Poison (20), Instant Poison (20), Rupture (20), Distract (22), Vanish (22).

**New ranks to buy:** Garrote at 22; Gouge at 18; Sinister Strike at 22; Stealth at 20.

**Talents:** 18 Opportunity, 19 Improved Ambush, 20-22 Serrated Blades, 23 Initiative.

**Keys to set:** C = Ambush; 3 = Vanish (at 22).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, 1 Garrote, C Ambush, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. Open with **Ambush**. From 22, with **Garrote** on a mob that will take a while to kill (a lot of health or armor).
3. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
4. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather.

- **Ambush** needs a dagger in your main hand and you behind the mob. **Improved Ambush** (19) raises its crit chance. The fight lab opens with **Ambush** on most pulls at 18 and on every pull at 20; **Garrote** rank 2 (22) makes the bleed bigger, and at 23 it opens with **Garrote** on about half the pulls.
- **Poised** (18) makes the opener 10 energy cheaper.
- **Rupture** (20) is never cast: the points are worth more as **Eviscerate**.
- **Serrated Blades** (20-22): your attacks ignore part of the mob's armor. **Initiative** (23): your opener may add a second combo point.
- Put **Instant Poison** on both weapons once you have it (20).
- **Vanish** (22) is the emergency exit: the mob loses you, and you walk away.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 18, 18 at level 20, 20 at level 23. Presses per kill at level 18: Melee 5.8, Melee (off-hand) 5.3, Sinister Strike 2.9, Ambush 0.8, Eviscerate 0.8, Garrote 0.2.*

## Level 24: Premeditation and Shank

**The trainer teaches:** Detect Traps (24), Mind-numbing Poison (24), **Shank** (24).

**New ranks to buy:** Eviscerate at 24.

**Talents:** 24 Premeditation.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 5 = Shank; Shift+Q = Premeditation.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, Mouse 5 Shank, 1 Garrote, 3 Vanish, C Ambush, Shift+Q Premeditation, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. **Premeditation** whenever it is ready (every 2 minutes): 2 combo points on the mob before you open.
3. Open with **Ambush**, or with **Garrote** on a mob that will take a while to kill (a lot of health or armor).
4. **Shank** whenever it is ready, unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum.
5. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
6. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather. **Vanish** is the emergency exit.

- **Stealth** and **Premeditation** are off the global cooldown: Stealth, Premeditation, then the opener. Use the points within 20 seconds.
- **Mind-numbing Poison** (24) is for casters in groups.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 24. Presses per kill at level 24: Melee 5.8, Melee (off-hand) 5.3, Sinister Strike 3.1, Shank 1.3, Eviscerate 0.9, Ambush 0.5, Garrote 0.5, Premeditation 0.1.*

## Levels 25-29: Ghostly Strike

**The trainer teaches:** Cheap Shot (26).

**New ranks to buy:** Ambush at 26; Kick at 26; Sap at 28.

**Talents:** 25 Ghostly Strike, 26-27 Remorseless Attacks, 28-29 Initiative.

**Keys to set:** Mouse 4 = Ghostly Strike.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, Mouse 5 Shank, Mouse 4 Ghostly Strike, 1 Garrote, 3 Vanish, C Ambush, Shift+Q Premeditation, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. **Premeditation** whenever it is ready (every 2 minutes): 2 combo points on the mob before you open.
3. Open with **Ambush**, or with **Garrote** on a mob that will take a while to kill (a lot of health or armor).
4. **Shank** whenever it is ready, unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum.
5. **Ghostly Strike** whenever it is ready, unless you have 5 points. Right after it, **Garrote** if none is bleeding on the mob and it has more than half its health: **Ghostly Strike** lets **Garrote** go without **Stealth**, and **Dirty Deeds** lets it go from the front.
6. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
7. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather. **Vanish** is the emergency exit.

- **Cheap Shot** (26): the rotation opens with it only when neither **Ambush** nor **Garrote** can go, which the fight lab never saw.
- **Remorseless Attacks** (26-27): the first **Ambush** or strike after a kill is likelier to crit. Pull the next mob within 20 seconds.
- **Initiative** (28-29): from 29 your opener always adds a second combo point.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 25. Presses per kill at level 25: Melee 5.9, Melee (off-hand) 5.4, Sinister Strike 2.4, Shank 1.4, Eviscerate 0.9, Ghostly Strike 0.8, Garrote 0.6, Ambush 0.5, Premeditation 0.1.*

3. Open with **Ambush**.
4. **Shank** whenever it is ready, unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum.

## Between fights

- Bandage when you are below 60% of your health, eat when you are below 40%. That is the tuned rule.
- Reapply your poisons when they run out, and **Stealth** before each pull from 14.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Ambush**, **Garrote** and **Eviscerate** have many ranks, and **Sinister Strike** until **Hemorrhage** replaces it at 32.

## Other ways to level this class
