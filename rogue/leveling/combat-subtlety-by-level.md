# Combat-Subtlety Rogue — leveling, level by level

[Rogue](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Combat-Subtlety](combat-subtlety.md) > Level by level

What a Combat-Subtlety rogue presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. This split plays Subtlety's rotation with a dagger in each hand: **Premeditation** and **Ambush** put combo points on the mob before your first builder, **Ghostly Strike**, its signature strike, goes in on its cooldown, and Combat's talents add hit, dodge and cheaper finishers. **Sinister Strike** stays your builder until **Hemorrhage** arrives at 60. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 5/25/21 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/rogue/60/023-32500300020123202-3051030313010001-12)), the one on the [Combat-Subtlety leveling page](combat-subtlety.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | **Sinister Strike** builds combo points, **Eviscerate** spends them. |
| 6-7 | **Gouge** stuns the mob for a few seconds: a breather. |
| 8-9 | **Evasion**: for half a minute the mob can barely hit you. |
| 10-11 | Talent points start with **Opportunity**: your openers hit harder. Nothing new in the fight itself. |
| 12-13 | **Kick** interrupts a caster mob's spell. This rotation uses it. |
| 14-17 | **Garrote**: open from stealth with a bleed. From here every fight starts in **Stealth**. |
| 18-23 | **Ambush** from stealth: one big hit with your dagger, your opener from here to 60. |
| 24 | **Premeditation** (talent): 2 free combo points before the opener. **Shank** strikes on a cooldown and gives energy. |
| 25-29 | **Ghostly Strike** (talent) on its 20-second cooldown: a big dagger strike and more dodge. A **Garrote** can follow it. |
| 30-39 | Nothing new to press. **Deadly Poison** goes on your off hand. |
| 40-59 | The rotation stays as it is until 60. The points go to Combat's energy and damage talents; buy every new rank. |
| 60 | **Hemorrhage** (talent) takes over from **Sinister Strike** as your builder. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; poisons, food, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the rarer tools. Every spell this spec shares with Combat sits on the Combat key, so your hands do not relearn anything when you switch specs. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the macros: the [rogue keybinds page](keybinds.md).

- The Combat layout for every spell the two share, and the Subtlety layout's keys for the rest: Mouse 4 holds **Ghostly Strike** (no **Riposte** in this build), C **Ambush** (no **Cold Blood**), Shift+Q **Premeditation**, and Q takes **Hemorrhage** at 60. **Garrote** keeps 1 as in every rogue layout: from 18 it is the bleed that follows **Ghostly Strike**. **Kick** stays on Shift+E although this rotation uses it.
- WoW switches bar 1 to a second page while you are in **Stealth** (the stealth bar, as in Classic). Put the same spells in the same slots on it, so every key does the same thing in and out of Stealth.
- Before each pull: Shift+1 (**Stealth**), Shift+Q (**Premeditation**, when it is ready), then C (**Ambush**; 1 for **Garrote** before 18). Stealth and Premeditation are off the global cooldown, so the three go one after the other.
- No key: the poisons, **Slice and Dice** and **Kidney Shot** (the tuned rotation never casts them), **Cheap Shot** and **Backstab** (not in this list), food, bandages, the mount and the Hearthstone.
- Levels 1-5 fight with Q and R. Q is a macro on the [keybinds page](keybinds.md) that also starts your swings, so the middle mouse button stays free; at 60 change its spell to **Hemorrhage**. Add a key when its spell arrives.

## Levels 1-5: Sinister Strike and Eviscerate

**The trainer teaches:** **Eviscerate** (1), **Sinister Strike** (1), Stealth (1), Backstab (4), Pick Pocket (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Sinister Strike; R = Eviscerate.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, R Eviscerate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
2. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

- Carry a dagger in each hand from the start: **Ambush** (18) needs one in your main hand, and **Ghostly Strike** hits much harder with it.
- **Backstab** (4) needs the mob's back, and a mob you fight alone turns to face you after your first hit: never Backstab.
- **Stealth** (1) has nothing to open with yet and is not worth the walk: mobs die in a few hits. It gets its key at 14, with **Garrote**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 10 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Sinister Strike 1.6, Melee 1.3, Melee (off-hand) 1.0, Eviscerate 0.2.*

## Levels 18-23: Ambush

**The trainer teaches:** **Ambush** (18), Poised (18), Crippling Poison (20), Instant Poison (20), Rupture (20), Distract (22), Vanish (22).

**New ranks to buy:** Garrote at 22; Gouge at 18; Sinister Strike at 22; Stealth at 20.

**Talents:** 18-19 Improved Ambush, 20-22 Serrated Blades, 23 Initiative.

**Keys to set:** C = Ambush; 3 = Vanish (at 22).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, C Ambush, Shift+E Kick, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. Open with **Ambush**.
3. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
4. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
5. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather.

- **Ambush** needs a dagger in your main hand and you behind the mob. With **Improved Ambush** it crits often, and the fight lab opens with it on every pull.
- **Poised** (18) makes the opener 10 energy cheaper.
- **Rupture** (20) is never cast: the points are worth more as **Eviscerate**.
- **Serrated Blades** (20-22): your attacks ignore part of the mob's armor. **Initiative** (23): your opener may add a second combo point.
- Put **Instant Poison** on your main hand once you have it (20).

- **Stealth** and **Premeditation** are off the global cooldown: Stealth, Premeditation, then **Ambush**. Use the points within 20 seconds.
- **Mind-numbing Poison** (24) is for casters in groups.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 18 at level 24. Presses per kill at level 24: Melee 5.8, Melee (off-hand) 5.2, Sinister Strike 2.4, Shank 1.4, Ambush 1.0, Eviscerate 0.7, Kick 0.3, Premeditation 0.1.*

- **Remorseless Attacks** (29-30): the first **Ambush** or strike after a kill is likelier to crit. Pull the next mob within 20 seconds.

## Between fights

- Bandage when you are below 60% of your health, eat when you are below 40%. That is the tuned rule.
- Reapply your poisons when they run out, and **Stealth** before each pull from 14.
