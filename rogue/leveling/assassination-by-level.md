# Assassination Rogue — leveling, level by level

[Rogue](../README.md) > [Leveling (solo)](README.md) > [Assassination](assassination.md) > Level by level

What an Assassination rogue presses from level 1 to 60, stage by stage, as the trainer and the talent tree hand you new buttons. Assassination levels with a dagger in each hand: build combo points with **Sinister Strike**, spend them on **Eviscerate**, open from stealth with **Garrote**, and from 40, the first level the tree allows it, build with **Mutilate**, the spec's signature strike. Each stage lists the full priority, so you can jump in at your level.

**The build:** 31/20/0 ([open the talent tree](https://talentsforever.com/rogue/60/02530021211451031-30232300020122--12)), the one on the [Assassination leveling page](assassination.md), which has the full talent table and the reasons behind the level-60 rotation.

## The short version

| Level | What changes |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | **Sinister Strike** builds combo points, **Eviscerate** spends them. |
| 6-7 | **Gouge** stuns the mob for a few seconds: a breather. |
| 8-9 | **Evasion**: for half a minute the mob can barely hit you. |
| 10-11 | Talent points start with **Malice**: more crit with every attack. Nothing new in the fight itself. |
| 12-13 | **Kick** interrupts a caster mob's spell. This rotation uses it. |
| 14-23 | **Garrote**: open from stealth with a bleed. From here every fight starts in **Stealth**. |
| 24 | **Shank**, a strike on a cooldown that also gives energy. Press it whenever it is ready. |
| 25-39 | **Cold Blood** (talent): a guaranteed critical **Eviscerate** every 3 minutes. |
| 40-60 | **Mutilate** (talent) takes over from **Sinister Strike**: both daggers strike, 2 combo points a press. |

## Keybinds by level

Your two action bars, slot by slot: bar 1 is Q, E, R, F, Mouse 5, Mouse 4, 1, 2, 3, 4, C and the middle mouse button; bar 2 is the same keys with Shift, and Shift+X in the last slot. Keys are for spells you press in a fight and for the things that help you there; poisons, food, the mount and the Hearthstone have no key, you click them between fights. The scheme is the same on every class: Q the attack you press most, E the control, R the payoff, F the save, the thumb buttons the reactions, 1-4 the rest, the Shift bar the rarer tools. Every spell this spec shares with Combat sits on the Combat key, so your hands do not relearn anything when you switch specs. A key keeps its job from 1 to 60 and only the spell on it changes. Bold marks what arrives in that stage. Free slots stay free until something earns them. Setup and the macros: the [rogue keybinds page](keybinds.md).

## Levels 1-5: Sinister Strike and Eviscerate

**The trainer teaches:** **Eviscerate** (1), **Sinister Strike** (1), Stealth (1), Backstab (4), Pick Pocket (4).

**Keys to set:** Q = Sinister Strike; R = Eviscerate.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, R Eviscerate.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
2. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

- Carry a dagger in each hand from the start: the build plays daggers, and **Mutilate** (40) needs them.
- **Backstab** (4) needs the mob's back, and a mob you fight alone turns to face you after your first hit: never Backstab.
- **Stealth** (1) has nothing to open with yet and is not worth the walk: mobs die in a few hits. It gets its key at 14, with **Garrote**.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 10 at level 1. Presses per kill at level 1: Sinister Strike 1.6, Melee 1.3, Melee (off-hand) 1.0, Eviscerate 0.2.*

## Levels 14-23: Garrote from stealth

**The trainer teaches:** Expose Armor (14), **Garrote** (14), Feint (16), Ambush (18), Poised (18), Crippling Poison (20), Instant Poison (20), Rupture (20), Distract (22), Vanish (22).

**New ranks to buy:** Eviscerate at 16; Garrote at 22; Gouge at 18; Sinister Strike at 14, 22; Stealth at 20.

**Talents:** 14 Malice, 15-17 Ruthlessness, 18-19 Remorseless Attacks, 20-21 Lethality, 22-23 Vigor.

**Keys to set:** 1 = Garrote; Shift+1 = Stealth; 3 = Vanish (at 22).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, 1 Garrote, Shift+E Kick, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. Open with **Garrote**.
3. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
4. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
5. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather.

- **Poised** (18) makes the opener 10 energy cheaper.
- **Ambush** (18) is not in this list: the rotation opens with **Garrote**, and the fight lab has not tried **Ambush** with it.
- **Expose Armor** (14) and **Rupture** (20) are never cast: the points are worth more as **Eviscerate**.
- **Ruthlessness** (15-17): a finisher often adds a combo point back. **Remorseless Attacks** (18-19) makes your first strike after a kill likelier to crit: pull the next mob within 20 seconds. **Lethality** (20-21) makes those crits bigger, **Vigor** (22-23) is 10 more energy.
- Put **Instant Poison** on your main hand once you have it (20).
- **Vanish** (22) is the emergency exit: the mob loses you, and you walk away.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 19 at level 14, 20 at level 20, 21 at level 23. Presses per kill at level 14: Melee 6.2, Melee (off-hand) 5.7, Sinister Strike 2.5, Garrote 1.0, Eviscerate 0.5, Kick 0.1.*

## Levels 25-39: Cold Blood

**The trainer teaches:** Cheap Shot (26), Deadly Poison (30), Disarm Trap (30), Kidney Shot (30), Wound Poison (32), Blind (34), Blinding Powder (34).

**New ranks to buy:** Eviscerate at 32; Garrote at 30, 38; Gouge at 32; Kick at 26; Sap at 28; Sinister Strike at 30, 38; Sprint at 34.

**Talents:** 25 Cold Blood, 26 Vile Poisons, 27-29 Improved Poisons, 30 Venom, 31 Relentless Strikes, 32 Improved Poisons, 33-34 Relentless Strikes, 35-37 Seal Fate, 38-39 Relentless Strikes.

**Keys to set:** C = Cold Blood; Shift+2 = Blind (at 34).

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Sinister Strike, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, Mouse 5 Shank, 1 Garrote, 3 Vanish, C Cold Blood, Shift+E Kick, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. Open with **Garrote**.
3. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
4. **Shank** whenever it is ready, unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum.
5. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
6. **Cold Blood** right before an **Eviscerate** at 5 points, whenever it is ready.
7. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather. **Vanish** is the emergency exit.

- Most fights below 30 end on a smaller **Eviscerate** that kills, so **Cold Blood** comes up rarely at first.
- **Cheap Shot** (26): off. The tuned rotation never casts it: **Garrote** is the better opener.
- **Vile Poisons** (26) and **Improved Poisons** (27-29, 32) make your poisons hit harder and more often.
- **Deadly Poison** (30) on your off hand, **Instant Poison** on your main hand: buffs you reapply between fights, no key.
- **Venom** (30): a **Shank** that lands makes your poisons stronger for 6 seconds. Nothing new to press.
- **Kidney Shot** (30): off. The tuned rotation never casts it.
- **Relentless Strikes** (31, 33-34, 38-39) gives a finisher a chance to refund 25 energy. **Seal Fate** (35-37): a builder that crits adds a second combo point.
- **Blind** (34) handles a second mob that joins mid-fight.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 22 at level 25, 22 at level 30, 26 at level 39. Presses per kill at level 25: Melee 7.4, Melee (off-hand) 6.9, Sinister Strike 3.6, Shank 1.5, Garrote 1.0, Eviscerate 0.5, Kick 0.3.*

## Levels 40-60: Mutilate

**The trainer teaches:** Safe Fall (40).

**New ranks to buy:** Eviscerate at 40, 48, 56, 60; Garrote at 46, 54; Gouge at 46, 60; Kick at 42, 58; Mutilate at 50, 60; Sap at 48; Shank at 44; Sprint at 58; Stealth at 40, 60; Vanish at 42.

**Talents:** 40 Mutilate, 41-42 Lightning Reflexes, 43-45 Coup de Grace, 46-48 Puncturing Wounds, 49-51 Precision, 52-53 Deflection, 54-55 Composure, 56 Flawless Execution, 57-58 Aggression, 59-60 Weapon Expertise.

**Keys to set:** Q = Mutilate.

**Your keys in this stage:** Q Mutilate, E Gouge, R Eviscerate, F Evasion, Mouse 5 Shank, 1 Garrote, 3 Vanish, C Cold Blood, Shift+E Kick, Shift+1 Stealth.

**What to press**, top to bottom:

1. **Stealth** up behind the mob with full energy.
2. Open with **Garrote**.
3. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
4. **Shank** whenever it is ready, unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum. With **Venom** it also makes your poisons stronger for 6 seconds.
5. **Mutilate** until you have 5 combo points. Wait for its 60 energy rather than press your old builder.
6. **Cold Blood** right before an **Eviscerate** at 5 points, whenever it is ready.
7. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**. Earlier when it would kill the mob.

**If the fight goes wrong:** Below half your health, while the mob has more than 30% of its own: **Evasion**. **Gouge** for a breather. **Vanish** is the emergency exit.

- Q keeps its job: change the Q macro's spell to **Mutilate** (the [macros page](../macros.md) has it).
- **Mutilate** hits 20% harder on a poisoned mob: keep **Deadly Poison** on your off hand.
- From 41 the points go to Combat: **Lightning Reflexes** (41-42) is dodge, **Coup de Grace** (43-45) a bigger **Eviscerate**, **Puncturing Wounds** (46-48) crit on **Mutilate**, **Precision** (49-51) hit, **Deflection** (52-53) parry, **Composure** (54-55) a cheaper opener, **Flawless Execution** (56) a cheaper **Eviscerate**, **Aggression** (57-58) a harder one, **Weapon Expertise** (59-60) fewer dodges and parries.
- **Adrenaline Rush**, **Blade Flurry** and **Riposte** belong to Combat builds. This one does not take them.

*Fight lab, seconds per kill: 24 at level 40, 23 at level 50, 21 at level 60. Presses per kill at level 40: Melee 8.0, Melee (off-hand) 7.6, Mutilate 2.7, Shank 1.8, Eviscerate 1.4, Garrote 1.0, Kick 0.2, Cold Blood 0.1.*

## Between fights

- Bandage when you are below 60% of your health, eat when you are below 40%. That is the tuned rule.
- Reapply your poisons when they run out, and **Stealth** before each pull from 14.
- Visit the trainer every even level: **Sinister Strike**, **Garrote** and **Eviscerate** have many ranks, and **Mutilate** has new ranks at 50 and 60.

## Other ways to level this class

- Combat with swords is the rogue's fastest leveling spec in the fight lab and builds with **Sinister Strike** all the way; the Subtlety and Combat-Subtlety builds open with **Premeditation** and **Ambush** and strike with **Ghostly Strike**. Each has a page in this folder.
