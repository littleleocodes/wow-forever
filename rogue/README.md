# Rogue

> Classic's pure melee DPS: Combat swords or daggers in raids, Seal Fate and Hemorrhage builds in PvP. Forever folds the
> weapon specializations into Hack and Slash, replaces Improved Backstab with Puncturing Wounds, builds Assassination around
> Mutilate and Venom and turns Subtlety into a Rupture spec. Playable by Dwarves, Gnomes, Humans, Night Elves, Orcs, Trolls,
> Undead and Skyborne.

## Forever headline changes (beta client build 1.60.1.70753, 2026-11-03)

Forever's rogue has 54 talents (17 Assassination, 19 Combat, 18 Subtlety). Twelve are new to the class and the others were kept, reworked or moved from Classic. Improved Eviscerate and Improved Sinister Strike are now Coup de Grace and Efficient Strikes.

- **Removed talents:** Improved Backstab (its place taken by Puncturing Wounds), Dagger, Mace, Sword and Fist Weapon Specialization (folded into Hack and Slash), Improved Sap (its place taken by Dirty Tricks), Sleight of Hand, Deadliness. Murder, Improved Distract and Quietus were in the September demo and are gone from the beta.
- **New talents:** Venom (poisons deal 30% more and apply 20% more often for 6 seconds after a Shank), Mutilate (both weapons strike for 90% weapon damage plus a flat amount, 20% more against poisoned targets, 2 combo points; it needs Venom) and Blood Splatter (Rupture 6 seconds and Garrote 9 seconds longer) in Assassination; Puncturing Wounds (Backstab +30% crit and a 45% chance of an extra combo point, Mutilate +15% crit), Composure (Poised costs 10 more energy less), Flawless Execution (Eviscerate costs 15 less), Hack and Slash (swords and axes 5% extra attacks, daggers and fists 5% crit, maces 15% armor ignored) and Flash of Steel (Shank gives 15 more energy and recovers 2 seconds sooner) in Combat; Dirty Tricks, Quick Getaway, Boot Knife (Shank adds 2 combo points, 100% at 5 ranks; it was Cutthroat in earlier builds) and Death Mark (a finisher that stores damage for 6 seconds; it was Thousand Cuts) in Subtlety.
- **Moved or reworked:** Improved Gouge moves to Assassination; Cold Blood is a 16-point talent and Premeditation an 11-point one; Seal Fate no longer needs Cold Blood and has 3 ranks. Deflection, Precision and Master of Deception have 3 ranks, Opportunity 5 (+20% to Backstab, Garrote, Ambush and Mutilate), Lethality 2 (+20% crit damage), Improved Kidney Shot 2, Vigor 2. Malice covers poisons; Improved Expose Armor makes Expose Armor 10 energy cheaper and hands back 2 combo points; Weapon Expertise takes 2% off your chance to be dodged or parried; Dirty Deeds lets Garrote be used from the front and makes it 20 energy cheaper; Coup de Grace also strengthens Death Mark by 20%; Setup is a 45% chance at 3 ranks.
- **New and changed spells:** Shank (level 24; 5 energy, 10-second cooldown, strikes with the off hand for 50% weapon damage and gives 15 energy, 25 from rank 2 at 44) and Poised (level 18; the first Energy ability after you reach full energy out of combat costs 10 less) are new, and Mutilate and Death Mark are talent-taught. Hemorrhage deals 100% weapon damage (145% with a dagger) and adds 15% to your Rupture and Garrote damage; Ghostly Strike deals 180% with a dagger and lets the next Ambush or Garrote within 7 seconds skip Stealth; Rupture hits about 40% and Instant and Deadly Poison about 30% less than in Classic, the poisons with more charges; Expose Armor removes 450 armor a point at rank 5; Riposte disarms for 6 seconds; Sap no longer breaks Stealth.
- **Beta notes (to 2026-10-08):** poisons go on a weapon together with a sharpening stone or weightstone, rogues can use one-handed axes (Blizzard's rogue deep dive, 2026-10-07).
- **Beta notes (16 Oct, build 70425):** no talent or spell changed; poisons no longer trigger against targets that attack you; Sap no longer triggers weapon procs; Cold Blood is used up when Mutilate crits; swapping specializations no longer keeps your energy full.
- **Talent data since 70425 (the 2026-11-03 snapshot):** Venom became a Shank effect and the prerequisite of Mutilate (it was a finisher), Mutilate went from 75% to 90% weapon damage, Seal Fate from 5 ranks to 3, Aggression from +6% to +15% (and from Backstab to Eviscerate and Sinister Strike), Dual Wield Specialization from +10% to +50% off-hand damage, and Cutthroat and Thousand Cuts became Boot Knife and Death Mark. Blood Splatter, Composure, Flash of Steel and Quick Getaway were added.
