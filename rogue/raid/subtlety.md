## How to play

**Keep up all fight:** **Slice and Dice**.

**Then press the first one that is ready**, top to bottom:

1. **Expose Armor**: keep it on the target, when you have 5 combo points
2. **Vanish** when Dice is active
3. **Premeditation**
4. **Garrote**
5. **Rupture**: keep it on the target
6. **Eviscerate** when you have 5 combo points and Slice and Dice is active
7. **Hemorrhage**

**Hemorrhage** is your filler: cast it whenever nothing above is ready.

**Advanced: your cooldowns and extras.** Once the list above feels easy, add these:

- **Cold Blood**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 2 times a fight)
- **Preparation**: a cooldown, use it at the start of the fight and again whenever it is ready (about 1 time a fight)
- Use your trinkets, your racial and a potion together with your big cooldowns.
