# Fight lab — rogue combat-daggers-raid (opt-combat-daggers-raid-leveling-st)

> Generated by `_reference/data/fightlab/run.py` (Monte Carlo fight simulator; 2000 fights per configuration, seed 1, mobs at player level +0, gear tier `greens`). Spell numbers: beta client 1.60.1.70753 (`_reference/data/forever-spells/rogue.json`); talent numbers: the beta tooltips; mob health, damage, armor and the caster share by level: the Classic 1.12 creature database (`_reference/data/fightlab/mob_stats.json`, unverified for Forever); everything else (gear, Classic combat formulas, drink rates) is an assumption listed in `_reference/data/fightlab/tables.py`. Do not hand-edit.

## What was simulated

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with two swords (the daggers models: two daggers, still Sinister Strike: the mob faces you after the opener, so no Backstab): open from stealth behind the mob with Cheap Shot (Garrote before 26; the mob turns to face you after the opener, so it can parry from then on; Poised makes the opener 10 energy cheaper), Slice and Dice at 1-2 points when it is down, Shank whenever its energy would not overflow (from 24), Sinister Strike to 5 points, Eviscerate (Kidney Shot instead when below 60% health, stunning for the tooltip's seconds per point), Eviscerate at fewer points when it would kill the mob, Cold Blood right before a 5-point Eviscerate, Riposte after a parry, Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush on the pull, Evasion when low; white swings with both hands (19% extra miss on white dual-wield hits), Instant Poison on the main hand and Deadly Poison on the off hand. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, a bandage below 60% health (First Aid, 60 s cooldown), food below 65%; a death costs 90 s. The tuned rules (rotations/rogue-<spec>.json) veto spells and move the thresholds on top of this list.

## Assumptions (edit `tables.py` to change them)

- Mob health Median health of a normal outdoor mob at the level, from the Classic 1.12 creature database (cmangos classic-db z2815, 2,262
templates weighted by spawn count: 187 at 10, 466 at 20, 1,002 at 30, 1,844 at 40, 2,672 at 50, 3,878 at 60; `mob_stats.json`).
Unverified for WoW: Forever (creature stats are server side; the Wowhead Forever tooltips carry level and type only).
- Mob melee Median melee swing of a normal mob before armor, from the Classic 1.12 creature database (13 at 10, 32 at 20, 48 at 30, 68 at 40,
95 at 50, 117 at 60 per 2.0 s swing; the earlier rule of thumb was 2-2.5x higher). Unverified for WoW: Forever.
- Caster mobs: the creature database share by level (Share of normal outdoor mob spawns whose unit class is mage or paladin, by level, from the Classic 1.12 creature database
(5% at 1-5, 14% at 10, 12-23% from 15 to 55, 9% at 60; `mob_stats.json`).); a caster stands at up to 30 yd and casts a bolt every 3.5 s for 1.6x its melee swing (spell damage, no armor) until interrupted or stunned, then runs in and melees until the lockout ends; Median health of a mage-class mob (Classic 1.12 creature database: 75-90% of the all-mob median).
- Runners: 0% of pulls flee at 20% health for 5 s (roots and stuns hold them; `RUNNER_SHARE`, 0 = not simulated, see sensitivity.md).
- Spell miss: Classic spell miss vs a mob `level_diff` levels above the caster: 4% at +0, 5% at +1, 6% at +2, 17% at +3. Partial resists cost 0.0% on average.
- Spell crit: 0.91% base plus Intellect / (Intellect per 1% spell crit for mages: the client's PlayerExpectedStat by level (59.5 at 60; 2026-10-09), else 59.5 x L / 60.)
- Pushback: 0.5s for the first melee hit taken during a cast, 0.5s per further hit; a direct hit breaks a freeze with 20% chance.
- Gear: Gear stats by level from the Classic 1.12 item database (`gear_stats.json`, built by gear_stats.py): every slot holds a typical
good pick among the fixed-stat green items (GEAR_TIER "greens", mostly quest rewards) or the blue items ("blues") the class can wear,
with required level 2-7 levels below the character (a mage at 40: Int 87, Spirit 66, Stamina 14; a warrior at 40: Str 86, Sta 79,
armor 2,369 in plate). Spell power and healing come from the items' on-equip spells (spell_template auras 13 / 135; a
school-specific bonus counts half). Classic items: unverified for WoW: Forever. GEAR_STAT_MULT scales the stats (sensitivity). Wand: Wand dps by level from the Classic 1.12 item database (median green wand with required level 2-7 below the character:
11 at 10, 17 at 20, 26 at 30, 33 at 40, 41 at 50, 55 at 60; blues 25 / 33 / 38 / 51 / 63 from 20).
- Regen: Vanilla mage/priest mana regen per 2-second tick while not casting: 13 + Spirit / 4. Drinking: Conjured water (mana per second while drinking; unverified against the 70425 items, audit of 2026-10-18 row 73), Forever beta tooltips (Wowhead Forever items 5350/2288/2136/3772/8077/8078/8079, read 2026-09-26) at the
Conjure Water rank levels 4/10/20/30/40/50/60: 145/18s, 420/21s, 803/24s, 1293/27s, 1915/30s, 2821/30s, 4038/30s mana.
- Between fights: loot 2s, travel 6s, drink below 35% mana, eat below 65% health, a death costs 90s.
- Not modelled yet: runners, adds, the stealth approach time, Expose Armor, Rupture by default (Eviscerate is used; the RUPTURE_HP knob lets rotation_opt try it at 5 points), Garrote's attack power bonus (its tooltip gives no number), Gouge-and-bandage, Vanish, Sprint, weapon skill, maces (Hack and Slash's armor share), gear upgrades within a level bracket, Death Mark's storage rate (an assumption, DEATH_MARK_STORE) and its cap, whether Shank is on the global cooldown (1.0 s assumed, like every rogue ability) and whether a dodged or parried Shank still gives its energy (assumed not), Ghostly Strike's stealthless Ambush (needs the mob's back).
