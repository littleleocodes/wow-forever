## subtlety-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Combat-Subtlety](../../leveling/combat-subtlety.md) ([`combat-subtlety-open-leveling`](builds/combat-subtlety-open-leveling.md)), [Subtlety](../../leveling/subtlety.md) ([`subtlety-leveling`](builds/subtlety-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: Daggers in both hands: **Instant Poison** on your main hand, **Deadly Poison** on your off hand. Stealth up behind the mob. **Premeditation** for two free combo points.

1. Open with **Ambush** while fights are short; from the 30s on, **Garrote** (its bleed ignores armor and the mob lives through it).
2. **Ghostly Strike** whenever it is ready (it also makes you dodge more).
3. **Sinister Strike** to build combo points.
4. Once you have **Hemorrhage**, it replaces **Sinister Strike** as the builder.
5. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder; earlier when it would kill the mob.
6. **Riposte** after you parry.
7. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
8. **Evasion** when your health gets low.
9. If **Evasion** is on cooldown when you need it, **Preparation** resets it.

Between fights: Bandage below 60% health, eat below half.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-15, on top of the priority below): eat below 40% health; never cast Slice and Dice; never cast Kidney Shot; never cast Kick. Together +8.5% time per kill saved for `combat-subtlety-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`combat-subtlety-open-leveling`](builds/combat-subtlety-open-leveling.md), [`subtlety-leveling`](builds/subtlety-leveling.md): not recorded (their times per kill come from the race records of the searches that found them, which keep times only; the table above has them).

## combat-swords-raid

Played by the page build of [Combat](../../leveling/combat.md) ([`combat-leveling`](builds/combat-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with two swords (the daggers models: two daggers, still Sinister Strike: the mob faces you after the opener, so no Backstab): open from stealth behind the mob with Cheap Shot (Garrote before 26; the mob turns to face you after the opener, so it can parry from then on), Slice and Dice at 1-2 points when it is down, Sinister Strike to 5 points, Eviscerate (Kidney Shot instead when below 60% health, stunning for the tooltip's seconds per point), Eviscerate at fewer points when it would kill the mob, Cold Blood right before a 5-point Eviscerate, Riposte after a parry, Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush on the pull, Evasion when low; white swings with both hands (19% extra miss on white dual-wield hits), Instant Poison on the main hand and Deadly Poison on the off hand. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, a bandage below 60% health (First Aid, 60 s cooldown), food below 65%; a death costs 90 s. The tuned rules (rotations/rogue-<spec>.json) veto spells and move the thresholds on top of this list.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Instant Poison** on your main hand, **Deadly Poison** on your off hand. Stealth up behind the mob.

1. Open with **Garrote**.
2. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
3. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**; earlier when it would kill the mob.
4. **Cold Blood** right before an **Eviscerate** at 5 points.
5. **Riposte** after you parry.
6. **Blade Flurry** and **Adrenaline Rush** at the start of a fight.
7. **Evasion** when your health gets low.

Between fights: Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40%.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-10-09, on top of the priority below): never cast Cheap Shot; eat below 40% health; never cast Slice and Dice; never cast Kidney Shot; never cast Kick. Together +15.6% time per kill saved for `leveling-combat-swords` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).
