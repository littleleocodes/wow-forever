## combat-swords-raid

Played by the page build of [Combat](../../leveling/combat.md) ([`combat-open-leveling`](builds/combat-open-leveling.md)).

Single-target leveling fights against normal mobs of your level with two swords (the daggers models: two daggers, still Sinister Strike: the mob faces you after the opener, so no Backstab): open from stealth behind the mob with Cheap Shot (Garrote before 26; the mob turns to face you after the opener, so it can parry from then on; Poised makes the opener 10 energy cheaper), Slice and Dice at 1-2 points when it is down, Shank whenever its energy would not overflow (from 24), Sinister Strike to 5 points, Eviscerate (Kidney Shot instead when below 60% health, stunning for the tooltip's seconds per point), Eviscerate at fewer points when it would kill the mob, Cold Blood right before a 5-point Eviscerate, Riposte after a parry, Blade Flurry and Adrenaline Rush on the pull, Evasion when low; white swings with both hands (19% extra miss on white dual-wield hits), Instant Poison on the main hand and Deadly Poison on the off hand. Between fights: 2 s loot, 6 s travel, a bandage below 60% health (First Aid, 60 s cooldown), food below 65%; a death costs 90 s. The tuned rules (rotations/rogue-<spec>.json) veto spells and move the thresholds on top of this list.

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: **Instant Poison** on your main hand, **Deadly Poison** on your off hand. Stealth up behind the mob at full energy: **Poised** (level 18) makes your opener 10 energy cheaper.

1. Open with **Garrote**.
2. **Shank** whenever it is ready (level 24), unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum.
3. **Sinister Strike** until you have 5 combo points.
4. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**; earlier when it would kill the mob.
5. **Cold Blood** (a talent; 3-minute cooldown) right before an **Eviscerate** at 5 points.
6. **Riposte** after you parry.
7. **Blade Flurry** and **Adrenaline Rush** at the start of a fight.
8. Do not interrupt a caster mob: let it cast and walk in.
9. **Evasion** below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health.

Between fights: Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40%.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-03, on top of the priority below): never cast Cheap Shot; never cast Slice and Dice; eat below 40% health; never cast Kick; never cast Kidney Shot. Together +15.7% time per kill saved for `leveling-combat-swords` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

## subtlety-leveling

Played by the page builds of [Combat-Subtlety](../../leveling/combat-subtlety.md) ([`combat-subtlety-open-leveling`](builds/combat-subtlety-open-leveling.md)), [Subtlety](../../leveling/subtlety.md) ([`subtlety-open-leveling`](builds/subtlety-open-leveling.md)).

The priority in the player page's words:

Before the fight: Daggers in both hands: **Instant Poison** on your main hand, **Deadly Poison** on your off hand. Stealth up behind the mob at full energy: **Poised** (level 18) makes your opener 10 energy cheaper. **Premeditation** for two free combo points.

1. Open with **Ambush** in short fights; **Garrote** once its bleed outlasts the fight (the fight lab picks **Garrote** from about level 22 with the Subtlety build; the Combat-Subtlety build opens with **Ambush** on every pull from level 18).
2. **Ghostly Strike** whenever it is ready (it also makes you dodge more). With **Dirty Deeds**, follow it with a **Garrote** when none is bleeding and the mob has more than half its health.
3. **Shank** whenever it is ready (level 24), unless the energy it gives would go over your maximum; with **Boot Knife** also to build combo points.
4. **Sinister Strike** to build combo points; once you have **Hemorrhage**, it replaces **Sinister Strike**.
5. Once you have **Death Mark** (level 40): press it whenever it is ready, from one combo point on (the tuned rule), while the mob has more than 40% of its health; it hits back with part of your damage 6 seconds later.
6. At 5 points: **Eviscerate**, waiting for the energy rather than pressing another builder; earlier when it would kill the mob.
7. **Riposte** after you parry.
8. **Kick** a caster mob's spell.
9. **Evasion** below half health while the mob still has more than 30% of its health; if it is on cooldown, **Preparation** resets it.

Between fights: Bandage below 60% health, eat below 40%.

**Tuned rules** (`fightlab/rotation_opt.py`, 2026-11-06, on top of the priority below): never cast Slice and Dice; eat below 40% health; DEATH_MARK_CP = 1; never cast Kidney Shot. Together +10.5% time per kill saved for `subtlety-open-leveling` (level-weighted, a third seed of 3000 fights per level).

Casts per kill of [`combat-subtlety-open-leveling`](builds/combat-subtlety-open-leveling.md), [`subtlety-open-leveling`](builds/subtlety-open-leveling.md): not recorded (their times per kill come from the race records of the searches that found them, which keep times only; the table above has them).
