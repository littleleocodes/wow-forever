## Summary

The burst PvP rogue: Cold Blood + Improved Kidney Shot + Seal Fate from Assassination, Improved Backstab from Combat, and Master of Deception/Opportunity from Subtlety for the Ambush opener. Combo points come fast (Seal Fate) so Kidney Shot -> Cold Blood Eviscerate is always available.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Seal Fate** — Extra combo point on crits — with Improved Backstab you build 5 points in two or three globals.
2. **Cold Blood** — Guaranteed crit on the next Eviscerate/Ambush.
3. **Improved Kidney Shot** — +9% damage taken during the stun.
4. **Lethality** — +30% crit damage on Backstab/Sinister Strike.
5. **Improved Backstab** — +30% Backstab crit chance feeds Seal Fate.
6. **Opportunity** — +20% Ambush/Backstab damage.
7. **Master of Deception** — Stealth level; get the opener.
8. **Elusiveness** — Vanish/Blind cooldown reduction.
