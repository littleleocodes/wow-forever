## Summary

The control-and-reset PvP rogue: Preparation doubles Vanish/Sprint/Evasion/Cold Blood, Hemorrhage is a cheap builder that works with any weapon, and Improved Ambush + Initiative give a devastating stealth opener. Less sustained damage than Seal Fate, far more outplay potential.

## Core talents (priority order)

1. **Preparation** — Resets Vanish, Sprint, Evasion, Cold Blood and Blind.
2. **Hemorrhage** — Cheap 35-energy builder, adds a physical damage debuff; works with a slow sword or mace.
3. **Cold Blood** — Guaranteed crit Ambush or Eviscerate.
4. **Improved Ambush** — +40% Ambush crit chance.
5. **Initiative** — Extra combo point on the opener.
6. **Dirty Deeds** — Cheaper Cheap Shot/Garrote.
7. **Serrated Blades** — Armor penetration and Rupture damage; prerequisite for Hemorrhage.
